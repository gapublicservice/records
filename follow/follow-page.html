Prefer the Commission's own alerts?

The Public Service Commission emails a short notice each time a new document is added to a docket you subscribe to. It needs a free account on the Commission's site.

  1. Go to psc.ga.gov and choose Login, then Register. Give your name, zip code, email and a password.
  2. Open the email the Commission sends and confirm your address.
  3. Sign in, search for the docket number with the Commission's search, and tick the Subscribe box.

The Commission's notices list the new document number only. Ours add the filer, the title and the plain-language markers from the record.

An independent project sponsored by GCVEF. Not a product of the Georgia Public Service Commission.
