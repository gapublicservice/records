Question: 
Refer to PIA 4-25 and the contingency amounts associated with Yates 320 MW BESS (Phase 1) and the Yates 250 MW BESS (Phase 2). Please explain why the contingency percent on Phase 2 (22%) is greater than Phase 1 (19%).

Response:
The variance in contingency between Yates 320 MW (Phase I) and Yates 250 MW (Phase II) is primarily attributable to differences in finalizing the footprint for each project. The site for Yates 320 MW (Phase I) was selected earlier in the development process, providing the Company with additional time to refine estimates for transmission and Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (“EPC”) costs. In contrast, the location for Yates 250 MW (Phase II) was finalized later, resulting in increased contingency to address potential uncertainties. Further, Yates 320 MW (Phase I) is situated on the existing Yates Units 8, 9, 10 laydown area, which minimizes civil construction challenges and offers closer proximity to the interconnecting substation. These factors contributed to reduced unknowns and, consequently, a lower contingency requirement for Yates 320 MW (Phase I).
