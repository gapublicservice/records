The “Embedded kW in Denom” denotes the capacity value assumed for the initial dollar per kilowatt (“$/kW”) cost inputs, as applicable. This applies to the following components that are listed as NA: Capital revenue requirements (“RRs”), Maintenance Cap RRs, Fixed O&M, Equity Costs, Fixed Fuel Costs, Existing power purchase agreement (“PPA”) Revenue, Transmission Costs, and Interconnection Costs. 

Capacity payments are calculated based on the PPA’s designated capacity, which means the embedded kW in the original $/kW cost input reflects that designated capacity. For the final Capacity Payments component, the designated capacity $/kW value is converted to a winter capacity $/kW basis. 

The Energy Benefits, Term Equalization, Derate Equalization, and Renewable Integration Credit components are already input on a winter capacity $/kW basis and are labeled as such.

Ultimately, the “Embedded kW in Denom” column is used to convert each component from its respective input cost format to a consistent winter $/kW basis.

The “Modify kW for ELCC” column is used to convert the “PV $/kW (Pre ELCC)” result to the “PV $/kW (Post ELCC result)” in columns K and M, respectively. It is used to denote which components were calculated based on the resource-specific winter capacity for the “PV $/kW (pre ELCC)” result and require a conversion to the resource-specific winter ELCC capacity $/kW basis. The Term Equalization, Derate Equalization, and Renewable Integration Credit components do not need to be modified as each is calculated independently on a $/kW basis and does not rely on resource-specific capacity inputs.

This component captures the projected replacement capacity costs for this period, adjusting for the change in capacity contribution during this time when only the solar resource is contributing.
