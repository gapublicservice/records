Dear Ms. Tanner:

On April 17, 2025, the Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) issued its Order
on Georgia Power Company’s Revision to Rules and Regulations Tariff Compliance Filing (the
“Order”). The Order requires Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) to
file contracts with large load customers at least thirty (30) days prior to the execution of the
contract.

In compliance with the Order, the Company is now filing a fourth large load contract for
Commission Staff review prior to execution. The unexecuted contract as well as all supporting
documents and information required by the Order are trade secret in their entirety.

Please call Mr. Steven Huling at (404) 506-1366 if you have any questions regarding this filing.
