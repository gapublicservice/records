Question: 
Is the Company expecting DOE Loan savings for its proposed Wadley BESS project?  If so, please provide the anticipated savings and provide workpapers, electronically with all formulae intact. If not, explain why not.

Response:
The Company anticipates receiving a Department of Energy (“DOE”) loan for the Wadley Battery Energy Storage System (“BESS”), but this benefit was excluded from the financial analysis due to uncertainty at the time of filing about the program’s continuation for BESS projects. STF-PIA-20-5

Question: 
In the event that the Commission certifies less than the five combined cycle projects proposed by the Company, please provide:
All analyses the Company has performed regarding the potential to sell, release, or otherwise mitigate the costs of the unneeded FT Capacity.
The proposed ratemaking treatment that the Company would request for all FT costs associated with the unneeded FT capacity.

Response:
There has been no analysis performed around mitigating the cost of unneeded firm transportation (“FT”) capacity.
All FT costs would be recovered through the Fuel Cost Recovery (“FCR”) tariff and any potential savings obtained by mitigating unneeded FT would be netted against any such recovery.


===== DKT 56298 & 56310 STF-PIA-20 PD/STF-PIA-20-3.docx =====
STF-PIA-20-3

Question: 
Refer to STF-PIA-17-13, specifically concerning FT related costs.
Provide a timeline of contract dates and reservation fees paid to date. 
If only the Wansley CC resources were approved, and the McIntosh and Bowen CC resources were not, what costs have been spent to date by resource, and that are not transferrable?
Please describe the process the Company would use to remarket the FT capacity?  

Response:
The Transco – Southeast Supply Enhancement (“SSE”) project (Plant Bowen and Wansley combined cycles (“CCs”)) projected in-service date is November 1, 2027, and the Southern Natural Gas (“SNG”) – South System 4 (“SS4”) project (Plant McIntosh CC) projected in-service date is November 1, 2028. No firm transportation (“FT”) reservation fees have been paid to date.  FT costs do not start until the project is declared in-service by the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (“FERC”). 
No costs have been spent to date. 
If remarketing FT were required, Southern Company Services (“SCS”), as agent for Georgia Power, would initiate a request for proposals (“RFP”) to market the FT on Transco and SNG to other pipeline shippers. SCS would evaluate proposals on the FT capacity that recover the highest savings for customers.
