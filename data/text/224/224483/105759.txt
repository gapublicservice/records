Dylan Drugan
Senior Consultant 
Dylan advises utilities and infrastructure developers advancing transmission and generation projects including wind, solar, storage, and gas. His experience includes integrated resource planning that optimizes generation resource selection and integrates transmission and distribution planning. He advises clients on how resource selections impact grid reliability, congestion, and operational flexibility. Dylan has testified before multiple state regulatory agencies.
