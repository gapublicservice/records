STF-PIA-35-1

Question: 

Refer to 2024 ASR Section 2 Page 2 of 2 “Sales of Electricity – Non- Fuel”. Allocate the $7,647 million among the following eight rate groups: Domestic, Small Business, Medium Business, Large Business, Agriculture, Government / Institutional, Outdoor Lighting and Marginally Priced. For each rate group provide calendar MWhs sold. Breakout RTP sales in dollars and MWhs separately for each rate group if applicable. Provide information in an unprotected Excel worksheet.

Response:

Please see STF-PIA-35-1 Attachment for a breakout of revenues identifiable by rate group for years 2020 through 2024, along with the associated MWhs. Further, a reconciliation of identifiable revenues by rate group to Total Retail Sales of Electricity is included. 

RTP revenues and MWhs are all included in the Marginal rate group and thus not broken out from other rate groups.
