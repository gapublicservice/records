STF-PIA-12-3
Question: 
Referring to the B2026 Load Realization Model and the Q2 2025 Large Load Economic Development Report, Column H, “Project Stage,” the Company explains that for certain projects “A Contract for Electric Service is currently signed for half of the Announced Load.” Please explain how the Company incorporates projects with this designation into the Load Realization Model, specifically discussing how the Company modifies announced load, load ramp, and project success assumptions, in addition to any other adjustments made for these projects.

Response:
The specific project referenced has an executed Contract for Electric Service (“CES”) for half of the announced load and a Request for Service (“RFS”) for the entirety of its announced load. The Company models the entirety of this project as fully contracted. However, due to the small difference between P2 probabilities for projects with a fully executed CES compared with projects with an RFS, if the Company were to apply separate assumptions to the two pieces of this project, the impact on the forecast would be negligible. The Company aims to separate such projects in future iterations of the forecast for enhanced accuracy of its projections.
