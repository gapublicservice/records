DIRECT TESTIMONY OF ACCION GROUP, LLC. 
INDEPENDENT EVALUATORS FOR THE GEORGIA POWER COMPANY 
ON BEHALF OF THE GEORGIA PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

In Re:  2029-2031 ALL SOURCE CAPACITY 
REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS

DOCKET NO. 55268

Q. Please state your name and business address.
A.My name is Harold T. Judd.  I am the President of Accion Group, LLC (Accion Group).  Accion Group served as the Independent Evaluator for the 2022-2028 Capacity (Capacity) RFP.  Accion Group’s business address is 244 North Main Street, Concord, NH  03301.
My name is Marcus Jackson. I am a Senior Consultant with Accion Group.
My name is Nicholas Wintermantel.  I am a Consultant with Accion Group.

Q.Please discuss your educational background.
A.  Mr. Judd:  I graduated from the University of Wisconsin at Madison in 1974 with a B.A. Degree.  In 1978, I received a law degree from the University of New Hampshire School of Law, formerly the Franklin Pierce Law Center.  Since then, I have continued my education through professional education courses.
Mr. Jackson: I graduated from Southern Illinois University in Carbondale with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering Technology, and graduated from 
Rockhurst University, Kansas City, Missouri with a Master of Business Administration.  My education includes Nuclear Engineering course work at the University of Missouri and at the United States Air Force Academy in Colorado Springs, Colorado.  
Mr. Wintermantel:  I graduated from the University of Alabama in 2003 with a B.S. Degree in Mechanical Engineering and graduated from the University of Alabama at Birmingham with a Master of Business Administration in 2007. 
Please discuss your professional experience.
A.Mr. Judd:  I began my career in 1978 in New Hampshire as the Deputy Consumer Advocate for the Legislative Utilities Consumer Counsel. I left that office as Acting Consumer Advocate in 1979 to become the Deputy General Counsel and Energy Advisor to the Special Assistant to President Carter in the White House Office of Consumer Affairs.  In 1981, I was appointed an Assistant Solicitor for the Department of Energy.  From 1985 through 1987, I served as the Economic Development Advisor to the Congress of the Federated States of Micronesia (FSM), and as Special Counsel to the President of the FSM.  From September 1989 until January 1994, I served in the Office of the Attorney General for the State of New Hampshire, first as an Assistant Attorney General and later as a Senior Assistant Attorney General.  Since then, I have represented regulators and utilities in the electric, telephone and water utility industries, with issues ranging from deregulation, siting and decommissioning nuclear power plants, and bankruptcies.  Since 2003 I have served as an Independent Evaluator or Independent Administrator for over 140 competitive  procurements in Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, North Carolina, Montana, Oregon, Puerto Rico, South Carolina, Louisiana, Mississippi, New Mexico, Alabama, New Hampshire, Texas and Wyoming.  I co-founded Accion Group, LLC in 2001. 
Mr. Jackson: Immediately before joining Accion Group I was Chairman of the Board, President and CEO of SEMCO Energy with headquarters in Michigan, a firm with diversified business lines including gas pipelines and construction companies.  SEMCO Energy also owned Enstar Natural Gas Company in Wasilla, Alaska.  For much of my career I was with Kansas City Power and Light, where I began as a Plant Maintenance Engineer, eventually rising to Executive Vice President and President of KCPL Power.  I served in a variety of position, including Chief Financial Officer of KCP&L and Senior Vice President of Power Production.  Also, I served as Chief Operating Officer for KCP&L, with responsibility for the operation of all generation units.  Prior to joining KCP&L I was the Project Engineer for Gibbs & Hill Engineering – Dravo Arabia for the construction of power generation in Alkhobar, Saudi Arabia.  
Mr. Wintermantel: I began my career in 2000 at Southern Company as a co-op student working within Southern Nuclear.  In 2003, I was hired as an engineer within the resource planning functions of Southern Company and served in a number of roles including integrated resource planning, structuring of wholesale power contracts, system model development, and asset management.  From 2009 to 2024, I was employed by Astrapé Consulting, and served as a consultant for Accion Group, serving as Independent Evaluator for multiple procurements in Georgia, Arizona, Oregon, California, and Colorado, reviewing modeling methodologies to evaluate supplies in each of those regions.   As a consultant, I have also performed system reliability and economic studies for regulators, utilities, and Regional Transmission Organizations (RTOs), including entities such as the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC), the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT), the Midcontinent Independent System Operator (MISO), the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA), and Pacific Gas & Electric (PG&E).    In April of 2024, Astrapé Consulting merged with PowerGEM, and I currently serve as Chief Services officer for PowerGEM.  PowerGEM provides state-of-the-art power system simulation software and services to ensure power grid reliability and optimize market efficiency in the electric power industry.

Q.Would you please describe your role in this proceeding?
A.Mr. Judd:   Accion Group was selected by of the Commission to be the Independent Evaluator for the Capacity Request for Proposals (RFP).  In that role we were involved in all major activities in the RFP, including the development of the RFP process, the drafting of RFP related documents, the receipt of bids, the evaluation of bids and post-selection discussions with the successful Bidders.  I participated in the Bidders Conference and participated in all meetings between Georgia Power and any Bidder, as required by the Rules of the Commission.  In addition, Accion Group created and maintained the website through which the RFP was conducted.  I directed the evaluation and analysis performed by Accion consultants, which is documented in the report prepared for the Commission.   
Mr. Jackson was responsible for reviewing the Asset Purchase Sale Agreement (APSA) for completeness. Mr. Jackson was responsible for review of MTR conformity and the on-site inspection of projects.  Mr. Wintermantel was responsible for reviewing the Company's evaluation methodology, conducting a mock bid process, and performing a parallel evaluation of bids submitted for the Capacity RFP.
