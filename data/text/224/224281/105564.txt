Performance Security

Structure and Rationale: Please explain the rationale for using a tiered performance security structure in the All-Source RFP PPA versus the fixed or customized structure in the Supplemental Resources PPA.

What considerations led to the declining collateral requirements over the term in the All-Source RFP PPA?

Collateral Form Requirements: Why does the All-Source RFP PPA require at least 50% of the performance security to be in the form of a letter of credit or cash if the guarantor is not investment grade? Was this requirement considered or applied in the Supplemental Resources PPA?

Draw and Replenishment Rights: Please describe any differences in the draw rights and replenishment obligations between the two PPAs. How do these differences reflect the perceived risk profiles of the respective projects?

Release Conditions: What are the specific conditions under which performance security is released in each PPA? How do these conditions differ, and what was the rationale for those differences?

Negotiation Flexibility: Was the performance security structure in the Supplemental Resources PPA the result of bilateral negotiation? If so, why was a more standardized approach used in the All-Source RFP PPA?

Change of Law
Definition and Scope: Please explain the differences in how “Change of Law” is defined in the All-Source RFP PPA versus the Supplemental Resources PPA. Why was a more detailed and capital expenditure-focused definition adopted in the All-Source RFP PPA?

Cost Recovery Mechanism: The All-Source RFP PPA includes a formal process for the seller to seek recovery of Change of Law Capital Expenditures. Why is this process not present in the Supplemental Resources PPA?

Threshold and Termination Rights: What was the rationale for including a 10% threshold and a seller termination right in the All-Source RFP PPA? Was a similar mechanism considered for the Supplemental Resources PPA?

GPC Discretion: In the All-Source RFP PPA, GPC has sole discretion to accept or reject proposed cost recovery. How does this compare to the approach in the Supplemental Resources PPA, and what are the implications for risk allocation?

Operational Alternatives: The All-Source RFP PPA allows the seller to propose operational alternatives to avoid capital expenditures. Was this concept considered in the Supplemental Resources PPA? If not, why?

Impact on Capacity Pricing: How does the All-Source RFP PPA’s Change of Law provision affect the Annual Capacity Price? Why is there no similar pricing adjustment mechanism in the Supplemental Resources PPA?
