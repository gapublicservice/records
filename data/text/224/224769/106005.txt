STF-PIA-21-3

Please refer to the Company’s response to 56298 & 56310 STF-PIA-18-4.

Question: 

Identify all customers since January 1, 2023 who have had a bill where fuel expense exceeded or equaled the total revenue the customer was contractually obligated to pay in each billing period. Please provide response in an unprotected Excel worksheet. For these customers provide: 
The start and end date of each billing period where fuel expense exceeded or equaled the total revenue the customer was contractually obligated to pay.
The cost of fuel in the billing period.
The total revenue the customer was contractually obligated to pay each billing period.

Response:

The requested information is provided through STF-PIA-21-3 Attachment TRADE SECRET.

The fuel accounting for Flex REC, Simple Solar, CRSP 1, CRSP 2, and REDI are completed at the program level and not at the customer level.  As a result, billing associated with those programs were excluded from this analysis.
