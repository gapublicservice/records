STF-ASR-7-1
Question:
Recent news announcements state that in August 2025, Georgia Power purchased River Park 10, an 825,000 square foot warehouse, located in Jackson, GA, from Waterloo Partners and ICM Property Group for a reported sale price of $94.75 million. 
In what account(s) did Georgia Power record the $94.75 million purchase of the River Park 10 warehouse?  Explain fully.
Please provide the accounting journal entries and any other documentation in support of the $94.75 million purchase.  
Provide a breakout of the purchase price between (1) land and (2) depreciable assets.
Over what period(s) will the depreciable assets be depreciated?  
Were there any tax benefits to Georgia Power from this acquisition? If so, please identify and explain each tax benefit. 
What is Georgia Power’s intended use for the River Park 10 warehouse? Explain fully.
Will employees or assets be moved from any existing locations into the newly acquired property? Explain fully.
Will Georgia Power be sharing the facility with any affiliates?  If so, which ones and for what purposes? Explain fully.
Does the Company plan to vacate any existing facilities as a result of having acquired the new property? If so, please explain which existing facilities would no longer be needed and the time frame for disposing of those and the time frame for when they would be put up for sale or transfer.
Does the Company anticipate that improvements will be needed to the newly acquired property? If so, please identify, quantify and explain the time frame of the improvements and the expected total cost of such improvements.
If the Company has no immediate plans for using the River Park 10 warehouse, is it reflected in Plant Held For Future Use?  If not, explain fully why not.  
What is the estimated in-service date for the River Park 10 warehouse?  Explain fully.
Response:
In July 2025, Georgia Power recorded the $94.75 million purchase of the River Park 10 warehouse in FERC Account 107 - Construction Work in Progress (CWIP). Of this total, $4.72 million was related to land and was subsequently reclassified to FERC Account 101  Plant in Service, as the land is ready for its intended use.
Please see STF-ASR-7-1 Attachment for a copy of the fully executed closing statement. The journal entries related to this transaction are as follows:

Entry to record the purchase of land and building
DR 107 CWIP $94,750,000 
CR 131 CASH $94,750,000
Reclass land portion to Plant in Service
DR 101 PIS     $4,720,365
CR 107 CWIP  $4,720,635

Of the $94.75 million purchase price for River Park 10, $4.72 million is related to land and $90.03 million is related to the warehouse.

Based on current depreciation rates, the warehouse will be depreciated over 55 years once it is placed in service.

No, there were no tax benefits associated with this acquisition.

The River Park 10 warehouse will serve as the Company’s new Material Distribution Center (MDC). It will house distribution, transmission, outdoor lighting, fiber, and technology materials.

Yes, both materials and employees from the current MDC located in Forest Park will be relocated to the newly acquired River Park 10 warehouse.

While the majority of the new MDC at River Park 10 will be used for Georgia Power materials, the technology materials stored there may be utilized across the Southern Company system. Affiliates will be billed accordingly for their use of the facility.

Georgia Power plans to vacate one of the facilities used as part of the current MDC located in Forest Park as a result of acquiring the new River Park 10 property. That facility is currently leased by the Company and the plan is to phase material out of the warehouse through the end of the lease term in June 2030.

Yes, improvements are needed for the newly acquired River Park 10 warehouse. These improvements are currently in the design phase and will include the development of office space, racking, technology, and security. The cost estimate for these improvements is still under development and is not yet available. The improvements are expected to be completed during the third quarter of 2026.

Please see the responses to subparts ‘f’ and ‘j’ above. The Company has immediate plans to use the River Park 10 warehouse, and therefore, the transaction was not recorded in Plant Held For Future Use.

The estimated in-service date for the River Park 10 warehouse is the third quarter of 2026, contingent upon the completion of the planned improvements.

STF-ASR-7-2

Question: 

With regards to uncertainty about supply chains, tariffs, and other exogenous global, national, and statewide economic stresses at a time of infrastructure growth for the Company:
a. Does the Company have a strategy to mitigate these risks? If so, please provide details.
b. Is the purchase of this warehouse part of a strategy to mitigate these risks?
c. Has the Company made any investments to mitigate these risks?
d. How much money has the Company directly paid in tariffs in 2025?
e. How much money has the Company indirectly paid in tariffs in 2025?
Response:

Mitigation strategies include strategic timing of orders and stocking levels, demand forecasting, monitoring market information, engaging suppliers proactively to understand exposure, and applying best practice guidance (i.e., collaboration amongst internal partners). 

The Company’s purchase of River Park 10 warehouse was not part of a strategy to mitigate risk associated with uncertainties about supply chains, tariffs, or other economic stressors.

There are no specific investments that are part of the Company’s mitigation strategy to minimize tariff risks. However, the Company implemented mitigation strategies as discussed in the Company’s response to subpart ‘a’.

The Company does not track the total cost of tariffs paid directly, as this data is frequently built into pricing and is not consistently isolated as a line item in the purchasing process. 

 The Company does not track the cost of tariffs paid indirectly.
