STF-PIA-17-10

Question:

Provide line items that include the impact of Plant Gaston and amounts (dollars) in the “Average Rate Base” statement for the 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021 and 2022 ASRs.
 
Response:

The Company does not have any investment related to Plant Gaston recorded to Plant In-Service or Accumulated Depreciation. Plant Gaston Units 1-4 and A (“Gaston Units”) have been in service to Georgia Power retail customers through a 1959 Power Contract between Georgia Power and the Southern Electric Generating Company (“SEGCO”). SEGCO is jointly owned by Georgia Power and Alabama Power, with fifty percent of the output of the Gaston Units committed to Georgia Power. The Company’s 50% ownership share of the investment costs, along with other costs of operating and maintaining the generating units, are billed to the Company monthly through its purchased power agreement with SEGCO.
