Question:

McGrau-Ford Battery. What is the current status of engineering, procurement and construction? What is the current expected commercial operation date? What was the CWIP balance as of June 30, 2023? What is the expected final capital cost?

Response:

The McGrau-Ford Battery project has not yet commenced construction and the Company is currently evaluating battery suppliers, integrators, and Engineering Construction and Procurement (“EPC”) contractors. The Company expects to file EPC and battery supply agreements in the calendar year of 2024. 
The expected commercial operation date is the first quarter of 2027. Because the project is in the development phase and has not commenced construction, there was no CWIP balance as of June 30, 2023. As of June 30, 2023, preliminary survey and investigation (“PS&I”) costs of REDACTED has been accrued for the project. The Company will file updates to the expected capital cost for McGrau-Ford when the final EPC and battery supply agreements are filed with the Commission.
