                 FIRST AMENDMENT TO NOTICE OF HEARING AND SCHEDULING ORDER
                   The Notice of Hearing and Scheduling Order (“Notice”) in this docket was issued on
          August 24, 2026. On October 2, 2026, Petitioners, pursuant to Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11,
          challenged VoltaGrid’s trade secret designation on discovery material and moved the Commission
          for access to information withheld by VoltaGrid as “trade secret.” VoltaGrid filed its opposition
          brief to Petitioner’s motion on October 14, 2026.

                   Under Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11(3), any party or intervenor in a docket, upon a
          petition for access to protected information submitted to the Commission pursuant to this rule, is
          entitled to a hearing from the Commission to consider the request. Similarly, under Commission
          Rule 515-3-1-.11(7), any party or intervenor in a docket, upon a motion challenging the
          designation of “trade secret,” is entitled to a hearing and in-camera inspection by the Commission.

       As the proper and lawful facilitation of discovery among parties is necessary for the
efficient and expeditious conduct of the hearing, the Hearing Officer hereby amends the existing
scheduling order as follows to accommodate a hearing on Petitioners’ challenge and motion:

       October 29, 2026      The Hearing Officer will hear Petitioners’ challenge of trade secret
                             information and request for access to trade secret information
                             immediately following the Commission’s regularly scheduled
                             Committees.
       November 13, 2026 An original and two (2) copies of any rebuttal to such direct
                             testimony must be filed by any Party desiring to file such rebuttal
                             testimony.


       November 24, 2026 The Parties are to confer in advance of the Pre-Hearing Conference,
                             and exchange documents proposed to be used at hearing as exhibits.


       December 2, 2026      Objections to pre-filed testimony and exhibits must be filed.


       December 4, 2026      Pre-Hearing Conference, 2:00PM. The Parties shall confer via
                             conference call by December 4, 2026, in a good faith attempt to
                             reach an agreement as to admissibility of any documents or tangible
                             items intended to be offered into evidence.
       December 9, 2026      The Hearing Officer, beginning at 10:00AM, will conduct a hearing
                             in this proceeding.


       By 4:00PM on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, Petitioners and VoltaGrid are instructed to 1)
identify any witnesses and 2) file an agreed protective agreement. If the parties cannot agree on
the language, each may file their own competing agreement. The hearing on the above-referenced
issues is scheduled in the main hearing room located at 244 Washington Street, S.W., Atlanta,
Georgia 30334, on Thursday, October 29, 2026, immediately following the Commission’s
regularly scheduled Committees. To the extent parties intend to present witnesses at the hearing,
witnesses may present their testimony verbally and will be subject to cross-examination. As
