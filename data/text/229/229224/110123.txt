COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before November 20, 2026. 

DEFINITIONS

As may be used in this document:  
1.“The Company,” “Georgia Power” or “The Utility” means Georgia Power Company and its present and former officers, employees, agents, representatives, directors, and all other persons acting or purporting to act on behalf of said Company.
The term “you” and “your” refer to “the Company.”
The term “person” means any natural person, corporation, corporate division, partnership, other unincorporated association, trust, government agency, or entity.
The term “document” or “documentation” shall have the broadest possible meaning under applicable law.  “Document” or “documentation” means every writing or record of every type and description that is in the possession, custody or control of the Company including, but not limited to, correspondence, memoranda, e-mails, drafts, workpapers, summaries, stenographic or handwritten notes, studies, notices, publications, books, pamphlets, reports, surveys, minutes or statistical compilations, computer and other electronic records or tapes or printouts, including, but not limited to, electronic mail files; and copies of such writing or records containing any commentary or notation whatsoever that does not appear in the original.  The term “document” or “documentation” further includes, by way of illustration and not limitation, schedules, progress schedules, time logs, drawings, computer disks, chart projections, time tables, summaries of other documents, minutes, surveys, work sheets, drawings, comparisons, evaluations, laboratory and testing reports, telephone call records, personal diaries, calendars, personal notebooks, personal reading files, transcripts, witness statements and indices.
The term “referring or relating to” means consisting of, containing, mentioning, suggesting, reflecting, concerning, regarding, summarizing, analyzing, discussing, involving, dealing with, emanating from, directed at, pertaining to in any way, or in any way logically or factually connected or associated with the matter discussed.
“And” and “or” as used herein shall be construed both conjunctively and disjunctively and each shall include the other whenever such construction will serve to bring within the scope of these discovery requests any information that would not otherwise be brought within their scope.
The singular as used herein shall include the plural and the masculine gender shall include the feminine and the neuter.    
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a person other than a natural person means to state:  the full name of the person and any names under which it conducts business; the present or last known address of the person; and, the present or last known telephone number of the person.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a document means to provide with respect to each document requested to be identified by these discovery requests a description of the document that is sufficient for purposes of a request to produce or a subpoena duces tecum, including the following:
the type of document (e.g., letter, memorandum, etc.);

a summary of the contents of the document;
the name and last known address of each person who presently has possession, custody or control of the document;
if any such document was, but is no longer, in your possession, custody or control or is no longer in existence, state whether it: (1) is missing or lost; (2) has been destroyed; or (3) has been transferred voluntarily or involuntarily, and if so, state the circumstances surrounding the authorization for each such disposition and the date of such disposition.
“Identify”, “identifying” or identity” when used in reference to a communication should be read to include information regarding the date of the communication, whether the communication was written or oral, the identity of all parties and witnesses to the communication, the substance of what was said and/or transpired and, if written, the identity of the document(s) containing or referring to the communication.

Unless stated otherwise, provide responses for calendar years 2022 through 2025 for the distribution system. Where a requested document, record, or analysis does not exist, state so explicitly rather than omitting the item. Provide data in unprotected Excel format where applicable. Do not respond by reference to a prior response; where a prior response in this or another docket contains responsive information, restate it and identify the response.
Program documents. 
Provide all written policies, procedures, manuals, or plans governing distribution vegetation management in effect from 2018 to present, with revision history. If no document exists other than the contractor specifications produced as STF-WG-2-17 Attachment B, so state.

Goals and targets. 
For each year, state the goals, progress measures, and outcome targets for the distribution VM program, how each is measured, and results against each. If none were set, so state.

Inspections and quality assurance. 
Please describe the inspection, or audit of distribution lines for vegetation management purposes, and mention any post-work audit of completed such trimmings. For each, please discuss:
trimming frequency versus trimming criteria, and provide frequency of trimming, share of system the miles it covered.
Please mention who performs it, how findings are recorded and, and how findings are used in work planning. Provide those results on a year-by-year basis.
Describe what the designated Company Inspectors do with the time sheets, Progress Maps and field records submitted under Section 19 of the distribution contractor specifications, including whether any completed work is field verified, the sample used, the criteria applied, and the results by year.

Cycle basis. 
Explain the basis for the distribution trim cycle in each year, and the reason for each change between 2022 and 2025. Provide any study or analysis supporting the cycle chosen. Reconcile the stated cycle with the three-year distribution cycle assumed in footnote 10 of the 2019 and 2022 Customer Benefit Studies.

Circuit trim dates.
 In unprotected Excel, for each distribution circuit as of December 31 of each year 2022–2025, please provide the following information:
The completion dates of the two most recent mechanical trims and herbicide treatments.
The next scheduled trim date and the assigned circuit cycle length.
Whether the most recent trim was completed within the required cycle.
If trim dates are not tracked by circuit, please confirm this and provide the most detailed available records of completed trimming work, including completion dates and circuit mapping (e.g., progress maps, field records, work forms, work orders, or contractor invoices).
Separately, please identify the records and calculations supporting the statements of zero distribution backlog in the 2023–2025 Annual Surveillance Reports.

Budget development.
 Explain how the annual distribution VM budget is built, including its components and FERC accounts. Explain how storm-related clearing and capitalized clearing are budgeted, recorded, and reported.

Actual cost by circuit. 
State whether GPC records actual distribution VM cost by circuit. If so, provide it by circuit and year. If not, identify the most granular level at which actual cost is recorded.

Outage cause coding.
 Provide GPC's outage cause-coding procedures and guidance, including the criteria for coding a tree or limb contact as weather versus vegetation, and any distinction between trees inside and outside the right-of-way. Identify any changes to these rules since 2020.


“Ineffective VM spend.” Referring to STF-REC-1-1, Table I.2:
Define “ineffective vegetation management spend” and provide the formula, data sources, and time period used.
State whether the spend input is actual recorded cost by circuit or disaggregated from system totals as described in Section I.P.
In unprotected Excel, for every circuit evaluated under this criterion (not only those selected), provide the circuit, the VM spend value used, the source of that value, the outage measure used, the resulting ratio or score, and the resulting quartile.
For each Table I.2 criterion for Packages 3 and 6, state whether it remains in use. If any criterion has changed, provide the current criterion and the date of and reason for the change.

Circuits selected under Packages 3 and 6.
 For each circuit that is qualified under Package 3 or Package 6, regardless of whether it appears on the STF-PIA-41-1 Distribution tab, please provide:
the qualifying criteria met,
selection date,
line miles,
approved and actual capital costs in unprotected Excel format.
Additionally, for each of the five years preceding the circuit's selection, provide:
Vegetation trimming dates and miles trimmed.
Actual vegetation management (VM) costs.
Number of tree/limb outages coded as vegetation-related and weather-related.
Please also provide any documents or analyses evaluating changes in vegetation management as an alternative to capital investment. If no such documents or analyses exist for a circuit, please state so.
For each circuit identified in response to this request:
State the installed distributed solar capacity (kW AC) connected to the circuit as of the selection date and as of the most recent date available, separately for (a) customer-sited/behind-the-meter, (b) third-party or QF-owned, and (c) Georgia Power-owned facilities, and state that capacity as a percentage of the circuit's peak load.
State whether Georgia Power uses any threshold or definition of high distributed generation or solar penetration for distribution planning, protection, or vegetation management purposes. If so, provide the definition and identify which of these circuits meet it. If not, please so state.
Explain whether and how distributed solar on a circuit affects the prioritization of vegetation management or tree-trimming schedules, including any consideration of vegetation-caused faults resulting in inverter trips, recloser operations, or feeder lockouts. Provide any supporting documents or analyses, or state that none exist.

Transmission scope
 Identify all transmission lines subject to the North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) FAC-003 Transmission Vegetation Management Reliability Standard, including line identifiers and the basis for determining applicability. Clarify whether the Transmission Vegetation Management policy in STF-WG-2-17, Attachment A, applies to transmission lines not subject to FAC-003. If not, identify the vegetation-management requirements applicable to those lines.

Reliability Indices and Vegetation Management Metrics:
 Please define Reliability Indices and Vegetation Management Metrics and discuss with most recent 5 years data:
SAIDI/ SAIFI accuracy and sensitivity
Vegetation Specific SAIDI/SAIFI
Measure of VM success
Data collection and accuracy
Learning points to achieve the target reliability indices.
What is Georgia Power’s SAIDI/SAIFI value after averaging 5 years data? Where does it fall in the standards from the base case as discussed in its STF-REC-1-1 attachment and described as the preferred solution. Please justify your answer with the present value of cost or net present value by investment scenario as discussed in the same attachment.
