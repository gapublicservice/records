       This letter is to inform the Georgia Public Service Commission and all Parties of Record

in the above-referenced proceeding of Sustainable Newton, Sara and Randall (“Randy”) Vinson,

and Theodosia and William (“Billy”) Wade’s (collectively, “Petitioners”) proposed factual

stipulations and the identity of witnesses expected at a hearing for Petitioners’ direct case.

                            Petitioners’ proposed factual stipulations

       Petitioners shared the below list of proposed factual stipulations with VoltaGrid’s counsel

in the evening of September 29. Today, at 3:00, VoltaGrid proposed a call for anytime after 3:20

p.m. On that call, VoltaGrid indicated that it agreed to stipulations #10, #11, #12, #17, and

#18, and it objected to the remaining stipulations. To ensure a timely response to the Hearing

Officer’s Notice of Hearing and Scheduling Order, Petitioners are filing their proposed stipulations

below. The parties will separately jointly file agreed upon stipulations with the Commission as

soon as practicable.

           In addition, as will be addressed in a separate motion to the Hearing Officer, VoltaGrid

refuses to provide Petitioners access to substantial documents, which it has withheld based on

“trade secret” claims, despite failing to provide an affidavit or basis for those claims.

           Petitioners anticipate further stipulations could lead to a more efficient resolution of this

matter before the Commission, but without access to a substantial number of documents filed in

this matter, Petitioners are unable to propose those stipulations at this time.
