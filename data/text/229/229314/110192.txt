===== DKT 44280 STF-ASR-15 PD/STF-ASR-15-2.docx =====
STF-ASR-15-2

Question: 

What was the main reason for the cause of the transformer failure? Please describe the investigation process and who performed the investigation (internal vs third party), and when was it completed or expected to complete?

Response:

The main reason for the cause of the transformer failure was a failed 230 kV bushing on the high-side of the transformer. Due to the catastrophic failure of the bushing and the resulting collateral damage, a formal failure investigation could not be conducted.  Despite this, past experience with catastrophic bushing failures coupled with the bushing’s porcelain debris field and available surveillance video footage of the failure led the Company’s internal subject matter experts to the conclusion that it was a bushing failure. This was determined the next day after the failure occurred.

Question: 

Please explain the type of fire detection equipment was in place in this substation during the time of incident? Did they operate as designed? Were there any gaps in their performance?

Response:

No dedicated fire detection or suppression systems were in place for this transformer at the time of the incident, although the operation of the transformer differential protection indicated an abnormal condition at the substation. Question: 

Were any assets fully retired due to the incident, and if so, what is the proposed accounting treatment for the retired plant?

Response:

The transformer and damaged breakers will be fully retired due to the incident, resulting in a debit to FERC Account 108, Accumulated Depreciation and a corresponding credit to Account 101, Plant in Service. The damaged breakers will be sent to the manufacturers for repair and refurbishment and, upon completion, will be recorded to Account 154, Plant Materials and Operating Supplies Inventory.
