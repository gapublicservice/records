 HOW WILL VOLTAGRID IMPACT YOU AND YOUR COMMUNITY?

 VoltaGrid, despite being in Georgia Power’s territory, is attempting to build a private

 power plant that will pollute our air and cause noise and light disturbances that could ruin

 the health and peace of our community. VoltaGrid will emit a lot of formaldehyde, which

is a cancer-causing pollutant. During my work in the biology department at Oxford

College we used formaldehyde as a preservative for various specimens. Whenever

students were doing dissections, we had to use proper personal protective equipment

(masks and gloves) and work in well-ventilated areas many times under hoods so that

professors and students would avoid contact with formaldehyde. We had to follow certain

protocols for disposing of the dissection waste so as not to release formaldehyde into the

environment. It was a big deal! I know that VoltaGrid will also emit particulate matter,

like PM2.5, and smog-forming chemicals like nitrogen oxides. These pollutants will get

into the lungs of my grandchildren, my children, and me, and will make all of us less

healthy. And I worry about the other pollutants from burning natural gas like the

greenhouse gases that change our climate.

Lalso fear for the impact on the wellbeing and vitality of my community from additional

pollution. Billy and I both know neighbors, people in the congregation, and people who

get help at the food bank who suffer from diseases like asthma and COPD, which can be

made worse by the kinds of pollution that VoltaGrid will emit. I am also very concerned

that the pollutants emitted by VoltaGrid could interact with and accumulate on the air

pollutants that already impose health burdens on people within my community, like

 ethylene oxide and emissions from other fossil-fuel burning operations. The noise and

 light impacts from VoltaGrid will also negatively impact our lives and this community.

 We drive by the VoltaGrid parcel on a near-daily basis on our way to-and-from town and

 would be subject to noise and light pollution up close and frequently. I fear that the noise

 pollution could also travel as far as our home, and disrupt our peace and quiet and affect

 how we enjoy our property. We love spending time outside on our property.

ARE YOU ANTI-POWER PLANTS?

No. I understand the need for electricity and power plants. But I do not want a polluting

power plant in my community, without proper regulation by the Public Service

petitioning the Public Service Commission to reconsider approving such a plant nested
