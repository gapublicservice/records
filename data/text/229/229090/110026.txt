Q.   State your name, business title, and business address.

Q.  State your business experience and educational background.
A.  I graduated from Texas Tech University in 2007 with a B.A. in History/Education. I have 19 years of experience working in the oil and gas industry, primarily focused on pressure pumping, which involves pumping fluids into oil and gas wells at high pressure to fracture the surrounding rock so that oil and gas can flow. My resume is attached as Exhibit 1. 

Q.  Have you previously testified before this Commission?
A.  No.

Q.  On whose behalf is the panel testifying in this docket?
A.  I am testifying on behalf of VoltaGrid.

Q.  What is your role at VoltaGrid?
A.  I oversee VoltaGrid’s operational activities and associated assets. My duties give me knowledge of VoltaGrid’s various projects, including the one located in the State of Georgia, which is referred to as the “ATL-1” project.


VOLTAGRID’S ONE CUSTOMER IN GEORGIA

Q.  To whom will VoltaGrid furnish service in Georgia?
A.  VoltaGrid’s only customer in Georgia is SF ATL2, LLC, known as “Serverfarm.” Serverfarm is building a data center that, I understand, it will own and operate on property it also owns, located at 10835 Hazelbrand Road NE in Covington, Georgia (the “Subject Property”). The data center is referred to as the “ATL2 Data Center.” VoltaGrid has not commenced operation of its ATL-1 project and therefore has not furnished any service to Serverfarm or the ATL2 Data Center to date.

Q.  Will VoltaGrid furnish service to any customer from the ATL-1 project besides Serverfarm?
A.   No.  Serverfarm is the only customer that VoltaGrid will serve from the ATL-1 project. To my knowledge, no other potential customer is located at the ATL2 Data Center or on the Subject Property. Furthermore, VoltaGrid has not solicited, nor to my knowledge is it otherwise in discussions with, any other potential customer about receiving service from the ATL-1 project. 
VoltaGrid will not invite members of the public to access the property where the ATL-1 project is located. Likewise, to my knowledge, Serverfarm will not invite members of the public to, or otherwise offer any public service from, the ATL2 Data Center or the Subject Property.

Q.  Does Serverfarm resell or redistribute, or plan to resell or redistribute, any electricity it receives to any other person?
A.  No. To my knowledge, Serverfarm will consume all the electricity produced from the ATL-1 project.

Q.  Does VoltaGrid furnish service to any other customer in Georgia besides Serverfarm?
A.  No. Serverfarm is the only customer in Georgia to which VoltaGrid will furnish service. To my knowledge, VoltaGrid is not engaged in negotiations with any person or entity, other than Serverfarm at the ATL2 Data Center, to furnish any service in Georgia. VoltaGrid is not soliciting, marketing to, or otherwise pursuing any additional customers in Georgia.
