TRADE SECRET FILING OF 
ATLANTA GAS LIGHT COMPANY


Atlanta Gas Light Company ("AGLC") hereby files with the Georgia Public Service Commission ("Commission") the Fiscal Year 2026 July True-up Summary Report data pertaining to Docket No. 42315.  The true-up information provides competitive data that is of economic value to AGLC and to Marketers because others do not generally know it.  Accordingly, AGLC protects against disclosure of this information.  AGLC is therefore filing this information pursuant to Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11 governing trade secrets.
Pursuant to the terms of Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11, AGLC is providing information under protective seal (marked "Trade Secret") and is providing a "public disclosure document."  AGLC is providing the original of this information to the Commission Staff.  The Company is maintaining a master list of this document.
