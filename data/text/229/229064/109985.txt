         Pursuant to the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (the “Commission’s”) rules regarding the
Request for Proposals (“RFP”) Procedure for Long-Term Supply-Side Options (the “RFP Rules”), enclosed
for filing are the original and five copies of Southern Power Company’s (“Southern Power’s”) Eleventh
Revised Bid Team List for use by the Independent Evaluator in the Phase 2 All Source Capacity RFP (for
years 2032-2033) to be conducted by Georgia Power Company (“GPC”) in Docket No. 56368 (the “Phase
2 All Source Capacity RFP”).

       The Eleventh Revised Bid Team List reflects the addition of one (1) Bid Team member. The new
Bid Team member has reviewed, executed and had notarized a compliance acknowledgement of the
Standards of Conduct for Soliciting Entities and their Affiliates During the RFP Process (“RFP Standards
of Conduct”) (implementing the Commission’s RFP Rules §§ 515-3-4-.04(3)(d) 3 through 5) that apply to
RFP-related activities.

        Also enclosed are copies of the executed RFP Standards of Conduct acknowledgements and a CD
containing the Eleventh Revised Bid Team list.
