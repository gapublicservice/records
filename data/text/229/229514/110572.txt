COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before December 21, 2026.

Distribution Reliability Reporting

Outage cause codes. With respect to the cause codes the Company uses to record interruptions on its distribution system:
Provide the complete list of cause codes, with each code's definition and any field guidance on how the code is applied.
Describe who assigns the cause code (for example, the field crew at restoration or other personnel after the event) and whether codes are reviewed or corrected afterward.
Map each cause code to one of the following categories: vegetation grow-in (vegetation inside the right-of-way or clearance zone); vegetation fall-in (trees or limbs from outside the right-of-way or clearance zone); wildlife; equipment; weather and other; and transmission or supply.

Vegetation inside vs. outside the right-of-way. With respect to vegetation-caused interruptions on the distribution system:
State whether the Company distinguishes interruptions caused by vegetation inside the right-of-way or clearance zone from those caused by trees or limbs from outside it.
If so, describe how the distinction is recorded.
If not, so state, and identify any other data the Company maintains (for example, hazard-tree or storm damage records) that could support the distinction.

Vegetation management tracking. With respect to distribution vegetation management activity and costs:
State whether activity (miles trimmed, hazard trees removed) and costs are tracked by distribution circuit.
If they are tracked by work area, cycle unit, or another unit, identify the unit and provide a mapping of those units to distribution circuit IDs.

Trim cycles. For each distribution circuit:
Provide the target trim cycle, in months.
Provide the completion date of the most recent full-circuit cycle trim.
Describe the criteria the Company uses to set trim cycle length.


Extreme weather and wildfire vegetation management costs. State whether vegetation management costs incurred to mitigate an identified extreme weather or wildfire risk are recorded separately from other distribution vegetation management costs.
If so, provide the amounts for each of the last five years.
If not, describe how these costs are recorded.

Regions and subregions. Provide the Company's definitions of its distribution operating regions and subregions.
Provide a list of all distribution circuits by region and subregion.

Reporting template data availability. Refer to Staff's draft Distribution Reliability Reporting Template, provided as Attachment A. Identify any field the Company cannot provide as specified, explain why, and propose the closest available alternative.
For each tab of the template, state the number of years of historical circuit-level data the Company maintains.
State whether the Company could provide the data in the template quarterly, and describe any limitations.
