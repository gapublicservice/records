GENERAL SUBSCRIBER SERVICES TARIFF

Knology of the Valley, Inc. f/k/a Interstate Telephone Company Section C
Georgia PSC28th Revised Sheet 2
Cancels 27th Revised Sheet 2

BASIC LOCAL EXCHANGE SERVICE

C.1General

Basic local communication service is provided by means of station, switching and other
 facilities, plant and equipment to enable the establishment of communications between
 stations in the same or different exchange at monthly rates herein shown.  The facilities,
plant and equipment used to provide such basic local communication service are also
used in the furnishing of toll communication services at rate applicable for such services.

Other non-basic types of services are available in the Interstate Telephone Exchange
area at rates and conditions provided for elsewhere in this tariff.

C.2Rates

Except as otherwise provided the rates and regulations contained in the General
Exchange Tariff are made a part of this Local Exchange Tariff.  The rates specified herein
entitle subscribers to access all exchanges within the subscriber’s county within the same
LATA, all exchange access lines bearing the central office designations of the
subscriber's exchange and all exchange access lines bearing the central office
designation(s) of additional exchanges in the basic service area as follows:

Exchange    Basic Service Area
    Additional Exchanges
West Point    LaGrange, GA; Hogansville, GA
    Pine Mountain, GA; (Troup County, GA)
    Lanett, AL; Fredonia, AL; Shawmut, AL
    Valley, AL; Huguley, AL

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. 46-5-166, effective May 2, 1996, basic single-line residence
rates and basic single-line business rates are to be capped for five years effective
May 2, 1996.  The rates for multi-line business subscribers are not capped.
