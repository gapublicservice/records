OPERATORS RESPONSE: The City’s original plan was to replace the pipe via HDD that is currently hanging on Starr’s Bridge Rd (Hwy 51) under North Fork Broad River to eliminate any atmospheric corrosion issues in the future.  The City ordered the materials and bid and awarded the work to a contractor, but the bore failed and the bore head and pipe were lost under the river.  The City had to wait to order more pipe until it could be reimbursed from the original boring contractor.  Once the new material had been ordered and a new contractor selected, the GA Department of Transportation’s permit requirements were changed and required an additional approval from River Keepers.  After many discussion on how to get approval from the River Keepers, the DOT permit was finally approved but with a new requirement to have the pipe cased, which is not feasible with an HDD.  Therefore, the project went back to Council to approve having the existing pipe re-coated and install new bridge hangers.  The contractor has been selected to do this work, but the coating installation has temperature requirements, so the repairs are anticipated to be done in the spring of 2027.

Hearing Requests:
The Operator has the right to request a hearing before the full Commission to contest the alleged probable violations, recommended civil penalties, and all other proposed actions of enforcement.  A request for a hearing must be submitted in writing and in accordance with Commission Rule 515-2-1-.04. The Operator must include a statement of the issues that you intend to raise at the hearing. The issues may relate to the allegations, new information, proposed compliance order, proposed civil penalty, or any other recommendation for enforcement action. Please refer to Commission Rule 515-9-3-.11 and O.C.G.A. § 46-2-91 for assessment considerations upon which civil penalties are based. An operator's failure to specify an issue may result in a waiver of the right to raise that issue at hearing. The request must also indicate whether or not the Operator will be represented by counsel at the hearing.
