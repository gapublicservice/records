Response:

“IB (Illinois Basin) mkt” refers to coal pricing received from market intelligence sources such as coal price publications or verbal quotes from coal suppliers. In contrast, “IB bid” refers to coal pricing received from actual coal bids that were received by Southern Company Services (“SCS”) from coal solicitations through a competitive request for proposals (“RFP”) process. The comment column is intended to explain the source for the coal price (FOB $/ton). 
The year or “vintage’ used to develop marginal costs aligns with the year of the marginal coal need. The Company utilizes a REDACTED price versus a REDACTED price or vice versa when the uncommitted coal need changes due to burn forecast changes, shifting the timing of the coal marginal need. Current bid pricing from a recent solicitation is generally used when available because it represents the best available indication of the Company's expected procurement cost for future uncommitted coal needs. As bid information ages or market conditions materially change, the Company evaluates available market intelligence and supplier information to determine whether market-based pricing more accurately reflects expected marginal procurement costs.
While the Company maintains a record of the referenced data, the Company does not track marginal coal price vs average stockpile and burn coal price by month for comparison purposes. The dispatch marginal coal price and the stockpile coal price are not analogous metrics. The dispatch price is a forward-looking marginal fuel cost used for economic dispatch decisions, while the stockpile price is an actual blended inventory cost reflecting historical coal purchases. Since one represents the cost of the next ton burned and the other represents the average cost of all coal in inventory, differences between the two are expected and do not necessarily indicate any operational or forecasting issue. Consequently, a direct comparison is of limited analytical value.
The monthly coal costs reported in FCR MFRH-5-13 (Tab (d)) are based on marginal costs.  They were calculated based on the weighted average of the daily marginal coal costs in effect during the month. For example, the cost reported in FCR MFRH-5-13 for January 2024 at Plant Bowen was REDACTED. The marginal cost was set at REDACTED effective for January 1, 2024, and subsequently updated on REDACTED, to REDACTED. As a result, the weighted average of daily coal prices across January 2024 was REDACTED.

Question: 

Refer to the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-6 and Trade Secret Attachment.
a. Please provide the same data on a projected basis, preferably through the entire 3-year forecast period (May 2029).
b. For 2025, the Company identifies approximately REDACTED in take- or-pay contract costs over the 12 months in the response to STF-JKA- 3-6. Please provide a breakdown of the REDACTED and reconcile the REDACTED to the approximately REDACTED in average/composite costs associated with generation classified as RtpRateRule = “Zero” in the filed hourly settlement data. 1 Hearing Transcript, p. 105, ll. 3-6.
c. Refer to the Company’s Supplemental 2-1 Attachment C and Attachment F. Please explain how the Company determined the $/MWh rates for each resource and if any assumptions were made to address generic new solar generation in the Aurora forecast.

Response:

Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-5-7.

The resources captured in the 45-day data and indicated as RtpRateRule = “Zero” are units of sufficient size to be metered and modeled by fleet operations at the by hour by unit level. Costs derived from that data will not include resources which are not modeled by unit on an hourly basis including small distributed generation, co-located, or behind the meter resources which are included in the costs presented in the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-6. Additionally, for resources modeled on an hourly basis but paid based on avoided energy costs, the 45-day data reflects zero for the average/fuel rate and therefore, does not capture a delta when RtpRateRule = “Zero” is applied, but the avoided energy costs are included in the overall fuel costs presented in STF-JKA-3-6. Finally, the 45-day data includes only bottom of stack generation costs, whereas the data in STF-JKA-3-6 also includes costs associated with participation in off-system sales. 

The projected power purchase agreement (“PPA”) energy costs in the Aurora forecast are developed based on the following categories:
Executed contracts: Projected costs for existing PPAs are based on the pricing terms in each contract applied to the projected energy deliveries.
Qualifying Facility (“QF”) and Distributed Generation (“DG”) resources [Energy-Only QFs, Proxy QFs, DG]: Until resource-specific pricing is known, projected energy is priced at the forecasted Associated Interchange Energy Rate (“AIER”) as a placeholder. This approach is consistent with how the Company values avoided energy in its REDI, CRSP, and CARES subscription programs (please see the Company’s responses to STF-JKA-1-1 in Docket No. 57171 and STF-JKA-4-3 in Docket No. 56765).
Near-term utility-scale resources [from Commission-approved procurements without final pricing]: Projected costs use current Clean and Renewable Energy Subscription (“CARES”) PPA pricing as a placeholder. This pricing reflects recent market-based results from Commission-approved RFPs (please see the Company’s responses to STF-PIA-3-6 and STF-JKA-1-17 Attachments A through G in Docket No. 56765).
