                                         Stipulation

The Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) Public Interest Advocacy Staff
(“PIA Staff”), Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”), and the
undersigned Intervening Parties (collectively, “Stipulating Parties”) agree to the following
Stipulation as a resolution to the Commission’s review of Georgia Power’s Real Time
Pricing (“RTP”) Revenue Credit and Allocation Methodology in Docket No. 57171.

 1.       Georgia Power’s RTP Fuel Credit Methodology, which determines how revenues
          recovered from RTP customers are recorded as between fuel and base revenues,
          shall continue to be derived from the average hourly marginal replacement fuel
          costs, as approved by the Commission in the 2010 base rate case in Docket No.
          31598, except as modified herein.

 2.       Beginning January 1, 2027, the following “fixed” cost categories will be included
          in the Company’s RTP Fuel Credit Methodology calculation, allocated based on
          energy. The calculation should be based on the costs incurred by the Company for
          these cost categories ($), the actual monthly eligible FCR load (MWh), and the load
          priced at RTP (MWh). Based on the timing of the available accounting data, the
          calculation will be based on a preliminary estimate and trued-up. This will be in
          addition to the revenues recorded based on the average hourly marginal replacement
          fuel cost rate.

             •   Existing natural gas firm transportation
             •   Existing natural gas storage
             •   Natural gas gains/losses on resales
             •   Coal quality penalties and premiums
             •   Coal rail car leases
             •   Coal rail car shortfall/liquidated damages costs
             •   Start-up costs for Purchased Power Agreements (PPAs)
             •   Non-subscription renewables program expenses
             •   Corporate overhead and labor

               •   Any new “fixed” costs introduced to the FCR for all customers not addressed
                   by the average hourly marginal cost methodology.

           Revenues covering the new natural gas firm transportation (“FT”) and new storage
           costs shall be recorded to fuel monthly based on an energy allocation of the projected
           load priced at RTP and FCR eligible load. This ensures that the RTP revenues
           booked to fuel will cover the large load causation of the new FT cost independent
           of the load materialization and is consistent with the Commission’s directive to
           protect existing customers from cost shifting if large load projects fail to materialize.

    3.     The costs and output (MWhs) of non-dispatchable power purchase agreements
           (“PPAs”) will be included in the hourly calculation in the RTP Fuel Credit
           Methodology.

    4.     The implementation of these modifications shall be credited to fuel revenues
           beginning January 1, 2027, and the corresponding change to base revenues shall be
           recorded in a regulatory asset and addressed in the next base rate case. The recovery
           of the regulatory asset in the next base rate case will be addressed in a manner that
           provides for recovery of the regulatory asset within 24 months following the end of
           the annual period of recording the related base revenues and regulatory asset. The
           Commission shall determine the customer classes from which the regulatory asset
           will be recovered in the next base rate case.

    5.     For the period beginning January 1, 2027 through the FCR-27 period, the Company
           shall file monthly reports or modify existing reports filed monthly with the
           Commission as follows:

           a. New monthly fuel reporting on FT and natural gas storage costs incurred, new
              contracts signed, and remarketing of gas capacity.1
           b. The monthly fuel report filed by the Company under Docket No. 3382 will
              include a new, separate line item in the Retail Fuel Revenues section showing
              RTP sales (MWhs) and RTP fuel revenues. The Company will file a separate
              report monthly with RTP sales and fuel rate calculations on an hourly and
              monthly basis.

    6.     The Historical and Projected Minimum Filing Requirements for FCR shall be
           modified as follows:
           a. The Company shall file reported costs associated with the cost categories not
              included in the RTP fuel credit methodology as part of the Minimum Filing
              Requirements – Historic (MFRH) and Minimum Filing Requirements –

       b. The Company shall include support for its marginal coal pricing in a format

 7.    The Company agrees to meet with Stipulating Parties prior to filing its next base rate
       case to discuss the RTP related topics that were raised in this docket, but outside the
       scope of this proceeding, including but not limited to:
       a. The determination and triggering of the reliability component.
       b. The recovery factor.

 8.    The Company agrees to meet with Stipulating Parties prior to filing its next base rate
       case to discuss methodologies for integrating large load data centers, such as
       segmentation and cost allocation, in the Company’s next filed cost of service study.
       The Stipulating Parties agree that nothing in this Stipulation, including but not
       limited to the modifications to the RTP Fuel Credit Methodology, shall serve as
       precedent or pre-determine the resolution of issues in the 2028 rate case, including
       cost and revenue allocation, class cost of service study methodology, or rate design.

 9.    The Company agrees to pay for any reasonably necessary specialized assistance to
       the Commission Staff in an amount not to exceed $75,000 annually for analysis of
       the Company’s RTP Fuel Credit reports prior to the filing of the FCR-28 case. The
       amounts paid by the Company under this paragraph shall be deemed a necessary
       cost of service and recovered from fuel revenues.
