(1) Volumes in bold font represent actual burn volumes for the corresponding hedge period, budgeted forward volumes are regular font.
(2) The prices in bold font are composite prices from actual physical gas transactions delivered to GPC plants. For forward months, the price is the Nymex contract month in column N.

(3) For months where there is no Mark-to-Market positions, Column J is calculated as G + H. For months with forward Mark-to-market positions, Column J is calculated as G + I. Since Mark-to-Market is calculated as net of option premiums, to include
option premiums with Mark-to-market would result in a double reporting of premium costs.
(4) Column J does not include administrative costs.

(5) NYMEX prices in bold font are settlement prices for contracts that are no longer traded due to expiration. NYMEX prices in normal font are current prices for contract months that are presently traded on the NYMEX as of the date of this report.
