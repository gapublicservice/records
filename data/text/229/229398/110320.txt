STF-PIA-47-5

Question: 

When conducting vegetation management, what is the schedule for tree trimming/line mitigation (3 years, 5 years, 10 years, etc.)? How is this schedule impacted when a circuit is upgraded, reconductored, or included in the Grid Investment Plan Capital Expenditure?

Response:

Vegetation management work plans are determined by an assessment of vegetation conditions, outage history, and criticality of circuits. Maintenance intervals typically range from 24 to 60 months, with approximately one quarter of the distribution system maintained each year.  Capital project schedules are considered as part of the annual work planning process.

Question: 

For feeders presented in ‘44280 - STF-PIA-41-1 Distribution’ tab, provide pre-performance and post-performance metrics (SAIDI (MED and non-MED), SAIFI (MED and non-MED), MAIFI, and MAIFE). If these metrics are not used, how is the Company comparing pre and post- performance?

Response:

Regarding the attachment referenced in the question, for feeders included in the Grid Investment Program, pre- and post-performance are evaluated using a combination of reliability and operational metrics. These measures include outage frequency, outage duration, customer interruptions (CI), customer minutes interrupted (CMI), outage cause information, restoration performance, and feeder reliability rankings. Please also note that post-performance metrics are captured at a minimum of 12 months after construction is complete.

Question: 

When performing vegetation management what standards does Georgia Power base its policy on (ANSI, NESC, OSHA, city/county ordinances, etc.)? Does Georgia Power meet or exceed minimum requirements outlined in these standards?

Response:

The Company’s vegetation management policies are based on applicable industry standards and requirements, including ANSI A300, ANSI Z133, OSHA 1910.269, NESC, and NERC FAC-003 standards. Company policies are designed to at least meet the applicable minimum requirements established by these standards.

The Company does not maintain vegetation-specific SAIDI or SAIFI indices, nor does it maintain pre- and post-vegetation management SAIDI or SAIFI values for individual feeders. SAIDI and SAIFI are system-level reliability indices, representing the System Average Interruption Duration Index and System Average Interruption Frequency Index. Consistent with IEEE Standard 1366, these indices are calculated as customer-weighted averages across the electric system and are intended to measure system-level reliability performance. Accordingly, the requested vegetation-specific SAIDI and SAIFI scores, feeder-level vegetation-specific SAIDI and SAIFI performance, and pre- and post-vegetation management SAIDI and SAIFI metrics are not available.

STF-PIA-47-2

Question: 

How does Georgia Power measure performance at the feeder level?

Response:

The Company evaluates feeder performance using a variety of reliability, outage, operational, and engineering measures. The specific measures and methodologies used vary depending on the purpose of the analysis, the data available, and the nature of the evaluation being performed. Accordingly, the Company does not rely on a single feeder-level metric for all analyses.
