===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-PIA-25 PD/STF-PIA-25-2.docx =====
STF-PIA-25-2

Question: 

Please provide copies of (preliminary/final) reports once completed and submitted by GE Vernova for Plant Bowen and Scherer.

Response:

At present, the Company has received one report from GE Vernova summarizing costs associated with equipment that would be installed in the Plant Bowen and Plant Scherer boilers to fire natural gas, see STF-PIA-25-2 Attachment. The costs in this report are for equipment only and do not include construction costs or any considerations for the balance of plant. This report also does not include the technical boiler studies for Plants Bowen and Scherer, which GE Vernova is projected to complete in the next few months. 

Once issued by GE Vernova, the Company expects to provide copies of technical boiler study reports, which will detail operational and performance impacts of gas addition, with the next Semi-Annual report due March 31, 2027.
