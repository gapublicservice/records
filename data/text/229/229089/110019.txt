REMOTE OPERATIONS CENTER


Real-time observation and
monitoring
The Remote Operations Center (ROC) is a component of VoltaGrid’s online
platform, which provides insights for our collaborative projects. Tailored for real-time
observation and monitoring of on-site equipment performance, the ROC promptly
identifies and notifies our technicians of any unexpected issues.

Delivering tailored solutions to meet your needs, our expert technicians operate remotely but
with a hands-on approach. We ensure reliability and optimization by leveraging data from our
network and providing comprehensive customer reporting. Our skilled technicians analyze
data from over 500 generators nationwide daily.
