Order Issuing Request for Proposal for the Regulated Provider of Natural Gas

This matter comes before the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) as a result of a request by the Commission Staff (“Staff”) to issue a Request for Proposal (“RFP”) (Exhibit A) for the Regulated Provider of natural gas term to commence service on September 1, 2027, through August 31, 2029.  

Background

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-4-166(f), the Commission shall employ an RFP process for selecting a Regulated Provider of Natural Gas for Group 1 and Group 2 customers.  To be eligible for Group 1 service, a customer must meet the definition of a “low-income residential consumer” set forth in the Natural Gas Competition and Deregulation Act, O.C.G.A. §§ 46-4-150 through 166, O.C.G.A. § 46-4-166(a)(1).  Group 2 customers include firm natural gas customers who have been unable to obtain or maintain natural gas commodity service or whose payment history was cited by the Regulated Provider as reason for transfer from Group 1 to Group 2.  O.C.G.A. § 46-4-166(a)(2).

On August 14, 2024, the Commission issued a Consent Order in Docket No. 55356, designating SCANA Energy Marketing (“SCANA Energy”) as the Regulated Provider.  The Consent Order set forth the terms and conditions under which the Regulated Provider would serve qualifying customers.  Paragraph 2.1 of the Consent Order provides that “SCANA Energy shall provide service as the Regulated Provider for a period of two (2) years, beginning September 1, 2024 and ending August 31, 2026.”  In addition, Paragraph 2.3 of the Consent Order provides that “If the Commission determines, in its discretion, that such an action is in the public interest, the Commission may extend the service of the Regulated Provider for a third year, or may terminate the service of the Regulated Provider after one year.”  During its July 15, 2025 Administrative Session, the Commission voted to extend the term of SCANA Energy as the Regulated Provider for the period of September 1, 2024 through August 31, 2027. Both of these Consent Order provisions track pertinent Georgia law.  See, O.C.G.A. § 46-4-166(f).  

The RFP
The term of the current Regulated Provider expires on August 31, 2027.  In order to have sufficient opportunity and time to solicit the most favorable bids from certificated marketers, Staff is recommending the issuance of the RFP shown as Exhibit A. All attachments to the RFP are part of Exhibit A. The term to be served by the next approved Regulated Provider is September 1, 2027 through August 31, 2029. All potential bidders must meet with the current Regulated Provider to review the accounts receivable by no later than January 6, 2027. The Commission will select the next Regulated Provider by no later than May 4, 2027.

Commission Decision

On November 17, 2026, the Commission voted to issue the RFP for the Regulated Provider of Natural Gas for the period beginning September 1, 2027 through August 31, 2029.  


******

WHEREFORE IT IS ORDERED, that the Commission issues this Request for Proposal for the Regulated Provider of Natural Gas for the period of September 1, 2027 through August 31, 2029.

ORDERED FURTHER, that any potential bidder must meet with the current Regulated Provider to review accounts receivables by January 6, 2027.

ORDERED FURTHER, that any certificated marketer that meet the requirements set forth by Commission Rule 515-7-13-.05 shall file a proposal consistent with the RFP and its attachments by January 20, 2027.

ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing, or oral argument or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of this Order, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.

ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over this matter is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order or Orders as this Commission may deem just and proper.

The above by action of the Commission in Administrative Session on the 17th day of November 2027.
