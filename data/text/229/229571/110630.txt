Pursuant to the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (the “Commission’s”) rules regarding the Request for Proposals (“RFP”) Procedure for Long-Term Supply-Side Options (the “RFP Rules”), enclosed for filing are the original and five copies of Southern Power Company’s (“Southern Power’s”) list of Bid Team members (primary, secondary and support) for use by the Independent Evaluator in the CARES 2027 US RFP to be conducted by Georgia Power Company (“GPC”) (the “CARES 2027 US RFP”).

The Bid Team list identifies each Bid Team member and provides his or her respective company affiliation, title and functional responsibility.  Each Bid Team member has received and read the Standards of Conduct For Soliciting Entities and their Affiliates During the RFP Process (“RFP Standards of Conduct”) (implementing the Commission’s RFP Rules §§ 515-3-4-.04(3)(d) 3 through 5) and has executed and had notarized an acknowledgement stating his or her compliance with the applicable provisions of the RFP Standards of Conduct.

Also enclosed are copies of the acknowledgements executed by each Bid Team member and a CD containing the Bid Team list.  To ensure full compliance with the Commission’s RFP Rules and the RFP Standards of Conduct referenced above, Bid Team members have been segregated from all activities undertaken in support of the Evaluation Team.
