Re:Sustainable Newton, et al. v. VoltaGrid, LLC
VoltaGrid’s Emergency Appeal From the Hearing Officer’s 
November 4, 2026 Order and Motion for Stay
Request for Addition to Energy Committee Agenda/November 12, 2026

Dear Commissioner Wise:

On behalf of VoltaGrid, LLC (“VoltaGrid”), this letter requests that its Emergency Appeal From the Hearing Officer’s November 4, 2026 Order and Motion for Stay be placed on the Energy Committee agenda for November 12, 2026.  The Emergency Appeal was filed this afternoon, and a copy is attached hereto as Exhibit “A.” 

In response to Staff’s Data Request STF-PIA-1, VoltaGrid produced approximately 4700 pages of documents, of which forty-five (45) documents (approximately 486 pages) remain designated as Trade Secret (the “Protected Information”) pursuant to Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11.  On October 2, 2026, Petitioners challenged VoltaGrid’s trade secret designation and requested access to the Protected Information.  VoltaGrid opposed Petitioners’ Motion on October 14, 2026.  A hearing was conducted before the Hearing Officer on October 29, 2026.  

On November 4, 2026, the Hearing Officer ruled in favor of Petitioners. VoltaGrid was ordered to execute a protective order with Petitioners and provide them with the Protected Information on or before November 12, 2026 (the “Order”).  VoltaGrid’s Emergency Appeal was filed in advance of the deadline of November 12, 2026 set by the Order.  VoltaGrid asks the Commission to add its Emergency Appeal to the Energy Committee for the same date.  Further, Volta Grid asks the Commission to stay the Order until action by the Commission.

VoltaGrid’s Emergency Appeal asserts the following:

The Order failed to follow Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11 which reserves disputes regarding Protected Information to resolution by the Commission, requires an in camera inspection to determine whether the Protected Information constitutes trade secrets and requires a thirty (30) day stay to allow an appeal; and

The Order improperly requires VoltaGrid to execute a Protective Agreement and disclose its Protected Information to Petitioners before resolving the issues of whether Petitioners have standing to pursue a claim under the Territorial Act and whether this Commission even has jurisdiction over VoltaGrid.

The Order defers consideration of these dispositive issues until the conclusion of the case, largely rendering VoltaGrid’s defenses meaningless.  Because VoltaGrid has been ordered to comply with the Order by November 12, 2026, VoltaGrid requests that the Commission hear its Emergency Appeal as an added agenda item to be presented during the Energy Committee on November 12, 2026.  

All parties to the docket are being notified of VoltaGrid’s request.  VoltaGrid appreciates the Energy Committee’s attention to its request.  Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.
