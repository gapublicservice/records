2027 Annual DOT Rider Adjustment
Please answer the following regarding Worksheet 4, Revenue Requirement:

In past DOT quarterly reports, there was Plant in Service for Small PI Distribution Mains for the months January 2025 – December 2025. Please provide why, for the months January 2025 – December 2025, Small PI Distribution Mains (Line 15) shows no plant in service in the annual rider model.

The Company added categories to Plant in Service for House Regulators, Meters, Services, and Distribution Measure/Reg Station Equip in the Q1 2026 DOT Report. Please provide how these categories have values in the months of January 2025 – December 2025, if they did not exist until 2026.

Please provide why Large PI Distribution Mains for the month of October 2025 in the Q2 2026 Report is different than that of the Annual Rider Model.

RESPONSE:

As shown in the annual rider model, the Company generally budgets DOT projects in FERC Account 376, Small PI Distribution Mains, because the primary scope and majority of the anticipated costs relate to main relocation or replacement work. As explained in the Company’s response to STF-24-2, DOT projects may also include associated facilities, such as services, meters, and regulators, when those facilities are affected by the main relocation or replacement.

The 2027 Annual Rider Model incorporated the re-allocation of the applicable actual dollars for the months of January 2025 – December 2025 from Small PI Distribution Mains to services, meters, and house regulators. The related additions were included in the quarterly DOT model when placed in service and were initially reflected in FERC Account 376 Distribution Mains. Accordingly, incorporating the additional FERC categories did not add previously omitted investment to the model, change the total additions, or result in duplicate recovery; it provided a more detailed classification of additions already reflected in the model. 

Because DOT projects are budgeted and managed at the overall project level, presenting the additions by the more detailed asset classifications required a review of the completed asset records after the related facilities were placed in service. The Company performed that review to confirm that the applicable costs were appropriately assigned and that the model classifications were consistent with the underlying Plant Accounting records. Following that validation, the additional FERC categories were incorporated into the Q1 2026 DOT model.

Please note that the overall additions for 2025 remain the same between the rider filing and prior reporting, totaling $59,988,442 for the twelve months ending 2025.

Regarding the Inputs worksheet, how was the $0.20 increase in Residential rates determined and what is driving this increase?

RESPONSE:

The $0.20 increase in the Residential rate is primarily driven by approximately $81 million of additional DOT investment reflected in the 2027 rider, which increases the rate by approximately $0.18, and the full-year revenue requirement associated with investment placed in service during 2026, which increases the rate by approximately $0.10. These increases are partially offset by approximately $0.08 associated with the projected over-recovery at the end of 2026. Other changes, including customer count, the state income tax rate, and the cost of capital, had a comparatively minor impact on the rate change.

Please explain why customer projections are different between the Q2 2026 DOT and SRR Report for the months of July 2026 – December 2026.

RESPONSE:


The difference in customer projections between the Q2 2026 DOT and SRR Report for the months of July 2026 – December 2026 is due to the use of different customer billing projections used between the two models. Customer billing projections are periodically reviewed and updated. As a result, billing unit projections for the same period may vary over time as more recent information becomes available and is incorporated into the latest projection. The Q3 2026 SRR model will be updated to align with the Q3 2026 DOT model and ECON-1 model customer projections.

Regarding the Q2 2026 SRR Report, please provide a detailed explanation of the i-CDP adjustment provided in Row 91 of the Capital Budget Reporting and how the adjustment was derived.

RESPONSE:

The originally approved 2026 i-CDP SRR Capital Budget was $148,465,116.87. Following the approved June 2026 Amendment, the SRR Total Budget was revised to $143,235,881.41 as reflected below in the table.

The adjustment reflected in Row 91 represents the amount necessary to reconcile the previously approved i-CDP budget to the revised SRR Total authorized through the Amendment. 

The adjustment was derived as follows:
$143,235,881.41 − $148,465,115.87 = ($5,229,234.46)

Accordingly, the ($5,229,234.46) adjustment brings the SRR Total into alignment with the approved June 2026 Amendment.
