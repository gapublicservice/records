STF-PIA-46-4

Question: 

Provide the company policies, system rules, or procedures governing previous location balance transfers during the period covered.

a. Criteria and conditions under which a previous location balance is transferred to a new or existing account.
b. Customer notification practices (method, timing, and content) when a transfer occurs.
c. Payment allocation hierarchy indicating how payments are applied between transferred balances and current charges.

Response:

The Company assigns each customer a unique customer number that remains associated with that customer throughout the customer lifecycle. Although a customer may have multiple service account numbers, the unique customer number enables the Company to identify accounts belonging to the same customer. The Company uses this identifier to transfer an unpaid balance from a customer’s prior account to the customer’s active account, helping ensure that the balance remains the responsibility of the customer who incurred it rather than being borne by other customers. 

Final or written-off balances may be transferred to an active account when the following criteria are met:
• The active account is associated with the same customer;
• The customer’s identifying information matches;
• The balance is transferred to a newly established account owned by that customer; and
• The balance remains within the applicable statute of limitations.

No separate notification is provided when a transfer occurs. The transferred amount appears on the active account bill as “Past Due, Previous Location Balance.” This amount reflects a balance for which the customer previously received applicable notifications. 

If an unpaid final balance is identified while new service is being established, a consultant will advise the customer that the balance remains the customer’s responsibility and may be billed to the account after service is established.

Please see the Company’s response to subpart (b) in the Company’s response to STF-PIA-46-2.

a. Whether the previous location balance transfer was initiated by the customer or the Company.
b. Whether the transferred balance was subsequently paid in full, partially paid, or remains outstanding.
c. Total amount subsequently paid by the customer on the transferred balance.

d. The Company does not separately track transferred balances that are subsequently written off as bad debt.
