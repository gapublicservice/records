LETTER ORDERDOCKET NO. 27825
November 17, 2026


In Re: Docket No. 27825 Petition by Cobb County Board of Commissioners For Assignment of Abbreviated N11 Dialing Code 3-1-1 for Non-Emergency Services In Cobb County, Georgia

ORDER ACKNOWLEDGING IMPLEMENTATION OF 3-1-1 SERVICE AND CONTINUING ASSIGNMENT OF ABBREVIATED N11 DIALING CODE 3-1-1

PROCEDURAL HISTORY

By its Order Approving Request, issued September 18, 2008 upon action taken in Administrative Session on September 16, 2008 (the “2008 Order”), the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) assigned the abbreviated N11 dialing code 3-1-1 to the Cobb County Board of Commissioners (“Cobb County”) to provide access to the county’s non-emergency government services. The Commission granted the assignment subject to the express condition that Cobb County implement and maintain a call routing solution that will route Cobb County originating calls to Cobb County’s call center while permitting other jurisdictions with shared central offices to route their calls to their own non-emergency call centers using the vendor of their choice. The 2008 Order further identified the fourteen central offices comprising the calling scope of Cobb County’s local calling area, provided that Cobb County shall be allowed six months from the date of the Order to implement its 3-1-1 service, and directed that Cobb County shall notify the Commission Staff (“Staff”) in writing upon full implementation of the 3-1-1 service.

On November 10, 2008, Cobb County filed a letter requesting a twelve-month extension of the implementation period. By its Order Granting Extension, issued December 5, 2008 upon action taken in Administrative Session on December 2, 2008, the Commission adopted Staff’s recommendation and granted the requested twelve-month extension, which extended the implementation period provided in the 2008 Order through March 18, 2010.
On October 13, 2009, Cobb County filed a letter requesting a further twenty-four-month extension. In that letter Cobb County reported that dialed 3-1-1 calls were being routed to the county’s existing general information line.
By letter dated October 27, 2009, Staff advised Cobb County that, based upon the information reported, approval from the Commission for an extension of time for Cobb County to implement its central call center was not required, and directed that Cobb County should continue to notify Staff in writing of its progress in placing the 3-1-1 abbreviated dialing code into service.
By electronic mail dated September 21, 2022, Staff advised Cobb County that a new petition would not be required and that Cobb County need only file a letter in this Docket confirming that 3-1-1 was implemented consistent with the 2008 Order.
On July 28, 2026, the Cobb County Board of Commissioners approved funding for the staffing of the 3-1-1 call center.
On October 20, 2026, Cobb County filed a letter in this Docket providing written notification of implementation of 3-1-1 service consistent with the 2008 Order. Cobb County states that the abbreviated dialing code will serve the citizens of Cobb County by providing rapid “one-stop” access to the county’s non-emergency governmental services through its Customer Service Center, and that the anticipated official launch date is January 7, 2027.

FINDINGS

Continuing Effect of the Assignment
The 2008 Order granted the assignment of the abbreviated dialing code 3-1-1 subject to a single express condition, namely the implementation and maintenance of the call routing solution described therein. The six-month implementation period was framed as a period Cobb County “shall be allowed,” and the 2008 Order contains no provision for forfeiture, revocation, or automatic termination of the assignment upon expiration of that period. The Commission accordingly finds that the assignment of the abbreviated dialing code 3-1-1 to Cobb County has remained in effect at all times since September 18, 2008, and that no new petition for assignment is required.
Implementation Period
The 2008 Order assigned the dialing code and required Cobb County to route its 3-1-1 calls to the county. It did not require that those calls be answered at any particular facility. Cobb County had proposed to answer them at a new Customer Service Center, and the 2008 Order described that proposal, but the Order set no date for opening it and conditioned the assignment only on call routing. Cobb County placed the dialing code in service in 2009 and routed 3-1-1 calls to its existing general information line, which satisfied what the Order required. Staff said as much on October 27, 2009, advising that no extension of time was necessary, and the Commission adopts that reading. To the extent the implementation period, as extended through March 18, 2010, might instead be read to have required the Customer Service Center to be open by that date, the Commission finds good cause to extend it, as set forth below.
The Commission finds good cause for the time that has elapsed since the 2008 Order. Cobb County placed the dialing code in service in 2009 and reported that 3-1-1 calls have been answered by the county since that time. Cobb County reported that financial constraints caused it to defer the Customer Service Center, and funding for staffing was not approved until July 28, 2026. Cobb County kept Staff apprised of its status and proceeded in reliance upon Staff’s guidance of October 27, 2009 and September 21, 2022.

The Routing Condition
The 2008 Order conditioned the assignment on a result: Cobb County originating calls reach Cobb County’s call center, while other jurisdictions sharing those central offices remain free to route their own 3-1-1 calls to their own non-emergency call centers, using the vendor of their choice. By its terms the condition requires both implementation and maintenance, and it therefore continues in effect. The particular method identified in 2008 was a Zip+4 call routing solution that BellSouth Telecommunications, Inc. d/b/a AT&T Georgia represented it had provided to Cobb County. Call routing methods change, and the Commission’s concern is with the result rather than the method. The condition is accordingly restated below in terms of the result required, so that the protection afforded to adjoining jurisdictions is preserved regardless of how 3-1-1 calls originate or are routed.

Notification
Cobb County’s October 20, 2026 letter states that 3-1-1 service has been implemented consistent with the 2008 Order, while also stating that the anticipated official launch date is January 7, 2027. The service the letter describes is the use of the abbreviated dialing code 3-1-1 contemplated by the 2008 Order. The Commission accepts the letter as notification that Cobb County is prepared to launch service and finds it appropriate to require written confirmation once service is in operation.

WHEREFORE IT IS ORDERED, that the assignment of the abbreviated N11 dialing code 3-1-1 to Cobb County made by the Commission’s Order of September 18, 2008 has remained in effect continuously since that date and is hereby continued, and that no new petition for assignment of the abbreviated dialing code 3-1-1 is required of Cobb County.
ORDERED FURTHER, that Staff’s construction of the 2008 Order as set forth in its letter of October 27, 2009 is ratified and adopted, and that, to the extent the implementation period provided in the 2008 Order, as extended through March 18, 2010, may be read to apply to the completion of Cobb County’s Customer Service Center, that period is hereby extended through January 7, 2027.
ORDERED FURTHER, that Cobb County’s request of October 13, 2009 for a twenty-four-month extension of the implementation period is dismissed as moot.
ORDERED FURTHER, that Staff’s construction of the 2008 Order as set forth in its letter of October 27, 2009 is ratified and adopted, and that, to the extent the implementation period provided in the 2008 Order, as extended through March 18, 2010, may be read to apply to the completion of Cobb County’s Customer Service Center, that period is hereby extended through January 7, 2027, and no lapse in Cobb County’s authority to use the abbreviated dialing code 3-1-1 is found to have occurred.
ORDERED FURTHER, that Cobb County’s letter of October 20, 2026 is accepted as notification that Cobb County is prepared to launch 3-1-1 service.
ORDERED FURTHER, that Cobb County shall notify the Commission in writing within thirty (30) days following the official launch of 3-1-1 service confirming that the service is in operation.
ORDERED FURTHER, that all findings, conclusions, and decisions contained within the preceding sections of this Order are adopted as findings of fact, conclusions of law, and decisions of regulatory policy of this Commission.
ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing, oral argument, or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of this Order, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.
ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over this matter is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order(s) as this Commission may deem just and proper.

The above by action of the Commission in Administrative Session on the 17th day of November 2026.
