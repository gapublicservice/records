ORDER APPROVING GEORGIA POWER COMPANY’S NUCLEAR UPRATES (NU-1) TARIFF

Docket No. 44280

In Re: Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Base Rate Case


BACKGROUND

On September 21, 2026, Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) filed its request for approval of the Nuclear Uprates (NU1) Tariff pursuant to the Order Adopting Stipulation in the Company’s 2022 Rate Case, in Docket No. 44280. 

The NU1 Tariff creates a voluntary subscription program for eligible large load commercial and industrial customers who add at least 100 megawatts of new load. Under the tariff, participating customers may subscribe to ZeroEmission Credits (“ZECs”) associated with incremental nuclear generation produced from the Company’s approved Extended Power Uprate (“EPU”) projects. Participants pay an upfront Program Enrollment Contribution (“PEC”) and monthly subscription payments based on the amount of ZECs retired on their behalf. The subscription program will be fully subscribed, contingent upon Commission approval of both the tariff and the Company’s Application for Approval of the 2026 Plant Hatch 1-2 Nuclear Extended Power Uprates projects in Docket Nos. 56002 and 56003.

The NU1 Tariff is structured to align subscriber payments with the timing of nuclear uprate investments, thereby reducing cost impacts on nonparticipating customers and supporting expanded carbonfree nuclear output. Subscription levels are limited to no more than 100 percent of a customer’s annual Georgia Power served load, and the Company may adjust subscription quantities if customer loads deviate from contracted levels. Customers must execute long term service agreements, up to 25 years, consistent with the operating life of the underlying nuclear assets.

SUMMARY

At the Commission’s October 1, 2026, Energy Committee, Staff recommended the Commission approve the NU-1 Tariff contingent upon also approving Georgia Power’s 2026 Plant Hatch 1-2 Nuclear Extended Power Uprates (EPU) project in Docket Nos. 56002 and 56003. 
The Commission unanimously approved Staff’s recommendation during its Administrative Session on October 6, 2026.  Subsequently, the Commission approved related EPU projects in the relevant dockets. 

WHEREFORE, it is

ORDERED, that the Commission approves Georgia Power Company’s Nuclear Uprates (NU-1) Tariff, as filed in Docket No. 44280. 

ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing or oral argument or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of this Order, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.

ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over this matter is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order or Orders as the Commission may deem just and proper.

The above by action of the Commission in its Administrative Session on the 6th day of October 2026.
