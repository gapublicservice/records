CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

      This CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into
this     day of ________________, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), by and between VoltaGrid,
LLC (“VoltaGrid”) and Jennifer Whitfield (“Recipient”).

                                         WITNESSETH:

      WHEREAS, Recipient represents the interests of Sustainable Newton, Sara and Randall
“Randy” Vinson, and Theodosia and William “Billy” Wade (“Clients” and together with
VoltaGrid, the “Parties”);

       WHEREAS, Recipient desires to obtain copies of certain documents and information for
the purpose of representing Recipient’s Clients before the Georgia Public Service Commission
(“PSC” or the “Commission”) regarding the Petition for Expedited Declaratory Judgment and
Enforcement Against VoltaGrid in Docket No. 57243;

        WHEREAS, to provide access to certain trade secret information that may be produced
to the Public Service Commission concerning issues raised and document requests posed in this
Docket, the Parties enter into this Agreement;

       NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties and Recipient hereby agree as follows:

         1. Definitions. “Trade Secret Information” means all documents, materials and
information in whatever format provided by VoltaGrid to Recipient under the above referenced
Docket and which are marked “Trade Secret”, and specifically including, without limitation,
VoltaGrid’s official filings and responses to PSC Staff data requests. Trade Secret Information
also includes information that is copied or transferred to notes from Trade Secret Information by
persons acting pursuant to this Agreement and that portion of all notes, working copies, computer
data storage, digests, summaries or abstracts prepared from this material containing Trade Secret
Information. Trade Secret Information does not include (i) information that is now in or hereafter
enters the public domain through no action on Recipient’s part in violation of the terms or
conditions hereof; (ii) information that Recipient can demonstrate was in Recipient’s possession
at the time of disclosure and was not acquired by Recipient directly or indirectly from VoltaGrid on
a confidential basis; (iii) information disclosed by VoltaGrid to others on an unrestricted, non-
confidential basis; or (iv) information provided to Recipient by VoltaGrid that is not marked
“Trade Secret.”

         2. Non-disclosure. Recipient agrees that all Trade Secret Information will be kept
strictly confidential pursuant to this Agreement. Recipient agrees that without the prior written
consent               of              VoltaGrid            or             a              decision

of the Commission under Rule 515-3-l-.11 that Trade Secret Information either can be used
without restriction or with specified restrictions, Trade Secret Information will not be disclosed in
whole or in part, to any person or entity in violation of this Agreement. Recipient agrees that the
only individuals who will be permitted access to Trade Secret Information other than Recipient
will be employees or contractors of Recipient or of Recipient’s Client who have first signed a copy
of the attached addendum (Exhibit 1). Recipient will provide VoltaGrid a copy of Exhibit 1
within five days after a final order from the PSC.

        3. Permitted Use. Recipient may use Trade Secret Information only for the purpose of
assisting and representing Recipient’s Client in this Docket and in any appeals from this Docket.
This “Permitted Use” includes using Trade Secret Information in the preparation and
presentation of testimony, briefs and advocacy before the PSC in this Docket, or appeals
regarding these proceedings. Recipient agrees that if it will use Trade Secret Information
pursuant to these permitted uses in a PSC or court proceeding, it will follow the trade secret
provisions and practices applicable to the PSC or applicable court to ensure that Trade Secret
Information is protected from public disclosure.

        4. Directors, Officers, Employees and Affiliates. The Parties agree to be responsible for
the actions, uses and disclosures of their respective directors, officers, employees, affiliates,
contractors, consultants and advisors (as applicable).

        5. Compelled Disclosure. If Recipient or any individual with authorized access to
Trade Secret Information becomes legally compelled to disclose any of the Trade Secret
Information, that person will provide VoltaGrid with prompt notice so that VoltaGrid may seek a
protective order or other appropriate remedy. If such protective order or other remedy is not
obtained, such person will furnish only that portion of the Trade Secret Information that is legally
required and Recipient and such person will cooperate with VoltaGrid’s counsel to enable
VoltaGrid to obtain a protective order or other reliable assurance that confidential treatment will
be accorded Trade Secret Information that is subject to disclosure.

       6. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed in all respects, whether as to validity,
construction, capacity, performance or otherwise, by and under the laws of the state of Georgia
(without giving effect to principles of conflicts of laws).

        7. Severability. All provisions of this Agreement are severable, and the unenforceability
or invalidity of any of the provisions of this Agreement will not affect the validity or enforceability
of the remaining provisions of this Agreement.

         8. Term. The term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and will
terminate forty (40) days after the date the final order in or from this docket is issued by the PSC or
appellate body, if any, rendering a decision. With respect to any Trade Secret Information that
constitutes a “trade secret” under applicable law, the covenants herein will apply for the life of the
trade secret. Within five (5) days of the termination of this Agreement, Recipient and all persons
authorized by this Agreement to be in possession of Trade Secret Information will return all
Trade Secret Information to the Public Service Commission or affirm in writing that such Trade
Secret Information has been permanently destroyed.

       9. Remedies. Recipient expressly understands and agrees that in the event of any breach
                                                                                             2

or threatened breach of this Agreement, VoltaGrid is entitled to all remedies available at law or in
equity.

      10. Execution. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which are
deemed an original, but all of which together constitute one and the same instrument.
Facsimile/electronic signatures hereto are deemed original signatures.


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Recipient and Parties hereto have caused this Agreement to be
executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the date and year first above written.
