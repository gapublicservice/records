On April 4, 2025, American’s for Affordable Clean Energy (“AACE”) filed comments on the Company’s Plan. 

AACE noted that “four (4) of the Company’s Community Chargers present an interesting issue because they are located within fifteen (15) miles of an existing fast charger.” And “…begs the question: whether placement of a Community Charger within fifteen (15) miles of an already existing Company or privately-owned charger at ratepayer expense advanced the goal of providing EV Chargers in truly unserved areas?” 

Per Staff and AACE’s findings, along with Company acknowledgement, three of the four locations are within a 15-mile radius of an existing charging station. The fourth location, Proposal 11, is “... located just over fifteen miles from Hazlehurst by road, but within fifteen (15) miles as the crow flies” per AACE. A further point of contention by both Staff and AACE is that one of the proposed locations, Proposal 4 - 2025, fell within a fifteen (15) mile radius of an existing electric vehicle charging station (Proposal 4 from the Company’s 2024 plan in Cedartown, GA). Figures 2 and 3 below identify the locations that Commission Staff and AACE had determined to be within 15 miles of an existing EV charging station.

The Community Charging program was intended to focus on underserved areas. Underserved was evidenced by being more than a mile from a designated Alternative Fuel Corridor, rural and income qualified (Tier 1 and Tier 2) counties and locations not being served or not being served or planned to be served by a Private Provider under a right of first refusal (ROFR) within a 15-mile radius. 
Staff recommended the Commission issue an order to approve eight of the eleven proposed Community Charging locations presented in GPC’s Plan for the year 2025 that do not fall within the 15-mile radius of existing non-Tesla DCFC locations. Staff also recommended the Commission order allow Georgia Power to select three additional locations that are not being served by existing charging stations within a 15-mile radius and file for approval, subject to the same ROFR process in the Electric Transportation Term Sheet. 


The Commission approved Staff’s recommendation at its June 3, 2025, Administrative Session with a vote of 3 to 1, with one Commissioner not present. 


* * * * * * * * * * * * *

WHEREFORE IT IS ORDERED that Georgia Power Company’s eight remaining Community Charging locations for the year 2025, are hereby approved and Proposals No. 3, 4 and 9 are not approved.  
ORDERED FURTHER, that the most current Georgia Department of Community Affairs Job Tax Credit Tier maps for eligibility consideration in the Community Charging program are hereby adopted.
ORDERED FURTHER, that a 15-mile radius restriction as measured around Georgia Power Company’s proposed locations for eligibility consideration in the Community Charging program is hereby adopted.
ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing, or oral argument or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of this Order, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.  
ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over this matter is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order or Orders as this Commission may deem just and proper.
The above by action of the Commission in Administrative Session on the 3rd day of June, 2025.
