                 APPLICATION FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE OF
           THE METROPOLITAN ATLANTA RAPID TRANSIT AUTHORITY


       COMES NOW, the Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority (hereafter “MARTA”),

pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§50-13-14 and 46-2-59 and Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06, and by and

through counsel, hereby files this Application for Leave to Intervene (“Application”) in the

above-referenced docket. In support thereof, MARTA respectfully shows the Commission as

follows.

                                                1.

       On Jun 24, 2022, Georgia Power filed its 2022 Rate Case, under Docket No. 44280, with

the Commission. MARTA intervened in that docket. On December 30, 2022, the Commission

issued its Order Adopting Settlement Agreement as Modified. In that Order, the Commission

approved a three-year Alternative Rate Plan (“ARP”), providing that the Georgia Power

Company must file a new base rate case by July 1, 2025. On May 19, 2025, Georgia Power

Company (hereinafter “Georgia Power”) and Commission Public Interest Advocacy Staff filed a

Joint Petition to Extend the Alternate Rate Plan with an accompanying Stipulation in Docket No.

44280, essentially extending or freezing rates for an additional three years, for which they seek

Commission approval. On May 20, 2025, the Commission approved a procedural and

scheduling order for this docket. Although already approved as an Intervenor in Docket 44280,

MARTA files this Application for Leave to Intervene in the proceeding related to the proposed

Stipulation to Extend the Alternative Rate Plan. Pursuant to the procedural and scheduling

order, this Application to allow MARTA to participate is timely.

                                                 2.

         MARTA is a public authority created by the MARTA Act, Georgia Laws 1965, page

2243, et. seq., as amended from time to time. Its mission is to advocate and provide safe, multi-

modal transit services that advance prosperity, connectivity, and equity for a more livable region.

MARTA provides service to 2.2 million residents of Metro Atlanta and is one of the top 10

transportation agencies in the United States. Its combined bus and rail services support over

24,000 jobs throughout Atlanta and the State of Georgia. MARTA’s rail service involves over

330 rail cars capable of carrying passengers to any of its 38 stations, and its 550 buses provide

service along 1,800 miles of road on 113 routes. MARTA currently operates six electric buses

with plans to expand its fleet of electric vehicles. MARTA’s services help drive economic

development in and around the metro-region. MARTA’s footprint in the metro-area is fairly

significant, with 905.7 acres of property, including 388.7 acres directly adjacent to its MARTA

stations. MARTA purchases electricity from Georgia Power to power its electric rail service and

provides Electric Vehicle charging stations to its patrons and employees. Cost effective, reliable

energy is important to its operations and services. Energy costs have a significant impact on

MARTA’s mission, operations, and its customers.

                                                 3.

         In furtherance of its interests and on behalf of its customers MARTA has intervened in

other rate case proceedings before the Commission, including Georgia Power’s 2013 (Docket

No. 36989), 2010 (Docket No. 31958), 2019 (Docket No. 42516), 2022 (Docket No. 44280) rate

cases.

                                                 4.

       MARTA has an interest in the proceeding related to the proposed Stipulation to Extend

the Alternative Rate Plan on its own behalf and on behalf of its employees and customers.

Georgia Power is MARTA’s electric service provider and its customers are subject to the

impacts of the Company’s services and rates. MARTA seeks to intervene in this proceeding to

continue to protect its interests and the interests of its employees and customers.

                                                 5.

       MARTA’s interests and those of its customers are not adequately represented by other

parties to these proceedings, including the other intervening parties, and MARTA’s intervention

and participation in this matter will not unduly delay the proceedings or prejudice the rights of

other parties. If this application is granted MARTA intends to fully participate in this docket,

including through the submission of direct expert testimony from MARTA staff and/or outside

experts.

                                                 6.

       MARTA respectfully requests that it be granted leave to intervene and participate fully as

a party in these proceedings, including the right to present testimony and exhibits, cross-examine

witnesses, and file briefs.
