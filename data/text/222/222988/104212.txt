   APPLICATION OF GEORGIA SOLAR ENERGY ASSOCIATION (“GA SOLAR”)
                     FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE

       Georgia Solar Energy Association (“GA Solar”) hereby applies to the Georgia Public

Service Commission (“Commission”) for leave to intervene in the above-referenced docket

pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 46-2-59 and 50-13-14 and Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06. In support

thereof, GA Solar respectfully shows as follows:

                                               1.

       On May 19, 2025, Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or “the Company”) and

the Public Interest Advocacy Staff (“Staff”) filed a Joint Petition and Stipulation to

Extend the Alternate Rate Plan (“ARP”). GA Solar hereby files this Application for

Leave to Intervene and participate in this docket. This Application is timely filed.

                                                 2.

       Georgia Solar Energy Association is a not-for-profit organization headquartered in

Atlanta, Georgia. GA Solar represents the interests of its members in promoting renewable

energy and related sustainable practices. GA Solar helps benefit Georgia's energy consumers by

advocating for solar power in Georgia by helping to inform local and state leaders about the

kinds of policies that make the benefits of solar accessible to all. This advocacy supports

investment and local economic growth that keeps Georgia’s economy strong.


       Additionally, GA Solar helps solar professionals stay abreast of the latest news, trends

and standards, as well as providing them with networking and business outreach opportunities.

By connecting home and business owners, GA Solar helps find skilled solar professionals and

provides guides to help consumers evaluate solar options. In addition, GA Solar helps consumers

decide how to access solar energy in a way that saves them money on their power bills and

reduces their environmental footprint. Finally, GA Solar helps consumers find skilled solar

professionals they can trust and provide guides to evaluate solar options.


                                                 4.

       GA Solar has an interest relating to the proposed Stipulation to Extend the Alternate Rate

Plan proceedings both on its own behalf and on behalf of its members. Many of GA Solar’s

members purchase electricity from Georgia Power Company, both for their businesses and for

their individual residences. Their electricity consumption and costs for electricity will be affected

by the Commission’s actions in these dockets.

                                                 5.

       GA Solar’s mission is directly implicated by Georgia Power’s rate plan choices as

reflected in its Rate Case Filing. GA Solar intends to address the concerns of both itself and its

                                                 2

members regarding how the Company’s choices impact the resulting bills that customers pay. As

a result, GA Solar has substantial and vital interests in the outcome of these proceedings and

seeks to intervene to protect these interests.

                                                  6.

        In furtherance of its interests and on behalf of its members, GA Solar has intervened in

many prior proceedings before the Commission. Among the Commission proceedings in which

GA Solar has intervened or otherwise participated are Georgia Power’s 2025 IRP, Docket

Number 56002 and 56003, Georgia Power’s 2022 Base Rate Case, Docket 44280, and being the

same as the docket in this case, Georgia Power’s 2022 IRP and DSM proceedings, Docket Nos.

44160, 44161, Georgia Power’s 2019 IRP and DSM proceedings, Docket Nos. 42310, 42311;

Georgia Power’s 2019 Base Rate Case, Docket No. 42516.

                                                  7.

        The interests of GA Solar and its members are not adequately represented by other parties

to these proceedings. Other parties that have applied to intervene do not have interests similar to

those of GA Solar. The applicants’ intervention would not unduly delay the proceedings or

prejudice the rights of other parties.

                                                  8.

        If this application is granted, GA Solar intends to participate as a party in this docket,

reserving the ability to cross-examining witnesses, filing and presenting direct testimony, and

filing proposed orders and/or briefs. GA Solar has not yet finalized plans for direct testimony.

                                                  9.


        GA Solar respectfully requests that the following persons receive all notices,

correspondence and copies of orders and other materials in this docket, and be placed upon the

official service list:
