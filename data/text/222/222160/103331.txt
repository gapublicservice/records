Georgia Power Company
Docket No. 44280
2022 Base Rate Case
Staff Data Request No. STF-PIA-26


STF-PIA-26-3 Supplemental

Question: 

Refer to the Monthly Operating Report in Docket No. 2663, “Schedule 71”, for transformer retirements. 
What has led to the decreased value in retirements December 2020 – Present, compared to December 2016 – December 2019? 
Please provide the total number of Distribution transformers associated to the December “Retirements” for the years ended 2016-2023.

Response:

As noted by the Company in the December 23, 2024 filing of STF-PIA-26-3, in preparing the response for this data request, the Company determined that the total population of actual retirements of distribution transformers in FERC account 368, Line Transformers, for 2019 through November 2024 was not accounted for correctly on the balance sheet when they were physically removed from operations. STF-PIA-26-3 Attachment_Supplemental provided herein reflects the retirements recorded in the accounting system by year for 2016 through 2024 and actual retirements removed from service in the corresponding period. The balance was trued up in December 2024 to account for actual retirements removed from service. Please note, the retirements do not have an impact on net retail rate base because retirements are recorded to both FERC 101 Plant in Service and FERC 108 Accumulated Reserve, which offset in Net Plant in Service.

Please see STF-PIA-26-3 Attachment_Supplemental.

STF-PIA-26-4 Errata

Question: 

What is the average age of transformers currently in the field. 
If data is available provide number of transformers currently in the field and in inventory by age range 0-5 years, 6-10 years, 11-15 years, 16-20 years, 21-25 years, 26-30 years, 31-35 years, 36-40 years, 41+ years.

Response:

Due to the result of the findings provided in the Company’s response to STF-PIA-26-3_Supplemental, the accounting lives previously provided by the Company in the December 23, 2024 filing of STF-PIA-26-4 are inaccurate. These assets are accounted for as mass property and the actual age of the transformers are not maintained in the Company’s property records.
