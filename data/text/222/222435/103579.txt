===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-PIA-15/STF-PIA-15-2.docx =====
STF-PIA-15-2
Question:
Please explain the methodology used by AEG in the Company’s 2024 energy efficiency potential study to forecast the number of customers adopting energy efficiency measures for each sector (residential, commercial, industrial) for the Company’s Proposed Case.
Response:
Applied Energy Group (“AEG”) used a bottom-up approach to perform the 2024 energy efficiency potential study and to forecast the number of customers adopting energy efficiency measures for each sector for the Company’s Proposed Case. This approach involved performing a market characterization of all sectors, defining and characterizing eligible energy efficiency measures, developing a baseline, end-use forecast of energy consumption, and estimating savings at the measure level at different levels of savings potential. The 2024 energy efficiency potential study’s technical achievable potential was primarily used to inform the Company’s Proposed Case. This forecast assumes that customers install the most efficient, technically feasible measure at time of replacement for end-use equipment or over an assumed period for retrofit measures and applies market penetration curves that are intended to represent market barriers, customer awareness and attitudes, program maturity, and other factors that affect market penetration of energy efficiency measures.

STF-PIA-15-3
Question:
Please describe any model used by AEG in the Company’s 2024 energy efficiency potential study to forecast the number of customers adopting energy efficiency measures for the 100 percent incentive scenario. In addition, please describe any variables or factors included in the models used to forecast the number of customers adopting energy efficiency measures for the Proposed Case.
Response:
Applied Energy Group (“AEG”) used its Microsoft Excel-based Load Management Analysis and Planning (“LoadMAP”) model to forecast the number of customers adopting energy efficiency measures for the 100% incentive scenario. Key factors used to forecast the number of customers adopting energy efficiency measures for reference in the Company’s Proposed Cased include: market size and segmentation, equipment saturations, vintage distributions, customer growth, efficiency options, codes and standards, purchase shares, and assumed customer participation rates.

===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-PIA-15/STF-PIA-15-4.docx =====
STF-PIA-15-4
Question:
When developing the estimates of energy efficiency potential for the scenario based on setting incentives at 100 percent of incremental costs, please explain if AEG considered the program participation experience of other utilities in the U.S. that have saved more than 0.75 percent of annual retail electric sales during the development of the 2024 Georgia Power TEAPOT.
Response:
Yes, Applied Energy Group (“AEG”) considered the program participation experience of other U.S. utilities that have saved more than 0.75% of annual retail electric sales during the development of the 2024 Technical Economic Achievable Potential (“TEAPot”) study. AEG’s analysis began with an assumption that over a 20-year period, 85% of the economic potential could be acquired if a utility was willing to pay the full incremental measure cost, which is consistent with the assumption in the 2021 Northwest Power and Conservation Council Plan, available here: https://www.nwcouncil.org/2021-northwest-power-plan/. With this assumption as an anchor point, AEG developed achievability assumptions for other incentive scenarios by benchmarking other utilities’ program savings and incentive levels to investigate the relationship between incentives and program savings.
