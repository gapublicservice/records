Please explain the increase in estimated costs of this project compared to the estimate provided in the 2023 IRP Update Filing.
Please explain the advanced Need Date of this project compared to the need date provided in the 2023 IRP Update Filing.
Response:
The cost provided in the 2023 Integrated Resource Plan (“IRP”) Update filing was based on planning grade estimates. The cost for projects is revised and refined through scoping, engineering, and design processes, which include, but are not limited to, the increase in material costs over the years. The updated cost for all projects is provided annually through the Ten-Year Plan process. 

The project need date proposed during the 2023 GPC IRP Update Transmission Supplemental Filing was specific to the load levels and the addition of the Yates CTs for the 2023 IRP Update  (Docket No. 55378).The project was not needed as part of the 2023 Transmission Planning Compliance Process, and was not part of the final 2023 GA Integrated Transmission System (“ITS”) Ten-Year Plan as the load levels in the 2023 GA ITS Ten-Year plan were based on the Budget 2023 (“B2023”) load forecast.  The new need date in the 2024 GA ITS Ten-Year Plan included in Technical Appendix Volume 3  for this project was based on Transmission Planning compliance needs associated with NERC Reliability Standard TPL-001-05 and the B2024 load forecast and generation commitments in the 2024 base cases. Refer to Section A.4 in Technical Appendix Volume 3.
