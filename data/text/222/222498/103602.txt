 5              Q.       WHAT CHANNELS DOES WALMART UTILIZE TO SECURE CLEAN

 7              A.       To meet its clean energy goals, Walmart utilizes three primary channels to secure

 8                       resources:

 9                              Development of off-site resources: These products are typically structured

10                               to replace other energy, both physically and on the bill. This mechanism

11                               allows Walmart to leverage its scale to drive the best project economics

12                               while simultaneously minimizing transaction time and costs. To date,

13                               Walmart has contracted for these resources in deregulated markets through

14                               Texas Retail Energy, LLC, a competitive electric supplier wholly owned by

15                               Walmart that serves as its electric supplier in most deregulated retail

16                               markets, to directly serve its load.

17                                      Walmart has also entered into "Virtual PPAs" and "Renewable

18                               Energy Certificate ("REC") Agreements" in deregulated wholesale markets

19                               for clean energy assets. These contracts do not directly serve Walmart's

20                               load, but do allow Walmart to bring new large-scale renewable resources to

21                               the market by entering into long-term financial agreements for clean energy

22                               power and environmental attributes.

21        projects in Illinois and New York, which will serve low-to-moderate income

22        households, public schools, and religious institutions, among others.
