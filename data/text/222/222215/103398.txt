Question:   
For all generic resource options presented in the 2025 IRP that were modeled in Aurora, provide the following information electronically, with all formulae intact. 
Please provide all workpapers used in the technology screening process.  Provide a list of resources that remained at the end of each step.  Please confirm the final step led to the resources that were modeled as selectable resources in the Aurora optimization analyses. 
Provide the workpapers used to derive the capital costs, fixed O&M costs, revenue requirements, variable O&M costs, and operating parameters as modeled in the 2025 IRP. Provide these workpapers electronically, in excel format, with formulas and calculations intact. Also, provide any workpapers that were used to prepare the data for input into Aurora.  
Provide workpapers detailing the development of all financial assumptions used in the Resource Mix study including the debt/equity ratio, the cost of debt and equity, the calculation of the after tax hurdle rate, and the calculation of the revenue requirement rate.  If different assumptions were used in other studies in the IRP, please provide a comparison of the assumptions in the other studies.

Provide earliest availability dates for all generic resource options.

Provide all workpapers, electronically, used in the development of the solar shapes (annual 8760 production profiles) for all solar resources modeled in the IRP Resource Mix Study. 
Please describe the solar tranches and costs that were modeled in the Aurora optimization studies.

Response:

The technology screening process is detailed in the 2025 IRP filing in Technical Appendix Volume 2, Technology Screening & Application Standards TRADE SECRET. Note that RICE is retained in the technology screening process but is not included in the Resource Mix Study. While RICE units have similar performance characteristics to CT w SCR, these units have substantially higher capital cost and are not expected to be selected over CT w SCR. Supercritical CO2, which is also retained in the screening process for generic resources, is assumed to be represented by CC w CCS in the Aurora analyses as indicated in the Technical Appendix Volume 2 Resource Mix Study. 
All capital expenditures and revenue requirements can be found in the SAM Files for Generics TRADE SECRET workpapers folder that was provided to Commission Staff on January 31, 2025, for Technical Appendix Volume 2 Resource Mix Study. Declining revenue requirements from the SAM files are inputs to STF-JKA-1-22 Attachment A TRADE SECRET and STF-JKA-1-22 Attachment B TRADE SECRET.  Outputs from STF-JKA-1-22 Attachments A & B are input to the Resource Mix Study workpaper titled “TAS to Aurora - FinalB25 TRADE SECRET.xlsx” to prepare the data for input to Aurora.
April 9, 2025 Supplemental Filing Update:  Please refer to STF-JKA-1-22 Supplemental Attachments A & B TRADE SECRET for updated spreadsheets providing the calculations of revenue requirements for generic technologies. Outdated versions of these spreadsheets were submitted with the original response to this data request. This update has no impact on the inputs or outputs of the Company’s Resource Mix Study, including the Aurora modeling and the “TAS to Aurora - FinalB25 TRADE SECRET.xlsx” workpaper, as filed on January 31, 2025.

Capital costs include land costs and the cost for 2 miles of Transmission Line to a substation. AFUDC and associated financing costs are included in the financial analysis that supports the Resource Mix Study as provided in the SAM Files referenced in part b. 
The revenue requirements for the generic resources were developed using the Standard Analysis Model (SAM), a tool the Company has developed to analyze financial impacts of potential and existing assets.  Transmission upgrades are not studied for generic resources, as the cost of resulting transmission interconnection and upgrade costs is only available after a lengthy interconnection study process which is sensitive to timelines and siting. SAM models are provided as stated in part b above, and natural gas firm transportation (“FT”) and fuel assumptions are provided in STK-JKA-1-22 Attachment H TRADE SECRET. 
Useful lives of all generic resources were provided in the initial filing in Technical Appendix Vol 2 Technology Screening & Application Standards. 
Availability dates of all generic resources were provided in the initial filing under Technical Appendix Vol 2 Technology Screening & Application Standards. 
Heat rates and outputs were developed using the Thermoflow suite of modeling software incorporating OEM performance data. The output shown in STF-JKA-1-22 Attachment I TRADE SECRET is derived from that software suite. 
STF-JKA-1-22 Attachment J TRADE SECRET presents a sample round-trip efficiency (“RTE”) calculation for the 4-hour battery energy storage system represented in Aurora.  Efficiency for medium duration energy storage was based on OEM specifications.
STF-JKA-1-22 Attachment K TRADE SECRET, STF-JKA-1-22 Attachment L TRADE SECRET, and STF-JKA-1-22 Attachment M TRADE SECRET provide results from solar performance models that support the generic solar resource profiles represented in the Resource Mix Study.
Generic solar PV resources are modeled as described in Technical Appendix Vol. 2 Resource Mix Study, sections 3.6.1 and 3.6.2.  Table 5 provides the associated costs used in the model.  Generic Solar PV is modeled in Aurora as 300 MW blocks, all having identical operating characteristics. 
Please see Technical Appendix Vol 2, section 1 “Technology Screening & Application Standards TRADE SECRET” Technical Appendix Vol 2 Resource Mix Study for detailed descriptions of all technologies included as expansion options in the 2025 IRP, including costs, capacity, and build limits. See STF-JKA-1-11 TRADE SECRET for discussion of differences between the 2023 IRP Update and the 2025 IRP.
