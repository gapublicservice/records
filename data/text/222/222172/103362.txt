===== DKT 56002 & 56003 HR-1 PD/HR-1-4.docx =====
HR-1-4

Information requested by:  Georgia Solar Energy Industries Association, Inc. – Steven Jones

Question:

QWhat percentage of Utility Scale is paired with storage?
A(Witness Mallard)  I’m not going to have that information in front of me.
QWould you mind getting that information and getting back to me with it?
A(Witness Mallard)  Sure.
QI’d like to make a hearing request for it, thank you.
A(Witness Mallard)  Yes.

Response:

Georgia Power currently has 27 Utility Scale solar projects online with a combined total of 2,460.33 MW. Two (7.4%) of the 27 Utility Scale solar projects have paired battery energy storage, for a combined total of 87.7 MW (3.6% of the total Utility Scale solar capacity). Excluded from this calculation are Company-Owned Utility Scale solar projects.
