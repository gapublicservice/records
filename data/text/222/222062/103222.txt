===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-JKA-4/STF-JKA-4-1.docx =====
STF-JKA-4-1

Question:

Please provide the workpapers/analyses that support the “Model Error” data shown in Table 1-3.  Include a description of the model that the Model Errors are derived from and provide a complete explanation of how the model errors were developed.

Response:

The model errors are the result of a comparison between the actual and predicted values from training loads shapes of the profile modeling software. A variance analysis and distribution was then created in Excel. See STF-JKA-3-2 Attachment B for the model error workpaper.


===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-JKA-4/STF-JKA-4-2.docx =====
STF-JKA-4-2

Question:

For the years 2005 through 2024, please provide a table showing the following for the Southern Company System (corresponding to the load forecast used in the SERVM model):
The summer and winter peak load forecast (seasonal) for the summer and winter season 4 years into the future (e.g., for 2015, provide the summer 2019 forecast and the winter 2019/2020 forecast).
The actual peak load for the summer and winter season for each year.
The weather normalized actual peak load for the summer and winter season of each year.

Response:

See STF-JKA-4-2 Attachment, tab “Historical Budget Forecast” for the requested peak load forecasts. The inclusion of Gulf Power and PowerSouth in the forecasts change over time as noted in the Attachment.

See STF-JKA-4-2 Attachment, tab “Actual Loads” for the actual peak load. Note that the actual values include the entities that were present in the year of the actual load. There could be a discrepancy, especially for Southern Power loads during which some customers may have been absent in the forecast, but present four years later when actual loads were recorded. The opposite may be true as well. A customer may have been included in the forecasted load but was absent from the System four years later.

Aggregate weather normalized actual peak loads have not been determined or calculated for the same pool of subsidiary companies included in the SERVM forecast.
