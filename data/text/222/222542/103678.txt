===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-BAI-2 PD/STF-BAI-2-3.docx =====
STF-BAI-2-3
Question:
Please refer to Georgia Power’s response to STF-BAI-1-8 and provide the following information:
Please provide any supporting calculations to support the statement that the Company “does anticipate that incremental revenue requirements associated with serving large load customers will be covered by the revenues collected from those customers.”
Please explain if the Company anticipates that the minimum bill rule proposed in Docket No. 55378 will shift costs to new customers with loads greater than 100 MW that will be greater or less than embedded costs for the applicable periods when the customers’ loads are projected to come online.
Response:
The Company will comply with the Commission’s April 17, 2025 Order on Georgia Power Company’s Revision to Rules and Regulations Tariff Compliance Filing in Docket No. 44280 (“Rules and Regulations Revision Order”), which requires the Company to exercise its discretion under the Rules and Regulations changes in a manner designed to protect existing customers from bearing any of the costs of adding these large load customers. Under the large load contract process approved by the Commission, the Commission Staff will be able to validate that the Company’s minimum bill requirements are compliant with the Commission’s Rules and Regulations Revision Order.
Please see the Company’s response to subpart (a). The minimum bill requirements included in the revised Rules and Regulations do not impact the allocation of embedded costs but do ensure that costs are not shifted to existing customers.

STF-BAI-2-4
Question:
Based on the Company’s Standard load growth sensitivity, please provide the forecasted demand for the following customer types for the period 2025-2030:
Interruptible Customers, please provide the expected demand contributions from these customers included in the peak demand forecast.
Interruptible Customers, please provide the expected demand from these customers during non-coincident peak periods, or when not being interrupted.
Non-interruptible Customers.

Response:
The Company cannot provide the forecasted demand of interruptible and non-interruptible customers because demand is forecasted at a class level rather than at the customer level. Therefore, the breakdown requested above does not exist.
