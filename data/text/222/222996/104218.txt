Re: Georgia Power Company’s Updated 2025 Community Charging Plan;
Docket No. 44280


Dear Ms. Tanner:

In accordance with the Georgia Public Service Commission’s Order Adopting Settlement
Agreement as Modified in Docket No. 44280 (the “Order”), enclosed for filing is Georgia
Power Company’s Updated 2025 Community Charging Plan, which identifies potential
locations for three (3) Community Chargers Georgia Power Company plans to install in the
forthcoming year. These locations replace locations originally filed in the 2025 Community
Charging plan filed on March 7, 2025. The 2025 Community Charging Plan budget for
2025 remains the same as approved in the Order.

If you have any questions, please contact Steven Huling at 404-506-1366.
