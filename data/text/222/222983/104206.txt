COMMENTS OF
AMERICANS FOR AFFORDABLE CLEAN ENERGY
ON THE STIPULATION TO EXTEND THE ALTERNATE RATE PLAN

Comes now, AMERCANS FOR AFFORDABLE CLEAN ENERGY (“AACE”), an Intervenor in the above styled docket and pursuant to the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (“Commission”) “Procedural and Scheduling Order,” (“PSO”) files these Comments on the “Stipulation to Extend the Alternate Rate Plan” (the “ARP Stipulation”) entered by and between Georgia Power Company (the “Company” or “Georgia Power”) and the Commission’s Public Interest Advocacy Staff (“PIA Staff”) on May 19, 2025.  
AACE does not oppose the provisions of the ARP Stipulation by which the Company and PIA Staff agree that current rates will continue until 2028, thereby eliminating the necessity of the Commission’s consideration of the Company’s rate case in 2025.  AACE seeks confirmation that (based on the terms of the ARP Stipulation) the Commission’s necessity review of the Company’s Community Charger program in 2025 as agreed to by the parties and ordered by the Commission in its "Order Adopting Settlement Agreement, as Modified” (the “2022 Rate Case Order”) should continue as planned.  In support thereof, AACE shows the Commission as follows:
AACE intervened in the above styled Docket to address issues related to provision of electricity to electric vehicles (“EV”), including EV charger deployment.  AACE signed the Settlement Agreement entered between the Company and the Commission’s Staff which concluded Georgia Power’s 2022 Rate Case.  The Commission’s 2022 Rate Case Order approved the Settlement Agreement on December 30, 2022.
Therein, the Commission approved the Company’s proposed Make Ready and Community Charging programs, allotting $3 million per year to deployment of Community Chargers.   The Electric Transportation Term Sheet (“ET Term Sheet”), Attachment 2 to the Settlement Agreement, set forth the terms and conditions of the Company’s Make-Ready and Community Charger programs.  As to the Community Charger program, the ET Term Sheet specifically provided:
The need for a Community Charging program will be reviewed in the Company's next base rate case.  (Emphasis added).

This was approved by the Commission, and all parties agreed to review the necessity of the Community Charging program in the “next” rate case – which was understood to be in 2025.  
Review of Community Charger program’s continued necessity is unaffected by the ARP Stipulation.  The ARP Stipulation does not mention the Community Charging program at all.  In contrast, the Make Ready program (an infrastructure deployment program approved in the 2022 Rate Case Order) is expressly extended in the ARP Stipulation.  The ARP Stipulation does not expressly continue the Community Charging program.  The omission of the Community Charging program is consistent with the parties’ expectation that the necessity of the Community Charging program would be reviewed in the 2025 Rate Case before it would be continued.  
Absent express modification thereof, ARP Stipulation, Paragraph 1 applies to the Community Charging program, as follows:
The terms and conditions of the ARP, as defined in the 2022 Rate Case Settlement Agreement as approved by the Commission in its Order Adopting Settlement Agreement as Modified, shall remain in effect unless expressly amended by this Stipulation to Extend the ARP. (Emphasis added).

The ARP Stipulation does not expressly amend the 2022 Rate Case Order pertinent to the Community Charging Program.  Therefore, the requirement that the Commission review the necessity of the Community Charging program continues unchanged from the 2022 Rate Case Stipulation.  
EV charging is still an emerging and evolving market.  Many changes have occurred in EV charging since the 2022 Rate Case Order was approved.  Since 2022, the number of EVs on the road has increased, the cost of EV chargers has declined, and market uncertainties have resulted from changing funding policies of successive administrations.  With these developments over the past three (3) years, review of the Community Charging program is appropriate, as the Commission and the parties recognized in 2022.  Therefore, the Commission should conduct the scheduled review of the Community Charging program to which all parties agreed in 2022 to determine whether its continuation is necessary.
AACE appreciates the opportunity to continue working with the Staff, the Company and interested parties to address these issues regarding the agreed upon review of the Community Charging Program as set forth in the 2022 Rate Case Order.  AACE will cooperate with the parties and Staff on the logistics required to review the program.
