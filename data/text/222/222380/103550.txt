===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-DEA-7 PD/STF-DEA-7-10.docx =====
STF-DEA-7-10
Question:
Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please explain why the Company chose to maintain a rolling large pipeline (whereby projects are added to and subtracted from the pipeline) as opposed to simulating different tranches of projects based on when they enter the pipeline.
Response:
The decision to maintain a rolling large load pipeline, where projects are continuously added and subtracted, is based on established principles in probability and statistics. Specifically, there is no definitive relationship between the percentile of the sum of two random variables and the percentiles of the individual random variables. This means that simulating different tranches or subsets of the large load pipeline based on their entry time does not provide meaningful insight into the overall distribution of the full large load pipeline.
To accurately determine the distribution, including the P50, of the large load pipeline, it is necessary to simulate all projects jointly. This approach ensures that the full scope of the pipeline is considered, providing a comprehensive and accurate representation.
Additionally, creating separate tranches of projects based on their entry time does not eliminate the need to account for projects that are no longer in the pipeline. Given the dynamic and frequently changing nature of the pipeline, it is more practical and efficient to maintain a consolidated and centralized pipeline. This approach allows for updates and accurate reflection of all changes, avoiding the complexity and inefficiency of managing multiple tranches or subsets.

STF-DEA-7-12
Question:
Referring to the Company’s Quarterly Economic Development Reports, please respond to the following questions:
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to accelerate or delay initially proposed load ramps? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the 2023 IRP update, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to accelerate or delay initially proposed load ramps? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization model?
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider, referring to the P1, P2, and P3 probabilities? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider, referring to the P1, P2, and P3 probabilities? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization Model?
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to materialize load as expected? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to materialize as expected? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization Model?

Response:
Since the 2023 Integrated Resource Plan (“IRP”) Update, the Company has not  analyzed the tendency for projects to accelerate or delay initially proposed load ramps. For details on changes to customer proposed load ramps and initial service dates please refer to the “Ramp Change” and “Initial Service Change” tabs of STF-PIA-8-2 Attachments A-D TRADE SECRET, which contain the Company’s quarterly 2024 Large Load Economic Development Reports. 
Since the 2023 IRP Update, the Company has observed certain projects accelerate their load ramp and other projects delay their load ramp. Such trends are incomparable to the assumptions in the Load Realization Model (“LRM”) as the LRM assesses the risk that a project’s initial service date and load ramp schedule delay beyond their proposed load ramp. As projects change their proposed load ramp that input is updated in the model and then subject to the delay assumptions within the LRM rather than comparing back to the original load ramp no longer applicable to the project.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, the Company has not analyzed the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, the Company has not observed any trends in the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, the Company has not analyzed the tendency for projects’ load to materialize as expected. Such an analysis would require a larger sample size of large load projects to have reached a mature stage in their load ramps. Given the early stages of large loads, the Company lacks the adequate sample size of mature projects to make such an assessment.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, the Company has not observed any trends in the tendency for projects’ load to materialize as expected. As previously stated, such an analysis would require a larger sample size of large load projects to have reached a mature stage in their load ramps.

===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-DEA-7 PD/STF-DEA-7-7.docx =====
STF-DEA-7-7
Question:
Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please explain if a project’s likelihood to 1) select Georgia Power, 2) change its load ramp schedule, or 3) materialize its load as expected, would change over time. For example, does the Company expect that a project introduced in the 2023 IRP update should present the same assumptions for the three variables identified above if it is still in the large load pipeline as of Q2 2024?
Response:
The likelihood of a project selecting Georgia Power may change as that project advances through the Company’s internal review process and shows increasing signs of selecting Georgia Power. The customer-provided load ramp input could also change. For example, as time progresses and the Company receives updated customer information, information such as load ramp may change and, as such, the Company would reflect the updated customer information in the Load Realization Model (“LRM”). It is important to note that an update to the customer ramp does not imply a change in the likelihood. Instead, it represents a modification to the input load ramp, which would then be evaluated within the framework of the LRM. 
A project’s load realization percentage remains unchanged over time, as the Company assesses load realization based on the customer’s specific class and segment. As long as the project's class and segment do not change, the project’s load realization ranges will also remain consistent.
