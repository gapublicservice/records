                       APPLICATION FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE OF
                                   WALMART INC.

         COMES NOW, Walmart Inc. ("Walmart"),1 pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-

59 and Georgia Public Service Commission ("Commission") Rule 515-2-1.06, and hereby files

this Application for Leave to Intervene ("Application") in the above-referenced docket. The

Application is timely filed. In support thereof, Walmart respectfully shows as follows:

                                                      1.

         On May 19, 2025, Georgia Power Company ("Georgia Power" or "Company") and the

Public Interest Advocacy Staff ("Staff") filed a Joint Petition for Approval of the Stipulation to

Extend the Alternate Rate Plan ("ARP") ("Joint Petition"). Pursuant to that filing, Walmart files

this Application for Leave to Intervene and participate in this docket. This Application is timely

filed.

                                                      2.

         Walmart is a global retailer of goods and services and also operates throughout the United

States. Walmart's principal energy management office is located at 2608 SE J Street, Bentonville,

AR 72716.

                                                           3.

           Walmart has the privilege of providing its retail services throughout the State of Georgia,

operating 209 retail facilities, including 151 Supercenters, two Discount Stores, 31 Neighborhood

Markets, 24 Sam's Clubs, one Other Pharmacy Formats, 10 distribution centers, and three

fulfillment centers.2 Within the Georgia Power's service territory, Walmart is a large commercial

customer, operating 125 retail stores, three distribution centers, and one fulfillment center.

Collectively, these facilities purchase approximately 500.8 million kWh annually from the

Company.

                                                           4.

           Walmart has an interest in this proceeding because electricity is one of its highest operating

costs, and the Joint Petition has the potential to directly impact Walmart's operations in Georgia.

As such, Walmart has a direct and substantial interest in the outcome of this proceeding.

                                                           5.

           Because of the unique nature of Walmart's operations – where its purchases substantial

amounts of electric and related services from the Company pursuant to multiple accounts at

multiple locations – Walmart is dissimilar from other large electricity users. Its interest in this

proceeding cannot be adequately represented by any other party.                Additionally, Walmart's

participation in this matter will not unduly delay this proceeding or prejudice any other party.

                                                           6.

           Walmart respectfully requests that it be granted leave to intervene and participate fully as

a party in this proceeding, including the right to present testimony and exhibits, cross-examine

witnesses, and file briefs. Walmart intends to participate actively in this docket.
