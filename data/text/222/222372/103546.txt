===== DKT 56002 & 56003 STF-WG-2 PD/STF-WG-2-1.docx =====
STF-WG-2-1
Question:
Refer to Case No. 44280, and to STF-WG-2-26 Attachment TRADE SECRET. This attachment provided 10 years’ information about subtransmission-level equipment outages, including line identification, dates and times, causes, equipment types, and other information, as well as customer interruptions (CI) and customer minutes of interruption (CMI) associated with the each equipment outage (if any). Please provide this Attachment with only transmission lines owned by Georgia Power.
Response: 
Each Integrated Transmission System (“ITS”) participant owns transmission assets based on parity with the investments of the other ITS participants. Georgia Power owns various transmission assets throughout the ITS, with ownership percentages varying throughout the system. Multiple segments of a single transmission line can be owned by different ITS participants, making it impractical to identify these outages on solely Georgia Power owned transmission lines. Therefore, the Company cannot provide the requested attachment due to the complexity of asset ownership within the ITS.
