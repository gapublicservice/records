LEGAL NOTICE

Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) has filed with the Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) a Joint Petition to Extend the Alternate Rate Plan. Copies of the filing are on file with the Commission and are also available for inspection at the region offices of Georgia Power.

This matter has been filed under Docket No. 44280 and has been scheduled for hearing before the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 50-13-13. The hearing for this matter will begin on June 26, 2025, at 9:30 a.m. and will be held in Room 110 of the Commission offices located at 244 Washington Street, SW, Atlanta, Georgia 30334-5701. Please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) to confirm the location of the hearing.

In accordance with O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59(c), persons wishing to intervene must file a Petition to Intervene with the Commission within thirty (30) days of the first publication of this notice.

Pursuant to the Commission’s Alternative eFile instructions, any pre-filed testimony or statements by intervenors must be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary by no later than 4:00 p.m. on June 12, 2025. All testimony must be pre-filed. Parties of record must also serve one another copies of any pre-filed testimony.

If you have a disability and will need assistance or accommodation to participate, or need further information, please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) at least one day prior to the hearing in this matter.

This notice is published at the direction of the Georgia Public Service Commission.


/s/ Jeremiah Haswell
Jeremiah Haswell
Director, Regulatory Affairs 
Georgia Power Company
