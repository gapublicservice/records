COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before April 25, 2025. 


DEFINITIONS

As may be used in this document:  
1.“The Company,” “Georgia Power” or “The Utility” means Georgia Power Company and its present and former officers, employees, agents, representatives, directors, and all other persons acting or purporting to act on behalf of said Company.
The term “you” and “your” refer to “the Company.”
The term “person” means any natural person, corporation, corporate division, partnership, other unincorporated association, trust, government agency, or entity.
The term “document” or “documentation” shall have the broadest possible meaning under applicable law.  “Document” or “documentation” means every writing or record of every type and description that is in the possession, custody or control of the Company including, but not limited to, correspondence, memoranda, e-mails, drafts, workpapers, summaries, stenographic or handwritten notes, studies, notices, publications, books, pamphlets, reports, surveys, minutes or statistical compilations, computer and other electronic records or tapes or printouts, including, but not limited to, electronic mail files; and copies of such writing or records containing any commentary or notation whatsoever that does not appear in the original.  The term “document” or “documentation” further includes, by way of illustration and not limitation, schedules, progress schedules, time logs, drawings, computer disks, chart projections, time tables, summaries of other documents, minutes, surveys, work sheets, drawings, comparisons, evaluations, laboratory and testing reports, telephone call records, personal diaries, calendars, personal notebooks, personal reading files, transcripts, witness statements and indices.
The term “referring or relating to” means consisting of, containing, mentioning, suggesting, reflecting, concerning, regarding, summarizing, analyzing, discussing, involving, dealing with, emanating from, directed at, pertaining to in any way, or in any way logically or factually connected or associated with the matter discussed.
“And” and “or” as used herein shall be construed both conjunctively and disjunctively and each shall include the other whenever such construction will serve to bring within the scope of these discovery requests any information that would not otherwise be brought within their scope.
The singular as used herein shall include the plural and the masculine gender shall include the feminine and the neuter.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a person other than a natural person means to state:  the full name of the person and any names under which it conducts business; the present or last known address of the person; and, the present or last known telephone number of the person.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a document means to provide with respect to each document requested to be identified by these discovery requests a description of the document that is sufficient for purposes of a request to produce or a subpoena duces tecum, including the following:

PUBLIC DISCLOSURE
Load Forecasting

Regarding the Company’s load forecast, please list any changes in the forecasting methodology or assumptions made since the 2023 IRP Update.

Please refer to the Company’s short-term load forecast model inputs in the document titled, 2_B2025_FC_Inputs_TRADE SECRET, tab “B25.” The Company notes that the economic data in this tab represents an xxxxxxxxx vintage. Please respond to the following questions:
Has the Company assessed an updated vintage of these inputs and calculated an updated load forecast? If so, please provide the results of any such analysis as well as an updated vintage of this data.

Please explain if the xxxxxxxxx forecast vintage differs at all from observed actuals in the most recent available vintage. 

Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, tab “Model,” the Company has changed its classification values in the “xxxxxxx” column (column D) when compared to the 2023 IRP Update. Please explain why and provide a summary of all the projects that have changed this classification level, including any relevant information that is otherwise provided in the Load Realization Model or the Quarterly Large Load Development Reports. 

Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, the total announced load in the filed model (xxxxxxx MW) is xxxxxxx than the total announced load in the Q2 2024 Large Load Economic Development Report and associated workpapers (22,762 MW). Please explain this discrepancy and identify all differences in the project lists making up these load estimates.


Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please provide a spreadsheet that shows, on a project level, the following additional information:
Territory (Inside or Outside GPC Service Territory),
Project Stage (Technical Review, Requestion for Electric Service, or Contract for Electric Service) 

Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please identify which projects introduced in the 2023 IRP Update are still in the current large load pipeline.

Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please explain if a project’s likelihood to 1) select Georgia Power, 2) change its load ramp schedule, or 3) materialize its load as expected , would change over time. For example, does the Company expect that a project introduced in the 2023 IRP update should present the same assumptions for the three variables identified above if it is still in the large load pipeline as of Q2 2024?

Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please explain if projects that have begun to materialize (commercially operating and energized) will continue to be included in the Load Realization Model as part of the large load pipeline? 

Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please explain the point at which successfully materialized large load projects are expected to be removed from the large load pipeline, if at all.

Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, please explain why the Company chose to maintain a rolling large pipeline (whereby projects are added to and subtracted from the pipeline) as opposed to simulating different tranches of projects based on when they enter the pipeline.


Referring to the Company’s B2025 TRADE SECRET Load Realization Model, have any of the projects in the pipeline begun commercial operation? If so, please identify these projects and provide their observed annual capacity and energy needs. Please provide your response in spreadsheet format. 

Referring to the Company’s Quarterly Economic Development Reports,  please respond to the following questions:
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company analyzed  the tendency for projects to accelerate or delay initially proposed load ramps? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the 2023 IRP update, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to accelerate or delay initially proposed load ramps? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization model?
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider, referring to the P1, P2, and P3 probabilities? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider, referring to the P1, P2, and P3 probabilities? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization Model?
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to materialize load as expected? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the 2023 IRP Update, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to materialize as expected? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization Model?

Referring to the Company’s response to STF-DEA-1-31, including the attached plots, please provide the data underlying each of the residual plots. Ensure that the units for each model are specified in the data. Please provide your response in spreadsheet format.
