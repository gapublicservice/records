Are there any other types of newly available conductors being planned for new overhead lines and replacement of existing conductors in the near future? 
Provide and describe the methods used to select overhead conductors and the economic or cost benefit analysis used to determine which conductors are selected.

Response:

Please refer to STF-REC-4-2 Attachment.

The Company is engaged in the testing and evaluation of Southwire’s new ACCS C7 conductor and may consider a test installation in the future. All current plans for new overhead lines and replacement of existing conductors in the near future will utilize existing standard ACSR, ACSS, or ACCR conductors.

Relevant general philosophies are laid out in Section A, Page 3 (bullets 3, 4, and 10) of the Technical Appendix Volume 3 in the 2022 Integrated Resource Plan in Docket No. 44160. The process for selecting overhead conductors is similar to the “Project Determination and Documentation” process laid out in Section A (pages 15 - 27) for developing projects. Additional considerations may include, but are not limited to, the following: 

Present-day maximum loading, expected load growth, and any other factors which might add to future line loading over and above the forecasted level. For example, an area where numerous solar facilities have been built might be expected to attract further solar development, thereby biasing the decision to a higher-capacity conductor than would otherwise be considered. 

The condition, height, and strength of the line’s structures and the terrain that the line crosses. 

A few standard sizes are favored to keep down the costs of stocking conductors, connectors, and tools needed to make repairs. 

The concerns of and input from maintenance personnel are also considered. For example, in certain conductor types, if a break occurs under tension, the core can withdraw away from the break and require longer amounts of conductor to be replaced. 

A good balance between cost and capacity is generally sought when selecting conductors for transmission line projects.
 
For an example of a cost-benefit analysis performed to select overhead conductor, please see the sample project documentation in Section G4 of Technical Appendix Volume 3 (“East Watkinsville – Russell Dam 230 kV Reconductor”), specifically Section V, “Discussion of Alternatives,” and the attached supporting information in Attachment D within that documentation.
