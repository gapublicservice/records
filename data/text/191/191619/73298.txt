STF-PIA-11-1

Question:

For the months of December 2019, December 2020, December 2021, May 2022, June 2022 and July 2022, please provide the following data points for residential customers in total and by zip code.   
Number of customers
Dollar amount billed
Total receipts
Number of customers charged a security deposit
Dollar value of security deposits charged for a new account
Total number of accounts past due
Total dollar value of accounts past due
Number of customers charged a late payment fee
Dollar value of late payment fees charged
Number of disconnection notices issued
Number of disconnections for nonpayment
Number of service reconnections after disconnection for nonpayment
Gross number of accounts written off as uncollectible
Net dollar value of accounts written off as uncollectible

Response:

Please see STF-PIA-11-1 Attachment for the requested information, which is also filed with the Commission on a monthly basis through the PSC Monthly Bad Debt Reports in Docket No. 42516 except December 2019. For December 2019, only select data is available as the Company did not begin tracking other items to be used in generating the monthly bad debt reports until May 2020.
Please note, the Company does not track the data requested at the zip code level. In addition, the net dollar value of accounts written off as uncollectible requested in item ‘n’ is tracked at the total Company level and not by customer class.

STF-PIA-11-2

Question:

Please indicate whether Georgia Power Company sells any of its uncollectible accounts, bad debt or written-off amounts to third party debt collectors or to affiliates. If so, please provide:
a.The amount sold to third party debt collectors or affiliates for calendar years 2019, 2020 and 2021, and year-to-date through July 31, 2022. 
b.The revenue received in calendar years 2019, 2020 and 2021, and year-to-date through July 31, 2022.
c.A description of the accounting procedures and journal entries used to record revenue received from the sale of uncollectible account balances, bad debt and written-off amounts to third party debt collectors.

Response:

The Company does not sell any of its uncollectible accounts, bad debt, or written-off amounts to third party debt collectors or to affiliates.
