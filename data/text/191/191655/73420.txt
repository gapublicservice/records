STF-WG-3-5

Question:

Refer to the Company’s response to STF-WG-1-30, Attachments A through F. 

Please define what is meant when “xmfr_pm” is listed as the Device Type for an outage.
Please provide the five most-common types of activities completed when “xmfr_pm” is listed as the Device Type for an outage.

Response:

For the purposes of this response, it is assumed that the question is asking about the device type “xfm_pm” in the referenced attachments.  

“xfm_pm” refers to a padmounted distribution transformer.

The most common activities completed for an event where a padmounted distribution transformer is the device type are:

Address a fault on a secondary or service cable leaving the transformer
Address a physical condition in the transformer compartment such as a failed termination or wildlife intrusion
Address a fault on a primary cable upstream from the transformer
Replace the transformer’s internal fuse
Replace the transformer
