STF-TAI-6-5

Question:

Please describe in detail the process that a Georgia Power customer would be required to follow in order for a third party to access its energy usage (hourly) data.

Response:

Georgia Power customers do not currently have the capability of providing a third party access to energy usage (hourly) data through My Power Usage.
After logging into My Power Usage, customers view their monthly, daily, and hourly usage data in graphical or table format. Customers do have the ability to export or print their energy usage data and share the information with a third party if they choose.
For commercial and industrial customers who have access to Energy Direct services, any customer who would like to grant access to their data to a third party must complete and submit a consent form to the Company.
