STF-TAI-5-1

Question:

The Company’s proposed rates and revenue proof contained in MFR Item F-4 as well as in response to STF-TAI-1-16 result in a base tariff revenue increase of $312.245 million (excludes DSM, ECCR, NCCR, MFF, and Fuel).  At the same time, it is Staff’s understanding that the Company is proposing a base tariff revenue (excluding the same riders noted above) increase of $738.661 million as set forth in the Direct Testimony of Abramovitz, Adams, Houston, and Robinson, page 9 as well as Exhibit APA-SPA-ADH-MBR-1, Schedule 2 Traditional Base, page 3.  

Please provide actual rates and revenue proof associated with the Company’s requested $738.661 million increase to base tariff rates.  In this response, please provide in the same format as MFR Item F-4 and in an executable Excel format.

Response:

Please see the Company’s response to STF-BAI-1-1, specifically STF-BAI-1-1 Attachment Legg A.
