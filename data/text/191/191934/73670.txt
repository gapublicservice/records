BEFORE THE GEORGIA PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

GEORGIA POWER COMPANY
DOCKET NO. 44280
Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Rate Case

Data Request No. STF-PIA-1-1 Supplemental

BASIS FOR THE ASSERTION THAT THE 
INFORMATION SUBMITTED IS A TRADE SECRET


As part of Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Rate Case filed in Docket No. 44280 (“2022 Rate Case”), Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) submits to the Georgia Public Service Commission its response to STF-PIA-1-1 (“Response”). In the Response, the Company has provided specific customer information (the “Information”). All of such information constitutes trade secret information of the Southern Company, Georgia Power, and its affiliates and is therefore protected from public disclosure under Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11.

The Information derives economic value from not being generally known to, and not being readily ascertainable by proper means by other persons who can obtain economic value from its disclosure or use. Specifically, the Information contains specific customer account information that is confidential. The Information provides customer names and addresses due to participation in the Company’s Electric Transportation Make Ready program. Customers trust Georgia Power to keep their data in confidence absent their permission to share it with third parties. If the Information were disclosed without customer permission, it would harm this trust relationship that Georgia Power maintains with its customers and the ability of Georgia Power to implement its rates and programs in the most efficient and economic manner for customers. Moreover, the disclosure of customer information could chill participation in the Company’s programs in the future. Competitors are not required to disclose similar customer information and to require Georgia Power to do so would put it at an economic disadvantageous.

The Information is subject to substantial procedures to maintain its secrecy. Only select Georgia Power and Southern Company Services personnel are granted access to the Information. Those personnel receive access only on a “need to know” basis. Parties outside the Company who have been granted access to the Information, if any, have been required to sign confidentiality agreements with respect to the Information.

STF-PIA-1-2 Supplemental

Question:

For each ET Make Ready site provide:
the total capital investment incurred by the Company as of March 31, 2022,  
total O&M incurred by calendar year and calendar quarter through March 31, 2022
total revenue from Make Ready facilities by calendar year and calendar quarter through March 31, 2022

Response:

Please see STF-PIA-1-2 Attachment Supplemental. At the request of PIA Staff, the Company is providing Distribution Work Orders for each project.

The Company has not incurred any Make Ready O&M expenses to date. 

Through the ET Make Ready program, the individual chargers are owned by the customer at each ET Make Ready site. Therefore, any revenue generated from the usage fee charged to the user of the electric vehicle chargers is retained by Georgia Power’s customer.

Since a new meter is not set with a Make Ready installation, the revenue collected by the Company from its retail electric customers for the kWh usage of the chargers is included in their total kWh sales, which includes all energy uses including the energy consumed by any charging equipment installed at an ET Make Ready site.
