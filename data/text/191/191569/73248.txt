STF-PCMG-2-23 Supplemental

Question:

Refer to the response to STF-LA-2-6 (Public Version) page 11. Please provide a monthly detailed breakdown and need of use, of all aircraft, drones and helicopters used in the provision of electric utility service for the years 2019, 2020, 2021, to date 2022 and projected rate years 2023-2025.  


Response:

Please see STF-PCMG-2-23 Attachment for the Company’s breakdown and need of use for aircraft and helicopters for the provision of electric utility service for 2019 through 2022 to-date. Drones are used across the Company from augmenting inspections in Power Delivery, Generation & Nuclear, to Economic Development / Construction (Mapping), Security, and Storm Damage/ Restoration. Drones are billed to Georgia Power based on use or allocation method. The Company does not have a monthly detailed breakdown and need of use for drone usage. The Company does not budget for use of aircraft, drones, or helicopters flights on a detailed individual basis for the projected years.
