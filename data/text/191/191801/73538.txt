STF-TAI-7-1

Question:

Please provide all data, studies and analyses that have been performed by, or on behalf of, the Company regarding cost shifting, revenue erosion, or that indicate rooftop solar customers on the RNR Monthly Netting tariff are not paying their full cost of service.  Please describe in detail how each analysis was performed and provide all studies and analyses in Excel format with formulas intact.

Response:

The Company will provide its data, studies, and analyses on the cost shifting and revenue erosion for customers on the RNR Monthly Netting pilot on October 20, 2022 along with the Company’s testimony as the Commission’s Second Amended PSO requires. In addition, the Company has agreed to the expedited discovery procedures in the Second Amended PSO to allow Commission Staff the opportunity to review and ask questions related to the data.
