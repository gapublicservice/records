RE: Georgia Power Company’s 2019 Rate Case - 2021 Annual Surveillance             Report, Docket 42516. 

Dear Ms. Tanner:
On July 21, 2022, the Georgia Public Service Commission (“GPSC” or “Commission”) approved Georgia Power Company’s (“Georgia Power” or “Company”) 2021 Annual Surveillance Report (“2021 ASR”).  As part of the 2021 ASR Order, the Company was required to document compliance within 90 days of the Order.  There are two items in the 2021 ASR Order which are subject to 90-day reporting.  These items include the timing for the application of customer sharing and reporting on vegetation management.  

As discussed in the 2021 ASR Order, for the twelve months ending on December 31, 2021, Georgia Power reported earnings of 12.09%.  This resulted in $14.167 million in revenues above the Commission authorized earnings band, making 80% of those revenues available for sharing with customers.  In accordance with the Commission’s Orders in this docket approving the 2019 Rate Case Stipulation (“Stipulation”), 40% or approximately $5.667 million has been applied to regulatory assets for the benefit of customers.  The remaining 40% or approximately $5.667 million will be refunded to customers through bill credits beginning with bills issued in December 2022.  

In addition, the Commission’s Order adopted a recommendation that the Company explain “in detail its plans to address and eliminate the remaining 3,500 miles of Distribution Vegetation Management backlog.” The Company can report that, as of May 2022, the backlog has been eliminated and does not currently have any backlog as of the date of this filing.

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me.
