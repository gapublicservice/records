20   Q.         WHAT APPROACH DID YOU USE IN DETERMINING YOUR ADJUSTMENTS
21              TO THE COMPANY’S O&M EXPENSES?

22   A.         In developing my adjustments to certain of the Company’s O&M Expenses, I determined
23              whether the expenses were reasonable, prudent, known and measurable and realistic, and
24              whether the Company should recover these expenses from ratepayers, and whether they
25              were a necessary part of the Company’s operations. I reviewed the Company’s historical
26              expense data, as well as the Company’s forecasted data to determine my recommended
27              O&M expense levels related to the Company’s category of expenses for the test year and
28              for each of the rate year periods. The more reliance on forecasted data the more subjectivity
29              was used to determine the estimates proposed in the Company’s rate year periods. My

 1        review and recommendation of the Company’s O&M Expense levels reflects cost that are
 2        recurring over time. My approach incorporates whether expenses should be normalized or
 3        averaged out based upon prior historical data and/or whether expenses should be recovered
 4        consistently over the rate year periods. The ultimate goal in setting rates for service is to
 5        provide the ratepayers with safe and reliable service at reasonable rates with the Company
 6        being provided the opportunity to earn a reasonable rate of return on its funded plant
 7        investments, and to recovery all known and measurable expenses.

19        million per year to $65 million per year which reflects the average annual storm damage
