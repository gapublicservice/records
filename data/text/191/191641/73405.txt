Dear Ms. Tanner:
Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) has identified an error in the projected test period revenues for the Fixed Price Alternative (“FPA”) rate, which were used in the Company’s Cost-of-Service Study (“COSS”) to calculate Return on Investment and parity ratios. Total revenues for Retail Rates were correct and did not change. Accordingly, a counterbalancing parity adjustment was made to the Power and Light – Medium rate. The revised data flowed through the COSS model and resulted in a revision to the FPA and PLM rates and corresponding Medium Business and Marginal rate groups’ Return on Investment ratios. The parity ratio for the FPA rate will increase from 11% to 39%. Outside of these isolated changes, the impact is negligible and does not affect the results of the COSS. The models have been recalculated and updated. The following tables outline the flow through impacts of this correction:
