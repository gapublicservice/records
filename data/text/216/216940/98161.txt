             APPLICATION OF GEORGIA SOLAR ENERGY ASSOCIATION
                          FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE

         Georgia Solar Energy Association (“GA Solar”) hereby applies to the Georgia Public

Service Commission (“Commission”) for leave to intervene in the above-referenced docket

pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 46-2-59 and 50-13-14 and Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06. In support of

this application, GA Solar respectfully show the Commission as follows:

                                                1.

           On October 27 2023, Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or “the Company”)

filed its 2022 Integrated Resource Plan (“IRP”) Update, for which it seeks Commission approval.

                                                2.

         On November 21, 2023, the Commission approved a Procedural and Scheduling Order in

the above-referenced dockets, according to which this application for leave to intervene is timely

filed.

                                                3.

         Georgia Solar Energy Association is a not-for-profit organization headquartered in

Atlanta, Georgia. GA Solar represents the interests of its members in promoting environmental

justice, renewable energy, and related sustainable practices. GA Solar helps benefit Georgia's

energy consumers by advocating for solar power in Georgia by helping to inform local and state

leaders about the kinds of policies that make the benefits of solar accessible to all. This advocacy

supports investment and local economic growth that keeps Georgia’s economy strong.


       Additionally, GA Solar helps solar professionals stay abreast of the latest news, trends

and standards, as well as providing them with networking and business outreach opportunities.

By connecting home and business owners, GA Solar helps find skilled solar professionals and

provides guides to help consumers evaluate solar options. In addition, GA Solar helps consumers

decide how to access solar energy in a way that saves them money on their power bills and

reduces their environmental footprint. Finally, GA Solar helps consumers find skilled solar

professionals they can trust and provide guides to evaluate solar options.


                                                 4.

       GA Solar has an interest in the 2023 Georgia Power IRP Update proceedings both on its

own behalf and on behalf of its members. Many of GA Solar’s members purchase electricity

from Georgia Power Company, both for their businesses and for their individual residences.

Their electricity consumption and costs for electricity will be affected by the Commission’s

actions in these dockets.

                                                 5.

       GA Solar’s mission is directly implicated by Georgia Power’s supply- and demand-side

choices as reflected in its 2023 IRP Update plans. GA Solar intends to address the concerns of

both itself and its members regarding how the Company’s choices impact the resulting bills that

customers pay. As a result, GA Solar has substantial and vital interests in the outcome of these

proceedings and seeks to intervene to protect these interests.

                                                 6.

        In furtherance of its interests and on behalf of its members, GA Solar has intervened in

many prior proceedings before the Commission. Among the Commission proceedings in which

GA Solar has intervened or otherwise participated are Georgia Power’s 2022 IRP and DSM

proceedings, Docket Nos. 42310, 42311; Georgia Power’s 2022 Base Rate Case, Docket No.

44280, and Georgia Power’s 2019 Base Rate Case, Docket No. 42516.

                                                  9.

        The interests of GA Solar and its members are not adequately represented by other parties

to these proceedings. Other parties that have applied to intervene do not have interests similar to

those of GA Solar. The applicants’ intervention would not unduly delay the proceedings or

prejudice the rights of other parties.

                                                  11.

        If this application is granted, GA Solar intends to participate as a party in these dockets,

reserving the ability to cross-examining witnesses, filing and presenting direct testimony, and

filing proposed orders and/or briefs. GA Solar has not yet finalized plans for direct testimony but

expect to explore a number of issues in testimony including but not limited to the Company’s

renewable energy programs available to customers, and demand side management portfolio.

                                                  12.
