PETITION FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE
OF HEELSTONE RENEWABLE ENERGY, LLC

Comes now, HEELSTONE RENEWABLE ENERGY, LLC (“HEELSTONE”) and pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14, 46-2-59, and Commission Utility Rule 515-2-1-.06, files its Petition for Leave to Intervene in the above-styled docket.  In support thereof, HEELSTONE shows the following:
1.
HEELSTONE is a limited liability company organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, maintaining its principal place of business at 1414 Raleigh Road, Suite 210, Chapel Hill, North Carolina 27517.  HEELSTONE develops solar electricity generation facilities, and it sells the electricity generated therefrom to investor owned utilities and others.  HEELSTONE is a leading utility-scale solar developer, having developed a diverse portfolio of solar projects in various locations and in different sizes.  HEELSTONE has developed and achieved commercial operation on numerous solar generation projects.

2.
In 2018, HEELSTONE entered the Georgia market seeking to develop utility scale solar generation facilities as a Qualify Facility (“QF”) pursuant to the Public Utilities Regulatory Policies Act, 16 U.S.C. § 2601, et. seq. (“PURPA”).  HEELSTONE, through wholly-owned special purpose entities, is actively developing and constructing utility scale solar generation facilities in Georgia.  
3.
HEELSTONE has a vital interest in this docket inasmuch as it continues the desire to develop and deploy utility scale solar generation facilities as a QF, and issues raised in this docket may significantly affect its business goals and objectives in Georgia.  
4.
Because of HEELSTONE’s development of utility scale solar generation facilities as a QF, no other party to this docket can adequately represent its interests. HEELSTONE seeks active participation in these proceedings to protect its interests and to address the issues being raised in this docket.
5.
