RE: Georgia Power Company’s 2023 Integrated Resource Plan Update; 
Docket No. 55378

Dear Ms. Tanner:

Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) files this errata to its December 4, 2023 Supplemental Filing to the 2023 IRP Update in Docket No. 55378. The Company identified an error within the Resource Mix Study Supplemental Update whereby Georgia Power’s capacity and energy allocation based on the system generic expansion plan was understated. The revision to the file “TS Capacity Expansion Plans Supplemental” is attached hereto. Notably, this change only impacts Georgia Power allocations and has no impact on the Company’s production cost modeling runs and results or the economic analyses supporting the resources requested within the Company’s 2023 IRP Update that have already been completed and filed.

This filing contains certain information that is being filed under the Commission’s trade secret rules as explained in the enclosed basis for assertion. Therefore, in addition to the electronic trade secret file, an electronic version of the redacted file is enclosed for public disclosure.
