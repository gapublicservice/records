COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before December 6, 2023.

1.“The Company,” “Georgia Power” or “The Utility” means Georgia Power Company and its present and former officers, employees, agents, representatives, directors, and all other persons acting or purporting to act on behalf of said Company.
The term “you” and “your” refer to “the Company.”
The term “person” means any natural person, corporation, corporate division, partnership, other unincorporated association, trust, government agency, or entity.
The term “document” or “documentation” shall have the broadest possible meaning under applicable law.  “Document” or “documentation” means every writing or record of every type and description that is in the possession, custody or control of the Company including, but not limited to, correspondence, memoranda, e-mails, drafts, workpapers, summaries, stenographic or handwritten notes, studies, notices, publications, books, pamphlets, reports, surveys, minutes or statistical compilations, computer and other electronic records or tapes or printouts, including, but not limited to, electronic mail files; and copies of such writing or records containing any commentary or notation whatsoever that does not appear in the original. Regarding Amended Certification for Residential Thermostat DR DSM Program


Please provide the historical performance of the Thermostat DR program for the period of 2020-2023 including the total number of events, the dates of the events, the total summer maximum peak reduction, the total winter maximum peak reduction, the average summer peak reduction, the average winter peak reduction, and the maximum and average level of Georgia Power customer participation for Summer and Winter.

Please provide the monthly historical customer participation for the Thermostat DR program for the period of 2020-2023, with a breakdown of BYOT participants and New – Rebated thermostats that entered the program each year. 

Please describe how the Company plans to maintain participation in the Thermostat Demand Response progarm at 50,000 customers given that customers are able to withdraw from the program at any time and the Company is able to remove customers for excessive opting out of curtailment events.

Please provide any modeling that the Company has conducted, or had conducted on its behalf, to identify the total potential demand savings from this program. The modeling could include, but not be limited to, the following areas of research:
An analysis of the total size of the market for homes in the Company’s service area that are eligible to participate in the program.  Please provide any modeling conducted to support the response.  
The annual technical, economic, and achievable kW savings that could be realized by this program.
The percentage of total potential demand savings and total potential participation that is captured by the proposed 50,000 participants.
Does the Company believe that there is potential for even greater demand savings beyond the proposed 50,000 participants? If so, why not propose a larger increase in customer participation?
What is the annual current sales estimate for thermostats in the Georgia Power service territory categorized by whether or not the thermostat is potentially eligible for this program?  
What is the estimated current number of residential customers with eligible thermostats, whether or not those costumers participate in the program?

The Company states that the Thermostat DR program shows a positive TRC value, which is reflected in the updated filing.  
Please provide an electronic copy of the benefit/cost model used to calculate the TRC test for the expanded program, including all inputs for the TRC calculation and all outputs.
Please describe the changes to the program or otherwise that resulted in the change of TRC results.  
Does the TRC test results include any kWh savings or only kW savings?

Please provide the most recent samples of information provided to program participants regarding how the curtailment events work and the notifications participants receive when events are scheduled to occur.

For 2024, the proposed Program Plans show an increase for Program Evaluation Spending from the approved 2022 IRP DSM Program Plans from $72,168 to $93,819, respectively.  Please provide a justification for the proposed increase to evaluate this program.
