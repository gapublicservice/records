LEGAL NOTICE

Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) has filed with the Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) its 2023 Integrated Resource Plan Update. This matter has been assigned Docket No. 55378 and has been scheduled for hearing before the Commission pursuant to the Integrated Resource Plan Act, O.C.G.A § 46-3A-1 et seq. The hearing for Docket No. 55378 will begin on January 16, 2024 at 9:30 a.m. and will be held in Room 110 of the Commission offices located at 244 Washington Street, SW, Atlanta, Georgia 30334-5701. Please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) to confirm the location of the hearing.

In accordance with O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59(c), persons wishing to intervene must file a Petition to Intervene with the Commission within thirty (30) days of the first publication of this notice.  

Pursuant to the Commission’s Alternative eFile instructions, any pre-filed testimony or statements by interveners must be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary by no later than 4:00 p.m. on February 15, 2024. All testimony must be pre-filed. Parties of record must also serve one another copies of any pre-filed testimony. The hearing on intervener testimony is scheduled to begin February 29, 2024 at 9:30 a.m.

If you have a disability and will need assistance or accommodation to participate, or need further information, please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) at least one day prior to the hearing in this matter.

This notice is published at the direction of the Georgia Public Service Commission.


/s/ Kelley M. Balkcom
