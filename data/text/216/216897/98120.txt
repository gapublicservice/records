COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before January 29, 2024. 

DEFINITIONS

As may be used in this document:  
1.“The Company,” “Georgia Power” or “The Utility” means Georgia Power Company and its present and former officers, employees, agents, representatives, directors, and all other persons acting or purporting to act on behalf of said Company.
The term “you” and “your” refer to “the Company.”
The term “person” means any natural person, corporation, corporate division, partnership, other unincorporated association, trust, government agency, or entity.
The term “document” or “documentation” shall have the broadest possible meaning under applicable law.  “Document” or “documentation” means every writing or record of every type and description that is in the possession, custody or control of the Company including, but not limited to, correspondence, memoranda, e-mails, drafts, workpapers, summaries, stenographic or handwritten notes, studies, notices, publications, books, pamphlets, reports, surveys, minutes or statistical compilations, computer and other electronic records or tapes or printouts, including, but not limited to, electronic mail files; and copies of such writing or records containing any commentary or notation whatsoever that does not appear in the original.  The term “document” or “documentation” further includes, by way of illustration and not limitation, schedules, progress schedules, time logs, drawings, computer disks, chart projections, time tables, summaries of other documents, minutes, surveys, work sheets, drawings, comparisons, evaluations, laboratory and testing reports, telephone call records, personal diaries, calendars, personal notebooks, personal reading files, transcripts, witness statements and indices.
The term “referring or relating to” means consisting of, containing, mentioning, suggesting, reflecting, concerning, regarding, summarizing, analyzing, discussing, involving, dealing with, emanating from, directed at, pertaining to in any way, or in any way logically or factually connected or associated with the matter discussed.
“And” and “or” as used herein shall be construed both conjunctively and disjunctively and each shall include the other whenever such construction will serve to bring within the scope of these discovery requests any information that would not otherwise be brought within their scope.
The singular as used herein shall include the plural and the masculine gender shall include the feminine and the neuter.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a person other than a natural person means to state:  the full name of the person and any names under which it conducts business; the present or last known address of the person; and, the present or last known telephone number of the person.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a document means to provide with respect to each document requested to be identified by these discovery requests a description of the document that is sufficient for purposes of a request to produce or a subpoena duces tecum, including the following:
the type of document (e.g., letter, memorandum, etc.);
the date of the document; the title or label of the document;
the Bates number or other identifier used to number the document for use in  litigation; the identity of the originator;
the identity of each person to whom it was sent;
the identity of each person to whom a copy or copies were sent;
a summary of the contents of the document;
the name and last known address of each person who presently has possession, custody or control of the document;
if any such document was, but is no longer, in your possession,  custody or control or is no longer in existence, state whether it: (1) is missing or lost; (2) has been destroyed; or (3) has been transferred voluntarily or involuntarily, and if so, state the circumstances surrounding the authorization for each such disposition and the date of such disposition.
“Identify”, “identifying” or identity” when used in reference to a communication should be read to include information regarding the date of the communication, whether the communication was written or oral, the identity of all parties and witnesses to the communication, the substance of what was said and/or transpired and, if written, the identity of the document(s) containing or referring to the communication.

Please refer to the Company’s response to STF-PIA-4-9, stating that “The Company will continue to look for opportunities to also leverage the domestic content or energy community bonus credits to bring further IRA benefits to customers where sensible.”
Do the battery storage sites at Robins and Moody qualify for the energy community tax credit 10% adder?
Do the battery storage sites at Robins and Moody qualify for the energy domestic content 10% adder?
Is the new prosed solar plus BESS facility eligible for the energy community tax credit 10% adder?
Is the new prosed solar plus BESS facility eligible for the energy domestic content 10% adder?
Has the Company evaluated the feasibility and economics of energy storage sites located in energy communities in Georgia? If so, please provide the results of any analysis, including workpapers if applicable.

Please refer to the December 4 Supplemental Filing (2023 Request For Information For Capacity Resources – Summary Of Results), stating that “There is not enough time for an RFP to be conducted, resources to be constructed following certification, and transmission projects to be identified and completed to allow delivery by the end of calendar years 2025, 2026 or 2027.”
Table 0-3-2 states that more than 2,700 MW of resources responding to the RFI are expected to be available by the end of 2027. Please provide a detailed explanation of the timeline issues that prevent these resources from being procured and placed in service prior to the end of 2027.
Why did the Company seek information on new capacity resources in the RFI if it had determined that there is insufficient time to procure a new resource?

Please refer to the December 4 Supplemental Filing (2023 Request For Information For Capacity Resources – Summary Of Results), section 2.3, stating that “the RFI participants were primarily interested in a PPA option for ESS with a Renewable Resource, with grid charging capability.” If the developer interest in PPA is stronger than BOTs, why is the Company pursuing company ownership only? Will this limit the competitiveness of procurements?

Please refer to the December 4 Supplemental Filing (2023 Request For Information For Capacity Resources – Summary Of Results).
Provide full copies of the RFI responses received by the Company.
Provide any documents or memos summarizing the RFI responses. 
Provide all pricing information received in the RFI responses.
Provide any analysis conducted by or for the Company comparing pricing of the RFI resources to cost of the Company-owned projects proposed in the 2023 IRP Update.
Provide internal Georgia Power Company, memos, or other related documents addressing RFI responses. 
Provide internal Southern Company, memos, or other related documents addressing RFI responses. 

Please refer to the Company’s Direct Testimony, p. 35, lines 22-26, stating that the Robins and Moody BESS projects “can be charged by the Company’s largest owned solar resources located adjacent to each site but may have opportunities to be charged by the grid depending on the availability of firm transmission capacity.”
Please explain why firm transmission capacity would be needed to charge the battery from the grid.
Are the Robins and Moody solar projects fully deliverable or is there curtailment? If the projects experience curtailment, please provide data detail curtailed hours and energy volumes.
Will the battery and solar projects be able to simultaneously discharge to the grid at full capacity, if needed or economic? Or will the discharge be limited to the interconnection size of the solar facility.
If the battery is unable to charge from the grid, please explain what advantages there are of siting the facility at the Robins and Moody locations, since the battery’s operations will be more limited than a site where grid charging is feasible.


Please refer to the Company’s Direct Testimony, p. 36, lines 15-19, regarding the proposed BESS and Solar facility.
What will be the total injection capability of the interconnection? Will both the BESS and Solar facilities be able to simultaneously discharge to the grid at full capacity, if needed or economic?
What is the total expected accredited capacity value of the combined facility?

Please refer to Technical Appendix 4 (“Robins and Moody BESS”), stating: “For the proposed Robins and Moody BESS resources, the base assumption is that these projects will meet the PWA requirements and qualify for the 30% ITC benefit.” What is the foundation of this assumption? Provide any documentation regarding the tax credit qualification of the projects.

Please refer to Technical Appendix 5 (“PD Technical Information Supporting the Proposal to Develop 200 MW BESS Co-located with 200 MW Solar”), stating that “The Company is seeking to acquire and construct an approximately 200 MW alternating current (“AC”) solar facility and an AC-coupled 200 MW/800 MWh BESS on a portion of the above-referenced property, which is under lease by Seller or its affiliates.”
Please explain further what the involvement of the Seller will be in the development, operation, and long-term ownership of the project.
Is the Company simply acquiring land in the transaction, or are there power generation and/or transmission assets being purchased?

Please refer to Technical Appendix 5 (“PD Technical Information Supporting the Proposal to Develop 200 MW BESS Co-located with 200 MW Solar”).
Provide all indicative pricing information received from the Seller supporting the cost estimate cited in the “Estimated Cost” section of this technical appendix.
Please provide a comparison of the cost of this project with the potential projects submitted in response to the RFI summarized in the Company’s supplemental filing.
What level of tax credits (PTC and/or ITC) is the project expected to qualify for?  Is tax credit qualification risk borne by the Seller, the Company, or Georgia customers?
