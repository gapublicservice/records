STF-PIA-1-1

Question:

Refer to presentation “Capacity Needs – September 12, 2023” slide 10.
For the values in column (3) and column (4), “Budget 2024 P50” and “Budget 2024 P95”, respectively, provide the calculation of those values. Provide the load to be served (MW) and the capacity available to serve the load used to calculate the values. For new load (MW), please differentiate between load expected in Georgia Power’s service territory and load outside Georgia Power service territory. Please provide this information (including the load split in and outside Georgia Power’s Service territory) in the same format as the file TS July Estimated Winter Needs Table.xlsx, and provide this electronically with all formula intact.


Response:

Please refer to STF-PIA-1-1 Attachments A and B to see how the referenced tables were developed, as well as the load to be served and the capacity available to serve the load used to calculate the values. Georgia Power has not developed a forecast that excludes projected economic development load (new load) outside its exclusive service territory. Rather, the forecast provided in the 2023 IRP Update filing appropriately reflects projected economic development load the Company anticipates serving. Refer to the “Georgia Power Territorial Base Case Load vs. Existing Capability Table” provided in the Resource Mix Study within the Technical Appendix to the 2023 IRP Update filing for the latest load and capacity information.
