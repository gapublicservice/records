STF-PIA-19-10

Question:

Provide the total annual capital expenditures (Georgia Power’s share) for Gaston 1, Gaston 2, Gaston 3, Gaston 4 and common for 2018 – 2022.
 
Response:

The Company does not have any capital investment related to Plant Gaston recorded to Plant In-Service or Accumulated Depreciation. Plant Gaston Units 1-4 and A (“Gaston Units”) have been in service to Georgia Power retail customers through a 1959 Power Contract between Georgia Power and the Southern Electric Generating Company (“SEGCO”). SEGCO is jointly owned by Georgia Power and Alabama Power, with fifty percent of the output of the Gaston Units committed to Georgia Power. The Company’s 50% ownership share of the investment costs, along with other costs of operating and maintaining the generating units, are billed to the Company monthly through its purchased power agreement with SEGCO. These monthly billings, recorded as purchased power expenses, are the only costs associated with Gaston Units that are reflected in the retail cost of service.
