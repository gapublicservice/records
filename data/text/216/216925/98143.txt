Re:
Georgia Power Company’s Response to Georgia Interfaith Power and Light’s Motion Challenging the Trade Secret Designation of Basic Identifying Information about Georgia Power Company’s Potential Purchase of a Publicly Undisclosed Asset; Docket No. 55378
 
Dear Ms. Tanner:
On December 1, 2023, Georgia Interfaith Power and Light (“GIPL”) filed a motion in the above-referenced docket challenging the trade secret designation of information regarding a potential asset acquisition by Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or “the Company”) that was identified in the Company’s 2023 Integrated Resource Plan Update. GIPL’s motion should be denied as moot. 
As reported in the Direct Testimony of Jeffrey R. Grubb, Francisco Valle, Lee Evans, and Michael A. Bush, filed in this docket on December 4, 2023, the transaction is no longer under consideration by the Company. Therefore, there is no issue before this Commission related to that potential transaction.
Accordingly, the Company respectfully requests that the Commission dismiss GIPL’s motion as moot.

Sincerely,
Steven J. Hewitson
