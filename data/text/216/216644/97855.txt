Data Request No. STF- PIA-3-5
 
BASIS FOR THE ASSERTION THAT THE  
INFORMATION SUBMITTED IS A TRADE SECRET 
 
As part of Georgia Power Company’s 2023 Integrated Resource Plan Update filed in Docket No. 55378 (“2023 IRP Update”), Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) submits to the Georgia Public Service Commission its response to STF- PIA-3-5 (“Response”). In the Response, the Company has provided hurdle rates and projections regarding the expected value of capacity for the Company’s Thermostat Demand Response program. All of such information (the “Information”) constitutes trade secret information of Southern Company, Georgia Power, and its affiliates and is therefore protected from public disclosure under Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11. 
 
The Information derives economic value from not being generally known to, and not being readily ascertainable by proper means by other persons who can obtain economic value from its disclosure or use. Specifically, the Information contains hurdle rates based on the Company’s weighted average cost of capital and projections regarding the expected value of capacity for the Company’s Thermostat Demand Response program. Publicly disclosing these costs would allow bidders in future solicitations to tailor their proposals and potentially set an artificial floor on bidding, which would prevent the Company from conducting a proper solicitation and obtaining the best cost estimates for customers. In addition, the Company’s competitors are not required to file similar information and to require the Company to do so would put it at an economic disadvantage. 

The Information is subject to substantial procedures to maintain its secrecy. Only select Georgia Power and Southern Company Services personnel are granted access to the Information. Those personnel receive access only on a “need to know” basis. Parties outside the Company who have been granted access to the Information, if any, have been required to sign confidentiality agreements with respect to the Information.   
 


STF-PIA-3-5

Question:
The Company states that the Thermostat DR program shows a positive TRC value, which is reflected in the updated filing.  
Please provide an electronic copy of the benefit/cost model used to calculate the TRC test for the expanded program, including all inputs for the TRC calculation and all outputs.
Please describe the changes to the program or otherwise that resulted in the change of TRC results.  
Does the TRC test results include any kWh savings or only kW savings?

Response:
Please refer to TS STF-PIA-3-5 Attachment.
The following changes resulted in the new TRC results:
Avoided costs were updated to reflect a more recent capacity price forecast.
Participation was updated to assume 50,000 participants.
Participation turnover was updated to assume a 15% annual turnover rate.
Delivery costs, administration costs, marketing costs, and evaluation costs were updated to reflect an updated contract with the implementor to provide 50,000 participants.
The TRC test results do not include any kWh savings, only kW demand savings.

STF-PIA-3-7

Question:
For 2024, the proposed Program Plans show an increase for Program Evaluation Spending from the approved 2022 IRP DSM Program Plans from $72,168 to $93,819, respectively.  Please provide a justification for the proposed increase to evaluate this program. 

Response:
Georgia Power will make a good faith effort to recruit new participants as soon as the Company receives approval of its request to expand the Residential Thermostat Demand Response Program so the participant’s experience in the program and resulting savings can be collected to inform future program needs. The additional evaluation budget will be used to assess new participants and appropriately assign participants/customers into treatment and control groups, review program data, survey new participants, analyze survey responses, collect and analyze metering data, and calculate gross savings.
