COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before January 17, 2024.

Attachment A provided in response to Question 1-1 DEA provides a list of customer choice customers with kW associated.  During the technical session on December 6, 2023 Georgia Power Staff noted that a “threshold” had been established for commercial and industrial customers that were considered to not be “organic” growth in the standard load forecasting approaches.  The thresholds noted were “GP set a threshold of 45 MW for Industrial and 150 MW for commercial customers who would be in the customer choice group and be additive to the structured models so these are not organic”.  
Are these the correct thresholds based on our notes from the session?
Please comment on each of the customer addition opportunities listed in this response that are lower than the threshold amount and indicate why they are noted as customer choice?  
If the thresholds were set after 2022, please indicate what statistics are captured in the “historical analysis” noted during the technical session as the approach used to establish the distributions and success factors utilized in the 2023 LF update.
Attachment A to the response to Question 1-1 DEA  is entitled Customer Choice projects and Attachment B is titled Economic Development which contains all the projects not locating in GP service territory – what is the difference between the two groupings and how is the data used in the development of the Customer Choice Model?

On page 54 of the GPC testimony it states “The Company only extends competitive offers to eligible customers that pass financial and economic screening requirements, including the RIM test.“  Please identify the financial and economic screening requirements other than the RIM test that are used in assessing eligible customers?  Please provide the associated analyses with all equations intact.

Please refer to the most recent Load Realization Model submitted by the Company, on the “Model” Tab. For each project, provide the following:
Project Name
Project Location
Project Location in relation to GPC territory (outside or inside)

Please refer to the most recent Load Realization Model submitted by the Company, on the "Correlation" and "Matrix Choices" tabs. 
Explain why the correlation matrix only includes 0 and 1 values, and no correlations between those two values.
Explain why the company assumes a Triangular distribution of Random Variation. Defend this choice with historical data. If data is not available, explain how this decision was made.
Explain why the Triangular Random Variation correlation assumption was "Independent across segments, perfect within." Explain why the other options were not appropriate. 
Given a "perfect within segments" assumption for the Triangular Random Variation , explain how to interpret this assumption. Does this suggest that if Project A in Segment X were to materialize at a certain % of load, then the likelihood that Project B within Segment X would materialize is perfectly correlated with that of Project A?
Explain why the Bernoulli Random Variation correlation assumption was "Independent". Explain why the other options were not appropriate.
