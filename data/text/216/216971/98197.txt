  PETITION OF GEORGIA WAND EDUCATION FUND INC (GEORGIA WAND) TO
                            INTERVENE

              Pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14, 46-2-59 and Rule 515-2-1-.06 of the Georgia

Public Service Commission (“Commission”), Georgia WAND Education Fund (Georgia WAND)

hereby gives notice of its intent to intervene in this proceeding and respectfully petitions the

Commission for leave to so intervene. In support of this petition, Georgia WAND shows as

follows:


                                                    1.

              Georgia WAND (Womens Action for New Directions) is a 35+ year

non- profit   organization   based    out    of     Atlanta, GA. We advocate for nuclear harm

reduction, energy/environmental       and     climate       social justice. Working statewide we bridge

the urban/rural divide for our frontline                Central      Savannah     River      Area (CSRA)

communities    along   the Savannah         River        including    Burke     County     where    Georgia

Power’s nuclear commercial power Plant Vogtle is located                  and     the     energy   burdened

Georgia    Power   customers in      City    of   Atlanta's Neighborhood Planning Unit V (NPU-

V).


                                                    1

                                                  2.

               Georgia WAND embodies the live/work/play model. Meaning over 50% of our

staff and board live, work and play in the frontline communities we serve. Many of the 14

CSRA communities are rural yet some even in Burke County and all Atlanta NPU-V

residents are retail residential customers serviced by Georgia Power. Over 50% of the

Georgia WAND staff, board as well as the Georgia WAND Atlanta office are also all

served by Georgia Power.

                                             3.

                Several Georgia WAND’s staff, Board and frontline communities will be

substantially affected by any action the Commission takes in this docket. Ratepayers who

purchase electricity from Georgia Power, either directly (if the service is in their name) or

indirectly (if the cost of electricity service is embedded in their rent) are adversely impacted and

find themselves unable at times to make due; spending a disproportionate amount of their

income on utilities. Additionally the CSRA communities who live near Plant Vogtle have

experienced various public health and environmental inequities including but not limited to an

increase in upper respiratory illnesses, cancer, brown water, poor air quality and bad soil

conditions.


                                             4.

               The interests of Georgia WAND and its frontline communities may be

substantially affected by decisions made by the Commission in this proceeding. The

interests of Georgia WAND's staff, Board and frontline companies who live near Plant Vogtle

and/or are served by Georgia Power are not adequately represented by other parties to this

proceeding, and Georgia WAND’s intervention will not unduly delay the proceedings or

prejudice the rights of other parties
                                                   2

                                                 5.

               Georgia WAND respectfully requests that it be granted leave to intervene and

participate fully as a party in these proceedings, including the right to present testimony

and exhibits, cross­examine witnesses, and file briefs where applicable.
