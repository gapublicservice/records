Subject: Opposition to Georgia Power Company's Five Community Charging Replacement Locations 
 Dear Ms. Balkcom, 
 I am writing on behalf of the Georgia Public Service Commission's Public Interest Advocacy Staff to formally oppose Georgia Power Company's proposal to establish five new community charging replacement locations. After careful review of the Electric Transportation (ET) Program Term Sheet, it is evident that replacement locations are not permitted under the terms and conditions outlined in the document. 
 The ET Program Term Sheet specifically states the following provisions:  
Georgia Power Company (GPC) will identify the location of up to (but not more than) eleven (11) Community Chargers each year. 
Should the Private Providers fail to break ground at the community charging location within 1.5 years (18 months), they would waive their Right of First Refusal (ROFR) and the right to install chargers at that particular location would revert to GPC. 
The Company’s actual costs incurred to own, operate, and maintain approved community charging equipment may be recovered in its rates for retail electric service.  

The stipulations outlined in the ET Program Term Sheet do not accommodate the establishment of replacement locations. The term "replacement" implies that new locations are being introduced in lieu of existing or planned ones, which is not sanctioned by the guidelines.  
Allowing the proposal for replacement locations would not only contradict the explicit terms of the ET Program but also potentially disrupt the planned allocation and efficient utilization of resources. It is essential to adhere to the defined process to maintain transparency, fairness, and regulatory compliance. 
In light of these considerations, we urge Georgia Power Company to adhere strictly to the existing provisions of the ET Program Term Sheet and reconsider the proposal for replacement locations. We believe this approach will best serve the interests of all stakeholders involved, including the public, by ensuring consistent and equitable implementation of the community charging initiative. 
Thank you for your attention to this matter. We look forward to your cooperation in upholding the integrity of the ET Program. 

Sincerely, 

     Rob Trokey
