BEFORE THE GEORGIA PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

GEORGIA POWER COMPANY
DOCKET NO. 55378

Data Request No. STF-PIA-14-1

BASIS FOR THE ASSERTION THAT THE 
INFORMATION SUBMITTED IS A TRADE SECRET

As part of Georgia Power Company’s 2023 Integrated Resource Plan Update filed in Docket No. 55378 (“2023 IRP Update”), Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) submits to the Georgia Public Service Commission its response to STF-PIA-14-1 (“Response”). In the Response, the Company has provided customer specific load information, contract terms, and service agreements (“Information”). All of such Information constitutes trade secret information of Southern Company, Georgia Power, and its affiliates and is therefore protected from public disclosure under Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11.

 The Information derives economic value from not being generally known to, and not being readily ascertainable by proper means by other persons who can obtain economic value from its disclosure or use. Specifically, the Information contains customer-specific load information and contract terms that resulted from customer choice projects. Publicly disclosing the terms and conditions included within these rate offers would provide the Company’s competitors an unfair advantage with future competitive customer choice. Other utilities could tailor their proposals and potentially set an artificial floor on competitive prices, which would harm customers by not allowing the Company to compete equitably and develop offers that provide sufficient RIM and put downward pressure on rates for all customers. In addition, the Company’s competitors are not required to disclose this type of information and to require the Company to do so would place it at an economic disadvantage. 

The Information is subject to substantial procedures to maintain its secrecy. Only select Georgia Power and Southern Company Services personnel are granted access to the Information. Those personnel receive access only on a “need to know” basis. Parties outside the Company who have been granted access to the Information, if any, have been required to sign confidentiality agreements with respect to the Information.

STF-PIA-14-2

Question:
Define or describe “Total Pipeline” customers?


Response:
The “Total Pipeline” includes all Economic Development and Customer Choice projects that are being tracked by the Company. The Total Pipeline includes projects above and below the large load threshold (45 megawatts for the industrial class; 110 megawatts for the commercial class). Only projects above the large load thresholds are included in the external adjustment to the Load Forecast. 


===== DKT 55378 STF-PIA-14 PD/STF-PIA-14-3.docx =====

STF-PIA-14-3

Question:
Define or describe “Large Load Economic Development Pipeline” customers?


Response:
The “Large Load Economic Development Pipeline” is a subset of the Total Pipeline, excluding non-large load projects that are considered organic growth. Therefore, the Large Load Economic Development Pipeline captures projects greater than or equal to 45 megawatts for the industrial class and 110 megawatts for the commercial class. The Large Load Economic Development Pipeline is the pool of potential load that qualifies for an external adjustment to the load forecast. 


===== DKT 55378 STF-PIA-14 PD/STF-PIA-14-4.docx =====

STF-PIA-14-4

Question:
What is the difference between “Total Pipeline” and “Large Load Economic Development Pipeline” customers?


Response:
The Large Load Economic Development Pipeline is a subset of the Total Pipeline tracked by the Company and includes only those projects that meet the megawatt thresholds described in response to STF-PIA-14-3.
