                              APPLICATION OF
                  SOUTHERN RENEWABLE ENERGY ASSOCIATION
                         FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE

       COMES NOW, the Southern Renewable Energy Association (“SREA”), and hereby files

this Application for Leave to Intervene (“Application”) in the above-referenced docket pursuant

to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59, Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) Rule 515-2-1-.06,

and the Commission’s Procedural and Scheduling Order (“PSO”) issued on May 8, 2024. In

support hereof, SREA respectfully states as follows:

                                                1.

       On October 27, 2023, Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) filed for certification

of three new self-build combustion turbine (“CT”) units at Plant Yates. On April 26, 2024, the

Commission issued an Order Adopting Stipulated Agreement, approving the three new CT units

at Yates as part of Georgia Power’s 2023 Integrated Resource Plan (“IRP”) Update. SREA is

already an intervenor in this Docket, but files this second intervention pursuant to the PSO, which

states that “[a]ny party that previously intervened in this Docket for the 2023 IRP Update wishing

to participate in the Plant Yates Certification process are required to file a new intervention

request…within 30 days following the first published notice of the proceeding.” Georgia Power


                                                1

published the notice of proceeding on May 15, 2024. Therefore, SREA’s Application is timely

filed within 30 days.

                                                  2.

       SREA is a non-profit regional trade association that works to promote the responsible

development and use of utility-scale wind energy, energy storage, solar energy and transmission

throughout the South. Georgia is one of eleven states in which SREA works. SREA members

have an interest in the proceeding because they develop and deploy facilities to generate and

produce energy. Additionally, SREA is a party to the Stipulated Agreement. As a result, SREA

has substantial interests in the outcome of this proceeding and desires to intervene in order to

advance and protect those interests.

                                                  3.

       As required in the Application Requirements adopted by the Commission in the PSO,

SREA has identified other intervening parties whose interests are similar to SREA, including the

Advanced Power Alliance. SREA’s specific interests in the development of utility-scale solar

energy, wind energy, energy storage, hybrid resources, and transmission are not adequately

represented by the other pending intervenors in this Docket. Further, SREA’s participation in this

matter will not unduly delay these proceedings or hinder the rights of other parties. SREA has not

determined the extent of its participation in this certification proceeding, including whether it will

file testimony.

                                                  4.

       SREA requests that the following persons be included on the service list in this Docket

and that all communications regarding this Docket be directed to them:
