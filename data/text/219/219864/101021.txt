COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before October 7, 2024. 

DEFINITIONS

As may be used in this document:  
1.“The Company,” “Georgia Power” or “The Utility” means Georgia Power Company and its present and former officers, employees, agents, representatives, directors, and all other persons acting or purporting to act on behalf of said Company.
The term “you” and “your” refer to “the Company.”
The term “person” means any natural person, corporation, corporate division, partnership, other unincorporated association, trust, government agency, or entity.
The term “document” or “documentation” shall have the broadest possible meaning under applicable law.  “Document” or “documentation” means every writing or record of every type and description that is in the possession, custody or control of the Company including, but not limited to, correspondence, memoranda, e-mails, drafts, workpapers, summaries, stenographic or handwritten notes, studies, notices, publications, books, pamphlets, reports, surveys, minutes or statistical compilations, computer and other electronic records or tapes or printouts, including, but not limited to, electronic mail files; and copies of such writing or records containing any commentary or notation whatsoever that does not appear in the original.  The term “document” or “documentation” further includes, by way of illustration and not limitation, schedules, progress schedules, time logs, drawings, computer disks, chart projections, time tables, summaries of other documents, minutes, surveys, work sheets, drawings, comparisons, evaluations, laboratory and testing reports, telephone call records, personal diaries, calendars, personal notebooks, personal reading files, transcripts, witness statements and indices.

Re: Hydro Modernization Investment Tax Credits  

On what date(s) were the Investment Tax Credits (ITC) recognized? 

What were the ITC amounts recognized? 

Were the amounts calculated by the U.S. Treasury department or by Georgia Power? 

What was the impact on ASR rate base and Operating Income statement when ITCs were recognized?  
How were the ITCs recorded in rate base?  
Would the ITCs be recorded as a regulatory liability?  
How were the ITCs recorded or reflected in Operating Income statement? 

Identify the line item(s) in ASR rate base and Operating Income statement where the ITC were recorded. 

If the ITCs are not sold what would have been the regulatory accounting treatment?  
Would the ITCs be amortized over time?  
What would be the time period?  
How would the Company’s revenue requirement have been impacted?  
Identify the specific changes to rate base line items and impact on revenue requirement calculation over time. 

Company statement: 
“… a public utility may elect to either reduce rate base by the Unamortized ITC or reduce cost of service by amortization of the credit over the life of the asset.  The Company’s long-standing election for regulatory treatment is to reduce cost of service by amortization of the credit.”
 
Please explain the difference between “reduce rate base by the Unamortized ITC” and “reduce cost of service by amortization of the credit over the life of the asset”. Specifically, identify the impact on ASR rate base and Operating Income statement under both options. 

Company statement: 
“In order to provide customers the benefit of exclusion from normalization rules, the Company is selling ITCs at a marginal discount to a non-affiliate, third-party.” 


Please provide the calculation of customer benefit from selling ITCs 

Please provide the calculation of customer benefit from not selling ITCs. 

Company statement: 
“The remaining tax credits of over $2 million will also be utilized for the benefit of the customers but will be subject to Normalization.” 

How will the $2 million tax credits be reflected in ASR rate base and Operating Income statement over time? 

Company statement: 
“The economics of selling tax credits even at a discount (for example market is in the low ninety cents on the dollar for ITCs) will trigger a more beneficial ratemaking structure for customers, via lower rate base.” 

What happens to the cash received from selling ITCs? 

How is the receipt of cash reflected in ASR rate base and Operating Income statement? 

Please explain how rate base is “lowered” from selling ITCs. What are the specific adjustments to rate base line items? 

Does claiming the hydro modernization ITCs have any impact on the Company's federal income tax basis in the hydro assets?  If so, explain fully and show the impact. 

Did the Company actively market the hydro modernization ITCs to monetize the highest benefit for ratepayer?  If so, explain the Company's marketing efforts.
   
Is the Company pursuing sale of the remaining hydro modernization ITCs?  If not, explain fully why not. 

If the ITCs were not sold, how would the benefit be conveyed to ratepayers?  

Provide a copy of all calculations, in Microsoft Excel with formulas intact, associated with the net present value benefit of these savings to customers.  


Provide a description of the regulatory accounting treatment of the ITC monetization. 
 
Please explain how selling tax credits at a discount will trigger a more beneficial ratemaking structure for customers via lower rate base. Provide associated calculations of the net present value benefits under both scenarios (selling the ITCs at a discount and not selling the ITCs).
