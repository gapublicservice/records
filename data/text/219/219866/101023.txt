===== DKT 44280 STF-WG-12/STF-WG-12-1.docx =====
STF-WG-12-1

Question: 

Please refer to Docket 44280 STF-LA-9-1 Attachment TRADE SECRET. Worksheet = Transmission. Row 712 Total Retirement. What was the accumulated depreciation balance of the assets retired each month for July 2022 – June 2024. Please provide information in an unprotected Excel spreadsheet.

Response:

Please see STF-WG-12-1 Attachment for the projected accumulated depreciation balance of all transmission assets by depreciation group. Please note, the Company generally calculates projected depreciation for transmission assets using group depreciation by each FERC account and does not track the accumulated depreciation balance for a specific asset or for retired assets as a whole. Therefore, the lowest level of detail available for projected accumulated depreciation balances is by depreciation group, which may include both in-service and retired assets.
