STF-LA-2-3

Question:
Track Record on Microgrids. For each microgrid that the Company has in service or expects to have in service by December 31, 2023, please provide the following information:
Name of the microgrid.
Location of the microgrid.
Cost of the microgrid, in total and by account.
Annual operating costs of the microgrid in total and by account.
A description of whether the microgrid is dedicated to a particular customer or location.
Depreciation period and depreciation rates being used for the microgrid.
A listing of the dates and times to date when the microgrid was used to supply power to the microgrid area during what would have otherwise been power outages to the microgrid area.

Response:
Tech Square Microgrid.
755 Williams St NW Atlanta GA 30308.
See STF-LA-2-1 Attachment for the project cost as of November 30, 2023.
$200,000.
The Tech Square Microgrid can feed the Georgia Tech High Performance Computing Center at Databank or tie into the distribution network.
See STF-LA-2-1 Attachment for the depreciation rates as approved in the 2022 Base Rate Case.
The fuel cell at the Tech Square Microgrid continuously provides power to the customer, but the microgrid has not needed to island the customer load because of system events. There are also two utilities at this site, so the microgrid would swap between utilities if there was an outage.
