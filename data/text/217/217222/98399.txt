BASIS FOR THE ASSERTION THAT THE 
INFORMATION SUBMITTED IS A TRADE SECRET

As part of Georgia Power Company’s 2023 Integrated Resource Plan Update filed in Docket No. 55378 (“2023 IRP Update”), Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) submits to the Georgia Public Service Commission its response to STF-DEA-8-10 (“Response”). In the Response, the Company has provided which contains confidential details regarding current and future transmission projects. All of such information (the “Information”) constitutes trade secret information of Southern Company, Georgia Power, and its affiliates and is therefore protected from public disclosure under Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11.

 The Information derives economic value from not being generally known to, and not being readily ascertainable by proper means by other persons who can obtain economic value from its disclosure or use. Specifically, the Information contains competitively sensitive information related to the Georgia Power transmission system infrastructure. Public dissemination of the Information would allow Georgia Power’s competitors and suppliers to have access and insight into the Company’s transmission planning process. Access to the Information would also allow competitors to gain specific insight into the Company’s technical analysis regarding planned projects. Competitors would obtain an unfair advantage because they are not required to reveal similar information and can structure the pricing for competing products based on the Information. In the event the Information was released, it is quite likely that suppliers would use the Information to set the floor in establishing their own prices, thus artificially and inefficiently setting a market price that may not be representative of the best cost that the market could offer. Competitors would also unfairly benefit in having access and insight into the Company’s planning processes and methodologies. This competitive advantage for the Company’s suppliers and competitors would mean that Georgia Power will potentially pay higher prices to suppliers, ultimately harming Georgia Power. 

The Information is subject to substantial procedures to maintain its secrecy. Only select Georgia Power and Southern Company Services personnel are granted access to the Information. Those personnel receive access only on a “need to know” basis. Parties outside the Company who have been granted access to the Information, if any, have been required to sign confidentiality agreements with respect to the Information.

STF-DEA-8-9

Question:
Please refer to the Company’s response to STF-JKA-2-21 a . 
Where in the Company’s procedures is the Native Load Request (Designation) study process defined? Please provide the material which defines the process.
When does the Company expect to conclude the formal study for the Santa Rosa PPA?
Provide any results and reports associated with Native Load Requests over the past five (5) years. Were screens performed for any of these requests? If so, what was the outcome of these screens?
For all of the resources being pursued in the 2023 IRP Update, please indicate which would need to have a Native Load Request (Design) study performed.

Response:
The Company’s procedures associated with Native Load Requests are available on the Southern Company OASIS website under the “Business Practices, Waivers, and Exemptions” folder within the “Business Practices” subfolder.
The formal study for the Santa Rosa PPA should be completed by March 2024.
A list of Designated Network Resources is available on the Southern Company OASIS website under the “Transmission Service Request (TSR)” folder within the “Designated Network Resources (DNR)” subfolder.
All resources being pursued in the 2023 IRP Update would need to have a designation study performed with the exception of those facilities being extended or that do not have an incremental change in output. For example, the resources supplying the Mississippi Power PPA did not require a designation study because those units are already on the system providing power to customers and are simply proposed to be extended in service under the terms of the PPA.
