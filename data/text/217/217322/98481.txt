===== DKT 55378 STF-DEA-8 Supp/STF-DEA-8-7 Supplemental.docx =====
STF-DEA-8-7 Supplemental

Question:
Please refer to the Company’s Transmission Screening Analysis.
Did the Company consider any cases in the screening analysis which assumed battery charging? If so, please identify the cases and provide the results. If not, please explain why not.
Does the Company consider any cases containing battery charging as part of its standard transmission planning? If so, please identify the process that this occurs under.

Response:
Please refer to the Company’s response to STF-DEA-4-15.
Battery charging studies were not performed as part of the transmission screens filed in the initial IRP Update filing and transmission supplemental filing because for the three sites studied, the BESS is charged by solar only behind the same point of interconnection. 

The Company conducts battery charging studies during the initial designation studies as well as once in-service embedded in the Company’s annual Transmission Planning processes. 

===== DKT 55378 STF-DEA-8 Supp/STF-DEA-8-9 Supplemental.docx =====
STF-DEA-8-9 Supplemental

Question:
Please refer to the Company’s response to STF-JKA-2-21 a . 
Where in the Company’s procedures is the Native Load Request (Designation) study process defined? Please provide the material which defines the process.
When does the Company expect to conclude the formal study for the Santa Rosa PPA?
Provide any results and reports associated with Native Load Requests over the past five (5) years. Were screens performed for any of these requests? If so, what was the outcome of these screens?
For all of the resources being pursued in the 2023 IRP Update, please indicate which would need to have a Native Load Request (Design) study performed.

Response:
The Company’s procedures associated with Native Load Requests are available on the Southern Company OASIS website under the “Business Practices, Waivers, and Exemptions” folder within the “Business Practices” subfolder.
The formal study for the Santa Rosa PPA should be completed by March 2024.
A list of Designated Network Resources is available on the Southern Company OASIS website under the “Transmission Service Request (TSR)” folder within the “Designated Network Resources (DNR)” subfolder.
The listing of completed studies, Completed Studies, for the last five years is located on the Southern Company Oasis website under the “Studies” folder within the “Transmission Studies” subfolder.  If any resources shown on the Completed Studies document were designated, those resources would be placed on the previously provided Designated Network Resource list.  The following screen captures can assist with correlating designation studies in the completed studies list to resources listed in the designated network resource list:

From the completed studies list, the facilities listed in the designation study shown in the last row can be found on the designated network resource under “Independent Power Producers.”


For the facilities shown in the previous screen capture, the designated facilities are listed by facility name (see highlighted items) under the “Independent Power Producers” section of the Designated Network Resource list.


All resources being pursued in the 2023 IRP Update would need to have a designation study performed with the exception of those facilities being extended or that do not have an incremental change in output. For example, the resources supplying the Mississippi Power PPA did not require a designation study because those units are already on the system providing power to customers and are simply proposed to be extended in service under the terms of the PPA.
