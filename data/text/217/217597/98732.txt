20   A.         Georgia Power states that since the 2022 IRP was approved by the Commission in its

21             Order issued on July 29, 2022, Georgia has experienced “extraordinary growth [that]

22             requires Georgia Power develop new supply- and demand-side capacity resources to meet

23             substantial increases in customer demand and energy needs of our state.”4 The Company

24             believes it will require quick action on the part of the Commission to enable Georgia

25             Power to serve these new large customer loads when they are ready for operation.

 9   A.         As mentioned above, in the 2022 IRP, Staff contended the Company’s 26% winter TRM

10              was overstated and should have only been 24.5%. Staff determined that the overstatement

11              was attributed to the Company’s unreasonable derivation of a load forecast error (“LFE”)

12              distribution table that was used in the Strategic Energy and Risk Valuation Model

13              (“SERVM”) reliability analysis that the Company performed. The LFE distribution table

14              was derived from 18 years of system energy forecasts that were compared to actual

15              weather normalized energy requirement results in the fourth year of the forecast. Because

16              the actual load forecast errors over the past 18 years showed the Company had

17              consistently over-forecast load, the Company elected not to use the actual data to develop

18              its SERVM LFE distribution table. Instead, the Company made an ad-hoc adjustment, in

19              which it subtracted the median percentage forecast error from the actual forecast error

20              each year. Staff found this to be inconsistent with any statistical modeling practice.

16         the Reserve Margin Study, for developing a lower load forecast sensitivity case.

17         Consequently, Daymark produced a P80 LRM scenario with the Uniform success

 1   A.    Staff used the Company’s Resource Mix Study database that contains a model of the

13         provide.
