1) Please describe the studies being performed.
           As discussed during the Transmission Technical Conference, the transmission studies
           currently being performed will study all requested generation resources as one generation
           portfolio along with incorporating the projected large loads that are included in the 2023 GPC
           IRP Update Load Forecast.

2) Please explain why the studies could not have been completed prior to filing the IRP Update.
           As discussed during the Transmission Technical Conference, the additional transmission
           studies could not be completed by the IRP filing date due to when the generation portfolio
           was finalized, which was late October. The transmission analyses provided in the IRP Update
           filing fully support the economics associated with integrating the proposed generation
           resources into the transmission system.
