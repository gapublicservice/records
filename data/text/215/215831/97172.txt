Question:
Refer to Attachment PSC Grid Reporting Template June 2023 TS.xlsx, tab “Grid Investment Program 23-25”.  PIA Staff observes that distribution spending has been cut by REDACTED 40% in compliance with the Commission’s Order in 44280, and that transmission spending has been cut by REDACTED 40% in compliance with the Commission’s Order.  
Please explain how the Company identified distribution projects to cut from those originally anticipated in the Company’s initial application amount.  If the Company identified the distribution projects to be deferred as less critical to Company objectives (such as reliability), explain how the Company determined that projects selected for deferral fit such a description.  
Refer to the Company’s response to subpart (a).  Provide a list of distribution projects selected for deferral, and provide a brief description of each project, including 1) a summary description of the work to have been completed (such as “replace towers from (location A) to (location B)); and 2) the original justification for the work (such as “60% of these towers failed their most recent structural inspection”)). 
Please explain how the Company identified substation projects to defer from those originally anticipated in the Company’s initial application amount.  If the Company identified the projects cut to be deferred as less critical to Company objectives (such as reliability), explain how the Company determined that substation projects selected for deferral fit such a description.  
Refer to the Company’s response to subpart (c).  Provide a list of substation projects selected for deferral, and provide a brief description of each project, including 1) a summary description of the work to have been completed (such as “replace circuit breakers over 50 years old”) and 2) the original justification for the work (such as “circuit breakers over 50 years old have an average annual failure rate of 2%, which is double the average annual circuit breaker failure rate”). 
Please explain how the Company identified transmission line projects to defer from those originally anticipated in the Company’s initial application amount.  If the Company identified the projects cut to be deferred as less critical to Company objectives (such as reliability), explain how the Company determined that substation projects selected for deferral fit such a description.  
Refer to the Company’s response to subpart (e).  Provide a list of transmission line projects selected for deferral, and provide a brief description of each project, including 1) a summary description of the work to have been completed (such as “replace towers from (location A) to (location B)); and 2) the original justification for the work (such as “60% of these towers failed their most recent structural inspection”).

Response:

All projects included in the Grid Investment Plan are critical to improving reliability; however, to comply with the Commission’s Order in the 2022 Base Rate Case to reduce the overall plan spending by 40%, the Company considered several factors to prioritize the distribution projects in this Rate Case period in order to maximize work efficiency and reliability benefits to customers. These considerations included rebalancing overhead and underground resources and prioritizing poorer performing feeders for investments in the rate case period, regardless of geographic region. Accordingly, the Company deferred 115 distribution projects from the 2023 – 2025 Grid Investment Plan. Consistent with the Grid Investment Plan objectives, the Company intends to extend the plan beyond 2031 and complete these deferred projects in the future.

Please see STF-WG-9-3 Attachment A.

All projects included in the Grid Investment Plan are critical to improving reliability; however, to comply with the Commission’s Order in the 2022 Base Rate Case to reduce the overall plan spending by 40%, the Company re-prioritized the plan with consideration for operational feasibility associated with the location and timing of project construction, the criticality of a substation to a particular area of the transmission system, and the impact of project construction on other existing or planned maintenance work. Additionally, consideration was given for projects that contained related capital investment drivers, such as enhanced system reliability, the prevention of equipment failures and high replacement costs, and compliance with NERC reliability standards. Accordingly, the Company removed 104 transmission line and substation projects from the 2023 – 2025 Grid Investment Plan. Consistent with the Grid Investment Plan objectives, the Company intends to extend the plan beyond 2031 and complete these deferred projects in the future.

Please see STF-WG-9-3 Attachment B.

Please see the response to subpart (c) above.

Please see STF-WG-9-3 Attachment C.
