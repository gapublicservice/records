Docket No. 44280
Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Rate Case
Hearing Request No. STF-HR-2


STF-HR-2-1

Question:

Please provide supporting documents and analysis to support witness Legg’s comments:

“Out of nearly 36k bills for customers on monthly RNR netting this year, through October, about 7,400 net billed zero kWh. So in over 20% of these bills, customers pushed back enough to be billed for zero kWh. This is for customers on the R rate, which makes up 84% of participants in the RNR monthly netting program.”

Response:

Please see STF-HR-2-1 Attachment, which includes records of all accounts that billed on the Residential rate and were on monthly netting in 2022, from January through the October billing months. There were 35,962 bills that generated under these criteria and 7,437 billed zero net kWh for the billing period (20.7%).
