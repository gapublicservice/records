                            TOP 10 REASONS TO REJECT                                           Exhibit ____
                       GEORGIA POWER’S COST-SHIFT ANALYSIS

 1. NO COST OF SERVICE ANALYSIS FOR RNR CUSTOMERS
         Georgia Power’s analysis is based only on revenue erosion, not the cost to serve RNR customers.
 2. UNREALISTIC ASSUMPTIONS THAT OVERSTATE SOLAR PRODUCTION.
         GPC assumed that every PV system had a 16.3% capacity factor and a 120% AC-to-DC ratio.
         These assumptions are only accurate for ideal, unshaded, south-facing roofs with insolation attainable in
          only some parts of the state.
 3. OVER-SIMPLIFIED DATA FOR NON-SOLAR CUSTOMERS: ONLY 4 CUSTOMERS ANALYZED.
         GPC decided to compress 1,110 customers into 4 customers, then expands its conclusions from those 4
          to present results for 5,000 customers
         Averaging 1,110 customers into 4 removes all the variation inherent in individual customer’s usages.
 4. UNJUSTIFIED SELECTION OF 4 “REPRESENTATIVE” NON-SOLAR CUSTOMERS
         GPC provides no explanations of whether these 4 customers are representative of broader customers,
          or anything about their usage profile (electric vs gas heat, EVs, work from home, kids, etc.)
         GPC confirmed that “there is not a formal model or worksheet that would be considered to be
          ‘analysis,’” and it only performed a perfunctory screen to ensure that the four customers were “in line
          with the annual usage targets from the RNR group.” No analysis of load shape, monthly usage, or
          peak/off peak usage was considered.
 5. UNUSUAL USAGE PATTERNS IN HOURLY METER DATA FOR THE 4 CUSTOMERS.
         Hourly data reveals usage values that unexpectedly spike to extremely high levels for one hour before
          returning to more typical usage. The 2nd quartile representative customer has repeated spikes from 1-
          2 PM throughout the year when load increase from 2-3 kW to over 10 kW, before falling back rapidly.
         It is highly unlikely that this load pattern is reflective of a broad set of customers.
 6. INCORRECTLY SCALED RESULTS FROM THOSE 4 CUSTOMERS TO ALL 5,000 CUSTOMERS.
         The Company expands the results from the 4 “representative” customers back to the 1,110 customer
          RNR Monthly Netting set as well as to a hypothetical set of 5,000 customers.
         The Company provided no evidence that the 5,000 customers were sufficiently similar to the 4
          representative customers to justify this expansion.
         The total “cost shift” also assumes all 5,000 customers would be residential, which is not accurate.
 7. INCORRECTLY USES PVWATTS “TYPICAL YEAR” INSTEAD OF ACTUAL SINGLE-YEAR DATA.
         Mixing apples and oranges: using “typical meteorological year” instead of actual weather-specific data,
          even though it is using actual customer data for that year, not data for a “typical” year.
 8. FAILS TO CORRECT PVWATTS SOLAR GENERATION DATA TO DAYLIGHT SAVINGS TIME.
         PVWatts hourly data is in Standard Time. GPC’s customer is in Daylight Prevailing Time.
         GPC doesn’t correct for this mismatch, as evidenced in the blank value for 2-3 AM on March 14, 2021,
          when clocks “sprang forward.”
         Between March and November, solar generation is calculated one hour too early, so it does not reflect
          the higher amounts of solar available during late summer afternoon hours when loads are high.
9. CONFLATES AVOIDED ENERGY SAVINGS AND FUEL SAVINGS IN PRESENTATION OF RESULTS
         GPC reports that customers only produce $21 in annual “avoided energy savings / benefits”. This value
          is calculated from the non-fuel variable O&M costs, but does not include the $254 in annual fuel savings
          that the Company itself identified.
10. INCORRECTLY DOUBLES THE COST-SHIFT IMPACT DUE TO A SPREADSHEET ERROR
         GPC has presented cost-shift results that are twice what their own numbers support.
