Question:

Using an anonymized customer ID that can be traced across files and years, 

Please provide hourly consumption and hourly export data for 2018, 2019, 2020, and 2021 for all RNR customers.  If some customers do not have hourly consumption or export data over this time period, provide the data that the Company does have. 

Please provide a separate file that indicates for each customer the date of final system interconnection, the AC and DC system capacity sizes, the tariff on which the customer takes service, and the zip code of the customer’s service location.  

Response:

Please refer to STF-TAI-8-9 Attachments A through C. Due to the voluminous nature, the attachments are being provided in electronic format only. Please note: Usage and Pushback data was not stored for 2018. 

Please refer to STF-TAI-8-9 Attachment D Supplemental. Please note that not all accounts in Attachments A-C are contained within Attachment D, as Attachment D only includes accounts that were active and billing under the RNR tariff, and does not include accounts that have been closed or are otherwise ineligible.
