STF-TAI-9-3

Question:

Please refer to the Company’s supplemental response to STF-TAI-7-1, Attachment B. The Group 1 sample profile (Sample Before tab) lists a demand of 9.147 kW on November 5 during Hour 19. This demand amount is nearly 17 times the demand during the prior hour, 2.4 times the amount listed for the following hour, and 1.7 times the next highest hourly demand amount listed for the month of November. Please explain in detail the reasons for this anomaly in the Group 1 sample load profile.

Response:

The 9.147 kW demand value on November 5 in Hour 19 is not an anomaly and represents the actual usage that was measured by this customer in this interval. While there may have been low usage occurring in the hours leading up to this specific hour, this level of demand is not uncommon for average residential customers.
