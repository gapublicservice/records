DOCKET NO. 44280

IN RE:     Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Rate Case

THIRD AMENDED PROCEDURAL AND SCHEDULING ORDER

The Chairman, pursuant to Commission Rule 515-1-1-.02, hereby amends the Procedural and Scheduling Order previously issued in this matter as set forth below.

The starting time for hearings on November 10, 2022 shall be 7:30 a.m.  More particularly, the description of the November 8-10, 2022 hearings on page 2 of the Second Amended Procedural and Scheduling Order shall be modified to read as follows:


November 8-10, 2022

Beginning at 9:30 a.m. on November 8th, the Commission will hear applications to intervene and any objections thereto, and any appropriate motions concerning PIA Staff’s and Intervenors’ pre-filed testimony. Following these motions, the Commission will conduct hearings on PIA Staff’s and Intervenors’ direct testimony. Following hearings on PIA Staff’s and Intervenors’ direct testimony, the Commission will conduct hearings on Georgia Power’s supplemental direct testimony.  Hearings will continue at 9:30 a.m. on November 9th and at 7:30 a.m. on November 10th.  On November 10th, public witnesses may be heard immediately following the Commission’s regularly scheduled 9:30 a.m. Committee Meetings.


*****
WHEREFORE IT IS ORDERED, that the Chairman hereby approves the Third Amended Procedural and Scheduling Order.  Except as otherwise expressly provided herein all other provisions set forth in the original Procedural and Scheduling Order shall remain in full force and effect.

ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing or oral argument or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of this Order, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.

ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over these matters is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order or Orders as this Commission may deem just and proper.


The above action by Order of the Chairman of the Georgia Public Service Commission on the __ day of November 2022.
