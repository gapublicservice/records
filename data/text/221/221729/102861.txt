                    APPLICATION FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE OF
 THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AND ALL OTHER FEDERAL
                       EXECUTIVE AGENCIES


       The United States Department of Defense and all other Federal Executive Agencies

(DOD/FEA), hereby petitions the State of Georgia Public Service Commission (Commission) for

leave to intervene in the above-captioned proceeding. DOD/FEA has a direct and significant

interest in this proceeding. This application to Intervene is supported by O.C.G.A. §§50-13-14

and 46-2-59, Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06, and the Commission's January 28, 2025, Procedural

and Scheduling Order. In support of this Motion, DOD/FEA states as follows:

       DOD/FEA will be represented by, with all documents served upon and all

communications referred to, its duly authorized representative pursuant to 40 U.S.C. § 501(c),

                                                  II

         DOD/FEA maintains numerous military installations and civilian agency activities within

the State of Georgia, many of which obtain electric service from Georgia Power Company

 ("GPC"). Earlier this year, GPC filed a petition with the Commission for approval of its

proposed Integrated Resource Plan. DOD/FEA has an interest in this proceeding, as certain GPC

generation assets are located on or adjacent to DOD installations. DOD/FEA has a further

interest in this proceeding as one of the largest customers on the GPC system. DOD/FEA has a

further interest in this proceeding because the military has unique resiliency requirements and the

IRP will determine, in part, the level of system resiliency that GPC will be expected to maintain

in future years.

                                                 III

         DOD/FEA purchases large quantities of electric service from GPC in Georgia.

DOD/FEA installations served by GPC include, but are not limited to, Robins Air Force Base,

Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay, Marine Corps Logistics Base Albany, Fort Stewart, Hunter

Army Airfield, Fort Moore/Benning, and Fort Eisenhower.

                                                 IV

         The interests ofDOD/FEA are such that they cannot be adequately protected by any other

party.

                                                 V

         DOD/FEA' s intervention and participation herein will not unreasonably broaden the

issues, burden the record, unduly affect the rights of participating parties, or unreasonably delay

the proceedings.


                                                 2

       WHEREFORE, The United States Department of Defense and all other Federal

Executive Agencies petitions the Commission for leave to intervene in the captioned proceeding.

DOD/FEA further requests that the Commission provide an accommodation to DOD/FEA to:

( 1) allow a remote video appearance by counsel on March 25 for the purpose of appearing at the

intervention hearing; (2) approve intervention without the need for an in-person appearance on

March 25 to save the expense of having to fly counsel from Virginia to Atlanta; or (3) approve

intervention even if no appearance is made if the federal government is in shutdown status and

DOD/FEA counsel is prohibited from working _o n March 25.
