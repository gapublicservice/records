Question:

The Company is required by the Commission Order approving the revisions to the Rules and Regulations to provide final terms and conditions intended to implement the revisions to the Company’s rules and regulations prior to utilizing them in contracting. Question: 

Please detail how the company determines the base revenue requirement of incremental investments related to a large-load customer’s electric service with regards to the calculation of the customer’s annual minimum bill. 

Response:

As detailed in discussions with PSC Staff on March 10, 2025, the Company determines the base revenue requirement of incremental investments related to a large-load customer’s electric service with regards to the calculation of the customer’s annual minimum bill by quantifying the full revenue requirement (inclusive of all O&M and capital revenue requirements) of all known and expected generation and transmission costs procured to serve the large load growth. Please see the Company’s response to STF-PIA-28-5 for additional details.


===== DKT 44280 STF-PIA-28 PD/STF-PIA-28-4.docx =====
STF-PIA-28-4

Question: 

Please detail how the company will determine the relevant tariff for large-load customers, as well as how the new tariff will meet the revenue requirement attributable to serving large-load customers.

Response:

The tariff revisions the Company filed with the Commission on February 28, 2025, collectively require new customers with an expected total peak demand of 100 MW or greater at one or more premises located on one tract or contiguous tracts of land to take service on the Time of Use Supplier Choice (TOU-SC) Monthly Access Charge (MAC) option.  MAC was approved in the 2022 base rate case, and multiple customers are either taking service or have contracted to take service on the rate. 
Per the TOU-SC tariff, customers on the MAC option pay a customer-specific Monthly Access Charge. All energy is billed at the hourly Real Time Pricing – Day Ahead price effective for that hour. The MAC is computed by the Company based on the cost to serve the customer’s load and location to meet the Company’s financial requirements.  Additionally, the minimum bill provisions in the Contract for Electric Service Addendum will serve as an additional assurance that expected base revenue will at a minimum recover the incremental expected annual base revenue requirements.  

===== DKT 44280 STF-PIA-28 PD/STF-PIA-28-5.docx =====
STF-PIA-28-5

Question: 

Regarding the revised Section D14 of the Company’s Rules and Regulations, please describe the process by which the Company identifies the costs incurred by the Company for distribution, transmission, and generation necessary to serve large-load customers, as well as how such cost recovery is implemented in the customer’s contract for service.

Response:

Customers 100MW and above are generally not served by the distribution system. The local, onsite costs of the customer-dedicated substation will be paid for up front by the customer. Transmission costs were identified as projects to serve new business and those driven by operations studies for generation changes allocated to large load customers. Generation costs for the 2023 IRP Update, the 2025 IRP, and the Company-owned proposals for the 2029-2031 All-Source RFP and BESS RFPs, along with future capacity replacement costs in future years are assumed to be included and allocated to large-load customers. The minimum bill established in the contract for electric service for these customers will be set to, at a minimum, recover the annual base revenue requirements. The Company provided details described in this response to Staff on March 12, 2025.
