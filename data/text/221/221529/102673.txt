          APPLICATION OF CAPITAL GOOD FUND FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE

       COMES NOW, the Capital Good Fund (“Good Fund”), and hereby files this Application

for Leave to Intervene (“Application”) in the above-referenced dockets pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-

2-59, Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) Rule 515-2-1-.06, and the

Commission’s Procedural and Scheduling Order (“PSO”) issued on December 28, 2025. In

support of this application, Capital Good Fund respectfully shows the Commission as follows:

       On January 31, 2025, Georgia Power Company filed its 2025 Integrated Resource Plan, for

which it seeks Commission approval. Georgia Power also filed an application for the certification

of its amended demand side management (DSM) plan. This application for leave to intervene is

timely filed pursuant to the Commission’s Procedural and Scheduling Order (“PSO”).

       Capital Good Fund is a nonprofit, U.S. Treasury-certified Community Development

Financial Institution (“CDFI”) that is headquartered in Providence, Rhode Island and operates in

11 states, including Georgia. Since its founding in 2009, Good Fund has pursued a mission to

create pathways out of poverty and advance a green economy through inclusive financial services.

                                               2

        In September of 2023, Good Fund launched the Georgia BRIGHT pilot program, which

offers discounted solar and battery storage leases and Solar Energy Procurement Agreements

(“SEPAs”) to low- and moderate- income (“LMI”) households and community-based

organizations across the state that have historically had less access to solar energy. These financial

products are offered at no upfront cost and generally offer the opportunity for day one energy

savings. Unlike traditional solar lessors, Good Fund does not approve participants for leases based

on credit score, but instead on a holistic assessment of ability to repay. To date, more than 90 LMI

households and 8 nonprofit organizations have received solar through these programs.

        In April of 2024, Good Fund was awarded a $156,010,000 grant from the Environmental

Protection Agency (“EPA”) through the Greenhouse Gas Reduction Fund’s Solar for All (“SFA”)

Program to expand the Georgia BRIGHT program and offer additional program options. This

program was funded by the Inflation Reduction Act (“IRA”) and is intended to deploy

approximately 100 MW of solar and serve more than 16,000 households across the state by the

spring of 2029. All participating households will enjoy at least 20% savings on their energy bills

net of any payments.

        In addition to its Solar for All award, Good Fund has received an $800,000 grant from the

Treasury’s CDFI Fund to launch an energy efficiency lending program in Georgia.

        Good Fund has an interest in the 2025 Georgia Power IRP and DSM proceedings both on

its own behalf and on behalf of its current and future clients. Several issues that are being addressed

in these cases will directly affect the operation of the Georgia BRIGHT pilot programs and the Solar for

All program. Additionally, the DSM proceedings will affect the design and operation of Good Fund’s

nascent energy efficiency lending program.


                                                   3

        The interests of Capital Good Fund are not adequately represented by other parties to these

proceedings. Other parties that have applied to intervene do not have interests similar to those of

Capital Good Fund. The applicant’s intervention would not unduly delay the proceedings or

prejudice the rights of other parties. This will be Good Fund’s first intervention in a Georgia Power

proceeding; however, Good Fund’s CEO did serve as an expert witness for the Georgia Coalition

of Local Governments in the 2023 IRP update.

        If this application is granted, Capital Good Fund intends to participate fully in this docket,

including cross-examining witnesses, filing direct testimony, and filing proposed orders and/or

briefs. Good Fund has not yet finalized plans for direct testimony but expects to focus its attention

on 1) the solar and battery storage pilot program 2) community solar and the related distributed

generation subscription program 3) other customer sited programs and 4) energy efficiency.

        Capital Good Fund respectfully requests that the following persons receive all notices,

correspondence and copies of orders and other materials in this docket, and be placed upon the

official service list:
