APPLICATION OF GEORGIA WAND EDUCATION FUND INC. AND VOTE SOLAR ACTION FUND’S FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE

COME NOW Georgia WAND and Vote Solar Action Fund (“Vote Solar”), pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59 and Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06, and hereby file this Application for Leave to Intervene("Application") before the Georgia Public Service Commission (the "Commission") in the above-referenced dockets. In support hereof, Georgia Conservation Voters Education Fund (hereinafter "Georgia Wand") respectfully show as follows:
1.
Georgia Wand Education Fund (“GEORGIA WAND”) is 501(c) (3) non-profit organization based in Atlanta Georgia that is over thirty five years old with numerous members and supporters across the state of Georgia and Georgia Power’s service territory. Georgia WAND advocates for nuclear harm deduction, energy/environmental and social climate justice. Georgia WAND works statewide to bridge the urban/rural divide for Georgia’s frontline, Central Savannah River Area (CSRA), communities along the Savannah River (including Burke County where Georgia Power’s nuclear commercial power Plant Vogtle is located) and the energy burdened Georgia customers in the City of Atlanta’s Neighborhood Planning Unit V (NPU-V). The commission’s actions in this proceeding will affect GEORGIA WAND’s interests in a sustainable future and right to a healthy environment. Further, GEORGIA WAND itself and many of its members, employees, and supporters are consumers of Georgia Power.  Many of the fourteen (14) CSRA communities that Georgia WAND advocates for and services are rural and are all serviced by Georgia power as well. Ultimately, Georgia WAND’s staff, board, and frontline communities they serve, including those who live near plant Vogtle, are not adequately represented by other parties to this proceeding and therefore will not unduly delay the proceedings or prejudice the rights of other parties. GEORGIA WAND has substantial interest in this proceeding and accordingly requests to intervene. 

Vote Solar is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit working to repower the United States with clean energy by making solar power more accessible and affordable through effective policy and advocacy. Vote Solar seeks to promote the development of solar at every scale, from distributed rooftop solar to large utility-scale plants. Established in 2002, Vote Solar has 110,186 members nationally, including 1,689 members in Georgia, some of whom reside within Georgia Power’s service territory. Vote Solar is not a trade group, nor does it have corporate members. Vote Solar has its principal place of business at: 360 22nd St, Suite 730, Oakland, CA 94612.
Vote Solar has actively participated in commission proceedings concerning resource procurement, solar valuation, and distributed generation program design in numerous states across the country, including Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Idaho, Louisiana, Massachusetts, Minnesota, Nevada, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, Utah, Vermont, and Wisconsin. This includes active participation in Georgia Power’s application for approval of its 2016, 2019, and 2022 Integrated Resource Plans.Vote Solar has a substantial interest in the subject matter of this proceeding. Vote Solar and its members support resource planning to foster the growth and accessibility of solar generation in Georgia. The outcome of this proceeding will significantly impact these objectives.

2.
On January 28, 2025, the Commission issued a procedural and scheduling order applicable to these dockets. Subsequently, on January 31, 2025, Georgia Power Company filed before the Commission for approval its 2025 Integrated Resource Plan ("IRP") but was published on February 11, 2025. Pursuant to the procedural and scheduling order, GEORGIA WAND and Vote Solar timely file this application for leave to intervene and participate in the above-referenced dockets.

3.
Having reviewed other intervening parties' applications, GEORGIA WAND and Vote Solar conclude that their rights and interests cannot be adequately represented by any other party to the above styled dockets.   GEORGIA WAND and Vote Solar respectfully request that they be granted leave to intervene and participate fully as a party in these proceedings, including the right to present testimony and exhibits, cross examine witnesses, and file briefs. While GEORGIA WAND  and Vote Solar have not yet determined all of the witness testimony they would offer, the GEORGIA WAND and Vote Solar anticipate providing expert testimony regarding and other relevant evidence related to their position.

4.
GEORGIA WAND and Vote Solar's participation in this matter will not unduly delay these proceedings or prejudice any other party. GEORGIA WAND and Vote Solar and its attorney of record have or will have performed their due diligence in understanding the Commission’s hearing procedures and applicable rules. Additionally, GEORGIA WAND has applied to intervene in a previous hearing in 2022 before the Georgia Public Service Commission and therefore has familiarity with the Commission's hearing procedures and applicable rules, ensuring efficient and orderly conduct in the proceeding.
