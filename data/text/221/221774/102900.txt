PETITION TO INTERVENE
OF AMERICANS FOR AFFORDABLE CLEAN ENERGY

Comes now, AMERICANS FOR AFFORDABLE CLEAN ENERGY (“AACE”) and pursuant to the provisions of O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14, 46-2-59 and Commission Utility Rule 515-2-1-.06, files this Petition to Intervene in the above-styled Dockets.  In support hereof, AACE shows the Commission, as follows:
1.

AACE is a non-profit organization, organized pursuant to the Internal Revenue Code, Section 501(c)(4), with members consisting of owners and operators of convenience stores, public travel facilities and truck stops that provide goods and services to the traveling public at locations throughout Georgia and across the United States, primarily consisting of retail consumer goods, vehicle repair and service products, food, and fuel.

2.
AACE’s principal office is located at 1330 Braddock Place, Suite 501, Alexandria, Virginia   22314.  
3.
AACE members purchase electricity from Georgia Power Company (the “Company”) at numerous business premises, and the rates they pay for electricity significantly impact their businesses and competitiveness.   
4.
As the property owners and operators of sites at which electric vehicle (EV) charging stations will be deployed, AACE members will play a critical role in advancing the public policy goal of developing electricity fueled transportation through private investment.  The policies established by the Commission in these Dockets will impact the ability of AACE members                     to provide EV charging services and fuel to their customers, which will, in turn, impact the deployment of EV charging stations and services throughout Georgia.  
5.
AACE membership includes several companies with the result that intervention by individual members would be unduly burdensome.
6.
The rights and interests of AACE’s members cannot be adequately represented by any other party in these Dockets.  


7.

AACE requests that it be permitted to intervene in these Dockets and participate fully as a party herein, including the right to present testimony and exhibits, cross-examine witnesses, and file briefs.  
8.
AACE requests that the following persons receive service of all notices, pleadings, and testimony on its behalf in these Dockets:
