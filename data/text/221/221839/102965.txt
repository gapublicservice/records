PETITION OF
THE GEORGIA SOLAR ENERGY INDUSTRIES ASSOCIATION, INC. 
FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE

COMES NOW, the Georgia Solar Energy Industries Association, Inc. (“GSEIA”) and, in accordance with O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59, Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) Rule 515-2-1-.06, and the Procedural and Scheduling Order issued by the Commission in the above-styled dockets (collectively, the “Dockets”), files its Petition for Leave to Intervene in the Dockets.  In support of this Petition, GSEIA respectfully shows the Commission the following:
  
GSEIA is a non-profit corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Georgia.  GSEIA is a trade association consisting of individuals, businesses, and other entities that promote the economic and environmental benefits of solar energy generation in Georgia.  

GSEIA maintains its principal place of business at 2451 Cumberland Parkway #3380, Atlanta, GA, 30339. 


GSEIA’s members have a vital interest in the pending Dockets as their members develop and construct in Georgia facilities to generate and produce solar energy. Many of GSEIA’s members are based in Georgia. GSEIA’s members have developed and constructed solar generation facilities throughout Georgia  and participated in numerous, if not all, of Georgia Power Company’s (the “Company”) solar programs. 
   
GSEIA’s membership includes several companies with the result that intervention by individual members would be unduly burdensome. 

No other party in either or both of the Dockets can adequately represent the interests of GSEIA and its members. 
   
GSEIA’s participation in the Dockets will not unduly delay such proceedings or prejudice any other party.  

GSEIA desires to actively participate in these proceedings to protect the interests of its members and the general public and address the issues raised in these Dockets. 
     
GSEIA intends to submit direct testimony by one or more to-be-determined expert witnesses and/or one or more representatives of one or more of its members on subjects germane to its members and the Georgia solar energy industry. 

 
GSEIA hereby requests that it be permitted to intervene in the Dockets and participate fully as a party therein, including, but not limited to, the right to present direct testimony and exhibits, cross-examine witnesses, and file briefs.  

All notices, correspondence, and service copies of notices, pleadings, testimony, and documents filed herein should be addressed to the following person, who should be placed upon the official service list in this proceeding:
