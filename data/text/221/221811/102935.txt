APPLICATION FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE OF
GEORGIA COALITION OF LOCAL GOVERNMENTS

COME NOW, the City of Atlanta, the City of Decatur, the Unified Government of Athens-Clarke County, the County of Gwinnett, and the County of DeKalb as the Georgia Coalition of Local Governments (“The Coalition”), and hereby file this Application for Leave to Intervene (“Application”) in the above referenced dockets pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59, Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) Rule 515-2-1-.06, and the Commission’s Procedural and Scheduling Order (“PSO”) issued on January 28, 2025. In support hereof, The Coalition respectfully shows as follows:

1.
On January 31, 2025, the Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) filed for approval of its 2025 Integrated Resource Plan (“IRP”) and an application for the certification of its amended demand side management (“DSM”) plan.  Pursuant to the PSO, The Coalition files this application for leave to intervene and participate in these dockets.  The Application is timely filed.

2.
The six city and county governments that compose The Coalition represent jurisdictions partially or wholly served by Georgia Power. The Coalition member governments collectively represent approximately 2.1 million residents, or roughly 19% of the population of Georgia. The member governments are themselves large buyers of electricity, together purchasing significant quantities of energy for their local operations each year. As both large customers and representatives of our residents, the Coalition members are particularly interested in issues of climate resiliency, renewable energy generation, demand growth, energy affordability, and robust demand-side management or energy efficiency programs that will be affected by the IRP process. Moreover, several of the participating governments have articulated a desire to transition operations away from the use of fossil fuels to clean and renewable forms of energy to reduce carbon emissions and improve reliability and resiliency.  In furtherance of its interests and on behalf of its members, the Coalition previously intervened in Georgia Power’s 2023 IRP Update, Docket No. 55378, Georgia Power’s 2022 Rate Case, Docket No. 44280, and Georgia Power’s 2022 IRP and DSM proceedings, Docket Nos. 44160 and 44161. 

3.
As required in the Application Requirements adopted by the Commission in the PSO, The Coalition has reviewed the other intervening party applications including applications by Georgia Interfaith Power & Light and Southface Energy Institute, Georgia Association of Manufacturers, Southern Renewable Energy Association, Clean Energy Buyers Association, Fermata Energy, Sierra Club, the Southern Alliance for Clean Energy, and the Natural Resources Defense Council, Capital Good Fund, Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority, Georgia Conservation Voters Education Fund, Georgia Watch, Georgia Center for Energy Solutions, Inc., United States Department of Defense and all other Federal Executive Agencies, Americans for Affordable Clean Energy, Georgia Wand Education Fund, and Vote Solar. The Coalition’s rights and interests cannot be adequately represented by any other party to the above-styled dockets, and its participation in this matter will not unduly delay these proceedings or prejudice any other party. 

4. 
The Coalition respectfully requests that it be granted leave to intervene and participate fully as a party in these proceedings, including the right to present testimony and exhibits, cross-examine witnesses, and file briefs.  If this application is granted, The Coalition intends to participate fully in this docket, including cross-examining witnesses, filing direct testimony, and filing proposed orders and/or briefs. The Coalition has not yet finalized plans for direct testimony, but expects to focus its attention on: (1) the residential and commercial solar and battery storage pilot program and benefits of a full-scale virtual power plant (VPP) program, (2) community solar and related distribution generation subscription program, (3) renewable energy RFP procurement strategy, (4) energy efficiency and DSM programs, (5) additional efforts to develop a climate resilient grid.
