VERIFIED APPLICATION OF
ADVANCED POWER ALLIANCE
FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE

COMES NOW, Advanced Power Alliance (“APA”) ("Intervenor") pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 50-13-14 and § 46-2-59, Georgia Public Service Commission (hereafter "GPSC" or "Commission") Rule 512-2-1.06, and the Procedural and Scheduling Order entered in the above-referenced matter on January 28, 2025, hereby files its Application for Leave to Intervene in this proceeding. In support of its application for Leave to Intervene, APA asserts the following: 
1.
The Intervenor in this matter, APA, is a non-profit corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Texas.  APA is a trade association whose members represent a diverse cross-section of the energy industries, with member companies owning or investing in nearly all forms of electric power generation. APA promotes an ‘all of the above’ energy strategy that leverages the best attributes of each energy resource to serve customers reliably and affordably. The organization is especially focused on helping the states develop their own internal energy resources including wind energy, solar power, and battery storage technologies. APA maintains its principal place of business at P.O. Box 28112 Austin, TX 78755.
2.
As a trade association with members who have participated in previous Requests for Proposal ("RFPs") for solar capacity by the GPSC and desires to participate in future such RFPs, APA has a legal interest as well as a property interest in the above referenced proceeding.  APA members have a vital interest in this docket because these members develop and deploy facilities to generate and produce solar energy.  APA members have participated in Georgia Power Company's Advanced Solar Initiative ("ASI"), the ASI Prime solar programs, the Georgia Renewable Energy Development Initiative ("REDI"), the Customer Renewable Supply Procurement ("CRSP"), the Clean And Renewable Energy Subscription (“CARES”) Program  and have deployed solar generation and battery storage projects throughout Georgia pursuant thereto, as well as around the country.
3.
No other company or trade association involved in this proceeding can adequately and accurately represent APA’s and its members' rights and interests in the above-referenced dockets. 
4.
APA’s participation in this proceeding will not unduly delay or prejudice the rights of other parties by intervening nor will it interfere with the rights of any other parties.
5.
APA intends to actively participate in these dockets to protect their interests including cross-examining witnesses, filing and presenting direct testimony, and filing proposed orders and/or briefs.  At this time, APA does intend to file pre-filed testimony on May 2nd, 2025 pursuant to the January 28, 2025 Procedural and Scheduling Order.
