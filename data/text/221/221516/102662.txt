COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before March 20, 2025. 


DEFINITIONS

As may be used in this document:  
1.“The Company,” “Georgia Power” or “The Utility” means Georgia Power Company and its present and former officers, employees, agents, representatives, directors, and all other persons acting or purporting to act on behalf of said Company.
The term “you” and “your” refer to “the Company.”
The term “person” means any natural person, corporation, corporate division, partnership, other unincorporated association, trust, government agency, or entity.
The term “document” or “documentation” shall have the broadest possible meaning under applicable law.  “Document” or “documentation” means every writing or record of every type and description that is in the possession, custody or control of the Company including, but not limited to, correspondence, memoranda, e-mails, drafts, workpapers, summaries, stenographic or handwritten notes, studies, notices, publications, books, pamphlets, reports, surveys, minutes or statistical compilations, computer and other electronic records or tapes or printouts, including, but not limited to, electronic mail files; and copies of such writing or records containing any commentary or notation whatsoever that does not appear in the original.  The term “document” or “documentation” further includes, by way of illustration and not limitation, schedules, progress schedules, time logs, drawings, computer disks, chart projections, time tables, summaries of other documents, minutes, surveys, work sheets, drawings, comparisons, evaluations, laboratory and testing reports, telephone call records, personal diaries, calendars, personal notebooks, personal reading files, transcripts, witness statements and indices.
The term “referring or relating to” means consisting of, containing, mentioning, suggesting, reflecting, concerning, regarding, summarizing, analyzing, discussing, involving, dealing with, emanating from, directed at, pertaining to in any way, or in any way logically or factually connected or associated with the matter discussed.
“And” and “or” as used herein shall be construed both conjunctively and disjunctively and each shall include the other whenever such construction will serve to bring within the scope of these discovery requests any information that would not otherwise be brought within their scope.
The singular as used herein shall include the plural and the masculine gender shall include the feminine and the neuter.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a person other than a natural person means to state:  the full name of the person and any names under which it conducts business; the present or last known address of the person; and, the present or last known telephone number of the person.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a document means to provide with respect to each document requested to be identified by these discovery requests a description of the document that is sufficient for purposes of a request to produce or a subpoena duces tecum, including the following:
the type of document (e.g., letter, memorandum, etc.);
the date of the document; the title or label of the document;
the Bates number or other identifier used to number the document for use in  litigation; the identity of the originator;
the identity of each person to whom it was sent;
the identity of each person to whom a copy or copies were sent;
a summary of the contents of the document;
the name and last known address of each person who presently has possession, custody or control of the document;
if any such document was, but is no longer, in your possession,  custody or control or is no longer in existence, state whether it: (1) is missing or lost; (2) has been destroyed; or (3) has been transferred voluntarily or involuntarily, and if so, state the circumstances surrounding the authorization for each such disposition and the date of such disposition.
“Identify”, “identifying” or identity” when used in reference to a communication should be read to include information regarding the date of the communication, whether the communication was written or oral, the identity of all parties and witnesses to the communication, the substance of what was said and/or transpired and, if written, the identity of the document(s) containing or referring to the communication.

Please refer to Page 3, of the Company’s Application (“Application”) for Certification, Decertification and Amended Demand-Side Management (DSM) Plan which states that “Appendix B contains a summary of the future projections of planned customer participation by programs”. Please provide all worksheets (with working formulas intact) that were used by the Company to forecast the future projections of planned customer participation by program. 

For each DSM measure included in the “Proposed” case, please provide for 2026 to 2037, the percentage (%) of the eligible market for each DSM measure that is forecasted by the Company to adopt the DSM measure.

For each DSM measure included in the “Supply-Side” case, please provide for 2026 to 2037, the percentage (%) of the eligible market for each DSM measure that is forecasted by the Company to adopt the DSM measure.

Please refer to Section 2.5 (DSM Program Cost Effectiveness) of the Company’s Application, please explain how the Company adjusts DSM measure per unit incremental annual kWh and kW savings at the customer meter for the following factors:
Transmission and distribution line losses between the customer meter and the generation level of the Georgia Power Company grid
Planning reserve margin

Please refer to the cost effectiveness tests discussed in Section 2.5 and Appendix E of the Company’s Application, please describe the source for the following  avoided costs:
Avoided capacity
Avoided energy
Avoided transmission and distribution infrastructure
Avoided generation reserves
The date that the above values were determined

Please provide a copy of any internal or external audits of the Company’s DSM program expenditures that has been conducted during the past five years.

Please provide a description of any actions taken by the Company in 2023 through 2025 to control DSM program costs relating to DSM program management, administration, data tracking and reporting, marketing, customer service call center, quality assurance and evaluation.

Please provide the DSM measure level cost effectiveness screening results for the Total Resource Cost Test, Utility Cost Test, Participant Test and Rate Impact Measure Test for each of the four DSM cases provided in the Company’s Application.

For each DSM program included in Appendix A in the Company’s Application, please identify any proposed program design features that are new for 2026 and are not included in the Company’s current certified DSM programs.

For each DSM measure included in Appendix H in the Company’s Application, please identify any measures that are not available as part of  the Company’s current certified DSM programs.

Please explain the methodology used by the Company to determine the appropriate incentive or rebate level for the DSM measures included in the Company’s Application for the Proposed and the Capacity and Affordability Cases. 

Please explain whether the Company used any of the following methods to determine incentive levels for the DSM measures included in its Application:
Percentage of incremental measure cost
Value of first-year energy and demand savings
Value of lifetime energy and demand savings

Please provide the most recent demand response potential study conducted by Georgia Power Company.
 
Please provide the Company’s latest cost effectiveness screening of residential water heater load control.

Please provide the Company’s latest cost effectiveness screening of commercial water heater load control.


Please provide a list of the recommendations from the latest program impact and process evaluations of the Company’s programs that have been implemented and are now included in the program design for the Company’s 2026 to 2028 DSM programs.

For each energy efficiency program included in Appendix A of the Company’s Application, please provide the cost of conserved energy per kWh saved for the 12-year period (2026 to 2037) by DSM program for each of the four DSM cases examined in the Company’s Application.

For each of the four DSM cases included in the Company’s Application, please provide the total present value of customer bill savings for the 12-year period (2026 to 2037) in the DSM plan for each case.

Please explain why the Company did not include solar water heating as a residential or non-residential DSM measure in its Application.

Please explain what generation resources are avoided or deferred in the Supply-side scenario compared to the other three DSM cases.

Please provide the present value of the total revenue requirements over the 12-year planning horizon for each of the four DSM cases.
