LEGAL NOTICE

Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) has filed with the Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) its 2025 Integrated Resource Plan and 2025 Application for the Certification, Decertification, and Amended Demand-Side Management Plan. Copies of these filings are on file with the Commission and are also available for inspection at the region offices of Georgia Power.

These matters have been assigned Docket No. 56002 and Docket No. 56003, respectively, and have been scheduled for hearing before the Commission pursuant to the Integrated Resource Plan Act, O.C.G.A § 46-3A-1 et seq. The Company direct hearing for both Docket No. 56002 and Docket No. 56003 will begin on March 25, 2025, at 9:30 a.m. and will be held in Room 110 of the Commission offices located at 244 Washington Street, SW, Atlanta, Georgia 30334-5701. Please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) to confirm the location of the hearing.

In accordance with O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59(c), persons wishing to intervene must file a Petition to Intervene with the Commission within thirty (30) days of the first publication of this notice.  

Pursuant to the Commission’s Alternative eFile instructions, any pre-filed testimony or statements by interveners must be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary by no later than 4:00 p.m. on May 2, 2025. All testimony must be pre-filed. Parties of record must also serve one another copies of any pre-filed testimony. The hearing on intervener testimony is scheduled to begin May 27, 2025, at 9:30 a.m.

If you have a disability and will need assistance or accommodation to participate, or need further information, please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) at least one day prior to the hearing in this matter.

This notice is published at the direction of the Georgia Public Service Commission.


/s/ Jeremiah Haswell
Jeremiah Haswell
Director, Regulatory Affairs 
Georgia Power Company
