13-14 and 46-2-59 and Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06, and hereby file this Application for Leave
to Intervene("Application") before the Georgia Public Service Commission (the "Commission")
in the above-referenced dockets. In support hereof, Georgia Conservation Voters Education Fund
(hereinafter "GCVEF") respectfully show as follows:
                                                  1.
        Georgia Conservation Voters Education Fund (“GCVEF”) is 501(c) (3) non-profit
organization based in Atlanta Georgia that was founded in 1996 with numerous members and
supporters across the state of Georgia and Georgia Power’s service territory. GCVEF’s mission
statement is to organize and mobilize Georgians to advance climate and environmental justice
through education, advocacy, and other forms of civic engagement. As part of their efforts,
GCVEF has made active efforts to fight for affordable bills, clean energy, and community centered
policies and programs. The commission’s actions in this proceeding will affect GCVEF’s interests
in a sustainable future and right to a healthy environment. Further, GCVEF itself and many of its
members, employees, and supporters are consumers of Georgia Power. Their consumption, costs,
and terms of service for purchase of electricity will likely be affected by the Commission’s actions
in these dockets. GCVEF has substantial interest in this proceeding and accordingly requests to
intervene.1
                                                  2.
        On January 28, 2025, the Commission issued a procedural and scheduling order applicable
to these dockets. Subsequently, on January 31, 2025, Georgia Power Company filed before the
Commission for approval its 2025 Integrated Resource Plan ("IRP"). Pursuant to the procedural
and scheduling order, GCVEF timely file this application for leave to intervene and participate in
the above-referenced dockets.2
