COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before March 27, 2025.

Reserve Margin Study 


Please confirm that the load forecast error distribution is reflected in the Loss of Load Expectation (“LOLE”) calculation in the Reserve Margin Study. If this is not correct, please explain.
Please provide all workpapers, in Excel with formulas, used to develop the load forecast error (“LFE”) distribution. This request includes all of the inputs and analyses for both components of the LFE (model errors and economic forecast errors).
Please explain how the Company considered the impact of large customer load and data center uncertainty in the load forecast error. Please provide an explanation of why the Company believes that there is not a potential double counting of this large customer load uncertainty in both the LFE and in the risk adjusted load forecast.
Please provide all information available to the Company and/or Astrape that identifies the number of electric utilities that include a LFE distribution in the calculation of a reserve margin. For each utility, indicate whether the utility uses Strategic Energy and Risk Valuation Model (“SERVM”) (if known) and the methodology used to calculate the LFE distribution.
Please identify all of the inputs into the SERVM analysis that are used to perform the Economic Optimum Reserve Margin (“EORM”) reserve margin calculation but not used in the SERVM LOLE calculation (e.g., cost of expected unserved energy (“EUE”), production costs, etc.).
Please describe the drivers of the increase in reserve margin compared to the 2022 IRP reserve margin and the 2025 planning reserve margin. 
Please describe any impacts related to increased energy requirements and higher load factor load on the planning reserve margin.
Please describe any studies performed to quantify the impacts of both shape and magnitude of the load forecast change.
Please describe impacts of weather normal load forecasting and load forecasts used in the evaluation for reserve margin.

Refer to page 37 of the Reserve Margin study describing the Existing System resources that needed to be removed to establish the initial system assumptions.
Please explain what impact the underlying system composition has on the target reserve margin.  
How has the analysis accounted for possible changes to the system composition over time? Does the study address the question of which new unit types (CC, CT, nuclear, Solar, BESS) could be or would be economically added to the system? Please explain.

Please provide all analysis, notes, and calculations produced by the Company or Astrape in relation to the seasonal ELCC values and seasonal resource accreditation associated with latest reliability studies. Explain if the increase in summer reserve margin has or would have a corresponding increase in ELCC value for summer peak resources, such as solar.

Refer to Figure 6 in the Reserve Margin Study describing the Summer Target Reserve Margin Floor as a function of Winter Reserve Margin.
Has GPC conducted any studies to determine the optimal way to manage the summer and winter planning reserve margins?  
Has the Company considered cost and/or resource availability in setting the winter and summer target along that curve? Provide the analysis conducted and explain the process and findings, if applicable. 
 
Refer to the Company’s Load and Resource table, “Georgia Power Territorial Base Case Load vs. Existing Capability Table with 2025 IRP Requests 2-14-25.xlsx”, and the footnote - “(E) Does not consider planning reserve sharing. Reflects GPC's Target Reserve Margin resulting from a System Target Reserve Margin of 25.50% (2025-2027) and 26% (2028 and beyond).”
Please explain the terms and conditions of the “planning reserve sharing” feature of the Southern Company ICC agreement and explain how it would change the table if planning reserve sharing had been considered.
Please provide a revised table that accounts for planning reserve sharing.
Please explain what year the Southern Company need date now is, and whether or not GPC could rely on reserve sharing during any part of the near-term period. 
Please provide all workpapers used to derive the Georgia Reserve Margin target based on diversity from the Southern Company reserve margin target. Provide this for both the 2023 IRP Update and the current 2025 IRP and provide it on a short-term and long-term basis.

Refer to page 9 of the Reserve Margin Study describing a calibration benchmark of modeled energy with market transactions.
Provide a copy of all transactions benchmark analysis performed in SERVM for market transactions.
Has the Company performed any benchmark analysis related to market transactions for its Aurora production cost modeling? If so, explain.  If not, explain why not?
Refer to page 19 of the Reserve Margin Study regarding the simulated and actual outage probability. Please provide the benchmark study.
Has the Company performed any benchmark analysis related to outage probability for its Aurora production cost modeling? If so, explain. If not, explain why not?
Has the Company performed any benchmark analysis related to production cost and dispatch accuracy related to its Aurora production cost modeling? If so, please explain and provide the latest informal or formal study.  If not, explain why not?
