                 APPLICATION FOR LEA VE TO INTERVENE OF
           THE METRO POLIT AN ATLANTA RAPID TRANSIT AUTHORITY

        COMES NOW, the Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority (hereafter "MARTA"),

pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§50-13-14 and 46-2-59 and Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06, and hereby

files this Application for Leave to Intervene ("Application") in the above-referenced dockets. In

support thereof, MARTA respectfully shows as follows.

                                                  1.

        On January 28, 2025, the Georgia Public Service Commission (the "Commission" or

"PSC") issued a procedural and scheduling order applicable to the above-referenced dockets.

On January 31, 2025, Georgia Power Company (hereinafter "Georgia Power") filed with the

Commission its 2025 Integrated Resource Plan ("IRP"). Pursuant to the Commission's

Procedural and Scheduling Order, any person or party seeking an application for leave to

intervene shall file the same within thirty (30) days following the first published notice of

proceeding. In accordance with the Commission's Order, MARTA hereby timely files this

Application to Intervene and to participate in the above-referenced dockets.

                                                 2.

       MARTA is a public authority created by the MARTA Act, Georgia Laws 1965, page

2243, et. seq., as amended from time to time. Its mission is to advocate for and provide safe,

multi-modal transit services that advance prosperity, connectivity, and equity for a more livable

region.     MARTA provides service to 2.2 million residents of Metro Atlanta and is one of the

top 10 transportation agencies in the United States. Its combined bus and rail services support

over 24,000 jobs throughout Atlanta and the State of Georgia. MART A's rail service involves

over 330 rail cars capable of carrying passengers to any of its 38 stations, and its 550 buses

provide service along 1,800 miles of road on 113 routes. MART A currently operates six electric

buses with plans to expand its fleet of electric vehicles. MART A's services help drive economic

development in and around the metro-region. MART A's footprint in the metro-area is fairly

significant, with 905.7 acres of property, including 388.7 acres directly adjacent to its MARTA

stations. MARTA purchases electricity from Georgia Power to power its electric rail service and

provides Electric Vehicle charging stations to its patrons and employees. Cost effective, reliable

energy is important to its operations and services. Energy costs have a significant impact on

MART A's mission, operations, and its customers.

                                                 3.

          MARTA has a distinct and direct interest in the Georgia Power's 2025 IRP proceedings

both on its own behalf and on behalf of its employees and customers. Georgia Power is

MARTA's electric service provider, and its customers are subject to the impacts of the

Company's services and rates. The Commission's approval, denial, or modification of Georgia

Power's proposals in its 2025 IRP will directly impact MART A's transportation and service

mandates as well as its property and business interests and the interests of its customers. As a

result, MARTA has a substantial interest in this proceeding and desires to intervene in this

proceeding to protect its interests and the interests of its employees and customers.


                                                 2

                                                   5.

        MART A's interests and those of its customers are not adequately represented by other

parties to these proceedings, including the other intervening parties, and MARTA's intervention

and participation in this matter will not unduly delay the proceedings or prejudice the rights of

other parties.

                                                  6.

        MARTA respectfully requests that it be granted leave to intervene and participate fully as

a party in these proceedings, including the right to present testimony and exhibits, cross-examine

witnesses, and file briefs, as may be needed.

                                                  7.

        MARTA respectfully requests that the following persons receive all notices,

correspondence and copies of pleadings, orders and other materials in this docket, and that all

communications regarding this docket be directed to:
