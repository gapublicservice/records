       COME NOW, the City of Atlanta, the City of Decatur, the Unified Government of Athens-

Clarke County, the County of Gwinnett, and the County of DeKalb as the Georgia Coalition of

Local Governments (“The Coalition”), and hereby file this Application for Leave to Intervene

(“Application”) in the above referenced docket pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59,

Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) Rule 515-2-1-.06, and the Commission’s

Procedural and Scheduling Order (“PSO”) issued on May 20, 2025. In support hereof, The

Coalition respectfully shows as follows:

                                                 1.

       On May 19, 2025, the Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) and the Public Interest

Advocacy Staff (“Staff”) filed a Joint Petition for Approval of the Stipulation to Extend the

Alternate Rate Plan (“ARP”) (“Joint Petition”). Pursuant to the PSO, The Coalition files this

Application for leave to intervene and participate in this docket. The Application is timely filed.


                                                 2.

       The five city and county governments that constitute The Coalition represent jurisdictions

partially or wholly served by Georgia Power. The Coalition member governments collectively

represent approximately 2.1 million residents, or roughly 19% of the population of Georgia. The

member governments are themselves large buyers of electricity, together purchasing significant

quantities of energy for their local operations each year.          As both large customers and

representatives of our residents, the Coalition members are particularly interested in issues of

energy affordability, energy data transparency, rate designs, and climate resiliency that will be

affected by this proceeding. Moreover, several of the participating governments have articulated

a desire to transition operations away from the use of fossil fuels to clean and renewable forms of

energy to reduce carbon emissions and improve reliability and resiliency, ambitions that could be

substantially supported or impeded by decisions made in this docket. In furtherance of its interests

and on behalf of its members, the Coalition previously intervened in Georgia Power’s 2025 IRP

and DSM proceeding, Docket Nos. 56002 and 56003, Georgia Power’s 2023 IRP Update, Docket

No. 55378, Georgia Power’s 2022 Rate Case, Docket No. 44280, and Georgia Power’s 2022 IRP

and DSM proceedings, Docket Nos. 44160 and 44161.


                                                  3.

       As required in the Application Requirements adopted by the Commission in the PSO, The

Coalition has reviewed the other intervening party applications, including applications by Georgia

Solar Energy Association, Metropolitan Atlanta Rapid Transit Authority, Georgia Interfaith Power

& Light, Sierra Club, the Southern Alliance for Clean Energy, and the Natural Resources Defense

Council, and Walmart. The Coalition’s rights and interests cannot be adequately represented by

any other party to the above-styled docket, and its participation in this matter will not unduly delay

these proceedings or prejudice any other party.

docket.
