RE:Docket No. 44280: Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Rate Case, Consideration of Joint Petition

Dear Ms. Tanner:

In accordance with the Commission’s May 20, 2025 Procedural and Scheduling Order in Docket No. 44280, please find enclosed the first errata to the pre-filed testimony of Tom Bond and Steven Roetger. 

At the time Public Interest Advocacy Staff filed its testimony, Georgia Power Company (the Company) asserted that certain information included in Staff’s testimony was trade secret.  Pursuant to Commission rules, Staff was required to redact that information from the public version of its testimony.  On June 18, 2025, the Company filed its response to Staff Data Request STF-PIA-30-1. Portions of the Company’s response were filed publicly, and these public portions now allow PIA Staff to publicly disclose previously confidential information in its testimony.

An electronic version of this filing has been served to all parties in this docket.
