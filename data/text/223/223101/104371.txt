Application for Leave to Intervene of Georgia Conservation Voters Education Fund

NOW COMES, Georgia Conservation Voters Education Fund (hereafter “GCVEF”) and pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59, and Public Service Commission Rule 515-2-1.06, files this application for leave to intervene in the above-referenced dockets.  In support of its application GCVEF states the following:
1.
On May 20, 2025, the Georgia Public Service Commission (hereafter “Commission”) approved a Procedural and Scheduling Order for Docket 44280.
2.
GCVEF is a public interest advocacy group which represents the interests of its members and supporters in matters regarding affordable energy utility services in Georgia before the Georgia Public Service Commission and in other forums.  GCVEF, it’s members and supporters are Georgia Power Company customers, and will be directly impacted by the decision and order of the Commission in this matter.
The interests of GCVEF and its members and supporters may be substantially affected by decisions made by the Commission in this proceeding.  Specifically, GCVEF’s members and supporters will be impacted by the rates and charges set by the Commission’s order in this docket.  


3.
GCVEF requests the following person receive all notices, correspondence, and copies of pleadings, orders, and other materials filed in this docket, and that all communications regarding this docket be directed to:
Juan Estrada
Juan Estrada Law
3675 Crestwood Parkway, Suite 400
Duluth GA 30096
Juan@JuanEstradaLaw.com  

4.
No other party in this docket can adequately represent the interests of GCVEF, and none of the other parties have interests identical to GCVEF.  GCVEF’s participation in this docket will not unduly delay or prejudice the rights of other parties.
WHEREFORE, GCVEF requests the Commission grant its application for leave to intervene and admit it as a full party of record in this proceeding with all rights attendant thereto.
