HR-2-2

Information requested by:  Walmart – Stephanie Eaton

Question:

Q.I’d like to make a Hearing Request for any additional information that GPC could share to understand the test that was done last week at Plant McDonough as it relates to the press release published by the Company.

Response:

As part of a Research & Design project, Georgia Power and Mitsubishi Power collaborated and performed a series of hydrogen co-firing demonstration tests at the Plant McDonough-Atkinson facility between May and June 2025. This is the first validation project of 50% hydrogen co-firing by volume on an advanced class gas turbine in the world. During the month-long test period, seven short duration tests burning between 5% and 50% hydrogen were performed to test the safety and capability of the equipment to use hydrogen as a fuel source with an expected carbon dioxide (“CO2”) reduction of approximately 22%. Over 25,000 lb of hydrogen were consumed with a peak flow rate of over 10,000 lb per hour. This test achieved safe and reliable short-term operation of the Mitsubishi gas turbine with 50% hydrogen by volume and showed improved operational flexibility while maintaining emissions compliance.

HR-2-1

Information requested by:  Georgia Interfaith Power & Light and Southface Energy Institute – Jennifer Whitfield

Question:

Q.Can we make a hearing request for the information about how Georgia Power treats large load customers when they have an option to buy land on which they are presenting a development project as opposed to actually owning the land?

Response:

As part of the Company’s large load customer intake process, large load developers are considered to have site control if any of the following categories apply:

Owns the Site (evidenced by a deed recorded in the county property records).
Leases the Site (under a current binding written agreement between Developer and the owner or lessor of the Site).
Holds an option to purchase the Site (under a current binding written agreement between Developer and the owner of the Site).
Holds an option to lease the Site (under a current binding written option agreement with the owner or lessor of the Site).
Holds a current binding easement or license from the owner or lessor of the Site that grants Developer express rights to develop, construct, install, operate, maintain, and repair the Project at the Site.
The criteria identified above are consistent with Georgia Power’s requirements for site control from participants in Company Requests for Proposals (“RFPs”).
