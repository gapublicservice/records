STAFF’S RESPONSE TO REQUEST 
FOR SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION

On May 21, 2025, Southern Environmental Law Center (SELC), on behalf of intervenor Georgia Interfaith Power & Light (GIPL), filed a request for supporting documents relating to the Stipulation to Extend the Alternative Rate Plan (ARP) filed by Public Interest Advocacy Staff (PIA Staff) and Georgia Power Company (Georgia Power or Company) on May 19, 2025.
 As an initial matter, Staff lacks the authority to release information that the Company has asserted to be confidential or trade secret. Further, neither SELC nor GIPL have discovery rights in this proceeding. 
That said, PIA Staff is aware that Georgia Power has attached to its testimony filed May 30, 2025, trade secret exhibits that include 1) Georgia Power’s Computation of Retail Revenue Deficiency Applicable to Base Rate Tariffs For The Twelve Month Periods Ending December 31, 2026-2028; and, 2) Estimated Expense Adjustments. Exhibit A to the Stipulation filed in this Docket also includes trade secret information on Investment Tax Credits (“ITCs”) and Production Tax Credits (“PTCs”). While all three of these exhibits contain trade secret information, it is PIA Staff’s understanding that SELC and GIPL already have access to the trade secret version of these documents through a confidentiality agreement with Georgia Power.
On May 30, 2025, Staff also filed testimony that contains information which the Company asserts is trade secret. While Staff requested that it be allowed to file the information publicly, and while the Company declined Staff’s request, it is also PIA Staff’s understanding that SELC and GIPL already have access to the trade secret version of Staff’s testimony through its confidentiality agreement with Georgia Power.
 During the settlement discussions between PIA Staff and Georgia Power, Georgia Power did provide other documents to Staff.   These documents consisted of various offers and calculations associated with the offers, including pro forma revenue deficiency calculations as well as the Georgia Power’s calculated impact on the revenue deficiency of various proposed offers. These documents were identified as confidential settlement discussions which would be neither discoverable nor admissible and which Staff is not authorized to release. See also O.C.G.A. 50-18-72(a)(41) and (42). The Company also asserted that the information provided was trade secret. As noted above, Staff is not authorized to release information asserted to be trade secret without an order of the Commission or a court, or agreement of the utility. See Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11 and O.C.G.A. 50-18-72(a)(34); see also, Theragenics Corp. v. Department of Natural Resources, 244 Ga. App. 829 (2000), aff'd, 273 Ga. 724 (2001). Attached hereto as Attachments 1 through 3, respectively, are the Trade Secret Assertions for Pro Forma Retail Revenue Deficiency, Estimated Expense Adjustments Workpapers, and Informal ARP Extension Responses and Attachments that the Company has provided to Staff.
