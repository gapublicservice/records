COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before September 3, 2025. 

DEFINITIONS

As may be used in this document:  
1.“The Company,” “Georgia Power” or “The Utility” means Georgia Power Company and its present and former officers, employees, agents, representatives, directors, and all other persons acting or purporting to act on behalf of said Company.
The term “you” and “your” refer to “the Company.”
The term “person” means any natural person, corporation, corporate division, partnership, other unincorporated association, trust, government agency, or entity.
The term “document” or “documentation” shall have the broadest possible meaning under applicable law.  “Document” or “documentation” means every writing or record of every type and description that is in the possession, custody or control of the Company including, but not limited to, correspondence, memoranda, e-mails, drafts, workpapers, summaries, stenographic or handwritten notes, studies, notices, publications, books, pamphlets, reports, surveys, minutes or statistical compilations, computer and other electronic records or tapes or printouts, including, but not limited to, electronic mail files; and copies of such writing or records containing any commentary or notation whatsoever that does not appear in the original.  The term “document” or “documentation” further includes, by way of illustration and not limitation, schedules, progress schedules, time logs, drawings, computer disks, chart projections, time tables, summaries of other documents, minutes, surveys, work sheets, drawings, comparisons, evaluations, laboratory and testing reports, telephone call records, personal diaries, calendars, personal notebooks, personal reading files, transcripts, witness statements and indices.
The term “referring or relating to” means consisting of, containing, mentioning, suggesting, reflecting, concerning, regarding, summarizing, analyzing, discussing, involving, dealing with, emanating from, directed at, pertaining to in any way, or in any way logically or factually connected or associated with the matter discussed.
“And” and “or” as used herein shall be construed both conjunctively and disjunctively and each shall include the other whenever such construction will serve to bring within the scope of these discovery requests any information that would not otherwise be brought within their scope.
The singular as used herein shall include the plural and the masculine gender shall include the feminine and the neuter.    
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a person other than a natural person means to state:  the full name of the person and any names under which it conducts business; the present or last known address of the person; and, the present or last known telephone number of the person.
“Identify” or “identifying” or “identification” when used in reference to a document means to provide with respect to each document requested to be identified by these discovery requests a description of the document that is sufficient for purposes of a request to produce or a subpoena duces tecum, including the following:
the type of document (e.g., letter, memorandum, etc.);
the date of the document; the title or label of the document;
the Bates number or other identifier used to number the document for use in litigation; the identity of the originator;
the identity of each person to whom it was sent;
the identity of each person to whom a copy or copies were sent;
a summary of the contents of the document;
the name and last known address of each person who presently has possession, custody or control of the document;
if any such document was, but is no longer, in your possession, custody or control or is no longer in existence, state whether it: (1) is missing or lost; (2) has been destroyed; or (3) has been transferred voluntarily or involuntarily, and if so, state the circumstances surrounding the authorization for each such disposition and the date of such disposition.
“Identify”, “identifying” or identity” when used in reference to a communication should be read to include information regarding the date of the communication, whether the communication was written or oral, the identity of all parties and witnesses to the communication, the substance of what was said and/or transpired and, if written, the identity of the document(s) containing or referring to the communication.

Please refer to the Company’s February 2025 Load Realization Model (“LRM”) sensitivity and any further updates to the large load forecasting methodology, and respond to the following questions:

Referring to the “P1” probability in the LRM which determines the likelihood that a project selects Georgia as its final location, please summarize the range of P1 likelihoods that the Company considers assigning to a project in the LRM.  The February 2025 sensitivity assigns two possible values. Are any other values used or considered by the Company? If so, please identify and explain.   

Referring to the “P1” probability in the LRM, for the range of values possible under the P1 probability, please clarify how the Company assigns possible values to each project. If a value assignment rubric or methodology exists, please provide this rubric and an explanation of what each likelihood value represents.

Referring to the “P1” probability in the LRM, please explain how the Company adjusts P1 assumptions based on the level of site control a project demonstrates.

Referring to the “P1” probability in the LRM, if at all, please explain how the Company differentiates between a project that has demonstrated 1) ownership of land, 2) leased land, 3) demonstrated an option to purchase land, or 4) any other demonstration of site control considered. 

Referring to the “P1” probability in the LRM, for customers that provide an option to purchase land as demonstration of site control, please provide the Company’s understanding of the following:
The typical cost to option land,
The ratio between the cost to option land compared to the total investment of the project, and 
The ratio between the cost to option land compared to the cost to purchase the land.

Referring to the Company’s Main Panel Rebuttal Testimony in Docket No. 56002 & 56003, Exhibit 1, section 7.1.5, regarding the “P2” probability in the LRM, which determines the likelihood that a project selected Georgia Power as its electric service provider in the state of Georgia, please explain if the Company has made any changes to the methodology for assigning P2 values described in the referenced exhibit.

Referring to the Company’s Main Panel Rebuttal Testimony in Docket No. 56002 & 56003, Exhibit 1, section 7.1.5, are there any additional steps or guidelines used to assign a P2 probability to a prospective project? If so, please provide a complete description of the P2 assignment rubric. 

Referring to the Company’s Main Panel Rebuttal Testimony in Docket No. 56002 & 56003, Exhibit 1, section 7.1.5, please define the “on-hold” stage of the P2 probability rubric and explain the following:
Is there a specific time period of lack of contractual development after which the Company classifies a project as “on-hold”? If so, please explain how the Company selected this time period.
Can a project be reclassified as “on-hold” after having signed a Timeline Review or completed a Transmission Study?
Please explain the point at which a project classified as “on-hold” is removed from the LRM pipeline. Clarify if this is a standard period of time that applies to all projects equally.

Referring to the Company’s Main Panel Rebuttal Testimony in Docket No. 56002 & 56003, Exhibit 1, section 7.1.5, please explain if it is possible for a project to have submitted a Request for Electric Service but not have completed a Transmission Study or Timeline Review. If so, how would the Company classify such a project’s P2 probability?

Referring to the Company’s Main Panel Rebuttal Testimony in Docket No. 56002 & 56003, Exhibit 1, section 7.1.5, please explain if it is possible for a project to have completed a Timeline review but not have completed a Transmission Study? If so, how would the Company classify such a project’s P2 probability?

Referring to the Company’s Main Panel Rebuttal Testimony in Docket No. 56002 & 56003, Exhibit 1, section 7.1.5, please define “Transmission Study” and explain:
What are the costs associated with completing a Transmission Study? 
What portion of these costs is the prospective customer responsible for paying?
What information is the customer required to provide in order to complete a Transmission Study?
How long does it typically take to begin a Transmission Study after the customer has expressed interest in becoming a Georgia Power customer?
How long does it typically take to perform a Transmission Study?

Referring to the Company’s Main Panel Rebuttal Testimony in Docket No. 56002 & 56003, Exhibit 1, section 7.1.5, please define “Timeline Review” and explain:
What are the costs associated with completing a Timeline Review? 
What portion of these costs is the prospective customer responsible for paying?
What information is the customer required to provide in order to complete a Timeline Review?
How long does it typically take to begin a Timeline Review either after the customer has expressed interest in becoming a Georgia Power customer (if no Transmission Study has been completed) or after a Transmission Study has been performed?
How long does it typically take to perform a Timeline Review?

Once a Transmission Study and/or Timeline Review has been completed, does the Company share the results of these studies with the customer and, if needed due to transmission or other constraints, allow the customer to revise its projected load based on such constraints?
If so and customers have revised their projected load/load ramps, please describe how these revisions are accounted for in updating the LRM reports.

For customers that execute a contract for service with Georgia Power, does the Company anticipate that the terms and conditions of the contract and/or tariff may allow for the customer to revise its peak load needs at a later date? For example, some utilities such as AEP Ohio and Indiana Michigan Power allow large load customers a one-time option to reduce contract capacity after being placed in-service. If so, how does the Company account for this load reduction risk in the LRM?

Referring to the Load Materialization Assumptions in the LRM, accounting for the difference between the metered load being served versus the load announced by the customer, please explain if the Company has altered any of the assumptions since the February 2025 LRM sensitivity.

Referring to the Load Materialization Assumptions in the LRM, please explain the extent to which the current assumptions for data center projects are informed by any existing literature examining the capacity utilization (ratio between metered peak demand and rated capacity of data centers) of existing projects. Please provide any literature used to develop the current data center materialization assumptions.

Referring to the Commercial Operation Date Delays in the LRM, please explain how the Company observes the cumulative delays and acceleration of a single project across multiple quarters. For example, if a project accelerates its in-service date by 6 months in one quarter but then delays it by 12 months in the next quarters, has the Company developed a system to track the net delay for the specific project?

Referring generally to the Company’s Load Realization Model, have any of the projects in the pipeline begun commercial operation? If so, please identify these projects and provide their observed annual capacity and energy needs. Please provide your response in spreadsheet format. 

Referring to the Company’s Quarterly Economic Development Reports, please respond to the following questions:
Since the February 2025 LRM Sensitivity, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to accelerate or delay initially proposed load ramps? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the February 2025 LRM Sensitivity, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to accelerate or delay initially proposed load ramps? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization model?
Since the February 2025 LRM Sensitivity, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider, referring to the P1, P2, and P3 probabilities? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the February 2025 LRM Sensitivity, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to select Georgia Power as the ultimate service provider, referring to the P1, P2, and P3 probabilities? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization Model?
Since the February 2025 LRM Sensitivity, has the Company analyzed the tendency for projects to materialize load as expected? If so, please provide any such analysis in its original format in addition to a narrative description of the analysis performed. For any spreadsheet responses, please ensure the file is provided in spreadsheet format with any formulas and references intact.
Since the February 2025 LRM Sensitivity, has the Company observed any trends in the tendency for projects to materialize as expected? If so, do these trends differ from the assumptions in the Load Realization Model?

Provide a list of all customers who have executed Request For Service contract during the period January 2021 – June 2025. Provide the date the RFS was executed and forecasted load (MW). Identify whether the customer subsequently executed a Contract for Electric Service, the date Contract for Electric Service was executed and customer’s load (MW). Provide the response in an unprotected Excel spreadsheet.
