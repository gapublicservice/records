===== DKT 56765 STF-JKA-1 Supplemental PD 2 24 26/STF-JKA-1-8.docx =====
STF-JKA-1-8

Question: 

For the years 2023, 2024, and 2025, and for the peak winter and summer days in those years, and for the peak load hour and the minimum load hour of those days, provide all energy components and associated cost components that made up the hourly energy balance for those hours. In other words, for those six hours, the following information should be supplied electronically on an hourly basis by component including such items as:

a.Company Load
b.Losses.
c.Generation by unit with all FCR fuel related cost components separately identified.
d.Energy Purchases by purchase, with all FCR cost components separately identified.
e.Energy Sales by sale, with all FCR cost component separately identified.
f.Any other energy component and cost component making up the energy balance separately identified.
Please demonstrate that there is an energy balance in each hour.

Response:

STF-JKA-1-8 Attachment provides the Company load, losses, generation, energy purchases, and energy sales by component hourly for the years 2023, 2024, and 2025, and for the peak winter and summer days of those years, and for the peak load hour and the minimum load hour of those days for a total of twelve hours. The attachment contains a summary tab and each tab for the generation and costs of individual resources for each peak and valley hour along with other tabs containing other requested information.

Due to its voluminous nature, this information is being provided in electronic format only and has been redacted in its entirety for purposes of public disclosure. Redaction of the information would require an in-depth manual process and would be overly time consuming and unduly-burdensome, which would impede the Company’s timely response.
