Enclosure

LEGAL NOTICE

Georgia Power Company has filed with the Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) its proposed Fuel Cost Recovery Rate, FCR-27. This matter has been assigned Docket No. 56765 and has been scheduled for public hearing before the Commission following the Administrative Session at 9:30 a.m. on May 5, 2026. This hearing will be held in Room 110 of the Commission offices located at 244 Washington Street, SW, Atlanta, Georgia 30334-5701. Please contact the Commission’s Executive Secretary’s Office, at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only), to confirm the location of the hearing.

In accordance with O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59(c), persons wishing to intervene must file a Petition to Intervene with the Commission within thirty (30) days of the first publication of this notice.  

Pursuant to the Commission’s Alternative eFile instructions, any pre-filed testimony or statements by interveners must be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary by no later than 4:00 p.m. on April 9, 2026. All testimony must be pre-filed. Parties of record must also serve one another copies of any pre-filed testimony. Hearings on intervener testimony will be heard immediately following the direct testimony of the Commission’s Public Interest Advocacy Staff, which follows the direct testimony of Georgia Power Company.

If you have a disability and will need assistance or accommodation to participate, or need further information, please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only), at least one day prior to the hearing in this matter.

This notice is published at the direction of the Georgia Public Service Commission.


/s/ Jeremiah Haswell
Jeremiah Haswell
