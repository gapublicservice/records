COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these March 25, 2026.

Please provide a complete list of the non-fuel expenses charged to the fuel balance (to be recovered from customers through the FCR), such as natural gas firm transportation, natural gas interruptible transportation, capacity and energy costs related to power purchase agreements, RNR costs, payments to QFs, RFP costs, etc. Please provide the amount charged to the fuel balance for each listed non-fuel expense for 2025.
 
For power purchase agreements, are there any categories of costs included in the fuel balance (to be recovered from customers through the FCR) that are associated with PPAs other than 1) the cost of fuel (ie, the cost of gas, oil, uranium, etc) or 2) emission allowance costs?

For power purchase agreements, please confirm that fixed capacity costs are not recovered through the fuel balance (to be recovered from customers through the FCR).

Do RTP customers pay the cost of natural gas transportation (both firm and interruptible)?  

If you answered “yes,” to the question, “Do RTP customers pay the cost of natural gas transportation,” is that revenue credited to the fuel balance (to be recovered from customers through the FCR tariff)?   
Please provide values for the credits on a monthly basis (or however else maintained by the Company) for 2022 through 2025. 
Which segment of the RTP Pricing structure is designed to recover for natural gas transportation costs? 

Are unit commitment costs (i.e., fuel and variable operating and maintenance costs experienced when a unit is running out of economic dispatch) paid for by RTP customers?   


If you answered “yes,” to the question, “are unit commitment costs paid for by RTP customers,” is that revenue credited to the fuel balance (to be recovered from customers through the FCR tariff)?   
Please provide values for the credits on a monthly basis (or however else maintained by the Company) for 2022 through 2025. 
Which segment of the RTP Pricing structure is designed to recover for unit commitment costs?
 
Is it correct to say that bottom of the stack system lambda, as used in the calculation of RTP hourly costs, consists of forward-looking hourly estimates of fuel (aka “System Territorial Spot Fuel Lambda”), variable operations & maintenance (“O&M”) including fuel handling; and relevant emissions allowances?  

Please refer to STF-JKA-1-4(b)’s definition of the “hourly average marginal fuel cost,” which is given the shorthand name, “RTP Fuel Rate.” 
Please confirm that “RTP Fuel Rate” calculated the amount of RTP revenue that will offset the fuel balance (to be recovered from customers through the FCR). 
Please confirm that “BOS” refers to “Bottom of the Stack.” 
Is there any difference between BOS unit costs and Bottom of the Stack Lambda?  If so, please explain. 
How does the Company calculate BOS unit costs? 
 
How does usage of PPA energy relate to  “BOS unit costs,” as that term is used in STF-JKA-1-4(b)? 

Per the RTP rate schedule, RTP “Hourly prices are determined each day based on projections of the hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs), provisions for losses, projections of hourly transmission costs and reliability capacity costs for each day (when applicable), and a three (3) mill/kWh recovery factor.”  
Please describe the function of the provision for losses, transmission costs, reliability capacity costs, and recovery factor – what costs are they intended to recover and describe how each component is calculated.  
Are any of the revenues for “provisions of losses” attributed to the fuel balance (used determine the FCR rate)?   
Are any of the revenues for “projections of hourly transmission costs” attributed to the fuel balance (used determine the FCR rate)? 
Are any of the revenues for “reliability capacity costs” attributed to the fuel balance (used to determine the FCR rate)? 
Are any of the “three mill/kwh recovery factor” attributed to the fuel balance (used to determine the FCR rate)? 

Please provide actual hourly prices charged to RTP-DA and RTP-HA customers, including a breakdown of the costs for each pricing component (i.e. hourly running cost of incremental generation, hourly provisions for losses, hourly transmission costs, hourly reliability capacity costs, and hourly recovery factor charges) for the years 2022 to current. 
 
Please refer to the Company’s response to STF-JKA-1-7(d).  The Company explains that the difference between lambda (used for RTP price) and the allocated fuel revenue (from RTP revenues to offset the FCR fuel balance) covers two pieces: “generation variable O&M and capital substitution.” 
How does the Company calculate RTP’s contribution to “generation variable O&M”?  
How does the Company calculate RTP’s contribution to “capital substitution”? 
Please quantify the value of the difference between lambda (used for RTP price) and the allocated fuel revenue (from RTP revenues to the FCR fuel balance).  At a minimum, provide the value of lambda and the allocated fuel revenue on an hourly basis to supplement JKA-1-4, Attachment B. 

Please refer to the Company’s response to STF-JKA-1-7(b).  The Company explains that the “average hourly marginal fuel cost,” which is credited to the fuel account on behalf of RTP customers, “is based on all Georgia Power generating units running in each hour plus power pool purchases or deliveries in each hour.” 
Are PPAs included in “Georgia Power generating units”?  If not, please explain why not and how those costs are captured. 
 

What governs the amount of wholesale market opportunity sales that Georgia Power can make in any given hour? Can Georgia Power engage in sales in any given hour that reduces the unit capacity in that hour to a point below the reliability threshold established in the most recent IRP (forecasted load plus Target Reserve Margin)? 
Are the terms “wholesale market opportunity sales” and “economy sales synonymous” for Georgia Power?
