NATURAL GAS CONTRACTS
Summary

The Company’s gas supply contracts are not negotiated on a plant-
specific basis. Gas supply is purchased at specific supply regions
based on power plant locations and the pipeline transportation rights
associated with these supply regions. Southern Company Services
("SCS"), acting on behalf of Georgia Power, will procure natural gas
under a combination of both long-term base load and short-term spot
market purchases. The attached information provides a summary of
SCS’ gas supply commitments for the historical period.
