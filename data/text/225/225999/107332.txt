Dear Ms. Tanner:

Pursuant to the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (the “Commission’s”) rules regarding the Request for Proposals (“RFP”) Procedure for Long-Term Supply-Side Options (the “RFP Rules”), enclosed for filing are the original and five copies of Southern Power Company’s (“Southern Power’s”) Fifth Revised Bid Team List for use by the Independent Evaluator in the Phase 2 All Source Capacity RFP (for years 2032-2033) to be conducted by Georgia Power Company (“GPC”) in Docket No. 56368 (the “Phase 2 All Source Capacity RFP”).

The Fifth Revised Bid Team List reflects the addition of two (2) Bid Team members and the removal of one (1) Bid Team member.  The new Bid Team members have reviewed, executed and had notarized a compliance acknowledgement of the Standards of Conduct for Soliciting Entities and their Affiliates During the RFP Process (“RFP Standards of Conduct”) (implementing the Commission’s RFP Rules §§ 515-3-4-.04(3)(d) 3 through 5) that apply to RFP-related activities.  In addition, the Bid Team member leaving the Bid Team has executed an acknowledgement verifying compliance with the RFP Standards of Conduct while a member of the Bid Team.

Also enclosed are copies of the executed RFP Standards of Conduct acknowledgements and a CD containing the Fifth Revised Bid Team list.

If you have any questions or need any additional information, please contact me at (404)-506-5328.
