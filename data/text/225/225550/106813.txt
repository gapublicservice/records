ORDER DENYING GIPL, SOUTHFACE INSTITUTE, SIERRA CLUB, AND SOUTHERN ALLIANCE FOR CLEAN ENERGY’S JOINT PETITION FOR RECONSIDERATION.

On January 9, 2026, Georgia Interfaith Power and Light, Southface Energy Institute, the Sierra Club, and the Southern Alliance for Clean Energy (“Joint Petitioners” or “Petitioners”) filed a Joint Petition for Reconsideration (“Joint Petition” or “Petition”) of the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (“Commission”) December 30, 2025, Order Adopting Stipulation and Granting Georgia Power’s (“Georgia Power” or “the Company”) Certification of Capacity from 2029-2031 All-Source RFP and Supplemental Resources for 2028-2031 Capacity in Docket No. 56298 & 56310 (“Order”).  The Stipulation Agreement (“Stipulation” or “Stipulated Agreement”) was originally presented and recommended for adoption by Public Interest Advocacy Staff (“PIA Staff”) and Georgia Power (“Stipulating Parties”) on December 10, 2025, prior to the 2029-2031 All-Source Hearing (“Hearing”).  On January 20, 2026, the Stipulating Parties filed separate Reply Briefs that addressed Petitioners’ named issues.  
On February 3, 2026, the Commission conducted a limited hearing, based on the Chairman’s December 19, 2025, ruling pursuant to Rule 515-1-1-.02 and 515-3-2-.11(3), where Georgia Power and Joint Petitioners (“Parties”) gave arguments on whether the Joint Petitioners should be granted access to trade secret designated large load revenue requirement financial workpapers used in support of or otherwise relied on for the Stipulation, under an appropriate non-disclosure agreement (“NDA”).  Georgia Power put on a witness panel, which underwent questioning from the Parties. The Commission then heard closing arguments from the Parties.  At the conclusion of the arguments, the Commission held its decision on the matter until February 18th, 2026, Administrative Session.  
During the February 18, 2026, Administrative Session, Chairman Shaw motioned to deny the Joint Petitioner’s Motion to access certain trade secret designated financial paperwork under an appropriate non-disclosure agreement.  The Motion passed 3-2, with Commissioners Pridemore, Shaw, and McDonald voting for and Commissioners Hubbard and Johnson voting against. Given the Commission’s decision to deny the Motion for access to the documents, Commissioner Advisory Staff recommended that the Motion for Reconsideration be denied as set forth in its written recommendation. The Commission adopted the Advisory Staff recommendation, with Commissioners Pridemore, Shaw, and McDonald voting for and Commissioners Hubbard and Johnson voting against
******************************************************************************
WHEREFORE IT IS ORDERD, that the GIPL, Southface Institute, Sierra Club, and Southern Alliance for Clean Energy’s Joint Petition for Reconsideration is denied.
ORDERED FURTHER, that all findings, conclusions, statements, and directives made by the Commission and contained in the foregoing sections of the Order are hereby adopted as findings of fact, conclusions of law, statements of regulatory policy, and Orders of this Commission.
ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing, or oral argument or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of the Order, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.
ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over these matters is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order or Orders as this Commission may deem just and proper.
The above by action of the Commission at it Administrative Session on the 18th day of February 2026.
