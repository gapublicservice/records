          Dear Ms. Tanner,

                  Please file this letter in the above-referenced dockets for the purpose of clarifying the
          record in both matters.

                  At a Special Administrative Session of the Public Service Commission held on December
          19, 2025, for those dockets, Commissioner Shaw, in accordance with Commission Rule 515-1-1-
          .02(3), made an oral ruling as follows: “The Commission does, however, take the administrative
          notice of the information that Georgia Power has provided to Staff that supports the minimum
          large load revenue and the total estimated large load revenue requirement calculation used in
          determining the eight dollars and fifty cents downward pressure.”

                  In compliance with the Chairman’s order, the Commission Public Interest Advocacy Staff
          hereby represents that the following information was relied upon by Staff in making the
          calculations referred to by the Chairman:

       1. A spreadsheet provided by Georgia Power Company to Staff and filed with the
          Commission on October 1, 2025, in response to Staff Data Request STF-PIA-7-1,
          which requested: “Please provide, in an executable Excel format, the Company’s
          forecasted annual Revenue Requirement for the period of 2025-2040 without the
          inclusion of any new Large Load customers that started taking service as of January 1,
          2024 as well as forecasted new Large Load customers. New Large Load customers are
          defined as customers with a contract demand of at least 100 MW….” The spreadsheet
          lists Georgia Power’s estimated incremental revenues for large load customers for the
          years: 2026, 2027, 2028, 2029, and 2030, and estimated incremental revenue
          requirement for each Company-owned project and PPA, for which the Company
          requested certification in dockets 56298 and 56310.

       2. An Excel workbook provided by Georgia Power Company to Staff and filed with the
          Commission on November 12, 2025, in response to Staff Data Request STF-PIA-20-1,
          which requested: “Refer to the revenue forecast provided in the Attachment to STF-
          PIA-7-1. Provide the calculations, assumptions, and derivation for the revenue
          projection.” The workbook shows Georgia Power’s projected revenues and rates for
          106 large load customers and projects by quarter from 2026 through the end of 2030.

       3. A list, emailed by Attorney Steven Hewitson, counsel for Georgia Power, to Staff on
          December 9, 2025, of projected annual incremental large load revenues for 2029, 2030,
          and 2031, discounted by a uniform discount factor. A copy of the list was filed on
          December 9, 2025, as attachment A of the Stipulation between Georgia Power and Staff
          and subsequently adopted by the Commission.

       4. Draft large load contract and supporting workpapers showing estimated revenue
          requirement for servicing customer, termination payments, and minimum bill
          calculations provided by Georgia Power to Staff on September 9, 2025.

       5. Draft large load contract and supporting workpapers showing estimated revenue
          requirement for servicing customer, termination payments, and minimum bill
          calculations provided by Georgia Power to Staff on September 16, 2025.

       6. Draft large load contract and supporting workpapers showing estimated revenue
          requirement for servicing customer, termination payments, and minimum bill
          calculations provided by Georgia Power to Staff on September 23, 2025.

       7. Draft large load contract and supporting workpapers showing estimated revenue
          requirement for servicing customer, termination payments, and minimum bill
          calculations provided by Georgia Power to Staff on November 26, 2025.

Pursuant to Chairman Shaw’s Order, the aforementioned documents are part of the official record
in dockets 56298 and 56310 in their unredacted form and should be included as part of the official
record in those proceedings.

An electronic version of this filing has been served to all parties in those dockets.
