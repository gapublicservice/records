BACKGROUND
On December 17th, 2025 Georgia Interfaith Power & Light (“GIPL”) and Southface Institute (“Southface,” collectively the “Petitioners”) filed a Petition to Supplement the Record and for Limited Hearing Concerning the Stipulation entered into these dockets by Public Interest Advocacy Staff (“Staff”) and Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company). Pursuant to Rule 515-3-1-1.11(3), Petitioners requested review of the large load revenue requirement financial workpapers used in support of or otherwise relied on for the Stipulation by the Parties with Trade Secret agreements in place. On December 18th, 2025, Georgia Power filed a letter in response to the Petition. 
On December 19, 2025, during the Special Administrative Session regarding these dockets, the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) granted Petitioner’s request to a hearing specifically on the Petitioner’s request to seek review of large load revenue requirement financial workpapers by Parties with Trade Secret agreements in place. Petitioners’ other requests to supplement the record and hold a supplemental hearing were denied. 
SCHEDULING ORDER
The Hearing in the above-referenced Petition is hereby scheduled in the main hearing room located at 244 Washington Street, S.W., Atlanta, Georgia 30334, at 9:30a.m. on Tuesday, February 3rd, 2026, immediately following the Commission’s regularly scheduled Administrative Session. 
To the extent parties intend to present witnesses at the hearing, witnesses may present their testimony verbally and will be subject to cross-examination. As Georgia Power has the burden of proving that the information constitutes a trade secret, Georgia Power shall present its witnesses first. Following the presentation of any witnesses, the Commission shall hear oral argument.  Argument shall be no longer than 15 minutes per side unless otherwise ordered by the Chair. Georgia Power shall present its oral argument first and may reserve up to five minutes of its time for response.
At the conclusion of the Hearing, the Commission will proceed to a Special Administrative Session for consideration of decision on this matter.
LEGAL AUTHORITY FOR HEARING
This hearing will be held under the authority and jurisdiction conferred upon the Commission by O.C.G.A. §§ 46-2-50-59, 46-3-13, and 50-13-13, 10-1-760 and Commission Trade Secret Rule 515-3-1-.11.

MATTER AT ISSUE
The Petitioners ask that the Parties with Trade Secret agreements in place be provided a copy of the large load revenue requirement financial workpapers used in support of or otherwise relied on for the Stipulation in the case. 
As the Commission has ruled the record supporting its decision to adopt the Stipulation to be complete, this hearing will not be considering whether the record in this docket should be further supplemented with the additional financial workpapers. However, regardless of the outcome of this hearing, the Commission takes administrative notice of the information that the Company has provided to Staff that supports the minimum large load revenue and the total estimated large load revenue requirement calculation used in degerming the $8.50 of “downward pressure.”
