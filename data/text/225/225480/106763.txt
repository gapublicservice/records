 APPLICATION OF NRDC, THE SIERRA CLUB AND THE SOUTHERN ALLIANCE
            FOR CLEAN ENERGY FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE


       COME NOW, Natural Resources Defense Council (“NRDC”), the Sierra Club and the

Southern Alliance for Clean Energy (“SACE”) pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59

and Commission Rule 515-2-1-.06, and hereby file this Application for Leave to Intervene

(“Application”) before the Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) in the

above-referenced docket. In support hereof, NRDC, Sierra Club and SACE respectfully show as

follows:

       Sierra Club is the nation’s oldest and largest grassroots environmental organization, with

over 600,000 members across the country, including nearly 9,000 in Georgia. Sierra Club’s

mission is to explore, enjoy, and protect the wild places of the Earth; to practice and promote the

responsible use of the Earth's resources and ecosystems; to educate and enlist humanity to

protect and restore the quality of the natural and human environment; and to use all lawful means

to carry out these objectives. As part of this mission, Sierra Club works on behalf of its members

to help transition the power generation industry towards a clean energy economy. Additionally,

Sierra Club members purchase electricity from Georgia Power Company. Their consumption,

costs, terms of service for purchase of electricity, and the resources that will ultimately serve

them may be affected by the Commission's actions in these dockets. As a result, Sierra Club has

substantial interests in the outcome of this proceeding and desires to intervene in order to protect

those interests.

        SACE is a regional nonprofit with over 3,600 members in the State of Georgia. SACE

also has staff across Georgia. SACE's purposes include education and research concerning the

environment, public health, and economic impacts of energy use and policy in the Southeast.

Additionally, SACE’s members and staff are ratepayers of Georgia Power Company. The

Commission's actions in this proceeding will affect ratepayers and their electric bills. Therefore,

SACE has substantial interest in the outcome of the proceeding, and respectfully requests to

participate.

        NRDC is a national nonprofit organization with more than 3 million members and online

supporters, including several thousand in Georgia. NRDC advocates for policies that protect

consumers, promote just and reasonable utility rates, and support a clean, reliable, and

economically efficient energy system. NRDC has staff based in Georgia working on energy,

climate, and regulatory matters. NRDC has a direct and substantial interest in this proceeding.

NRDC’s members in Georgia are utility customers whose rates and service are affected by fuel

cost recovery decisions. In addition, NRDC’s Georgia-based staff regularly participate in matters

concerning utility regulation, energy policy, and consumer protection. These member and

organizational interests may be affected by the outcome of this docket.On February 17, 2026,

Georgia Power filed testimony and supporting documentation in Docket No. 56765. On January

21, 2026, the Commission issued its Procedural and Scheduling Order (“PSO”) in the

above-referenced docket. Pursuant to that order, NRDC, Sierra Club and SACE timely file this

Application.

       The PSO also laid out the following requirements:

               In addition to the requirements prescribed by O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59 for applications
               for leave to intervene, the application must (1) identify other intervening parties
               or intervening party applications whose interest is similar to that of the applicant,
               along with an explanation of why the identified intervening party or intervening
               party applicant will not adequately represent the applicant’s interest; and (2) state
               the applicant’s present intention to submit direct testimony and by whom and on
               what subject. The requirements identified herein shall constitute a continuing
               obligation of the applicant or intervening party.
PSO at 4. In response to the PSO’s requirements, NRDC, Sierra Club and SACE have verified

that there are currently no other intervenors with sufficiently similar interests that would

adequately represent Sierra Club and SACE’s diverse and unique interests. NRDC, Sierra Club

and SACE do not anticipate that other parties would adequately represent their interests in the

responsible transition away from fossil fuel-based electric generating plants towards meeting

electricity demand with clean energy, and adequate energy planning for both economic interests

and public health. NRDC, Sierra Club and SACE intend to submit direct testimony from

GridStrategies in this case on fuel costs and economic dispatch. NRDC, Sierra Club and SACE’s

intervention will not delay the proceedings or prejudice the rights of other parties.
