COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these February 19, 2026.

General

Please provide electronically, with all formulas intact, monthly operations reports that the Company files each month with the Commission, covering the period of January 1, 2023 through the most recent month that data exists.  
Please provide any audits that were conducted by any party internal or external to GPC for any purpose relating to fuel procurement, IIC bills, system dispatch, wholesale transactions, or market purchases.  Audits that should be provided are those that were available to the Company between January 1, 2023 and the most recent month that data exists.  
Has the Company introduced any new cost categories in its FCR accounting?  For any new categories that have been added, please provide a narrative description of the category, the rationale for introducing the new category, and identify the specific costs by each new category that have been added.
RTP Customer Impacts

Regarding the Company’s tariffs that include a real-time-pricing (RTP) component in the forecasted or billed customer rate.
Please identify all current tariffs that the Company has that rely on RTP for billing.  
Concerning the historic period of FCR-27, please explain how the actual RTP price was derived and how fuel costs were assessed and assigned to RTP tariff customers. 
For the peak hours of the summer and winter periods of the year 2025, provide workpapers showing how the hourly RTP prices for the two hours were derived. 
Please explain how the Company’s RTP customer revenues were included in the FCR balance over the historic period. 
Provide a roadmap that reconciles the hourly RTP revenues against the hourly incurred System cost.  At a minimum, as part of this reconciliation, provide the following data for the 2025 summer and winter peak months and by schedule (RTPHA vs RTPDA): the hourly RTP rate by schedule, the hourly RTP loads, the System hourly load, and System hourly average fuel cost, and System hourly marginal cost.  
For the specific summer and winter projected peak hours of 2027, explain how the Company’s projected RTP price was derived and how fuel costs were determined that will be assessed to RTP customers and accounted for in determining the FCR balance. Provide all workpapers used to determine the RTP prices and fuel costs included in the FCR balance.

Refer to the current RTP-HA-12 rate tariff, which states, “Customers are notified each day of forecasted electricity prices for each hour of the following day, then prices are updated each hour, sixty minutes before becoming effective. Prices are based on projections of the hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs), provisions for losses, projections of hourly transmission costs and reliability capacity costs for each day (when applicable), and a two (2) mill/kWh recovery factor.”  
How are the forecasted prices for RTP-HA-12 derived on a day ahead basis? 
Provide all workpapers, memos, and formula documentation used to derive the prices for the same two historic peak winter and summer hours of 2025 as discussed in the prior question.    
How are the prices for RTP-HA-12 prices derived on a 60 minute ahead basis? 
Provide all workpapers, memos, and formula documentation used to derive the prices for the same two historic peak winter and summer hours of 2025 as discussed in the prior question.    
Explain the purpose of the 2 mill/kWh recovery factor and how it was derived. 

Refer to the current RTP-DA-12 rate tariff, which states, “Hourly prices are determined each day based on projections of the hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs), provisions for losses, projections of hourly transmission costs and reliability capacity costs for each day (when applicable), and a three (3) mill/kWh recovery factor. The amount of fuel charges from hourly incremental kWh usage are applied to the recovery of fuel cost at the hourly average marginal fuel cost for the applicable hour”
How are the prices for RTP-DA-12 prices derived each day? Provide all workpapers, memos, and formula documentation used to derive the prices and how the prices are turned into rates.  This should focus on the same two historic peak winter and summer hours of 2025 as discussed in the prior question.    
Explain the purpose of the 3 mill/kWh recovery factor and how it was derived.
 
For RTP customers who have a Customer Base Line (CBL) and an RTP billing component:
Since 2015, please provide a timeline describing changes to the CBL and RTP billing process, derivation of rates, and/or eligibility.  
How does the Company verify that RTP customers pay a fair amount for the fuel costs the Company incurs in order to serve their load?  
Provide any memos, reports, calculations, and any other documentation that exists that supports the reasonableness of the approach that the Company uses.
Explain how RTP customers are assessed for costs such as firm transportation costs that are fixed and not necessarily reflected in marginal cost rates.  How does the Company validate that the revenues collected cover the costs incurred to serve their load.
