                 Georgia Power Company; Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P.; RC Kettle Holdco, LLC; and

                              between Georgia Power Company and Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P.

           Georgia Power Company, a Georgia corporation (“GPC”); Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P., a Delaware limited partnership
           (“Counterparty”); RC Kettle Holdco, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Parent”); and Harbert Azalea
           Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Purchaser”), enter into this Change of Control Consent and
           Amendment Agreement (“Consent and Amendment”) effective as of the date signed by GPC as shown on the
           signature page when fully executed (“Effective Date”). GPC, Counterparty, Parent, and Purchaser may sometimes
           be referred to herein individually as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties”.
                GPC and Counterparty entered into that certain Power Purchase Agreement for Firm Capacity, Firm Energy,
                    and Ancillary Services from a Combined Cycle Facility, dated as of July 2, 2025 (“PPA”), under which GPC
                    has contracted to purchase, and Counterparty has contracted to sell, electricity generated from the Facility,
                    located in 200 Oaky Woods Rd., Kathleen, GA 31047, consisting of a total capability of approximately 317
                    megawatts of electric power;
                Counterparty and GPC also entered into that certain Negotiated Contract for the Purchase of Firm Capacity
                    and Energy from a Qualifying Facility entered on August 7, 1995 (incorporated by this reference and, along
                    with all attached or referenced exhibits, attachments, appendices, or other agreement documents, and any
                    amendment/modification, referenced collectively as the “IA”), for the purpose of interconnecting Counterparty’s
                    Facility with the Transmission System;
                Counterparty is contemplating undergoing a Change of Control Transaction resulting from an acquisition of all
                    of the issued and outstanding membership interests of SEPG MGA Holdings, LLC, the upstream parent entity
                    of Counterparty, by Purchaser (the “Change of Control Transaction”), subject to GPC’s consent; and
                GPC is willing to consent to the Change of Control Transaction as provided in this Consent and Amendment.
           The Parties, in consideration of the mutual representations, warranties, and covenants expressed in this Consent and
           Amendment, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt, adequacy, and sufficiency of which each Party
           acknowledges, and intending to be legally bound, agree:

           PART 1      REPRESENTATIONS, WARRANTIES, AND CONSENT
           1.1       Mutual Representations and Warranties. Each Party represents and warrants to the other Parties that: (i) it
           is validly organized under the laws of the state of its organization and has full authority to execute, deliver, and
           perform this Consent and Amendment; (ii) its execution, delivery, and performance of this Consent and Amendment
           have been authorized by all necessary organization action and do not contravene its organizational documents; and
           (iii) it has validly executed and delivered this Consent and Amendment.
           1.2    Insurance. Counterparty and Purchaser each represents and warrants that the insurance policies providing the
           coverage required by Section 13 of the PPA and Section 13 of the IA will be replaced with insurance policy coverages
           in form, substance and amount in compliance with the PPA, and returned with no gap in adequacy or sufficiency of
           such insurance policy coverages.
           1.3  GPC Consent. In consideration of the representations, warranties, covenants, and agreements made by
           #Counterparty# and Purchaser, GPC consents to the Change of Control Transaction.

           PART 2      AGREEMENT
           2.1    Closing. The closing of the Change of Control Transaction (“Closing”) shall occur after the Effective Date.
           2.2   Performance Security. The Eligible Collateral, currently in the form of three Letters of Credit held by GPC in
           the aggregate amount of $54,100,000, as required by the PPA and IA, will be remain in full force and effect following
           the Change of Control Transaction.

July 15, 2026
Ms. Sallie TannerExecutive SecretaryGeorgia Public Service Commission244 Washington Street, S.W.Atlanta, GA 30334-5701
Re:
Georgia Power Company Consent to Change of Control for Mid-Georgia Cogen PPA and QF Contract, Docket No. 56298 
 
Dear Ms. Tanner: 
Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or “GPC”) and Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P. are parties that certain Power Purchase Agreement for Firm Capacity, Firm Energy, and Ancillary Services from a Combined Cycle Facility, dated as of July 2, 2025, which was certified by the Commission in Docket No. 56298 (“PPA”). In addition, Georgia Power and Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P. are parties to that certain Negotiated Contracts for the Purchase of Firm Capacity and Energy From a Qualifying Facility entered on August 7. 1995 (“QF Contract”). Both contracts relate to the 310 MW cogeneration facility located in Kathleen, Georgia. As detailed further below, Georgia Power hereby requests approval by the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) of Georgia Power’s consent to Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P.’s proposed change of control transaction and corresponding amendments to both contracts (“Consent and Amendment”). 
As proposed, the change of control transaction would result from the acquisition of all of the issued and outstanding membership interests of SEPG MGA Holdings, LLC, the upstream parent entity of Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P, by Harbert Azalea Holdings, LLC. Harbert Azalea Holdings, LLC is a subsidiary of Harbert Power, which is the power and infrastructure investment firm of Harbert Management Corporation (“HMC”), a private alternative asset manager in the United States and Canada. Collectively, Harbert Power and HMC own and manage a diversified generation portfolio of renewable and dispatchable resources. Georgia Power maintains an existing relationship with Harbert as a counterparty to several existing PPAs. Harbert has expressed to Georgia Power its intent to maintain continuity of the operations of the Mid-Georgia Cogen Facility and to fulfill the plant’s current contractual obligations under the QF Contract and future contractual obligations under the PPA.
The QF Contract and PPA require Mid-Georgia Cogen to secure Georgia Power’s prior written consent for any change of control transaction. Georgia Power has conditioned its consent upon the Commission’s approval of such consent to Mid-Georgia Cogen L.P.’s request.
Georgia Power has negotiated the appropriate Consent and Amendment to provide Georgia Power’s consent to the proposed change of control transaction and has provided a copy of the document to Commission Staff. Accordingly, Georgia Power hereby requests the Commission’s approval to consent to the Mid-Georgia Cogen change of control transaction and to execute the Consent and Amendment document.
