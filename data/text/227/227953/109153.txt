Dear Ms. Tanner:

Enclosed for filing in the above-referenced proceeding is Georgia Power Company’s (“Georgia Power” or “Company”) Monthly Bad Debt Report (“Report”), reflecting data for July 2026. This Report includes the number of charge-offs, the number of installment plan enrollments, PrePay status, and total disconnections for nonpayment. This information along with some other data points requested by Commission Staff are provided in the Report. Importantly, the totals arrears should not be confused with those eligible for disconnection under the Commission’s disconnection rule. For example, 30+ day arrears do not alone qualify a customer for disconnection under the Commission rule. In addition, please note that the information provided in the report represents data through each respective month. Data will change on a regular basis between monthly filings.
