===== STF-2-1.docx =====
STF-2-1

Question: 

Please provide copies of all written communications, including all email messages and attachments addressed to or from Southern Company Services Director of Generation Procurement, Kristin Kurylo, relating to Georgia Power Company’s estimate that “between 2,000-6,000 MW of Capacity Resources are necessary to satisfy GPC’s capacity needs,” as stated on page 17 of the Company Revised Final Draft 2026 All-Source Capacity Request for Proposals for Need Years 2032-2033 filed with the Commission on May 27, 2026.

Response:

This data request is overly broad, unduly burdensome, and seeks information without limitation that is not relevant to issuing or conducting the 2026 All-Source Capacity Request for Proposals (“RFP”) for Need Years 2032-2033 approved by the Commission in Docket No. 56368. In addition, documents responsive to the request would include information protected by attorney-client privilege or the attorney workproduct doctrine. Moreover, the RFP Documents, including the referenced statement, have been approved for issuance by the Commission. 

Subject to and not waiving the foregoing, the Company responds that written communications between the Company and Commission Staff addressing the statement “between 2,000-6,000 MW of Capacity Resources are necessary to satisfy GPC’s capacity needs” are included in the 2026 All-Source Capacity RFP for Need Years 2032-2033. As stated in the RFP Documents, this estimated capacity target is based on expiration of capacity power purchase agreements, potential retirement of certain Georgia Power generation units, and projected load growth. The analysis supporting this statement was previously provided to Commission Staff on April 16, 2026. These written materials are also provided in response to STF-2-5. Ms. Curylo, in her role as Southern Company Services Director of Generation Procurement, has reviewed the written materials provided in STF-2-5 and confirms that these are the written communications conveyed to Commission Staff and the Independent Evaluator to support the analysis underlying the statement cited in STF-2-1.

In providing the supporting documents to Staff, the Company included the following explanation:

Based on a current system needs assessment, the Q4 2025 Large Load Economic Development Report project pipeline, recent large load contracting developments, and further anticipated large load contracting developments, the capacity requirement for the 2032–2033 delivery period is expected to be between 2,000-6,000 MW depending on the capacity need year. This range will appropriately signal the Company’s capacity needs to the market and may help inform bidding strategy to RFP Participants. The Company intends to include this range in the RFP Documents filed with the Commission and in the RFP ultimately issued to the market.  

The indicated range serves as a target and may be adjusted prior to short list determination and certification. As you are aware, the final capacity portfolio must go through a certification process for approval by the Commission. At the time Georgia Power files for certification, the then-current load forecast, capacity needs, and other relevant planning considerations will inform the portfolio of capacity resources ultimately put forth for Commission certification.    
  
We would like to schedule a meeting for the week of April 20th to discuss the documentation supporting the capacity target and would be happy to receive your questions in advance of that time so that we can use our time together most efficiently.
