STF-PIA-21-2

Question: 

Please refer to the project marked with an asterisk in the load change tab in the Q1 2026 large load report. The Company said, “This project was previously reported as a single project in the Large Load Economic Development Report. Based on differing characteristics among its components, the Company is now splitting the project into two.” Please describe the differing characteristics that caused the Company to make the change.

Response:

The project was separated into two components to reflect the differing contractual commitments associated with each portion. The first half of the customer’s request is supported by a fully executed Contract for Electric Service (“CES”). The remaining half is currently associated with a Request for Service (“RFS”).
Although this change does not significantly impact the risk adjusted load for this project, it better reflects the contractual status of each project phase in the forecast. CES and RFS represent different levels of contractual commitment and, therefore, are subject to different forecasting assumptions and probabilities within the Company’s Load Realization Model.

STF-PIA-21-1

Question: 

Please refer to data response PIA-20-6 in Docket No. 55378. There was one project removed after the transmission study and timeline review. Was the removal the Company’s decision or the customer’s? Please explain.

Response:

The project in question was a data center developer who grew unresponsive due to challenges in attracting a data center operator for their site. The project is no longer marketing the site for data center use. Given the change in project plans and lack of responsiveness, the Company decided to withdraw the project from the Large Load Pipeline. Withdrawals like this, although uncommon, are contemplated in the Company’s P2 methodology for projects having progressed through timeline review.
