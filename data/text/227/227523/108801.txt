              Appendix
              GPC Charge It - Electric Vehicle Charger Rider "CIEV-1"


Georgia Power Company's 2022 Rate Case Order; Paragraph 25
"The company agrees to file quarterly reports regarding the location, peak demand, usage, and revenue of the Charge It Electric Vehicle Rider"

Defined Terms

(1) Customer Count
Active customers on the CIEV-1 Rider during the reportable month. Quarterly rollups will report this number as current customer count according to the most recent reportable month.

(2) Bills without Charge It
Billing is calculated with the Charge It rider removed according to the customer's current tariff. Bills do not include sales tax.

(3) Bills with Charge It
Billing is calculated with the Charge It rider added according to the customer's current tariff. Bills do not include sales tax.

(4) Savings with Charge It
This calculation is a function of (2)-(3)

(5) % Savings
This calculation is a function of (4)/(2)

(6) Avg of Energy Usage (kWh)
The Average monthly energy usage of all customers in that month.

(7) Avg of Billing Demand (kW) without Charge It
The monthly billing demand will be calculated according to the applicable PL tariff. That billing demand is not further adjusted and is representative of demand that would occur on a stand alone PL tariff. It is the average monthly demand for all customers in that month.

(8) Avg of Billing Demand (kW) with Charge It
The monthly billing demand will be calculated according to the applicable PL tariff. That billing demand will then be adjusted according to the CIEV schedule. It is the average adjusted monthly billing demand for all customers in that month.

(9) Location
A count of current Charge It customer accounts by city.

(10) Billing Demand Adjustments
The monthly billing demand will be calculated according to the applicable PL tariff. That billing demand will then be adjusted according to the CIEV schedule.
The billing demand as modified by this rider shall be the product of the billing demand as determined by the applicable rate and the Bill Demand Adjustment Factor (BDAF).

(11) Bill Demand Adjustment Factor (BDAF) Schedule
Year 1 (bills 1-12) BDAF: 0.20                Year 3 (bills 25-36) BDAF: 0.60
Year 2 (bills 13-24) BDAF: 0.40               Year 4 (bills 37-48) BDAF: 0.80
