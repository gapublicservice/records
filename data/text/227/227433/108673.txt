STF-ASR-12-1

Question: 

Uncertain Tax Positions.

a. Did Georgia Power Company file Schedule UTP to report uncertain tax positions with its 2024 federal income tax return? If not, explain fully why not. If so, please provide a copy of the filed Schedule UTP.
b. Did Georgia Power Company file Form 3115 to report accounting changes with its 2024 federal income tax return? If not, explain fully why not. If so, please provide a copy of the filed Form 3115.
c. Does Georgia Power Company plan to file Schedule UTP to report uncertain tax positions with its 2025 federal income tax return? If not, explain fully why not. If so, please provide a copy of the draft Schedule UTP.
d. Does Georgia Power Company plan to file Form 3115 to report accounting changes with its 2025 federal income tax return? If not, explain fully why not. If so, please provide a copy of the draft Form 3115.

Response:

a. The Company did not file Schedule UTP with its 2024 federal income tax return because the Company did not have any uncertain tax positions required to be disclosed on Schedule UTP. As part of Southern Company's participation in the Internal Revenue Service’s (“IRS”) Compliance Assurance Process (“CAP”), significant federal income tax issues are generally identified, reviewed, and resolved with the IRS on a real-time basis before the federal income tax return is filed. As a result, the Company did not have any unresolved federal income tax positions for the 2024 tax year at the time the 2024 tax return was filed (in the fall of 2025) that required disclosure on Schedule UTP. 

b. The Company did not file Form 3115 with its 2024 federal income tax return because the Company had no accounting method changes.

c. The Company does not plan to file Schedule UTP with its 2025 federal income tax return since the Company expects to resolve any uncertain positions with the IRS during the CAP review before the 2025 tax return is filed (in the fall of 2026).

d. The Company does not plan to file Form 3115 with its 2025 federal income tax return because the Company does not expect to have any accounting method changes.
