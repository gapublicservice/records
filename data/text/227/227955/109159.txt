RE:  Notice to Commission of Transfer to Affiliates, Docket No. 9355 
Dear Ms. Tanner:
Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) hereby provides notice to the Georgia Public Service Commission pursuant to the Affiliate Transaction Standards adopted in Docket No. 9355-U of Georgia Power’s proposed purchase of certain technology equipment from Alabama Power Company (“APC”), an affiliated company, having a net book value of $2,705,393. Georgia Power expects to complete the transaction following expiration of the required 60-day notice period. Commission approval is not required.
The equipment being transferred consists of GridNet routers, switches, and related network hardware used to support network operations. APC currently owns the equipment, and Georgia Power proposes to acquire the assets for use in its network infrastructure.
Georgia Power intends to use the equipment to support and enhance communications systems that facilitate grid operations. Acquiring these existing system assets will provide reliable, proven technology needed to meet operational requirements while maximizing the remaining useful life of the equipment. The transaction will support operational resiliency and avoid costs that would otherwise be incurred to procure comparable new equipment.
Based on Georgia Power’s analysis, the fair market value of the equipment is higher than the net book value. Therefore, APC and Georgia Power have agreed to transfer the equipment to Georgia Power at APC’s net book value. Because the transfer will occur at net book value and is based on APC’s cost, the transaction is consistent with the Affiliate Transaction Standards adopted by the Commission in Docket No. 9355-U. The proposed purchase price is fair and reasonable and will enable Georgia Power to obtain needed equipment in a cost-effective manner.
For the reasons described above, the transaction will allow Georgia Power to utilize existing system assets in a cost-effective manner while supporting reliable and resilient grid operations. Accordingly, the transaction is consistent with the Affiliate Transaction Standards, is in the public interest, and will support Georgia Power’s continued delivery of clean, safe, reliable, and affordable energy to its customers.
