STF-ASR-13-1

Question: 

Vogtle property tax over-assessment.

a. Has the Georgia Department of Revenue recently lowered the taxable value of Georgia Power’s Plant Vogtle property in Burke County for property tax assessment purposes?
b. If the answer to part a is yes, please identify the provide the corrected Vogtle property tax assessments for each year, and indicate the amount of assessment reduction in each year, and also indicate the resulting impact on the amount of property taxes that would theoretically be reduced if Georgia Power’s payments for Butler County property taxes on Vogtle units 3 and 4 in each year had been based on the corrected assessments.
c. A recent news article reported that, on June 29, the Burke County Tax Assessor stated the county had overstated Plant Vogtle’s taxable value by $7.7 billion for 2023, $6.4 billion for 2024, and $2.7 billion for 2025. Based on Georgia Power’s knowledge, are these amounts accurate? If not, please fully explain and provide the property tax overstatement amounts for each year that Georgia Power believes are correct.
d. Identify the amounts of Butler County property tax expense recorded by Georgia Power for Plant Vogtle units 3 and 4 in each year showing the dollar amounts recorded in each account.
e. Identify what the comparative amounts of Butler County property tax expense for Plant Vogtle units 3 and 4 in each year would have been had the corrected assessment amounts been used. Please also show the dollar correct amounts that would have been recorded in each FERC USOA expense account.
f. Has Georgia Power announced that it will not seek changes or property tax refunds from Butler County for prior years? If yes, explain the analysis upon which that announcement was based.
g. Which specific property tax years and what amounts of forgone property tax refunds for each year are affected by those Company announcements?
h. How will the 2025 reduction affect Georgia Power’s property tax expense for Vogtle units 3 and 4 in the year 2025, and separately in the year 2026? Explain fully and show calculations of the estimated impacts for each year.
i. Please identify and provide the original pre-correction Butler County property tax assessments applicable to Vogtle 3 and 4 for each year for which corrections have been determined by the Georgia Department of Revenue.
j. Please identify and provide the Georgia Department of Revenue corrected Butler County property tax assessments applicable to Vogtle 3 and 4 for each year for which corrections have been determined by the Georgia Department of Revenue.
k. Please identify all communications Georgia Power has received from the Burke County Tax Assessor concerning alleged overstatements of Plant Vogtle’s taxable value for tax years 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. For each communication, provide the date, and summary of the content.
l. Identify and describe all asset categories (Units generation assets, transmission, substation, or site facilities) included in the Georgia Department of Revenue’s $2.8B reassessment.
m. Provide a narrative description of Georgia Power’s methodology for determining the fair market value of Plant Vogtle for property tax purposes. Identify any differences between Georgia Power’s methodology and Burke County’s valuation practices that may have contributed to any discrepancies.
n. Provide all correspondence between Georgia Power and the Burke County Tax Assessor from 2022 through 2026 related to Plant Vogtle valuation, including any notices of assessment, digest corrections, objections, or appeals.

Response:

The Georgia Department of Revenue (“GDOR”) has not yet issued its 2026 property tax assessment. The Company filed its 2026 property tax return indicating a reduction in the taxable value for Burke County as compared to the 2025 return, and the GDOR communicated the upcoming change to Burke County. The GDOR has not changed any prior year assessments.

Please see the response to subpart (a) above.

The amounts reported in the cited news article are historical capital costs that were duplicated as both CWIP and Plant in Service costs for Plant Vogtle Units 3 & 4 for property tax valuation purposes. While the general ledger balance was correct, the duplicate costs were included in the capital project balances used to determine taxable costs for the property tax calculation of Plant Vogtle Units 3 & 4. 

The Company is assessed as a single unit for all property in the state, and the GDOR has stated the unit valuations for tax years 2024 and 2025 are reasonable. Therefore, the total 2024 and 2025 property tax returns (corresponding with taxable amounts as of December 31, 2023, and December 31, 2024, respectively) are final and will not be adjusted.

The GDOR has not performed any property tax reassessment for prior years associated with the issue identified in the response to subpart (c) above.  

Please see the response to subpart (c) above for the reason for the overstated taxable value for Vogtle Units 3 and 4. 

The Company is centrally assessed by the GDOR as a unit for all of the Company’s property located in Georgia. In determining the fair market value of the Company’s property as a whole, the GDOR applies standard property tax appraisal methodologies, including the income approach, cost approach, and sales or market approach. The GDOR has discretion to determine the weighting assigned to each valuation approach, the applicable capitalization rates, and other factors that may affect the final unit value. Because there are typically no true sales of comparable properties, the cost and income approaches are generally the primary approaches used by both GDOR and the Company.

Counties do not value utility property. It is handled at the state level by GDOR since it is centrally assessed.

The Company is not aware of any direct correspondence with Burke County regarding valuation other than annual assessment notices from Burke County. Please see STF-ASR-13-1 Attachments A through D.
