COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before August 3, 2026.

Please provide a copy of the presentations and supplemental materials referenced at the July 28, 2026 RTP Technical Conference.

Refer to the July 14th data production and the forecasted B2026_EBUD_GPC RTP Fuel Credit Resource Detail files provided.  
Provide the data for a projected period of 10 years (2026-2036)
Provide the Incremental RTP, GPC, and SoCo system load for each hour of the full 10 year forecast period.  If forecasted RTP load is not available hourly, please provide it monthly.

Refer to the July 14th settlement data production for the historic period.  For each hour of the historic period, please provide the MWh and costs by GPC unit assumed to serve associated interchange energy (“AIE”) sales and pool opportunity interchange energy (“OIE”) sales.  

Please explain how interchange energy purchases (AIE and OIE) are priced and included in the FCR balance and compare that to how interchange energy sales to the pool are priced and included in the FCR balance. Please describe the impacts of Variable O&M, emissions, and marginal fuel price.  Please describe the treatment of any margins computed for both AIE and OIE.

Please explain how margins on OIE sales are treated in the FCR balance. To the extent there is any sharing between the FCR and rate base, provide all calculations on the historic period (2023-2025) describing the allocation process.

Refer to the Company’s response to STF-JKA-10-1 in Docket 56765, which sought information about costs in the FCR balance that are not reflected in the dispatch protocols, average marginal fuel costs (variable), and costs in the FCR balance that are considered “fixed” like FT costs. 
For each component of fixed costs that are not included in the settlement data used to derive the RTP fuel credit, please identify the fixed cost components and estimate the amount of the fixed cost components included in the FCR balance by month of the historic period.
For each component of fixed costs that are not included in the settlement data used to develop the dispatch costs used to derive system Lambda, please identify the fixed cost components and estimate the amount of the fixed cost components included in the FCR balance by month of the historic period.

Regarding Variable O&M costs.
Please explain how the Company currently forecasts and recovers variable O&M costs. Please explain which variable O&M costs are collected in base rates and which in FCR rates.  
Why are certain variable O&M costs not included in the FCR?
Please confirm that variable O&M costs are captured in the Company’s dispatch process and RTP rates charged to customers. 

Regarding GPC Labor and Company Overhead
Please confirm that the RTP fuel credit does not include any allocation of labor and overhead costs.
Please confirm that the FCR balance does include an allocation of labor and overhead costs.
For the FCR balance, how much GPC labor and Company overhead was included in the historic 2023-2025 period? Provide a component breakdown of such costs by month and description of different cost centers, purposes, or personnel group if possible.  
For the FCR balance, please provide the monthly labor and overhead costs included in the projected period by month.  
Provide a description of which lines in “MFRP-1 FCR-27 Budget TS.xls” include these GPC labor and overhead costs.

Please describe the Company’s hydro dispatch process. Please explain if the Company pre-determines a schedule based on unit commitment and/or if the Company uses any real-time shadow price costs to determine hourly dispatch.  Provide any documentation or memos describing the methodologies used.

Refer to the Company’s TOU-SC MAC charge tariff rates.
Please confirm that a portion of revenues collected via the TOU-SC MAC tariff will be assigned to fuel cost recovery.  If not, please explain.
Please confirm that the portion of revenues assigned to fuel cost recovery from TOU-SC MAC customers will be based on the hourly RTP fuel credit methodology consistent with existing RTP customers (e.g. actual hourly usage and the RTP fuel credit rate hourly $/MWh will be used to derive the allocated dollars). If so, please identify the amounts estimated for TOU-SC MAC customers to contribute to fuel balance in the projected period (and historic period, if applicable).  
Please explain how customers under the “Flat Bill” option will contribute fuel cost recovery revenues and the process by which that amount is derived and assigned to fuel currently.  Please provide an example calculation.

 Based on the historical period data, please provide the MWhs and associated RTP customer charges associated with load above the customers CBL (incremental costs) and those credits associated with load below the customers CBL (incremental credits) deriving a net effect by month.

In a base rate case, please confirm that the Company estimates revenues collected from RTP customers under the transmission component of the RTP rate, and that such revenues are applied to transmission revenue requirements, reducing the revenue requirement burden related to transmission for all other customers.  If not confirmed, please explain the process by which transmission revenues are estimated to be collected from RTP customers and accounted for the Company’s base rate cost of service study.

In a base rate case, please confirm that the Company estimates revenues collected from RTP customers under the reliability component of the RTP rate, and that such revenues are applied to generation plant revenue requirements, reducing the revenue requirement burden related to generation for all other customers.  If not confirmed, please explain the process by which reliability revenues are estimated to be collected from RTP customers and accounted for the Company’s base rate cost of service study.


Please confirm that the Company’s RTP fuel credit methodology is designed to mirror the incremental cost dispatch cost metric of Lambda, and not designed to mirror the totality of fuel costs included in the FCR mechanism. Please explain.

Please explain if FT costs could be recovered through base rates and what Commission approval would be required for such a change. Please explain the pros and cons of recovery through each mechanism.
