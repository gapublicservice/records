STF-PIA-44-4

Question: 

Has Georgia Power discussed with any customers or potential customers the possibility of receiving bridge power from any other potential electric supplier? If so, what are the name(s) of the potential electric suppliers, how many customers were there, and what are the estimated number of MW for each such customer?

Response:

Given the increase in electricity demand in Georgia and elsewhere, Georgia Power has had numerous conversations with customers or potential customers who are interested in receiving power before Georgia Power is able to provide it. Some of those customers or potential customers have raised the concept of bridge power. As a practice, Georgia Power explains to these customers or potential customers how the Territorial Act prohibits many of the bridge power arrangements seen in jurisdictions outside of Georgia. While many of these conversations are initial discussions with customers through key account managers, Georgia Power is aware of at least four instances where customers have proposed receiving bridge power from another electric supplier. Specifically, one customer raised the concept of receiving bridge power from REDACTED to cover REDACTED on a temporary basis until Georgia Power can provide power to the site. Another customer was interested in receiving REDACTED of bridge power from REDACTED. One customer discussed the potential use of REDACTED for more than REDACTED of bridge power. Another customer discussed the possibility of using REDACTED for REDACTED of bridge power. Georgia Power informed those customers that proposed arrangements in which third parties own and supply bridge power to a customer that will ultimately be served by Georgia Power would violate the Territorial Act. Georgia Power did not have advance knowledge of the arrangement between Serverfarm and Voltagrid, but upon learning of the arrangement, notified both parties that the arrangement appeared to violate the Territorial Act and infringe upon Georgia Power’s exclusive right to serve the Premises.
