    APPLICATION FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE OF GEORGIA WAND EDUCATION
                       FUND INC. “GEORGIA WAND”

Pursuant to O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59 and Rule 515-2-1-.06 of the
Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”), Georgia WAND hereby files this
Application for Leave to Intervene (“Application”) in the above-referenced docket. In support of
this Application, Georgia WAND shows as follows:

​      ​       ​      ​       ​      ​       I.

On or before February 17, 2026, Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”)
will file its application for adjustments for the recovery of under-recovered storm damages with
the Commission. Previously, on January 21, 2026, the Commission issued a procedural and
scheduling order applicable to this docket. Pursuant to that order, Georgia WAND files this
application for leave to intervene and participate in the above-referenced docket. The application
is timely filed.

​      ​       ​      ​       ​      ​       II.

Georgia WAND is the State’s leading nonprofit consumer advocacy organization
dedicated to addressing needs and interests of Georgia citizens statewide on a wide range of
matters, including energy issues through its Consumer Energy Program. Many Georgia WAND
members, all its employees, and the Georgia WAND office are served by Georgia Power, and as
such, the interests of Georgia WAND and its members may be substantially affected by decisions
made by the Commission in this proceeding. As a result, Georgia WAND has substantial
interests
in the outcome of this proceeding and desires to intervene in order to protect those interests.

​      ​       ​      ​       ​       ​       III.
The interests of Georgia WAND’s employees and members who are served by
Georgia Power are not adequately represented by other parties to this proceeding and Georgia
Watch’s intervention in this matter will not unduly delay these proceedings or prejudice any
other party.

​      ​       ​       ​      ​       ​       IV.

WHEREFORE, Georgia WAND respectfully requests that the Commission grant
its application to intervene in the above-captioned docket, thereby allowing Georgia WAND to
participate fully as a party of record.
