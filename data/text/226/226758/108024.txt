Question: 

Provide a step-by-step description of the process used to translate the Aurora hourly forecast to RTP fuel credit for FCR-27 projection period.  Provide all intermediary workpapers used to make a determination of forecasted fuel revenues associated with RTP load.
Response:

The Company uses the Aurora production cost model hourly simulation results to forecast the Real Time Pricing (“RTP”) fuel credit rate for the projected test period. The process produces an hourly fuel credit rate ($/MWh) representing the average marginal replacement fuel cost credited to the fuel cost recovery (“FCR”) balance for each hour of the projection period.
The methodology underlying this forecast is consistent with the RTP fuel credit methodology approved by the Commission in Docket No. 31958 (Georgia Power’s 2010 Base Rate Case), which credits the fuel account at the average hourly marginal replacement fuel cost based on all Georgia Power generating units running in each hour plus power pool purchases or deliveries in each hour.
RTP Fuel Credit Formula
The RTP fuel credit rate is calculated as follows:

This is estimated using Aurora outputs with the following formula:

Fuel Credit  =  
Total Fuel Cost  +  Total Emission Cost  +  (Market Sales + Market Purchases) × BOS Lambda

Total Resource Output  +  Market Sales  +  Market Purchases
Where:
Total Fuel Cost = sum of hourly fuel costs ($) for all GPC portfolio resources from the Aurora hourly simulation
Total Emission Cost = sum of hourly emission costs ($) for all GPC portfolio resources from the Aurora hourly simulation
Market Sales = hourly pool/market sales (MWh) from the Aurora hourly simulation for the GPC portfolio
Market Purchases = hourly pool/market purchases (MWh) from the Aurora hourly simulation for the GPC portfolio
Resource Output = total hourly GPC resource output (MWh) from the Aurora hourly simulation
BOS Lambda = the final bottom-of-stack system lambda ($/MWh) from the same Aurora simulation case, representing the marginal cost of the last unit dispatched to serve system load (excluding off-system sales)

The RTP Fuel Credit components are matched with the forecast formula as follows:
GPC BOS unit cost = Total Fuel Cost + Total Emission Cost
Pool Purchases = Market Purchases (MWh)
Pool Sales = Market Sales (MWh)
Generation = Resource Output
Market components (MWh) are multiplied by BOS lambda ($/MWh) to estimate the cost in the numerator.

Step-by-Step Process:
Step 1: Identify the Aurora Simulation Case
The RTP fuel credit forecast for the FCR-27 projected test period uses the approved 2026 Energy Budget (“EBUD”) Aurora hourly simulation case. This is the same Aurora simulation used to develop the system lambdas, generation dispatch, fuel costs, and other components of the FCR-27 filing.
Step 2: Extract Hourly Generation and Cost Data from Aurora
A SQL query is executed against the Aurora hourly results database to extract the data elements required for the fuel credit calculation. The query joins two Aurora output tables — PortfolioResourcesHour1 (which contains hourly fuel and emission costs by resource) and PortfolioSummaryHour1 (which contains hourly market sales, market purchases, and total resource output) — filtered to the GPC portfolio.
The SQL query text is:
Select PortfolioResourcesHour1.Time_Period,   PortfolioResourcesHour1.Portfolio_Name,   Sum(PortfolioResourcesHour1.Total_Fuel_Cost) * 1000 As Total_Fuel_Cost,   Sum(PortfolioResourcesHour1.Total_Emission_Cost) * 1000 As Total_Emission_Cost,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Time_Period, PortfolioSummaryHour1.Portfolio_Name,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Market_Sales_Output_Total,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Market_Purchases_Output_Total,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Resource_Output_Total From PortfolioResourcesHour1 Inner Join PortfolioSummaryHour1 On   PortfolioResourcesHour1.Time_Period = PortfolioSummaryHour1.Time_Period Where PortfolioSummaryHour1.Portfolio_Name = 'GPC' And   PortfolioResourcesHour1.Portfolio_Name = 'GPC' Group By PortfolioResourcesHour1.Time_Period,   PortfolioResourcesHour1.Portfolio_Name, PortfolioSummaryHour1.Time_Period,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Portfolio_Name,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Market_Sales_Output_Total,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Market_Purchases_Output_Total,   PortfolioSummaryHour1.Resource_Output_Total
The query returns one row per hour with the following fields:

Sum of fuel costs for all GPC resources in the hour

GPC market/pool purchases in the hour

MWh
 
Step 3: Obtain Final BOS Lambdas
The BOS (bottom-of-stack) lambdas used in the fuel credit calculation are the final BOS lambdas produced from the same Energy Budget Aurora run. These hourly values represent the system marginal cost and are used in the formula to value market sales and purchases at their marginal cost contribution.
These are the same BOS lambdas used in the RTP price forecast for development of FCR-27 budget.
Step 4: Populate the Fuel Credit Calculation Workbook
The calculation workbook, provided as STF-JKA-8-8 Attachment, contains the following worksheets:

Hourly market sales (MWh) parsed from query data
Market_Purchases_Output_Total
Hourly market purchases (MWh) parsed from query data

Hourly resource output (MWh) parsed from query data

The workbook is populated as follows:
The sorted Aurora query results (from Step 2) are pasted into the _Query_Fuel Credit Calc worksheet.
The workbook formulas automatically parse the query data into the individual component worksheets (Total_Fuel_Cost, Total_Emission_Cost, Market_Sales_Output_Total, Market_Purchases_Output_Total, Resource_Output_Total).
The 10 years of final adjusted BOS lambdas (from Step 3) are copied as values into the BOS Lambda worksheet.
The Fuel Credit – CPT worksheet automatically calculates the hourly fuel credit rate using the formula described above.
The Fuel Credit – EPT worksheet converts the CPT results to Eastern Prevailing Time by applying the appropriate one-hour time shift to align with Georgia Power's billing time convention.
Step 5: Derivation of RTP Fuel Revenues
The hourly RTP fuel credit rates, in conjunction with projected RTP load (MWh), are used to determine the total forecasted RTP fuel revenues credited to the FCR balance for the projected test period.
Monthly RTP Fuel Revenue ($) = Monthly Average Fuel Credit Rate ($/MWh) × Monthly RTP Incremental Load (MWh) x Loss Expansion Factor
The monthly and annual totals of these forecasted fuel revenues are incorporated into the FCR-27 Budget filed by the Company in MFRP-1.
Intermediary Workpapers
Attachment STF-JKA-8-8 provides the intermediary workpapers that were used in the determination of forecasted fuel revenues associated with RTP load for the FCR-27 projected period.

Question:

This creates 4 categories of costs as summarized below.  For each category, provide a description of the resource types included in the category and explain if those units are averaged in the RTP fuel credit methodology.
Can Set BOS Lamda = YES, Dispatchable with Marginal Replacement Fuel Costs = NO.  These appear to be solar PPAs. What other categories are included?
Can Set BOS Lamda = NO, Dispatchable with Marginal Replacement Fuel Costs = YES.  These are nuclear and hydro units. What other categories are included?
Can Set BOS Lamda = YES, Dispatchable with Marginal Replacement Fuel Costs = YES.  These are traditional fossil generation units (CT, CCs, Steam). What other categories are included?
Can Set BOS Lamda = NO, Dispatchable with Marginal Replacement Fuel Costs = NO.  These include both 3rd party PPAs as well as some Company owned solar/BESS Projects. What categories are included?  
Please confirm one category includes company owned resources with small to no marginal replacement fuel costs (Solar).  Why does the Company believe these are not dispatchable? 
Please confirm the other category is third party PPAs, that have a fixed take-or-pay contract structure and every delivered MWh would have an imputed average $/MWh cost. Couldn’t the Company consider these costs in the RTP fuel credit methodology? 

Response:

The inclusion of costs of resources in the RTP fuel credit methodology is not determined by the resource type, but rather based on the dispatchability and fuel cost structure of the unit. For non-Company-owned resources, the contract or offtake arrangement dictates the determination of dispatchability and fuel costing. 

Non-dispatchable PPAs. 

Resources for which Georgia Power retains all the output as part of the Intercompany Interchange Contract (“IIC”) and pool settlement calculations. 

Dispatchable resources with marginal replacement fuel costs. 

Energy Only Qualifying Facilities (“QFs”) and Proxy QFs which are contractually entitled to deliver the entire energy output from the facility when operating.

There is no marginal replacement fuel cost for these resources. They are not dispatchable as the Company cannot dictate when the facility produces power (for example, they cannot produce energy at night). 
Under the Commission approved methodology, only marginal replacement fuel costs are included in the RTP fuel credit. As such, the Company cannot include other costs under the current methodology.
