Response:

The Company will look to continue to diversify its supply of coal to Plant Bowen. Though the traditional Illinois Basin and NAPP coals , which are the most economic, will comprise a very large portion of the port into the future, other sources need to be taken into consideration to provide enhanced supply diversity as well as reliability. Future coal burns at Plant Bowen are projected to increase and this supports the need for added diversity. Including a historically reliable supplier Blackhawk tons creates a more diverse portfolio and enhances both rail diversity and supplier/mine diversity. It is important to note that the additional Blackhawk tons recommended for purchase is a small amount (less than 10 percent) compared to the overall supply mix.
Please see the response to subpart (a).
Demonstrated by the Company’s portfolio mix, the lower cost Illinois Basin and NAPP mines have large volume commitments in place at Plant Bowen for future years, approximately 90 percent of the supply. It is anticipated that those volumes will increase even more over time as coal burns increase.    
No, there is no indication that Blackhawk is currently having any major operating issues nor are there any long-term operating issues anticipated with the current mine plan. Blackhawk has indicated that opening another section of the mine would only increase their mining costs which would result in increased coal costs for its customers. It is not the intent for Blackhawk to open another section of the mine at this time.
