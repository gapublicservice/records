Question: 

For RTP sales
Please confirm projected RTP sales are expected to more than double from approximately xxxxxxxxxx MWh in June 2026-May 2027  to xxxxxxxxx MWh in June 2028-May 2029.  (See MFRP-1 FCR-27 Budget TS.xlsx, Tab: RTP).

Projected unit commitment is based on serving total system load, and therefore, any changes on unit commitment could be driven by several various factors and not just RTP incremental load.  Per the Company’s response to STF-PIA-3-6, fuel costs associated with unit commitment costs are included in the calculation of the RTP Fuel Rate that contributes to fuel recovery balance and are reflected in the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-10 Attachments F and G.  

Units at minimum are not the next marginal unit serving the next kilowatt hour (“kWh”) on the system but are part of the unit commitment process to minimize total production cost while maintaining reliability. As load changes on the system and a unit is dispatched to run above minimum to serve additional load, then that unit is considered for setting system lambda. As explained in the response to subpart (d) above, fuel costs associated with any unit commitment costs are included in the calculation of the RTP Fuel Rate.

Short-term purchases or long-term PPAs are considered in setting lambda, but if they are above the marginal system running costs in a given hour, they do not set system lambda because they exceed the marginal running cost of the system.   

Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-5-7 subpart (f).
