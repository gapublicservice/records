STF-SCR-7-7

Question: 

Provide a detailed description of the Contractor Application for Time and Expense Entry (CATEE) used for many of the contractor invoices processed for the 2022-2025 storms at issue in this proceeding. Be sure to include at a minimum such things as which contractors are eligible to participate, which party keys in the time and expense information, whether it is a mobile app or some type of computer program, whether applicable contract pricing is pre-entered for each contractor by Georgia Power.

Response:

CATEE is a Company utilized system used to support the submission, review, and approval of contractor labor and expenses associated with construction and storm restoration activities. CATEE is a web‑based application that can be accessed through standard computing devices, including mobile devices and desktop computers.
Contractors performing authorized work for Georgia Power under an executed contract may be eligible to use CATEE once access is established by the Company. Applicable contract pricing is pre‑loaded into the system by Georgia Power based on the executed contract. Contractors enter time and expense information directly into the system. Contractors select from predefined labor and equipment rates and do not enter or modify pricing, which helps ensure consistency with contractually approved rates and supports invoice accuracy and auditability. Submitted entries are routed to the appropriate Georgia Power inspector or supervisor for review and approval to confirm the work performed is consistent with contract terms and work authorization.
