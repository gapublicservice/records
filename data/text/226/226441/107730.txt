ERRATA TO STF-JKA-2-29

Question: 

Refer to page 23, lines 12-22, “As presently approved, all RFP-related costs and revenues not otherwise recovered by Bid Fees or Winners Fees are recovered through the fuel clause. Such costs may include the Company’s labor costs from dedicated procurement personnel and the time spent by internal partners supporting the development of a given RFP (e.g. Transmission, Resource Planning, Environmental Affairs, etc.) as well as the cost of the Independent Evaluator, costs for the economic and transmission evaluations, and termination fees. For example, bid security retained by Georgia Power if a Short List bidder elects not to execute a PPA after being named to the Short List is credited to the fuel clause to the benefit of all customers. The same is true of any termination payment received for a PPA that is terminated early. In addition, once PPAs are in effect, all payments to counterparties are processed through the FCR.”

a.Please identify the amounts of these costs included in the historic balance and identify where they are disclosed in the MFRH.
b.Please identify the amounts of these costs included in the projected period and identify where they are reported in the MRFP.

Response:

Please see STF-JKA-2-29 Attachment for the Bid Fees and Winners Fees related to RFPs and their associated costs included in FCR during the historical period. These amounts are included, along with all the other renewable revenues and costs recoverable through FCR, in MFRH-2, Line “Net Renewable Program Expenses/(Revenues)” submitted in the Company’s initial filing on February 17, 2026.

Please see the FCR-27 budget “MFRP-1 FCR-27 Budget TS.xls” provided in MFRP-1 submitted in the Company’s initial filing on February 17, 2026, Tab “Renewables”, and the monthly information shown under the programs “Distributed Generation” (Excel rows 94-96) and “Utility Scale” (Excel rows 103-105) for the projected Bid Fees and Winners Fees related to future RFPs and their associated costs included in FCR for the projected test period.

Errata to Response: 

The Company incorrectly cited to two rows of the referenced “MFRP-1 FCR-27 Budget TS.xls” file, which pulled in renewable program expenses unrelated to renewable RFPs. The appropriate projected utility scale and distributed generation RFP expenses can be found on the “Renewables” tab in the attached spreadsheet in rows 94-96 for distributed generation and rows 103-105 for utility scale. The revisions to the original response subpart (b) are shown in redline above. In addition, Georgia Power has reduced the projected hosting capacity costs included in row 113 to more accurately reflect the ongoing cost of offering this tool.
