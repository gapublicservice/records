STF-JKA-4-2

Question: 

Refer to MFRH-2, STF-JKA-1-34 and STF-JKA 1-35.

a. Please provide a breakdown of MFRP-2 by general ledger FERC account by unit by expense type. For example, provide all entries supporting row 35, including details for each account 50100100, 50100105, 50100110, 50100135, 50100140, 50100320, by unit, including a description of each sub-account. If additional accounts or sub-accounts are included, please itemize individual expenses by type and include a description of each sub-account and expense category. For Revenue accounts, please provide specific customer class level details, for example, itemize the RTP revenues collected and credited to FCR for each month of the historic period.
b. Reconcile the costs and revenues reported for fuel accounts to the totals reported on the FERC Form 1. If any costs are included in the Form 1 reporting are not included in the Fuel balance, please explain and provide a build up of the costs excluded for a full reconciliation. Specifically itemize any revenues that are excluded for each account attributed to sharing of sales revenue.
c. Refer to STF-PIA-3-1. Identify all non-fuel expenses charged to fuel and the individual cost components in the breakdown provided.

Response:

Please see STF-JKA-4-2 Attachment A for FERC level details of fuel revenues and expenses provided in MFRH-2 by month. For incremental RTP revenues credited to FCR by month by customer class, please see STF-JKA-4-2 Attachment B.

Please see STF-JKA-4-2 Attachment C. The reconciliation is provided by year and the sum of the yearly amounts tie to the cumulative amounts for the historical period provided in MFRH-2. There are minor differences for some of the expense items due to monthly true-ups recorded in the following month.

Please see the Company’s response to STF-PIA-3-1 for costs associated with fuel charged to FCR balance during the historical period by cost component.
