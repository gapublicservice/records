STF-JKA-6-1

Question: 

Refer to “STF-JKA-1-8 Attachment TRADE SECRET.xlsx” that includes individual unit dispatch data for selected peak and valley hours, and the RTP credit methodology described in STF-JKA-1-4 Attachment B TRADE SECRET.xlsx.

a.For each hour of the historic period, provide the amount of RTP credited energy, costs, and $/MWh.
b.For each hour of the historic period, provide the amount of take-or- pay contract PPA energy, costs, and $/MWh included in the FCR balance.
c.For each hour of the historic period, provide the total amount of energy, costs, and $/MWh that included in the FCR balance.
d.Confirm that the amounts associated with non-dispatchable take-or- pay contracts are not included in the GPC BOS or Purchase cost sums used to determine the RTP fuel credit.
e.Had the take-or-pay contract costs been included in the average marginal cost calculation used to set the RTP fuel credit rate, please explain what additional revenues would have been credited to the fuel balance by the RTP load for each hour of the historic period.
f.Provide a listing of all units on the GPC system during the historic period with a mapping as to whether the unit can be used to set system lambda and whether the unit is used in the average marginal cost methodology underpinning the RTP credit calculation for every hour. For all units excluded from consideration of RTP fuel credits, please provide the terms of the contract including generation by hour, cost by hour, and totals by month.

Response:

Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-4 Attachment for the incremental real time pricing (“RTP”) fuel revenues and STF-JKA-4-5 Attachment for the incremental RTP MWh.

The Company does not calculate costs of “take or pay” contract PPAs by hour in its FCR balance. Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-2 for the monthly MWh, costs, and $/MWh for solar, biomass, and wind PPAs.

The Company does not record costs in its FCR balance by hour. Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-2 for the total monthly MWh, costs, and $/MWh recorded to FCR.

The marginal replacement fuel costs associated with non-dispatchable, take-or-pay renewable contracts are zero in the numerator of the RTP fuel credit formula calculation because those resources are non-dispatchable and have no marginal replacement fuel cost.  

The request in subpart (e) is a hypothetical that the Company cannot answer because the data for which does not currently exist. The marginal replacement fuel costs associated with non-dispatchable, take-or-pay renewable contracts are zero in the numerator of the RTP fuel credit formula calculation as explained in the response to subpart (d). To determine the revenues that would have been credited to the fuel balance by RTP load for each hour of the historic period if the non-dispatchable, take-or-pay renewable contract costs had been included in the average marginal cost calculation would be overly burdensome to create and produce due to the retroactive back calculations, change of assumptions, and customer specific billing period adjustments that would be required to compile such a data set.

Please see STF-JKA-6-1 Attachment for a listing of all units on the Georgia Power system during the historical period. The copies of the contracts for each solar, biomass, and wind PPA were provided in the Company’s update to MFRH-17 and its response to STF-JKA-1-17.
