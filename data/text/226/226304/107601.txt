On February 17, 2026, Georgia Power Company (the “Company”) filed its 2026 Storm Damage Recovery Application, including witness testimony, exhibits, and minimum filing requirements (“MFRs”) in Docket No. 44280. Enclosed for filing is an Errata to Company’s filing, including updated Exhibits.

The Errata contains a list of updates to the Company’s originally filed exhibits, due to an update to estimated available tax credits as well as certain inadvertent errors, some of which were discovered in the process of responding to discovery requests issued by the Commission’s Public Interest Advocacy (“PIA”) Staff. The items below are a summary of the issues along with the associated revenue requirement impacts.

Correction of the labor estimate for Hurricane Helene capital transfers to remove labor adders for contractor costs (follow up to STF-SCR-1-17) – ($2.6M)
Correction of the labor estimate for Hurricane Helene capital transfers to reflect actual costs incurred – ($1.8M)
Update of IRC 45U production tax credits estimate for 2025 – $1.9M
Correction of the storm revenue requirement included in current rates (STF-SCR-2-6) – $0.3M
Correction of capital costs transferred for Other Storms on Line No. 14, Column No. 6 in tab ‘MFR 3 Other’ from $36,856 thousand to $49,013 thousand (STF-SCR-6-1) - $0
Correction to the number of broken poles due to Hurricane Helene on Page 4, Line 27 in the Direct testimony from 12,239 to 12,329 - $0

Exhibits___(CLF-JJS-MSB) Pgs. 1-5_Errata include the updated revenue requirement, including impacts to the Company’s proposed revenue increase. As shown on Page 1 of the Errata Exhibits, the total impact of the adjustments listed above reduces the Company’s originally filed forecasted test period revenue deficiency by $2.3 million to $267.4 million.
