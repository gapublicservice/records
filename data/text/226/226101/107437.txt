STF-JKA-4-15

Question: 

How are opportunity sales priced and reflected in the FCR balance?

a.Please explain how opportunity sales are priced and reflected in the FCR balance? Are the revenues associated with opportunity sales priced at system lambda or a marginal price similar to RTP? Please explain.
b.Compare and contrast the revenues generated through opportunity sales and those revenues associated with RTP sales. Are the revenues allocated to the FCR balance derived on the same basis? Why or why not?

Response:

The negotiated transaction terms with the counterparty determine the total revenues from opportunity sales, and they are not priced at a marginal price such as system lambda. The marginal fuel and emissions costs incurred by the Company to make opportunity sales are credited back to the FCR balance. These costs are calculated hourly by sale and by unit in pool settlement for each opportunity sale transaction. 

Please see the response to subpart (a) above for the pricing of opportunity sales. The revenues for RTP sales are determined by the RTP-HA and RTP-DA hourly prices. The GPC RTP fuel credit calculation determines the hourly rate to credit from RTP revenues to the FCR balance using the average marginal fuel costs of the Georgia Power units running in a given hour. In contrast, the fuel credit associated with opportunity sales is based on the specific units identified in each opportunity sale transaction. The opportunity sales revenues above the costs incurred to serve the load identified in each opportunity sale transaction is a benefit above marginal costs, which is credited to FCR at 75% for the benefit of all customers. RTP fuel credit revenues are based on the actual average marginal fuel costs incurred by Georgia Power.
