Question: 

Please provide a description of the OE AI agent tool that the Company utilizes to review the INPO database. Please include the original release date of the AI agent tool as well as any software or personnel training updates the Company has developed to date.

Response:

The Southern Nuclear Operating Company, Inc. (“SNC”) OE AI agent is a tool created in Microsoft CoPilot that uses SNC’s internal operating experience database to help provide relevant materials for engineers, planners, and others within the organization. It was first deployed for testing and use in May 2025. SNC deployed an updated SharePoint OE site and AI agent in September 2025. The INPO operating experience database is not fully accessible with this agent today. SNC is part of a pilot program with INPO to access the INPO database with AI but this project is limited to a small group for testing. The INPO AI access is projected to be expanded to all Southern Nuclear personnel in 2026. AI training is provided through multiple methods, including small group training and demonstrations and on-demand resources available to all employees.

STF-RTI-1-16

Attachment N - Vogtle 3 – November 2024 – 11/17/24

Question: 

What lessons were learned from reviewing the original outages scheduled in at the China AP1000 reactors and how will the Company incorporate additional learnings into future refueling outages?

Response:

The main lesson learned was that the first outage requires more time than originally planned. Performance of the first-time tests and procedures created more opportunities for failures, procedure updates, and learnings. Lessons learned from the Plant Vogtle Unit 3 outage were used in planning and execution of the first Plant Vogtle Unit 4 outage (fall 2025). This outage, with a similar scope of work, was completed in 28 days.
