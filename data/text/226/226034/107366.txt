STF-PIA-3-16

Question: 

Reference Response STF-JKA-1-4 and TRADE SECRET Attachments

a.Please provide workpapers showing all calculations with formulas intact and an explanation of how the values in the column labeled “RTP-HA Lambda (¢)” on the RTP-HA Prices and RTP-DA Prices worksheets were calculated.
b.Please explain and provide workpapers with formulas intact how and why the values in the column labeled “RTP-HA Lambda (¢)” on the RTP-HA Prices and RTP-DA Prices worksheets in TRADE SECRET Attachment A are different than the values for the column labeled “System Hourly Average Fuel Cost (without loss expansion factors) ($/MWh)” in TRADE SECRET Attachment B, Sheet 2 for corresponding time periods.
c.Please provide a reconciliation of the differences referenced in b., in spreadsheet format with all formulas intact, showing all calculations, identifying the source of each component of the difference(s) and please explain the differences.

Response:

Please see STF-PIA-3-16 Attachment A for the requested workpapers.

For RTP-HA, the price is inclusive of losses from the generator through the bulk transmission system.  

For RTP-DA, the price is inclusive of losses from the generator through the bulk transmission system. In addition, the RTP-DA prices have hourly losses applied to the lambda price to account for losses past the bulk transmission system.   

In responding to this data request, the Company identified a labeling error for STF-JKA-1-4 Attachment A. The headers on the ‘RTP-DA Prices’ tab needs to be updated to reflect that the information provided is for RTP-DA instead of RTP-HA. There is no correction needed for data values. Please see STF-PIA-3-16 Attachment B for the correction for STF-JKA-1-4 Attachment A. Please note, STF-PIA-3-16 Attachment A provided in this response reflects the corrected headers.

The “RTP-HA Lambda (¢)” and “RTP-DA Lambda (¢)” values are reflective of the system hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs) known as lambda.  The “System Hourly Average Fuel Cost (without loss expansion factors) ($/MWh)” values are reflective of the Georgia Power hourly average marginal fuel cost without loss expansion factors as described in the Company’s response to STF-JKA-1-4 subpart (e). The values are different because they represent different costs.

Please see the response to subpart (b) above.

STF-PIA-3-13

Question: 

Please refer to the Company’s response to STF-JKA-1-7(d). The Company explains that the difference between lambda (used for RTP price) and the allocated fuel revenue (from RTP revenues to offset the FCR fuel balance) covers two pieces: “generation variable O&M and capital substitution.”

a.How does the Company calculate RTP’s contribution to “generation variable O&M”?
b.How does the Company calculate RTP’s contribution to “capital substitution”?
c.Please quantify the value of the difference between lambda (used for RTP price) and the allocated fuel revenue (from RTP revenues to the FCR fuel balance). At a minimum, provide the value of lambda and the allocated fuel revenue on an hourly basis to supplement JKA-1-4, Attachment B.

Response:

Generation variable operations and maintenance (“O&M”) is a component of lambda that is broken out as shown in the Company’s response to STF-JKA-1-8. 

Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-11.

The generation variable O&M and capital substitution amounts are real time pricing (“RTP”) revenues that are credited to base. That entire sum (the delta between Lambda and RTP fuel credits) is directly assigned to the marginal group and an offsetting benefit is allocated to all embedded load to reduce costs to be recovered from all other customers. 

Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-5 subpart (a) for the requested lambda values. Please see the Company’s response to STF-JKA-3-4 subpart (b) for the allocated fuel revenues.
