===== DKT 56002 STF-PIA-21 PD/STF-PIA-21-7.docx =====
STF-PIA-21-7

Question: 

Please refer to STF-PIA-23 response. Despite the last time of the response, has the war in Iran necessitated any change in the focus of Company’s actions?

Response:

While Georgia Power is not currently aware of any targeted activity toward the Company, Georgia Power activated a heightened state of awareness in response to the conflict and implemented the following measures:
Expanded targeted threat hunting focused on Iranianaligned tactics, techniques, and procedures.
Lowered alerting and reporting thresholds across Security Operations to increase sensitivity to early indicators of malicious activity.
Close coordination with industry peers, government partners, and commercial intelligence providers to maintain shared situational awareness and preparedness.
Focused review of external assets, including validation of internetconnected devices and thirdparty dependencies.

Georgia Power maintained this elevated posture for several weeks, then resumed normal operations once active conflict lessened and negotiations restarted.

STF-PIA-21-3

Question: 

Please refer to STF-PIA-18-4, STF-PIA-18-5, and STF-PIA-18-8 responses.
a.Even if there is no formal notification of correspondence, does the Company maintain any record of these informal contacts and information sharing?
b.If so, how is it broken down and how does the Company use the information?
c.If not, why?

Response:

Yes. Correspondence varies based on the subject matter and applicable business unit and may occur via phone call, virtual meeting, or inperson meeting. For meetings, the Company typically retains calendar invitation records, which include the meeting agenda and list of attendees. 

Informal communication logs related to cyber threat intelligence are retained using various applications and mediums. They are appropriately labeled using the Traffic Light Protocol (“TLP”) for cyber threat information sharing. These trade secrets are protected under bilateral and multilateral non-disclosure agreements, carrying the TLP marking of Amber+Strict, which prohibits external disclosure.

The retained information is maintained in forms appropriate to the nature of the communication and is used to support the Company’s physical and cyber security operations. This includes: 
maintaining situational awareness; 
coordinating and sharing information with internal stakeholders and applicable external agencies; 
aligning roles and responsibilities in response to evolving conditions affecting the electric sector and critical infrastructure environment; 
assessing, adjusting, and validating the Company’s physical security posture; 
proactively identifying previously unknown cyber threats within Company data; 
establishing an internal knowledge base of threat actor techniques; and
modeling real-world threats during tabletop exercises.

Not applicable.
