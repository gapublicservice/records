VIA ELECTRONIC FILING

Re: Docket No. 44280; Georgia Power Company’s Storm Damage Recovery Application

Dear Chairman Shaw and Members of the Commission: 

Georgia Watch files this letter in response to the Stipulated Agreement reached among Georgia Power Company, Commission Staff and Georgia Manufacturers Association in this docket and Docket No. 56765. While we recognize that stipulated agreements generally balance the positions of participating parties, we believe that the terms of this agreement do not adequately address the interests of residential ratepayers. 

Since 2023, the Company’s residential customers have experienced six rate increases, adding on average $38 to their bills, while the Company’s shareholders have continued to earn profits above the national average for investor owned utilities. Though the terms of the agreement do increase the average monthly reduction on residential bills over the Company’s initial proposals, they do not provide as much reduction in rates as we believe Staff effectively argued for in their case.

In order to better serve the interests of residential customers, we urge the Commission to amend the agreement to:

Not allow the Company to earn its full rate of return on storm damage recovery costs, but instead authorize no profit or a debt-only rate of return on the storm cost regulatory asset.
Extend the recovery period at a minimum to 72 months and calculate recovery of the regulatory asset on a levelized basis to further reduce the impact on customers’ monthly bills.

At a time of rising costs, stagnant wages, and deepening financial insecurity, the Commission should exercise its authority to modify the Stipulated Agreement and approve an order that more fairly balances the needs of ratepayers with the interests of Georgia Power’s shareholders. 

Sincerely, 

Liz Coyle
Executive Director
Georgia Watch
