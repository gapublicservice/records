===== Letter to Commission - GAM Support of Proposed Stipulation - 5-20-26.pdf =====
                                                       May 20, 2026

VIA ELECTRONIC FILING
Ms. Sallie Tanner
Executive Secretary
Georgia Public Service Commission
244 Washington Street SW
Atlanta, Georgia 30334-9052

               Re:     Docket No. 44280; Georgia Power Company’s Storm Damage Recovery
                       Application

Ms. Tanner:

GAM files this letter in support of the Stipulation filed in this docket and in Docket No. 56765
on behalf of GAM, Georgia Power Company and the Commission Public Interest and Advocacy
Staff to resolve all issues in both dockets.

The Stipulation represents a reasonable compromise of the positions of the signing parties,
achieving a much more substantial reduction to overall customer rates than originally proposed
by Georgia Power. The key issue of affordability impacts GAM members as well as all other
Company ratepayers. While energy costs will remain a challenge, the Stipulation would move
rates in a more affordable direction and provide relief. We therefore support the Stipulation and
urge its adoption.

The Stipulation also would benefit other non-signatory interveners by lowering rates for all
customers, placing more reasonable limits on the Company’s hedging program, and providing a
means for parties to address concerns about the fuel credit methodology. As with all stipulations,
neither GAM nor any other party would receive everything they asked for – nor would the final
order in this case reflect the entirety of a single party’s wish list. Thus, despite the likely wishes
of some to pick and choose only those provisions of the Stipulation with which they agree, the
Commission should adopt the Stipulation in its entirety as a negotiated document that fairly
balances the positions of the parties.

GAM thanks the Commission, Staff, the Company and all parties for their participation in this
case and in Docket 56765 and again urges the Commission to vote in favor of the Stipulation.

                                                       Best regards,


                                                       Charles B. Jones, III
cc:    Parties of Record
