STF-JKA-1-4 Supplemental

Question: 

Regarding the Company’s tariffs that include a real-time-pricing (RTP) component in the forecasted or billed customer rate.

a.Please identify all current tariffs that the Company has that rely on RTP for billing.
b.Concerning the historic period of FCR-27, please explain how the actual RTP price was derived and how fuel costs were assessed and assigned to RTP tariff customers.
c.For the peak hours of the summer and winter periods of the year 2025, provide workpapers showing how the hourly RTP prices for the two hours were derived.
d.Please explain how the Company’s RTP customer revenues were included in the FCR balance over the historic period.
e.Provide a roadmap that reconciles the hourly RTP revenues against the hourly incurred System cost. At a minimum, as part of this reconciliation, provide the following data for the 2025 summer and winter peak months and by schedule (RTPHA vs RTPDA): the hourly RTP rate by schedule, the hourly RTP loads, the System hourly load, and System hourly average fuel cost, and System hourly marginal cost.
f.For the specific summer and winter projected peak hours of 2027, explain how the Company’s projected RTP price was derived and how fuel costs were determined that will be assessed to RTP customers and accounted for in determining the FCR balance. Provide all workpapers used to determine the RTP prices and fuel costs included in the FCR balance.
Response:

Below are the tariffs that rely on RTP for billing:
Real Time Pricing – Day Ahead (RTP-DA) 
Real Time Pricing – Hour Ahead (RTP-HA) 
Time of Use – Supplier Choice – Monthly Access Charge (TOU-SC MAC)
Electric Arc Furnace (EAF)
Price Protection Products (PPP)


Actual RTP-DA hourly prices are based on projections of the hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs) known as lambda, provisions for losses, projections of hourly transmission costs and reliability capacity costs for each day (when applicable), and a three (3) mill/kWh recovery factor. The amount of fuel charges from hourly incremental kWh usage are applied to the recovery of fuel cost at the hourly average marginal fuel cost for the applicable hour.

Actual RTP-HA hourly prices are based on projections of the hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs) known as lambda, provisions for losses, projections of hourly transmission costs and reliability capacity costs for each day (when applicable), and a two (2) mill/kWh recovery factor. The amount of fuel charges from hourly incremental kWh usage are applied to the recovery of fuel cost at the hourly average marginal fuel cost for the applicable hour.

RTP customer fuel revenues as calculated in the response to subpart (b) above are included in Retail Revenues as part of the FCR balance.

Please see STF-JKA-1-4 Attachment B for the requested data on the 2025 summer and winter peak price months. 

Please note, the hourly marginal fuel cost based on all Georgia Power generating units running in each hour plus power pool purchases or deliveries in each hour is being provided for ‘Hourly Incurred System Cost’.  The Georgia Power hourly average marginal fuel cost without loss expansion factors is being provided for ‘System Hourly Average Fuel Cost’.  The associated interchange energy rate (AIER) representing fuel costs is being provided for ‘System Hourly Marginal Cost’.

The projected RTP-DA hourly price for the projected summer and winter peak price hours of 2027 are based on the projected hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs) known as lambda, provisions for losses, projections of hourly transmission costs and reliability capacity costs, and a three (3) mill/kWh recovery factor. The amount of fuel charges from hourly incremental kWh usage that will be assessed and applied to the recovery of fuel cost are based on the projected hourly average marginal fuel cost for the applicable hour.

The projected RTP-HA hourly price for the projected summer and winter peak price hours of 2027 are based on the projected hourly running cost of incremental generation (including approved environmental costs) known as lambda, provisions for losses, projections of hourly transmission costs and reliability capacity costs, and a two (2) mill/kWh recovery factor. The amount of fuel charges from hourly incremental kWh usage that will be assessed and applied to the recovery of fuel cost are based on the projected hourly average marginal fuel cost for the applicable hour.
