STF-PIA-19-3

Question: 

For each project in Table 2, please provide a description and explanation for each change to the Estimated Cost – GPC. Please explain the cause of each increase or decrease to Georgia Power’s estimated cost for each project.

Response:

Cost estimates in the Georgia ITS Ten-Year Plan are updated annually to reflect the best available information at the time of the plan preparation. The following list, although not exhaustive, includes general causes for changes in cost estimate:
•Correction of preliminary estimates as projects advance through the gated process
•Refined project scope following detailed engineering reviews or field assessments 
•Changes in project schedule that affect escalation of costs 
•Revised right-of-way acquisition cost estimates 
•Updated material and equipment pricing 
•Updated construction labor and contractor cost estimates
