Dear Ms. Tanner: 
As part of the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (“Commission”) July 29, 2022 Final Order approving Georgia Power Company’s (“Georgia Power”) 2022 Integrated Resource Plan (“IRP”) in Docket No. 44160, the Commission approved the use of the incremental renewable integration costs approach when determining renewable integration costs for future renewable procurements until a future Renewable Integration Study (“RIS”) is completed and approved. As such, the Commission approved $1.93/MWh for solar resources up to the 2,300 MW of new renewables approved in the 2022 IRP. 
As part of the Commission’s July 31, 2025 Final Order in Georgia Power’s 2025 IRP in Docket No. 56002, the Commission approved Georgia Power to use the updated RIS for planning purposes. The 2025 IRP Final Order also directed the Company and Commission Staff (“Staff”) to meet prior to the next Request for Proposals (“RFP”) to determine how specific integration costs and Flex Credit would be determined using the results of the 2024 RIS. Georgia Power and Staff agreed to meet and discuss Staff’s recommended changes to the RIS and make a compliance filing to provide (i) the timeline for completion of the RIS prior to the next IRP and (ii) the process to be used by Georgia Power to determine the integration costs and Flex Credit for each procurement. Georgia Power and Commission Staff have met as directed and agreed that based on the current RIS, the renewable integration cost and Flex Credit will be revised to $0.00/MWh, effective immediately and applicable to Georgia Power RFP going forward. Georgia Power intends to complete the next RIS prior to filing the 2028 IRP. With this filing, Georgia Power has satisfied its obligations in paragraph 17 of the Supply Side Plan of the approved 2025 IRP Stipulation.
