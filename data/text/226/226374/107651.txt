Dear Ms. Tanner: 
On December 30, 2025, the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) issued its Order Adopting Stipulation and Granting Certification of Capacity from the 2029-2031 All-Source RFP and Supplemental Resources for 2028-2031 Capacity (“All-Source Certification Order”). Pursuant to Paragraph 8 of the Stipulation approved in the All-Source Certification Order, Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) and the Commission Public Interest Advocacy Staff (“PIA Staff”) agreed to work collaboratively to investigate the potential benefits of load flexibility. The Company and PIA Staff also agreed to report back to the Commission within 120 days of the All-Source Certification Order, which is April 29, 2026.
Given the multiple regulatory proceedings currently ongoing, Georgia Power and PIA Staff have agreed to extend the time to meet and file the report regarding load flexibility to June 30, 2026. This extension will allow time for the parties to collaborate and will provide PIA Staff with time necessary to provide feedback to the Company in advance of filing the report.
If you have any questions regarding this filing, please call me at (404) 885-3475.

Best regards,
Steven J. Hewitson
