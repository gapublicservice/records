             APPLICATION OF GEORGIA WAND EDUCATION FUND INC.
                            FOR LEAVE TO INTERVENE


       NOW COMES Georgia WAND Education Fund Inc. (Georgia WAND) and pursuant to
O.C.G.A. §§ 50-13-14 and 46-2-59, and Rule 515-2-1-.06 of the Rules of the Georgia Public
Service Commission, files this application for leave to intervene in the above-referenced
proceeding. In support of this application Georgia WAND shows the Commission the following:

                                              I.

Georgia WAND is a statewide nonprofit organization dedicated to advancing nuclear harm
reduction, environmental protection, and climate and social justice. Through community
engagement, policy advocacy, and coalition leadership, we work to protect the health, safety, and
economic well-being of Georgia residents—particularly those in frontline and overburdened
communities disproportionately impacted by energy infrastructure and utility decisions.

​      ​       ​       ​      ​       ​       II.

Georgia WAND engages thousands of Georgians across both urban and rural communities,
including ratepayers served by Georgia Power. Our members, staff, and the communities we
represent are directly affected by decisions of the Commission, including those related to fuel
cost recovery, rate design, and utility accountability. These proceedings have real and immediate
implications for energy affordability, public health, and environmental outcomes across the state.

​      ​       ​       ​      ​       ​       III.

Georgia WAND has a substantial interest in ensuring that this process is transparent, equitable,
and grounded in the public interest. We are particularly concerned with how fuel costs are
incurred, allocated, and passed on to ratepayers, and whether those costs reflect prudent utility
practices and long-term planning aligned with a just transition to clean energy. Accordingly,
Georgia WAND seeks to intervene in this proceeding to represent the interests of its members
and the broader public, to advocate for fair and reasonable outcomes, and to ensure that
Georgia’s energy future prioritizes affordability, reliability, and the health and well-being of all

communities. All communications regarding this proceeding should be directed to Georgia
WAND for the attention of the following name and address:
​    ​       ​       ​       ​      ​
                                           IV.

WHEREFORE, Georgia WAND respectfully requests that the Commission grant
its application to intervene in the above-captioned docket, thereby allowing Georgia WAND to
participate fully as a party of record.
