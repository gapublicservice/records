STF-SCR-6-1

Question: 

Identify each storm event or “other emergency situation” (as defined in Georgia Power Company Accounting For Storm Damage & Other Emergencies June 21, 2024) included in worksheet “MFR 3 Other”, the date of the storm event or other emergency situation, and a very brief description of underlying weather that caused the damage or the emergency situation. Replicate the cost table of worksheet “MFR 3 Other” for each storm event or other emergency situation. Provided response in an unprotected Excel worksheet.

Response:

Please see STF-SCR-6-1 Attachment for the description and costs related to each storm event or other emergency situations recorded to the storm regulatory asset from January 2022 through December 2025. The Company does not have this information categorized by each storm event in a similar format to that used in ‘MFR 3 Other.’
In responding to this data request, the Company discovered an incorrect value in ‘MFR 3 Other’ Line No. 14, Column No. 6, “Total Distribution and Transmission Costs Reclassified to Capital Plant Accounts” in the Storm Damage Recovery Application Filing. The correct value should total $49,013 thousand as reflected in STF-SCR-6-1 Attachment, tab ‘Other Storms-Capital.’ There is no resulting impact to the requested revenue requirement deficiency in this proceeding.
