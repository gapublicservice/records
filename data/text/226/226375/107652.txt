Question: 

Please provide actual hourly usage in kWh, for years 2024 and 2025, for 100 customers on the following residential tariffs:

a. R-30
b. TOU-RD-11
c. TOU-REO-18
d. TOU-OA-14

Response:

Please see STF-PIA-42-2 Attachment for the hourly usage for a random sample of 100 customers on the requested tariffs in 2024. 

Please note that data for 2025 will not be available until the end of 2026. Load shapes by class, rate, and level for a particular calendar year are typically developed in the first quarter of the following year. However, as part of a major transition in the Company’s customer information platform/database used to develop these load shapes, 2025 data will not be available until the end of 2026.
