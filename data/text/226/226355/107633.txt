The planning worksheet provided in STF-JKA-7-1 Attachment AA is representative of the tools the Company uses to align unit operations and needs of the entire system, with the coal inventory management strategy, including any projected target burn needed to ensure reliability of fuel supply and to maintain inventories within safe, acceptable limits. Definitions of the terms referenced within the spreadsheet and how they are used are provided below.  The planning worksheet is not inclusive of all factors considered when committing a unit and only considers targeted burn needed for coal inventory management.  
Legend
Gas Price Forecast – Monthly gas price forecast for the upcoming year from the preceding December, used in developing the annual Ratable Plan. 
Actual Gas Price – Monthly average actual gas price. 
Ratable Plan (Row 5) – A fuel strategy that establishes a steady, predictable level of coal deliveries to each plant, based on forecasted burn, to ensure reliable supply and manageable inventory levels without reacting to shortterm market conditions. The Ratable Plan is established at the beginning of the calendar year. 
This plan is an initial guideline to achieve the target burn needed to support consistent set delivery and is not indicative of ratable burn. 
OpsView Gas Price FC for Operational Plan – Refers to the latest gas price forecast used in the development of the specific plant’s operational plan going forward.
Operational Plan (Row 8) – A flexible plan that complements the ratable plan by aligning specific unit operation with the monthly burn targets to manage inventories and ensure system reliability based on current conditions. The operational plan is modified throughout the year during monthly deep dive meetings to stay aligned with targets, while meeting all operational constraints amid changing conditions. 
Includes the targeted burn equivalent needed, along with the combination of units to achieve the targeted burn, as well as unit specific outage information.  
Number in parenthesis is the target tons of burn that were needed, while actuals appear next to this value.  Columns C, E, G, etc. are the cumulative delta between the Actuals and the projected targeted burn. 
Green – Actuals above targeted burn.
Red – Actuals below targeted burn.
These plans can be altered and optimized for economic benefit by Fleet Operations.
