Question:

Refer to the July 14th data production and the forecasted B2026_EBUD_GPC RTP Fuel Credit
Resource Detail files provided.

   b. For the provided Excel mapping file, including the list of all contracts modeled in Aurora.

   c. Provide the forecasted system lambda for each hour of the forecast period (2026-2028)

   d. Confirm that the forecasted AIER rate for each hour is provided in the “Hourly Summary”

      forecast period.


Response:

   a. Please see STF-JKA-2-1 Attachment A for 2026 Fuel Credit Resource Detail and STF-
      JKA-2-1 Attachment B for 2028 Fuel Credit Resource Detail.

   b. Please see STK-JKA-2-1 Attachment C for the updated mapping file with the PPAs
      modeled in Aurora and what costs are included in the RTP Fuel Credit Forecast.

   c. Please see STK-JKA-2-1 Attachment D for the forecasted system lambda for each hour of
      the forecast period (2026-2028).

   d. BOS_lambda and forecasted AIER are definitionally the same thing in Aurora output,
      because there is no settlement in forecasted views. The forecast is the expected settlement
      in projections.
