                                       LEGAL NOTICE

Consistent with the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (“Commission”) May 28, 2026 Short
Order Adopting Stipulation in Docket No. 56765, the Commission will review the methodology
by which Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) credits real time pricing (“RTP”) revenue
to the Fuel Cost Recovery (“FCR”) revenues and the allocation Georgia Power is making under
such methodology. This matter has been assigned Docket No. 57171 and has been scheduled for
hearing before the Commission. The Company direct hearing will begin on September 1, 2026, at
9:30 a.m. and will be held in Room 110 of the Commission offices located at 244 Washington
Street, SW, Atlanta, Georgia 30334-5701. Please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office,
Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only)
to confirm the location of the hearing.

In accordance with O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59(c), persons wishing to intervene must file a Petition to
Intervene with the Commission within thirty (30) days of the first publication of this notice.

Pursuant to the Commission’s Alternative eFile instructions, any pre-filed testimony or statements
by intervenors must be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary by no later than 4:00
p.m. on September 23, 2026. All testimony must be pre-filed. Parties of record must also serve
one another copies of any pre-filed testimony. The hearing on intervenor testimony is scheduled
to begin October 15, 2026, at 9:30 a.m.

If you have a disability and will need assistance or accommodation to participate, or need further
information, please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission
at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) at least one day prior to the hearing in
this matter.

This notice is published at the direction of the Commission.
