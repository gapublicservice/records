COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and, pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively, “data requests”), to be answered under oath by designated representatives or agents of Georgia Power Company.  Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).  Staff requests that an original and five (5) copies be filed with the Executive Secretary of the Commission.  Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets.  As contemplated by law, responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before October 22, 2026.

PUBLIC DISCLOSURE

Program Structure and Zero Emission Credit (ZEC) Framework

Regarding proposed uprate ZEC program, please explain whether the Company is proposing a standalone, distinct program specific to nuclear uprates, or if this represents an expansion of the existing Flex-REC program.  Compare and Contrast the proposed program to the Flex-REC program and identify limitations with the Flex-REC program.

Please confirm whether the recent xxxxxx xxxxxxxxx constitutes the entirety of the eligible credit pool for this uprate offering, or if the Company anticipates additional counterparties or credit pools.

Provide the current subscription status of the Flex-REC program. Additionally, please clarify how existing nuclear ZECs/RECs are currently accounted for or assigned to specific customers, and whether any are uncommitted.  If Company produced RECs are not being monetized to the benefits of customers, please explain why not.

Please state whether the Company anticipates similar contractual structures, ZEC offerings, or rate impacts resulting from potential or planned uprates at Plant Vogtle.

Please provide a copy of the ZEC program tariff and any internal documentation the Company used to develop or create the program. This should include estimates for both program pricing, participation, revenues, and detail how the ZEC program costs and associated revenue will be tracked.  Specifically, please explain if ZEC revenues will be used to offset capital and/or flow through FCR as a renewable program.

Financial and Tax Assumptions

In the 2025 IRP, the Company assumed a State Investment Tax Credit (ITC) of approximately $xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx. State ITCs appear to be omitted from the current Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) model. Please explain if the Company is no longer anticipating State ITCs for these uprates and provide the regulatory or economic rationale for this change.

Please provide the analytical justification for the changes in the State Tax Rate (decreased from xxxxxxxxxxxxxx) and the Property Tax Rate (decreased from 0.52% to 0.43%) utilized in the current GPC evaluation versus prior filings.

Google Agreement & Revenue Accounting

Please confirm that the "xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx xxxxxxxx" line item represents upfront capital provided by xxxxxxx.

In the file B26_AV_Tool_HatchEPU - GPC Ownership TRADE SECRET.xlsx on the "Annual Economics" tab, the PEC payment schedule does not reflect the xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx outlined in the Term Sheet, and instead reflects a credit extended over xx years. Please reconcile this discrepancy, detail exactly how the xxxxxxxxx total contribution will be applied, and explain why the Company is not utilizing the contributions in-year to avoid financing charges.

Refer to B26_AV_Tool_HatchEPU - GPC Ownership TRADE SECRET.xlsx , “Cost Inputs” tab
Please explain what the "Regulatory Asset" line item (Row 8) is accounting for in the same file specified above. Clarify whether this asset is being established to bridge costs to the 2028 Rate Case or for another designated accounting treatment.
If the Regulatory Asset is intended to defer costs until the 2028 rate case, please provide the Company’s preliminary estimate of the retail rate impact (in percentage and total dollars) this specific deferral will have on typical residential and commercial customer bills.

Confirm that the estimated $xxxxxxx ZEC credit from the xxxxxxx agreement is modeled without escalation and please explain the precise accounting mechanism for this revenue. Please specify whether these inflows will be credited through the Fuel Cost Recovery (FCR) mechanism, base revenues, or as an offset to Capital and/or Operation and Maintenance (O&M) expenses.

Cost-Benefit Analysis Methodology 

Regarding the Company’s Production Cost Energy Value Calculation
Why did the company assume a 1200 MW unit to determine the energy savings, instead of a smaller block?
Please provide the annual MWh modeled on both a system and GPC ownership share bases as modeled in Aurora (1200 MW) and as scaled to the uprate.  If the modeled energy value does not equal the energy used for PTC and ZEC calculations on “PTC_ZEC” tab (row 9).  Please explain.

Please confirm that the current energy benefit profile utilizes Aurora system generation modeling updated to Budget 2026 (B2026) fuel price assumptions. 
Provide a copy of the B2026 fuel price forecast used in this analysis and a side by side comparison to the B2025 assumptions used in the IRP.
Please provide the assumed average cost of Hatch generation.  Provide fuel, average heat rate, and average $/MWh for each year of the study.
Please provide the avoided cost system average of the replacement generation by  year. 

Please provide the Aurora run project files, inputs, and outputs for the updated uprate evaluation cases. 

Regarding the MG0 analysis, please explain how much of the decrease in energy benefit is driven by change in gas price and how much is driven by a decrease in expected energy output.  

Please explain the basis for the Company’s B2026 capacity value and retail capacity price forecast (RCPF)/Economic Carrying Charge analysis.  Please explain how the net capacity value of the uprates are impacted by the increase of the capacity value ($/kW-yr) increase, but a decrease in realized increase capacity (MW). Please clarify if this capacity valuation is consistent with the assumptions used in the All-Source Request for Proposals (ASRFP).

The current CBA includes xxxxxxx in transmission costs that were not included in the 2025 IRP. Please state whether this addition is tied to the plant output increase or excess capacity at the Point of Interconnection (POI). Provide the status and expected completion date of the Company’s facilities impact and delivery studies (e.g., August 2026). Provide a copy of all internal studies used to derive the estimated transmission costs, this is an ongoing request. 

Capital Expenditure & Project Risk Mitigation

Provide the capital spend curve and underlying assumptions as well as the revenue requirement model (e.g. SAM model) used to develop the associated revenue requirements.  Please compare the capital spend curve assumptions provided in this latest analysis to those provided in the informal February filing.

The 2026 CBA model separates baseline capital from incremental capital, showing incremental costs extending through the end of the plant's life. This contrasts with the 2025 IRP, which showed a planned spending offset from 2031–2039 and zero incremental capital thereafter. Please reconcile this accounting change and provide a detailed itemization of the specific projects subject to accelerated capital tracking.

Please clarify whether the Company's latest evaluation includes all capital costs required to execute the uprate, or if certain costs have been omitted under the assumption they would occur in the normal course of business. If omissions exist, provide the specific timeline of when those baseline costs would have otherwise been incurred.

Does the Company plan on implementing a strict project Cost Cap, a minimum MW/MWh generation guarantee, or assigning cost-of-service overruns to large-load customers via a specialized tariff mechanism (e.g., a MAC charge). Please explain what safe guards are provided to customers through this proposed tariff and resource request?


Outage Planning and Regulatory Approvals

Please provide the estimated incremental outage duration required to implement the uprate. Quantify the economic and capital impact if the scheduled outage time is significantly increased.

In the original 2025 IRP analysis, the Hatch Unit 2 uprate was scheduled to be in-service earlier (2029) than Unit 1. The updated estimates shows Unit 2 coming online in xxxxx, after Unit 1 (2030). Please explain the operational or regulatory drivers behind this schedule reversal.

Please explain why the Company anticipates less uprate capacity than what was modeled in 2025 IRP?

Provide the current timeline, specifications, and approval status of all applications submitted to the Department of Energy (DOE) technical team and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) regarding the pending Hatch Units 1 & 2 Extended Power Uprate (EPU) environmental review and project filings. Please provide a copy of all information submitted to both the DOE and NRC.
