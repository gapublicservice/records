STF-ASR-14-5

Question: 

Preliminary Surveys and Investigations (PS&I).  

Please explain fully and in detail the Company’s efforts to analyze the PS&I accounts, and explain whether and why the costs of certain PS&I projects were recategorized in 2025.

Please provide a reconciliation that shows which specific PS&I projects (and their related costs) were recategorized in 2025.  Identify, quantify and explain each cost that was recategorized.


Response:

Please note, the recategorization process was performed for the balances as of December 31, 2023, for the 2024 ASR filing and the recategorized balances continued for future years. No specific recategorization occurred in 2025.

With the implementation of new accounting systems in 2022, all PS&I balances and transactions that were previously tracked through individual accounts based on the type of PS&I activity were merged into a single consolidated PS&I FERC sub account. During this transition, most of the data was converted with key identifiable information such as project number, description, and project organization in order to track the costs by the original category type. However, some existing PS&I costs were transferred without preserving identifiable information. As a result, categorizing transactions precisely within the PS&I account became significantly more difficult.

To address the categorization issue, each reconciler for various PS&I categories was tasked with reviewing and reconciling total PS&I transactions from 2022 through 2024. The review process included reviewing details from both the original and consolidated accounts for this time period. For transactions lacking segment data, reconcilers reviewed other sources such as payables, payroll, and journal entries to determine the correct category. Through the review of these sources, any identifiable transactions were re-categorized and/or new categories were added as reflected in the 2024 ASR. Any unidentified transactions that could not be tied to specific projects, in the amount of $27 thousand, were written off to expense in March 2025.

Please see STF-ASR-14-5 Attachment for a reconciliation of recategorized PS&I balances as of December 31, 2023. No specific recategorization occurred in 2025.

STF-ASR-14-4

Question: 

Preliminary Surveys and Investigations (PS&I).  Refer to the responses to STF-ASR-9-64 and STF-ASR-5-65 from the 2024 ASR.  On a 13-month average basis, the Company’s PS&I costs included in retail rate base increased by $48.375 million ($92.173 million - $43.198 million), or approximately 110% from 2024 to 2025.  A comparison of the attachments provided in STF-ASR-9-64 and STF-ASR-5-65 from the 2024 ASR indicates that largest increases were with the New Generation, Fossil and New Nuclear & Nuclear Uprates categories. 

Refer to the STF-ASR-9-64 Attachment.  Why was the New Generation category $7.456 million, or 53.2% higher (on a 13-month average basis) in 2025 than in 2024?  To what specific new generation does this relate?  Explain fully.

Refer to the STF-ASR-9-64 Attachment.  Why was the Fossil category $24.822 million, or 229.7% higher (on a 13-month average basis) in 2025 than in 2024?  To what specific fossil generation does this relate?  Explain fully.

Refer to the STF-ASR-9-64 Attachment.  Why was the New Nuclear & Nuclear Uprates category $15.022 million, or 1026.9% higher (on a 13-month average basis) in 2025 than in 2024? To what new nuclear and nuclear uprates does this relate?  Explain fully.

Does the Company anticipate that the cost of New Nuclear generation will be economical?  If so, on what basis?  If not, why is cost being incurred for that category?

Are any of the increased PS&I costs that were recorded in 2025 related to serving new large load customers, such as data centers?  If so, how much PS&I cost in each category relate to serving new large load customers?  Explain fully and identify the amount.


Response:

The increase in New Generation PS&I costs is attributable to potential future Solar, Battery Energy Storage, and Thermal projects that are currently proposed in active Requests for Proposals (“RFPs”) or being evaluated for future RFPs. PS&I costs incurred in 2025 also included the Company-owned Proposals that were certified in the 2029-2031 All Source RFP in December 2025.

The increase in Fossil PS&I costs is attributable to weatherization and carbon sequestration initiatives at various plants.

The increase in New Nuclear & Nuclear Uprates is attributable to costs associated with site studies for land in Stewart County and power uprates at existing Company nuclear generating units. Please also see the Company’s response to STF-ASR-14-6.

Consistent with the Company’s 2025 IRP Filing, preserving nuclear as a long-term option has value for customers. As noted in the 2025 IRP in Chapter 8.12, nuclear resources are selected in the resource mix study for several planning scenarios.  Accordingly, the Company continues to perform in-depth assessments of potential project sites, evaluate available and emerging technologies, and engage with stakeholders in developing improved methods to potentially deploy new nuclear generation projects.

Any PS&I costs incurred in 2025 were not specifically related to serving new large-load customers.
