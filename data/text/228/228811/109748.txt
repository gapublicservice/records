INSTRUCTIONS
1.   If you contend that any response to any data request may be withheld under the attorney-client
     privilege, the attorney work product doctrine or any other privilege or basis, please state the
     following with respect to each such response in order to explain the basis for the claim of privilege
     and to permit adjudication of the propriety of that claim:
         a. the privilege asserted and its basis.
         b. the nature of the information withheld;
         c. the subject matter of the document, except to the extent that you claim it is privileged.
2.   The answers provided should first restate the question asked and also provide the name of
     the Company employee(s) or agents responsible for compiling and providing the information
     contained in each answer.
3.   These data requests are to be answered with reference to all information in your possession, custody
     or control or reasonably available to you. These discovery requests are intended to include requests
     for information, which is physically within your possession, custody or control as well as in the
     possession, custody or control of your agents, attorneys, or other third parties from which such
     documents may be obtained.
4.   If any data request cannot be responded to or answered in full, answer to the extent possible and
     specify the reasons for your inability to answer fully.
5.   These data requests are continuing in nature and require supplemental responses should information
     unknown to you at the time you serve your responses to these interrogatories subsequently become
     known.

Ten-Tear Transmission Plan


STF-DEA-8-1      There are several projects that did not carry over from the 2022
                 Ten-Year Plan (TYP) into the 2025 TYP. These projects are not on
                 the cancelled project list provided in the 2025 TYP. The following
                 projects have ISDs beyond 2025 and are not included in the 2025
                 TYP: 19611, 20005, 18492, 19997, 19991, 20067, 19623, 20000,
                 19599, 20019, 20020, 14222, 19628, 20003, 12063, 15228, 20004,
                 19187, 17993. Please provide reports or documentation that describe
                 the status of each of those projects.
STF-DEA-8-2      Please identify all projects that were completed between the release
                 of the 2022 TYP and the 2025 TYP. Please provide the project
                 identification number, date of project completion, project cost, and
                 any modifications between the project scope detailed in the 2022
                 TYP and the final project design.
STF-DEA-8-3      Please provide all procedure documents, planning manuals,
                 processes, and all other material on how Georgia Power's
                 transmission planning team conducts the Ten-Year Plan process. This
                 includes assumptions based on powerflow analyses, load forecasting,
                 and other location-based considerations around transmission
                 planning infrastructure.
STF-DEA-8-4      From the 2025 IRP Main Document 11.3 Strategic Transmission, “A
                 key component of an IRP is the ability to consider impacts to the
                 transmission system when making generating resource decisions.”
                 Explain the separate transmission evaluations being made now. Does
                 it also include large loads as well as generation?
STF-DEA-8-5      Please provide planning reports or any other documentation that best
                 describes project progress from 2022 to present
STF-DEA-8-6      Other than the semiannual board meetings, please provide any
                 outgoing material on project statuses and progress given to the
                 stakeholders through meetings, email, etc. This can include memos,
                 PowerPoint slides, and graphics.

                 TEAMS numbered project in the 2025 plan.
                     a. Please confirm whether TEAMS numbers are ever reused or
                        reassigned to a different project scope, and if so, provide the
                        protocol for how that is tracked and disclosed.
                     b. If any of the projects from the 2022 TYP are listed under
                        different TEAMS numbers in the 2025 TYP, please provide a
                        reconciliation of the project numbering.
STF-DEA-8-12     If a project scope changes in any way, will the TEAMS number be
                 maintained? What would cause a project’s TEAMS number to
                 change?
STF-DEA-8-13     For the projects that persist across both the 2022 TYP and 2025 TYP,
                 cost estimates increased by more than xxxxx for many of them,
                 including projects with the following TEAMS numbers: 18832,
                 18679, 19610, 18960, 20015, 19632, 19601, 19996, 19992, 20002,
                 18668. For each of these, please provide the historical cost estimate
                 data and a brief narrative explanation for what specifically changed
                 (e.g., scope, contingency, materials, etc.).

                 forecast category. The version of Table 5 in the 2025 TYP introduces
                 a new "Large Load Forecast" column for each entity, separate from
                 the general coincidence forecast. Please explain in what year this
                 methodology was officially implemented and were there large loads
