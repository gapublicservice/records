      COMES NOW the Staff of the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) and,

pursuant to the authority vested in it by the Commission pursuant to O.C.G.A. § 46-2-57, herein

propounds the following interrogatories and requests for production of documents (collectively,

Power Company. Staff requests that a complete set of the responses and supporting documents be

filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary in the manner set forth in Utility Rule 515-2-.04(4).

Commission. Accompanied therewith shall be an electronic version of the filing, which shall

be made on a 3 ½ inch diskette or a CD ROM containing an electronic version of its filing in

Microsoft Word® for text documents or Excel® for spreadsheets. As contemplated by law,

responses to these Data Requests are expected from Georgia Power Company on or before October

19, 2026.

STF-PIA-23-1    Does Georgia Power currently track grid utilization metrics at the
                transmission, substation, and distribution level? If so, please identify the
                metrics and provide a brief description of each. If not, what information would
                be needed to develop such metrics?
STF-PIA-23-2    Does Georgia Power currently calculate an aggregate system utilization figure
                (energy delivered divided by available capacity) for generation, transmission,
                and distribution? If so- please provide the details.
STF-PIA-23-3    Given that Georgia Power already maintains a target reserve margin above
                the industry standard of ~15%, can some of that existing transmission capacity
                headroom be used more efficiently to serve uncertain large-load growth
                before additional infrastructure is built, and if so, how much new large-load
                demand could that reserve margin accommodate before triggering new
                transmission investment?
STF-PIA-23-4    Is there a feeder- or substation-level constraint report (which parts of the
                distribution system are already near their limits) that staff could review,
                similar to a hosting-capacity map?
STF-PIA-23-5    Does the Large Load Economic Development Report show where (which
                substations or transmission zones) new load is expected to connect, so staff
                can judge whether it lands in areas with spare capacity?
STF-PIA-23-6    Do any DSM/energy-efficiency programs get credited toward reducing
                system peak demand in a way that shows up in the reserve margin or IRP
                planning numbers?
STF-PIA-23-7    How does SAIDI/SAIFI performance get weighed against utilization goals
                — is there a risk that running assets harder to raise utilization could work
                against the reliability metrics Georgia Power is already held to?
STF-PIA-23-8    Which department/division team at Georgia Power currently reviews “system
                utilization” - planning, distribution, engineering, regulatory affairs- or other?
                What is the Company’s current practice of system utilization?
