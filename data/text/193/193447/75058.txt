Docket No. 44280

COMPLIANCE ORDER
In Re:Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Rate Case


On January 30, 2023, Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or “Company”) filed with the Georgia Public Service Commission (“Commission”) tariffs and the Rules and Regulations of the Company in compliance with the Commission’s Final Order Adopting Settlement Agreement as Modified in Docket No. 44280, adopted December 20, 2022, and filed on December 30, 2022. 

To wit:

ORDERED FURTHER, that for purposes of the rate increase in the year 2023, Georgia Power shall file compliance tariffs within 30 days of the issuance of this Order, reflecting rates to implement the rate increases ordered herein.  These tariffs shall reflect the rate allocations adopted in this Order, and shall be subject to the Commission's review for final approval.

The Company proposed an effective date of March 1, 2023, for the compliance filing.  The Commission’s Public Interest Advocacy Staff (“Staff”) reviewed the filing made by the Company to ensure it complies with the Commission’s Order in this docket.  During this review, Staff met with the Company and reviewed the revenue model provided to Staff for further in-depth analysis and review.  The compliance filing included the Company’s Rules, Regulations, and Rate Schedules as approved by the Commission. 

The revised tariffs included in the Company’s compliance filing were designed to increase revenues by $194 million for Traditional Base Rates, $37 million for Demand Side Management ($18.4 million DSM-R and $18.5 million DSM-C), $6 million for Municipal Franchise Fees, and reduce revenues by $21 million for Environmental Compliance Cost Recovery as approved by Commission Order.

Staff concluded that the tariffs, Rules, and Regulations filed by Georgia Power were designed to collect the proper level of revenue as ordered by this Commission, based on its review to day.  In the Commission’s Administrative Session held February 21, 2023, Staff recommended approval of the Company’s compliance filing, with an effective date of March 1, 2023. An additional review period through February 24, 2023 was approved as part of the recommendation. 

In the event Staff subsequently identifies any issues on or before February 24, 2023, that may impact the March 1, 2023, effective dates of the tariffs and the Rules and Regulations, Georgia Power will suspend the effective dates of such tariffs and the Rules and Regulations and the Commission will resolve such issues at its regularly scheduled March 7, 2023, Administrative Session.

*****

WHEREFORE, IT IS ORDERED, that the permanent Tariffs, Rules, and Regulations filed by Georgia Power Company on January 30, 2023, in compliance with the order in Docket No. 44280 are hereby approved.

ORDERED FURTHER, that if Staff subsequently identifies any issues, errors or inaccuracies in the Company’s compliance filing, on or before February 24, 2023, Georgia Power will suspend the effective dates of such tariffs and the Rules and Regulations and the Commission will resolve such issues at its regularly scheduled March 7, 2023, Administrative Session.

ORDERED FURTHER, that all findings, conclusions, statements, and directives made by the Commission and contained in the foregoing sections of this Order are hereby adopted as findings of fact, conclusions of law, statements of regulatory policy, and orders of this Commission.

ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing, oral argument or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of this Order unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.

ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over these matters is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order or Orders as this Commission may deem just and proper.

The above by action of the Commission at its Administrative Session on the 21st day of February 2023.
