STF-PIA-13-1

Question:

Please describe the Company’s Knowledge Base and identify when it was implemented.

Response:

The Company’s Knowledge Base, as used by its Customer Services organizations, is a collection of information in the form of searchable articles that provides users with access to job aids, policy and standards, and frequently asked questions, among other useful documents.

The Company transitioned its Customer Service Standards to a digital format in 1999 in the form of internal intranet pages via the platform TeamSite. Customer Service Standards were then included as content in the form of articles when the eGain knowledge base was launched in 2018.

The current knowledge base was developed using the Oracle Knowledge Advanced (OKA) platform, which is a component of the Oracle Service Cloud CX platform. The OKA knowledge base was implemented in November of 2022.

STF-PIA-13-10

Question:

Can you provide an example of a complex query that the Knowledge Base system can handle?

Response:

An example of a complex query handled by the knowledge base is the process that allows a customer to rate a Frequently Asked Question (FAQ) article they are viewing from the Company’s external website.
To complete this query, the Company’s online customer care page communicates via Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC), which calls more than one OKA application programming interface (API). The first API call retrieves the article record ID and the next API call sends the selected record ID of the customer’s rating of 1-5.

STF-PIA-13-13

Question:

What type of knowledge does the Knowledge Base system cover, and in what formats?

Response:

The Knowledge Base is currently configured for six Content Types, all of which are articles: Announcement, Department Page, FAQ, Job Aid, Knowledge Document, and Policy & Standards.

FAQ articles are intended for external customer consumption to address common inquiries.

Job Aid, Knowledge Document, and Policy & Standards articles are for internal use primarily by customer service employees.

Announcement articles provide insights as to new features, policy updates, or other useful information relevant to customer service employees.

Department Page articles include contact information and other general information that specific customer service departments want to make available to internal agents.

Question:

Please explain the architecture and underlying technologies used in the knowledge base system.

Response:

Oracle Knowledge Advanced (OKA) is a component of Oracle Service Cloud configured by Southern Company to meet business needs. Knowledge Administrators utilize the Agent Desktop known as the Console to maintain user roles, views, workflows, and to author and publish knowledge Content Types, Categories, and Articles. Administrators can also view and respond to feedback submitted by Knowledge Users in the Customer Portal.

Knowledge Users utilize the Customer Portal to access knowledge articles and documents based on the role(s) and view(s) assigned to them. Users can provide feedback, rate articles, and recommend new content within the Portal.

STF-PIA-13-6

Question:

How does the Knowledge Base system handle synonyms and related terms in queries?

Response:

The system dictionary uses concepts and synonyms to associate different words that have the same meaning with one another. A concept is simply a word and its synonyms, which the dictionary treats as a single object.

Intelligent Search uses concepts to match words in both users’ questions and in the Knowledge Base content, based on their meaning. It also recognizes that some concepts are more important to users than others and uses that information to prioritize answers within search results.

STF-PIA-13-7

Question:

Please describe the process of updating and maintaining the Knowledge Base system.

Response:

Oracle Knowledge Advanced (OKA) was configured to include several console level roles. These include KB_Author, KB_Reviewer/Approver, and KB_Manager. These roles must be requested from the Southern Company CoolCompliance. Requests must be reviewed and approved by an application owner and the employee’s manager.

Once approved, the user is provided access to the OKA Console where they can review, respond to, and implement suggestions for changes from end users to existing content within published articles.

All changes to article content are subject to independent review and must be approved by a KB_Reviewer/Approver or KB_Manager before publishing. An audit history of changes is captured within the platform for comparison with the current version of published articles. The audit history captures Date and Time, User, Version, Action Taken, Workflow Used, and Comments. If needed, the author can perform a side-by-side comparison of two versions of the article and can roll back changes if needed.

Question:

How does the Knowledge Base system handle natural language queries?

Response:

A “locale” designates a language as it is used in a specific region or by a specific population. In applications that support multiple locales, each supported locale has a corresponding interface.

A locale or language is assigned to a “collection” to determine the available interfaces. Collections can be assigned to locales to make them available to only one interface, assigned to languages to make them available to all interfaces based on that language, or assigned to “Auto” to make documents in the collection available to any interfaces based on the detected language.

STF-PIA-13-9

Question:

How does the Knowledge Base system determine the relevance of search results?

Response:

Natural language search is enhanced by using a pre-packaged industry specific search dictionary to provide relevant search results to the word or phrase being searched. Articles can also be manually associated with one another, which provides additional relevant search results. In addition, Oracle Knowledge Advanced (OKA) will, over time, identify which articles relate to one another based on which articles are regularly viewed in succession by users.
