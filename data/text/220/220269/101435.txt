===== DKT 44280 STF-PIA-25 PD/STF-PIA-25-3.docx =====
STF-PIA-25-3

Question: 

Has the Company investigated the use of amorphous core transformers?

 If so, please identify the pros and cons of amorphous core transformers in relation to standard transformers traditionally used by the Company.  

 If not, please explain.  


Response: 

The Company currently has amorphous core transformers on its distribution system. 
The pros and cons are as follows:
Pros:
Lower no-load (core) losses compared to silicon steel core transformers. 

Cons:
Requires a larger transformer core and more windings, which leads to an increase in weight and dimension.
Larger dimensions could lead to larger required pads for pad-mounted designs.
Larger dimensions and increase in weight could lead to re-design of existing pole installations and require larger poles in new installations. 
Transportation and material handling procedures and practices could change due to larger, heavier units. 
The amorphous core material can be more expensive to produce than silicon steel cores.
Core noise is increased in comparison to a silicon steel core transformer. 
Ferroresonance is more likely with a low-loss transformer. 
There is mixed data on the short-circuit withstand of amorphous core transformers vs. silicon steel designs.

STF-PIA-25-5

Question: 

What is the current approval process and timeline from initiating a market request (request for information (RFI), request for proposals or request for quotes) to signing of contract for the purchase of transformers?
 
Are amorphous transformer suppliers among the manufacturers that the Company has engaged with or negotiated with for the supply of transformers? If available, please provide a breakdown on lead times.
   
Referencing STF-PIA-24-19-c. Identify all suppliers for pole mount and pad mount transformers that the Company is in the process of negotiating with.  

If available, provide a lead time on when a contract could be procured. 
Provide the state in which the transformers are manufactured.  


Response: 

The process for a transformer manufacturer to be approved is as follows:

Product / Supplier Overview
Request for Customer Assessment (when necessary)
Conformance to Specifications
Approval Drawing and Test Report Review
Evaluation of Samples (when necessary)
Additional Product Testing (when necessary)
Supplier Plant Evaluation 
Field Pilot Project (depending upon the nature and complexity of the product)
Product Approval

The timeline for completing the technical approval process typically ranges from 12 to 24 months.
Requests for Information (RFI) typically range from 4 to 6 weeks.
Requests for Proposals (RFP) typically range from 4 to 12 weeks, depending on the scope of supply included in the request.
Executing a master contract covering terms and conditions can range from 4 to 24 weeks.

Yes, the Company currently receives amorphous core units from one manufacturer, REDACTED.  Please see the Company’s response to STF-PIA-25-2 for the current lead time for REDACTED.  There is currently no difference in the lead-time for amorphous core units versus silicon core units.  The number of suppliers supplying amorphous core units compared to silicon core units may increase in the future as manufacturers make design changes to comply with the new DOE ruling.

The Company is not actively pursuing any new supply agreements.  REDACTED.
