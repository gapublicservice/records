STF-PIA-26-8

Question: 

What is the Company’s desired level of reserve Distribution transformers and how is it determined and calculated? Please provide an example.

Response:

The Company’s stocking level for transformers is determined by several factors, including internal transformer usage rates, current lead times, job requirements/forecasted demand and safety stock. Day-to-day working inventory levels are based on these factors, while additional inventory is maintained specifically for emergency response and is informed by historical storm usage and the needs of critical infrastructure. The process is fluid as all factors change based on demand and market conditions. Since the onset of the pandemic, inventory levels have increased due to heightened demand, extended lead times, and limited manufacturing availability. The Company continually reviews its inventory parameters and updates them as necessary to ensure it has adequate emergency reserve inventory as well as adequate reserve working stock to support normal operations. These parameters can change as internal transformer needs change based on economic growth (internal usage rate) and transformer market lead times.  The GPC inventory level as of December 12, 2024, for distribution transformers is approximately 28,000 and can change daily based on usage in the field and supplier transformer shipments/receipts into inventory.

                                 SECTION III - RFP BACKGROUND

Background

Southern Company is embarking on a supply chain transformation effort and taking a holistic approach to
managing external spend and suppliers. Category management is expected to deliver value creation
benefits in addition to cost reductions including:

   Addressing and reducing supply chain risks by providing reliable and timely supply/services

   Supporting continuous improvement in quality and services levels

   Enabling greater supplier collaboration to better manage base business and drive
    innovation/performance improvement

Distribution transformers is a key category for Southern Company. This RFP initiative is focused on
identifying a set of manufacturers that meet high quality standards at the right price while identifying
opportunities for continuous improvement across the electric utilities. We are seeking to identify, increase
collaboration with, and shift more volume towards selected suppliers to build stronger relationships and to
drive efficiency throughout the purchasing process.

This new process includes (1) increasing transparency of our forecasted volume, (2) understanding
suppliers’ current and developing capabilities, (3) introducing new suppliers, and (4) collaborating with
suppliers on continuous improvement.

    (1) Transparency of forecasted volume: We intend to create contracts with provisions for volume and
        time-based commitments. Therefore, this RFP provides the opportunity for suppliers to convey
        any manufacturing efficiencies driven by more accurate demand forecasting and/or strong volume
        indications or guarantees. Pricing and lead times should be quoted within the provided volume
        tiers; Bidders are encouraged to consider discounts or rebates. Volume ranges are provided in the
        Scope of Work section of this document. In addition, suppliers should provide insight into their
        freight minimums and order minimums as the volume ranges are reviewed.

    (2) Understanding suppliers’ current and developing capabilities: We understand that suppliers may
        have capabilities that Southern is not aware of. This RFP provides the opportunity for Bidders to
        submit alternate solutions, or include any developing capabilities. In responding to this RFP,
        Bidders are encouraged to include Functional Equivalents to the provided specifications, and/or
        any new or developing capabilities into their response.

    (3) Introducing new suppliers: Southern believes in strong supplier relationships and aims to continue
        to work closely with suppliers. However, in the coming years, we expect to increase our demand,
        in particular with pad-mounted transformers, as we invest in our grid at Georgia Power. Therefore,
        we expect new suppliers, or alternate plants of current suppliers, to help supply the increased
        demand. Responding to this bid does not pre-qualify any new suppliers or new plants; a
        comprehensive quality assessment will be required of any new supplier or new plant. Bidders are
        requested to quote qualifications and pricing for all capable plants to ensure a comprehensive
        assessment of the Bidder’s capabilities and capacity.
