Enclosure
LEGAL NOTICE

Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power”) has filed with the Georgia Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) its Application for Certification of Robins, Moody, Hammond, and McGrau Ford Phase II Battery Energy Storage Systems, as well as its Engineering, Procurement, and Construction Agreement Compliance Filing for the McGrau Ford Phase I Battery Energy Storage System. This matter has been assigned Docket No. 55378 and has been scheduled for hearing before the Commission pursuant to the Integrated Resource Plan Act, O.C.G.A § 46-3A-1 et seq. The hearing for Docket No. 55378 will begin on October 31, 2024 at 9:30 a.m. and will be held in Room 110 of the Commission offices located at 244 Washington Street, SW, Atlanta, Georgia 30334-5701. Please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) to confirm the location of the hearing.

In accordance with O.C.G.A. § 46-2-59(c), persons wishing to intervene must file a Petition to Intervene with the Commission within thirty (30) days of the first publication of this notice.  

Pursuant to the Commission’s Alternative eFile instructions, any pre-filed testimony or statements by interveners must be filed with the Commission’s Executive Secretary by no later than 4:00 p.m. on October 11, 2024. All testimony must be pre-filed. Parties of record must also serve one another copies of any pre-filed testimony. Hearings on intervener testimony will be heard immediately following the direct testimony of the Commission’s Public Interest Advocacy Staff, which follows the direct testimony of Georgia Power.

If you have a disability and will need assistance or accommodation to participate, or need further information, please contact the Executive Secretary’s Office, Georgia Public Service Commission at (404) 656-4501 or 1-800-282-5813 (inside Georgia only) at least one day prior to the hearing in this matter.

This notice is published at the direction of the Commission.


/s/ Jeremiah Haswell
Jeremiah Haswell
Director, Regulatory Affairs 
Georgia Power Company
