RE:Georgia Power Company’s Update of Base Tariffs; Docket No. 44280

Dear Ms. Tanner:

In accordance with O.C.G.A. § 46-2-25 and the Georgia Public Service Commission’s (“Commission”) Order Adopting Settlement Agreement in Docket No. 44280 (“2022 Rate Case Order”), Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) hereby files an original and one copy of the updated traditional base tariffs, the Environmental Compliance Cost Recovery (“ECCR”) tariff, the Demand Side Management (“DSM”) tariffs (residential and commercial) and the Municipal Franchise Fee (“MFF”) tariff, along with the temporary rate adjustment riders. The revised tariffs become effective on January 1, 2025.  


The primary updates to the tariffs are as follows:  

Traditional Base Rates – The traditional base rate tariffs will collect an additional $194 million on an annual basis. This includes a $315 million increase for costs to be incurred by the Company in 2025 associated with additional capital investments and operating expenses previously approved by the Commission, partially offset by $122 million of total savings being returned to customers resulting from the Georgia income tax rate change as approved by the Commission on September 4, 2024. In addition, the Company is recovering revenue erosion associated with the Time of Use – Food and Drink tariff (“TOU-FD”) conversions from all TOU-FD rate customers as approved by the Commission.

Environmental Compliance – The ECCR tariff will collect an additional $126 million on an annual basis for ECCR costs to be incurred by the Company in 2025, which is comprised of a $135 million increase in CCR ARO compliance costs, partially offset by a $9 million reduction in traditional ECCR costs. The increase in the CCR ARO compliance costs reflects the actual CCR ARO regulatory asset balance through July 2024, as well as updated CCR ARO estimates for August 2024 through December 2025, as authorized by the Commission in the 2022 Rate Case Order and presented in the latest semi-annual CCR ARO filing in October 2024. Further, the incremental increase from 2024 to 2025 is primarily attributable to the application of the 2022 ASR sharing of $117 million to the CCR ARO regulatory asset, which significantly reduced the 2024 CCR ARO revenue requirement included in the 2024 Compliance Filing. The decrease in traditional ECCR costs excluding CCR ARO compliance costs reflects the costs associated with environmental capital investments and operating expenses previously approved by the Commission.

DSM – The DSM tariffs will collect $22 million less on an annual basis for DSM costs to be incurred by the Company in 2025. This decrease reflects the costs and Additional Sum associated with the Company’s previously approved DSM programs, and the true-up of 2023 actual results which resulted in an over-recovery of $34 million.

MFF – The MFF tariff will collect an additional $9 million on an annual basis primarily for the net increases noted above.

Income Qualified Discount (IQD) Expansion- Domestic tariffs have been increased to collect an additional $10 million on an annual basis to reflect the increased participation in the IQD.  This increase reflects the expansion of the IQD that was approved by the Commission as part of the Vogtle Prudency Order in Docket No. 29849.


The total increase reflected in this filing is approximately $306 million, which is approximately $97 million less than the 2025 increase contemplated in the 2022 Rate Case Order. 

Each of the increases/decreases outlined above have been calculated in accordance with the principles established in the 2022 Rate Case Order. Specifically, the Company has (1) utilized the most current CCR ARO compliance cost estimates for purposes of calculating the ECCR tariff; (2) utilized the projections for DSM costs approved by the Commission in Docket No. 44161 and a true-up of 2023 actual results; and (3) adjusted the MFF rate to reflect the updated actual percentage of MFF revenues within municipalities. The 2025 tariff designs for these increases/decreases utilize the most current kWh sales forecast.

The temporary rate adjustment riders will be applied beginning January 1, 2025 to facilitate the proration of the increase on that date.  

If you have any questions, please call me at 404-506-7106.

Sincerely,

/s/ Jeremiah Haswell

Director, Regulatory Affairs
