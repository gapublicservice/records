The locations are pursuant to the following conditions: 

Locations – The 2022 Rate Case Order provides an annual budget of up to $3 million for the program, and a stipulation that any proposed location is subject to a 60-day Right of First Refusal (“ROFR”) process. 

Eligibility – The location falls within a Tier 1 or Tier 2 county under the most current Job Tax Credit Rankings. The most recent Job Tax Credit Rankings for 2024 was presented to Governor Brian Kemp on January 3, 2024.

15-mile restriction – ROFR locations must be within a 15-mile radius from the Crossroads presented in the Company’s filing. 

On April 5, 2024, American’s for Affordable Clean Energy (“AACE”) filed comments on the Company’s Plan. AACE, noted that all but one of the Company’s proposals fall within the guidelines. 

This exception is for proposed location 5 (Hwy 52 & Hwy 411) located in Murray County running within 1 mile of a Pending Alternative Fuel Corridor (“AFC”) identified in round 7 by the Federal Highway Administration on November 7, 2023. 

Using the criteria above, five (5) proposed locations have been selected under ROFR. On March 30, 2024 Country Corner/Fuel Express identified one (1) location subject to ROFR with proposed site 3 located in Jesup, GA. On May 2, 2024 Circle K identified two (2) locations within a 15-mile radius of Georgia Power’s proposed sites; one site located in Jesup has also been identified as a ROFR by Country Corner. This leaves site 10 in Alma being added to the ROFR list. On May 3, 2024 Pilot Travel Centers LLC identified three (3) locations within a 15-mile radius of Georgia Power’s proposed sites: 2, 5, and 8 located in Toccoa, Chatsworth, and Royston respectively.

In its response on April 15, 2024, the Company is aware of the pending AFC in Chatsworth. The Company does not intend to build a site within a mile of the eventual AFC and uses the crossroads presented as a guideline. Furthermore, the Company attests that it can’t go into agreement with a prospective site host until a site has been approved by the Commission. 

On May 16, 2024 Staff recommended the Commission issue an order to approve the six (6) remaining Community Charging locations presented in GPC’s Plan for the year 2024. 

The Commission unanimously approved Staff’s recommendation at the May 21, 2024, Administrative Session. 
* * * * * * * * * * * * *

WHEREFORE IT IS ORDERED that Georgia Power Company’s six (6) remaining Community Charging locations for the year 2024, are hereby approved. 
ORDERED FURTHER, that the most current Georgia Department of Community Affairs Job Tax Credit Tier maps for eligibility consideration in the Community Charging program are hereby adopted.
ORDERED FURTHER, that a 15-mile radius restriction as measured around Georgia Power Company’s proposed locations for eligibility consideration in the Community Charging program is hereby adopted.
ORDERED FURTHER, that a motion for reconsideration, rehearing, or oral argument or any other motion shall not stay the effective date of this Order, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.  
ORDERED FURTHER, that jurisdiction over this matter is expressly retained for the purpose of entering such further Order or Orders as this Commission may deem just and proper.
The above by action of the Commission in Administrative Session on the 21st day of May, 2024.
