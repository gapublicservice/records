Re: Georgia Power Company’s Updated 2024 Community Charging Plan;               Docket No. 44280


Dear Ms. Tanner:

In accordance with the Georgia Public Service Commission’s Order Adopting Settlement Agreement as Modified in Docket No. 44280 (the “Order”), enclosed for filing is Georgia Power Company’s Updated 2024 Community Charging Plan, which identifies potential locations for five (5) Community Chargers Georgia Power Company plans to install in the forthcoming year. These locations replace locations originally filed in the 2024 Community Charging plan filed on March 6, 2024 that were accepted by the electric vehicle charging market under the right of first refusal process outlined in the Order.  The 2024 Community Charging Plan budget for 2024 remains the same as approved in the Order.

If you have any questions, please contact Chris Defnall at 404-506-2393.
