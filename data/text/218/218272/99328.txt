BEFORE THE GEORGIA PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION

GEORGIA POWER COMPANY
DOCKET NO. 55378

Rebuttal Hearing Request

BASIS FOR THE ASSERTION THAT THE 
INFORMATION SUBMITTED IS A TRADE SECRET

As part of Georgia Power Company’s 2023 Integrated Resource Plan Update filed in Docket No. 55378 (“2023 IRP Update”), Georgia Power Company (“Georgia Power” or the “Company”) submits to the Georgia Public Service Commission its response to this rebuttal hearing request (“Response”). In the Response, the Company has provided information used in the Company’s load forecast sensitivity models. Such information (the “Information”) constitutes trade secret information of Southern Company, Georgia Power, and its affiliates and is therefore protected from public disclosure under Commission Rule 515-3-1-.11.

 The Information derives economic value from not being generally known to, and not being readily ascertainable by proper means by other persons who can obtain economic value from its disclosure or use. Specifically, the Information contains competitively sensitive details on the Company’s forecast methodology for determining the impacts of new large loads. Public dissemination of the Information would allow Georgia Power’s competitors, suppliers, and customers access to the Company’s anticipated load strategies and objectives, thereby bestowing insight into the Company’s strategic initiatives and forecasting capabilities. Competitors would obtain an unfair advantage because they are not required to reveal similar information and can structure their offers for competitive choice opportunities based on the Information rather than their own costs, capacity, abilities, strategies, or analysis.    

The Information is subject to substantial procedures to maintain its secrecy. Only select Georgia Power and Southern Company Services personnel are granted access to the Information.  Those personnel receive access only on a “need to know” basis. Parties outside the Company who have been granted access to the Information, if any, have been required to sign confidentiality agreements with respect to the Information.  


HR-2-2

Information requested by: GIPL – Whitfield (Tr. 2188-2189)

Question:
MS. WHITFIELD:  Could I make a hearing request for the sensitivities that have been run by the Company on the load growth since this docket was filed?
CHAIRMAN SHAW:  Okay.  That's fine.  

Response:
The Company has not officially updated its forecast since the 2023 Integrated Resource Plan (“IRP”) Update was filed on October 27, 2023. However, as businesses continue to express interest in coming to Georgia or expanding operations here, and they commit to obtaining electric service from Georgia Power, the Company has completed initial sensitivity analyses on the external adjustment to the Company’s base load forecast to incorporate newly announced loads. The results of these sensitivities, in comparison to the 2023 IRP Update Load Forecast external adjustments, are detailed in TS HR-2-2 Attachment. This attachment indicates minimal change in the load projections through the end of 2027. Thus, no change is needed to the Company’s capacity resource requests through the Winter of 2027/2028, as amended by the Stipulation in the 2023 IRP Update. However, by the end of 2028, expected external adjustments for additional large load customers in the “IRP Rebuttal” sensitivity are projected to be nearly 900 MW higher than what was initially filed. These expected external adjustments are anticipated to exceed 1,300 MW by 2030, and by 2038, they are projected to be nearly 1,600 MW higher.
