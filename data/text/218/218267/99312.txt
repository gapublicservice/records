                 PROPOSED COMMISSION MOTION OF
 THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE AND ALL OTHER FEDERAL

       The United States Department of Defense, on behalf of itself and all other Federal

Executive Agencies (DOD/FEA) respectfully files this Proposed Commission Motion for the

Commission’s consideration in Georgia Power Company’s (Georgia Power) 2023 Integrated

Resource Plan (IRP) Update proceeding (GPSC Docket No. 55378). DOD/FEA requests that the

Commission make a motion to amend the Stipulation filed in this proceeding on 27 MAR 2024

by inserting the following new paragraph:

(22)   Georgia Power is directed to remove existing caps and to expand its current and open

request for proposals for the Georgia Clean and Renewable Energy Subscription Program

(CARES) by at least 1,000 MWs of carbon pollution-free electricity.

       In support of its Motion, DOD/FEA asserts that expanding the existing CARES program

will substantially reduce the need for additional carbon-emitting generation resources in Georgia

Power Company’s generation fleet.

       If the proposed new paragraph (22) is inserted into the Stipulation, DOD/FEA will

support the Commission approving the Stipulation as so modified.
