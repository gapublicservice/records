Re:Georgia Power Company’s 2022 Rate Case, My Power Usage Data Compliance Filing; Docket No. 44280

Dear Ms. Tanner:

In response to Paragraph 44 of the Georgia Public Service Commission’s Order (the “Order”) in the above-referenced Docket, Georgia Power Company (the “Company”) now provides hourly usage data through the My Power Usage tool to all accounts with a few limited exceptions.  The company continues to assess and improve the My Power Usage tool.  The Company’s My Power Usage tool also has been modified as of March 27th, 2024 to export hourly usage data in bulk.  This new feature allows customers to export 12 months of hourly data in an 8760 format.  With this improvement, the Company complies with the hourly usage data requirements laid out in Paragraph 44 of the Order.   

Please call me at 404-506-3044 if you have any questions regarding this filing.
