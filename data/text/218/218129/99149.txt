                         PROPOSED COMMISSION MOTION
                     OF CLEAN ENERGY BUYERS ASSOCIATION

       Clean Energy Buyers Association (CEBA), by its undersigned attorneys Keyes & Fox LLP,

respectfully files this Proposed Commission Motion for the Commission’s consideration in

Georgia Power Company’s (Georgia Power) 2023 Integrated Resource Plan (IRP) Update

proceeding. CEBA requests that the Commission make a motion to amend the Stipulation filed in

this proceeding on March 27, 2024 by inserting the following new paragraph:

       “22.   The Company will file a proposal for a sleeved PPA program in the 2025 IRP. The

       sleeved PPA program will allow any commercial or industrial customer to work with the

       Company and a third-party developer to bring a proposed clean energy resource to the

       Company that will be dedicated to that customer. The Company will procure the resource

       on the customer’s behalf. The customer to which the resource is dedicated will sign a

       contract with Georgia Power through which the customer will pay the full cost of the

       resource along with reasonable administration costs. The Company will confer the

       environmental and financial benefits of the resource to the customer to which the resource

       is dedicated based on the resource’s actual energy production. The Company shall hold at


                                               1

       least two collaborative meetings with Staff and interested parties prior to finalizing its

       sleeved PPA proposal to be presented in the 2025 IRP filing.”

If the above new paragraph is inserted into the Stipulation, CEBA would support the Commission

approving the Stipulation as so modified.
