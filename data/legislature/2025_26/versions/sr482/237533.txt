Senate Resolution 482 By: Senators Bearden of the 30th, Cowsert of the 46th, Echols of the 49th, Kirkpatrick of the 32nd, Albers of the 56th and others
A RESOLUTION
Urging the Georgia Public Service Commission (PSC) to create a study committee for the purpose of making recommendations to the Senate with regard to how the State of Georgia can use advanced nuclear reactors to increase power generation and economic development in this state; and for other purposes.
WHEREAS, advanced nuclear reactors (ANRs) are capable of supporting Georgia's growing energy needs by providing clean, reliable power for urban centers, industrial facilities, data centers, and desalination plants; and
WHEREAS, nuclear power is more reliable than coal, wind, and solar power, offering the resilience essential for everyday use and extreme weather conditions; and
WHEREAS, ANRs can co-locate with data centers and support heavy industries by providing process heat and powering desalination plants, enhancing production, and creating high wage jobs across this state; and
WHEREAS, Georgia has the chance to lead the national competition in advanced nuclear power and an efficient ANR strategy can bring jobs, manufacturing support, and supply chain gains to this state; and
WHEREAS, the global nuclear market is projected to triple by 2050, and establishing Georgia as the preferred supplier for American ANR technology can open international opportunities and offer an alternative to Chinese and Russian nuclear reactor technology for allies and partners; and
WHEREAS, with enhanced safety, scalability, and integration potential, ANRs are a key solution for Georgia's energy reliability and stability to meet the growing energy demands of this state; and
WHEREAS, Georgia can gain competitive advantages by cutting red tape and establishing incentives to accelerate advanced nuclear deployment, overcome regulatory hurdles, and attract investment to solidify Georgia as the global nuclear energy hub.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE SENATE that the members of this body urge the Georgia Public Service Commission (PSC) to create a study committee for the purpose of making recommendations to the Senate with regard to how the State of Georgia can use advanced nuclear reactors to increase power generation and economic development in this state.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Secretary of the Senate is authorized and directed to make appropriate copies of this resolution available for distribution to the Chairman and Commissioners of the Public Service Commission.
