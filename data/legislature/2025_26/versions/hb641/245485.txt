House Bill 641 (COMMITTEE SUBSTITUTE) By: Representatives Lim of the 98th, Mathiak of the 82nd, Werkheiser of the 157th, Drenner of the 85th, Lupton of the 83rd, and others
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED AN ACT
To amend Chapter 3 of Title 46 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to electric services, so as to require certain electric suppliers to have in place a written policy regarding discontinuation of electric service to residential consumers with serious, life-threatening conditions; to provide for furnishing of such policy to consumers; to provide an effective date and applicability; to provide for related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:
SECTION 1. Chapter 3 of Title 46, relating to electrical service, is amended in Code Section 46-3-11, relating to application by electric supplier of discriminatory rates, charges, or service rules or regulations and prohibited acts by electric suppliers generally, by adding a new subsection to read as follows: "(c) Beginning January 1, 2027, any electric supplier that serves residential consumers shall have in place a written policy regarding discontinuation of electric service to residential consumers with serious, life-threatening conditions, and shall furnish such written policy upon request by a consumer or potential consumer."
SECTION 2. All laws and parts of laws in conflict with this Act are repealed.
