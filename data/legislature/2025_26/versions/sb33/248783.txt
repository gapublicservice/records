Sen Floor Amend 1 AM 44 0292
MOOT Senators Kirkpatrick of the 32nd, Parent of the 44th, and Robertson of the 29th offered the following amendment #1:
Amend the House Committee on Agriculture and Consumer Affairs substitute to SB 33 (LC 44 3506S) by inserting "to provide for compliance with federal rules and regulations;" after "so as" on line 2.
By replacing "paragraph (3)" with "paragraphs (3), (11), (14), and (16)" on line 15.
By inserting after line 20 the following: "(3.5) 'Container' means the innermost wrapping, packaging, or vessel in direct contact with a consumable hemp product in which the final such product is enclosed for retail sale to consumers, including a jar, bottle, bag, box, packet, can, carton, or cartridge. Such term does not include bulk shipping containers or outer wrappings that are not essential for the final retail delivery or sale to an end consumer for personal or household use." "(11) 'Hemp' means the Cannabis sativa L. plant and any part of such plant, including the seeds thereof and all derivatives, extracts, cannabinoids, isomers, acids, salts, and salts of isomers, whether growing or not, with a total delta-9-THC THC concentration that does not exceed the legal limit. Such term does not include: (A) Any viable seeds from a Cannabis sativa L. plant that exceed a total tetrahydrocannabinols concentration, including tetrahydrocannabinolic acid of 0.3 percent in the plant on a dry weight basis; (B) Any intermediate hemp derived cannabinoid product containing: (i) Cannabinoids that are not capable of being naturally produced by a Cannabis sativa L. plant; (ii) Cannabinoids that:

(I) Are capable of being naturally produced by a Cannabis sativa L. plant; and (II) Were synthesized or manufactured outside the plant; or (iii) Constitute more than a 0.3 percent combined total of: (I) Total tetrahydrocannabinols, including tetrahydrocannabinolic acid; and (II) Any other cannabinoids that have similar effects or are marketed to have similar effects on humans or animals as a tetrahydrocannabinol; (C) Any intermediate hemp derived cannabinoid products which are marketed to be sold as a final product or directly to an end consumer for personal or household use; or (D) Any consumable hemp products containing: (i) Cannabinoids that are not capable of being naturally produced by a Cannabis sativa L. plant; (ii) Cannabinoids that: (I) Are capable of being naturally produced by a Cannabis sativa L. plant; and (II) Were synthesized or manufactured outside the plant; or (iii) Greater than 0.4 milligram combined total per container of: (I) Total tetrahydrocannabinols, including tetrahydrocannabinolic acid; and (II) Any other cannabinoids that have similar effects or are marketed to have similar effects on humans or animals as a tetrahydrocannabinol." "(14) 'Industrial hemp product' means any hemp product that is not a consumable hemp product or an intermediate hemp derived cannabinoid product. (14.5) 'Intermediate hemp derived cannabinoid product' means a hemp product other than a consumable hemp product or an industrial hemp product which: (A) Is not yet in the final form or preparation and is marketed or intended to be used or consumed by a human or animal; or (B) Is a powder, liquid, tablet, oil, or other product which is marketed or intended to be mixed, dissolved, formulated, or otherwise added to or prepared with or into any other substance prior to administration or consumption."
