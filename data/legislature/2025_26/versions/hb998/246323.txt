SECTION 4. Said title is further amended in revising Chapter 2, relating to the Public Service Commission, by adding a new Code section to read as follows: "46-2-25.4. (a) As used in this Code section, the term 'large load customer' means customers with an expected total peak demand of 100 megawatts or greater at one or more premises located on one tract or contiguous tracts of land. (b) Notwithstanding the provisions of Code Section 46-3-11, no costs incurred by an electric utility, including, but not limited to, costs associated with increased fuel requirements, generation costs, and transmission costs that: (1) Are substantially related to the provision of electric services to large load customers; and
