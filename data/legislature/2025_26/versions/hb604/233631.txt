House Bill 604 By: Representatives Jasperse of the 11th, Cox of the 28th, Tarvin of the 2nd, Powell of the 33rd, and Ridley of the 22nd
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED AN ACT
To amend Chapter 2 of Title 21 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to elections and primaries generally, so as authorize local election superintendents to conduct manual recounts of ballots following the computation, canvassing, and tabulation of returns in an election; to address discrepancies raised by such recounts; to provide for the termination of such recounts if they conflict with other duties of the superintendent; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:
SECTION 1. Chapter 2 of Title 21 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to elections and primaries generally, is amended in Code Section 21-2-493, relating to computation, canvassing, and tabulation of returns, investigation of discrepancies in vote counts, recount procedure, posting of digital images of scanned paper ballots, certification of returns, and change in returns, by adding a new subsection to read as follows: "(h.1)(1) Upon completing all tasks required by subsections (a) through (h) of this Code section, the superintendent may conduct a manual recount of all ballots cast within a county or municipality. Such manual recount shall occur at the same location as the
computation and canvassing provided for in subsection (a) of this Code section and shall be open to the public. For the purpose of such a manual recount, the superintendent may organize his or her assistants into sections, each of whom may simultaneously proceed with the manual recount provided for by this subsection. (2) The results of such manual recount shall be unofficial, but if the results of such a manual recount indicate a discrepancy with the official results of the computation, canvassing, and tabulation provided for in this Code section, any such discrepancy shall be addressed as provided for in subsections (b) through (h) of this Code section. (3) The manual recount provided for in this subsection shall in no way delay or inhibit the superintendent from complying with any requirements of this chapter. If an ongoing manual recount will prevent a superintendent from meeting any of the requirements of any provision of this chapter, such recount shall be immediately terminated."
SECTION 2. All laws and parts of laws in conflict with this Act are repealed.
