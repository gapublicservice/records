The Senate Committee on Regulated Industries and Utilities offered the following substitute to SB 553:
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED AN ACT
To amend Title 43 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to professions and businesses, so as to repeal Chapter 14, relating to electrical contractors, plumbers, conditioned air contractors, low voltage contractors, and utility contractors, and enact a new Chapter 14; to provide for the State Construction Industry Licensing Board; to provide for the Division of Electrical Contractors; to provide for the Division of Master Plumbers; to provide for the Division of Conditioned Air Contractors; to provide for the Division of Low Voltage Contractors; to provide for the Division of Utility Contractors; to provide for membership, powers, and duties of each; to provide for licensure or certification within the fields of plumbing, electrical, air conditioning, low voltage, and utility contracting; to provide for application; to provide for requirements for licensure or certification; to provide for recertification; to provide for continuing education; to provide for prohibitions; to provide for exceptions; to provide for reciprocity; to provide for licensing or certification of current or former members of the military; to provide for a misdemeanor; to provide for definitions; to provide for legislative intent; to provide for conformity with federal law; to amend Code Section 48-7-38 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to deduction for payments to classified subcontractors, disclosure of tax returns, and maintenance of list of classified subcontractors, so as to provide for a definition; to provide for related matters; to repeal conflicting laws; and for other purposes.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF GEORGIA:
SECTION 1. Title 43 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to professions and businesses, is amended by adding a new paragraph to subsection (d) of Code Section 43-1-34, relating to licenses for transitioning members of the armed forces, to read as follows: "(5) The provisions of this subsection shall be cumulative to those provided in 50 U.S.C. Section 4025a, and nothing in this subsection shall be interpreted as limiting or imposing additional obligations on portability pursuant to 50 U.S.C. Section 4025a."
SECTION 2. Said title is further amended by repealing Chapter 14, relating to electrical contractors, plumbers, conditioned air contractors, low voltage contractors, and utility contractors, and enacting a new Chapter 14 to read as follows:
"CHAPTER 14
43-14-1. This chapter is enacted for the purpose of safeguarding homeowners, other property owners, tenants, and the general public against faulty, inadequate, inefficient, or unsafe electrical, plumbing, low voltage wiring, utility contracting, or conditioned air installations. The practices of electrical contracting, plumbing contracting, low voltage contracting, utility contracting, and conditioned air contracting are declared to be businesses or professions affecting the public interest; and this chapter shall be liberally construed so as to accomplish the purposes stated in this Code section.
43-14-2. As used in this chapter, the term: (1) 'Alarm system' means any device or combination of devices used to detect a situation, causing an alarm in the event of a burglary, fire, robbery, medical emergency, or equipment failure, or on the occurrence of any other predetermined life safety event. Such term shall also include any alarm system communication system used or required to transmit notice of a life safety or intrusion event to a local or remote monitoring facility. (2) 'Board' means the State Construction Industry Licensing Board. (3) 'Conditioned air contracting' means the installation, repair, or service of conditioned air systems or conditioned air equipment, which includes, but is not limited to, the service to or installation of the electrical circuit from the electrical distribution panel to the conditioned air equipment, provided that the electrical service to the building or site is a single-phase electrical circuit not exceeding 200 amperes performed by conditioned air contractors licensed under this chapter. (4) 'Conditioned air contractor' means any person engaged in conditioned air contracting under express or implied contract or that bids for, offers to perform, purports to have the capacity to perform, or does perform conditioned air contracting services under express or implied contract. (5) 'Conditioned Air Contractor Class I' means any individual licensed under this chapter to conduct business as a Residential and Light Commercial entity for air conditioning and heat pump systems of 60,000 BTUs of cooling or less, and for gas heating systems of 175,000 BTUs or less. The scope of practice for a Conditioned Air Contractor Class I shall include, but is not limited to, the business of permitting jobs, bidding on projects, and performing maintenance, service, and repairs of equipment and components, as well as installing new equipment and components, including ductwork, refrigerant lines, gas piping, controls, and accessories.
(6) 'Conditioned Air Contractor Class II' means any individual licensed under this chapter to conduct business as a residential and commercial entity, without limitations or restrictions on project size or equipment types, except for the installation of boilers, which is licensed under the Office of the Commissioner of Insurance and Safety Fire. The scope of practice for a Conditioned Air Contractor Class II shall include, but is not limited to, the design, bidding, permitting, and the performance of maintenance, service, and repairs of equipment and components, as well as the installation of new and replacement systems, including split air conditioning and heat pump systems, package units, variable refrigerant flow systems, chillers, ventilation systems, and all associated components. (7) 'Conditioned air equipment' means heating and air conditioning equipment covered under state codes and the fuel gas piping system on the outlet side of the gas meter. (8) 'Electrical contracting' means the installation, maintenance, alteration, or repair of any electrical equipment, apparatus, control system, or electrical wiring device which is attached to or incorporated into any building or structure in this state but shall not include low voltage contracting. (9) 'Electrical contractor' means any individual licensed under this chapter as Electrical Contractor Class I or Electrical Contractor Class II by the Division of Electrical Contractors, engaged in the business of electrical contracting under express or implied contract or that bids for, offers to perform, purports to have the capacity to perform, or does perform electrical contracting services under express or implied contract. (10) 'Electrical Contractor Class I' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Electrical Contractors to perform work as an electrical contractor on residential multifamily structures of not more than two levels or single-family dwellings of up to three levels, provided that structures shall have single-phase electrical installations which do not exceed 400 amperes at the service drop or the service lateral.
(11) 'Electrical Contractor Class II' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Electrical Contractors to perform work as an electrical contractor without restriction, including, but not limited to, the installation of raceway systems, including pull boxes, junction boxes, conduit bodies, service entrances, metering devices, cabinets, switchboards, service risers, and fasteners, overcurrent protective devices, disconnecting means, ground electrodes, main bonding jumpers, ground fault protectors, switches, disconnects, and controls that supply lighting fixtures, appliances, electrical circuits, controls for heating and air conditioning equipment, and other utilization and general use equipment, according to installation requirements set by the state minimum standard codes, the National Electrical Code, and any successor code. (12) 'Executive director' means the executive director of the State Construction Industry Licensing Board. (13) 'Full-time experience' means working at least 2,000 hours in a calendar year in a qualifying capacity, with each 2,000 hours of documented work equating to one year of experience for purposes of licensure or qualifications requirements. (14) 'General low voltage system' means any electrical system, other than an alarm or telecommunications system, that involves low voltage wiring and consists of a group or combination of interconnected components functioning together to perform a specific purpose using low voltage power or signaling, including devices, wiring, control equipment, software, and network connections operating as a single, integrated system. (15) 'Journeyman plumber' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers who engages in the installation, maintenance, alteration, or repair of piping, fixtures, and appliances, including water heaters, and all appurtenances thereto, in connection with sanitary drainage systems, storm drainage systems, venting systems, fuel gas piping systems on the outlet side of tanks or gas meters, or public or private water supply systems, whether located within or adjacent to any building, structure, conveyance, or manhole under the direction of a
plumbing contractor. A journeyman plumber shall have the ability to install or service a single phase of the electrical connection between the electrical disconnect and water heater, plumbing appliance or appurtenance, provided that the work performed adheres to the state minimum standard plumbing code, and state minimum standard fuel gas code. (16) 'License' means a valid license or certification issued by a division of the board, which authorizes the named person to engage in the activity prescribed thereon. (17) 'Low voltage contracting' means the installation, alteration, service, or repair of a power limited or signal based system that utilizes low voltage wiring and components to transmit information, control devices, or monitor conditions. Such term shall not include electrical power distribution systems. (18) 'Low voltage contractor' means any person licensed under this chapter by the Division of Low Voltage Contractors who purports to have the capacity to perform, or does perform low voltage contracting services under express or implied contract, including the installation, alteration, servicing, or repair of integrated systems made up of wiring, devices, software, and networked components that function together as a complete solution. (19) 'Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-A' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Low Voltage Contractors to perform work restricted to alarm systems and general low voltage systems. (20) 'Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-G' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Low Voltage Contractors to perform work restricted to general low voltage systems. (21) 'Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-T' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Low Voltage Contractors to perform work restricted to telecommunications systems and general low voltage systems. (22) 'Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-U' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Low Voltage Contractors to engage in low voltage contracting
without limitation as to specialty or system type, and who is authorized to design, install, alter, service, repair, and maintain all low voltage systems, including general low voltage systems, alarm and life-safety systems, and telecommunications systems for voice, data, and network infrastructure, based on demonstrated competence across multiple low voltage disciplines. (23) 'Low voltage wiring' means wiring systems and associated control circuits which include, but are not limited to: (A) Wiring systems of 50 volts or less; (B) Wiring systems having a voltage in excess of 50 volts, provided such systems consist solely of power limited circuits meeting the definition of a Class II and Class III wiring system as defined in Article 725 of the National Electrical Code; or (C) Line voltage wiring having a voltage not in excess of 300 volts to ground and installed from the load-side terminals of a suitable disconnecting means which has been installed for the specific purpose of supplying the low voltage wiring system involved or installed from a suitable junction box. (24) 'Master Plumber Class I' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers, who is authorized to perform plumbing work on residential single-family dwellings, townhomes, and multifamily dwellings, including duplexes, hotels, motels, and apartment buildings, not exceeding three stories in height, and on commercial buildings not exceeding 20,000 square feet in total building area. (25) 'Master Plumber Class II' means any individual licensed under this chapter by the Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers, who is authorized to perform work and contract for all scopes of plumbing work, without limitation, as defined in the state minimum standard plumbing code and the state minimum standard fuel gas code. Such work shall include, but is not limited to, plumbing work on commercial buildings
of any size; industrial facilities; institutional occupancies; multistory structures of any occupancy classification; and specialized use facilities. (26) 'Plumbing' means: (A) The practice of installing, maintaining, altering, or repairing piping fixtures, appliances, and appurtenances in connection with sanitary drainage or storm drainage facilities, venting systems, medical gas piping systems, natural gas piping systems on the outlet side of gas meters, or public or private water supply systems within or adjacent to any building, structure, conveyance, or manhole; and (B) The practice of and materials used in installing, maintaining, extending, or altering the natural gas, storm-water, sewerage, and water supply systems of any premises to their connection with any point of public disposal or other acceptable terminal. (27) 'Plumbing contractor' means any person licensed under this chapter as a Master Plumber Class I or Master Plumber Class II by the Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers, who is engaged in the business of plumbing contracting, including bidding for, offering to perform, purporting to have the capacity to perform, or performing plumbing contracting services under an express or implied contract, and who is responsible for obtaining required permits, ensuring compliance with applicable codes, coordinating inspections, performing or correcting work as necessary, and securing final approval of permitted work. Such term shall not include a licensed journeyman plumber. (28) 'Telecommunications system' means a system of transmission facilities, conductors, equipment, and associated apparatus used to convey voice, data, video, or other information by electromagnetic means, whether wired or wireless, consistent with telecommunications circuits and systems recognized under the state minimum standard codes, including systems capable of local or remote monitoring, management, or administration through secured access. (29) 'Utility contracting' means undertaking to construct, erect, alter, or repair or have constructed, erected, altered, or repaired any utility system.
(30) 'Utility contractor' means a sole proprietorship, partnership, or corporation licensed under this chapter by the Division of Utility Contractors which is engaged in utility contracting under express or implied contract or which bids for, offers to perform, purports to have the capacity to perform, or does perform utility contracting under express or implied contract. (31) 'Utility foreman' means any individual certified under this chapter by the Division of Utility Contractors who is employed by a licensed utility contractor to directly supervise a project performing the construction, erection, alteration, or repair of utility systems in the field. (32) 'Utility manager' means any individual certified under this chapter by the Division of Utility Contractors who is employed by a licensed utility contractor to oversee multiple projects performing the construction, erection, alteration, or repair of utility systems. (33) 'Utility system' means: (A) Any system at least five feet underground, when installed or accessed by trenching, open cut, cut and cover, or other similar construction methods which install or access the system from the ground surface, including, but not limited to, gas distribution systems, electrical distribution systems, communication systems, water supply systems, and sanitary sewerage and drainage systems; and (B) Reservoirs and filtration plants, water and waste-water treatment plants, leachate collection and treatment systems associated with landfills, and pump stations, when the system distributes or collects a service, product, or commodity for which a fee or price is paid for said service, product, or commodity or for the disposal of said service, product, or commodity.
43-14-3. (a) There is created within the executive branch of state government the State Construction Industry Licensing Board. The board shall be assigned to the Secretary of State's office for
administrative purposes and shall be under the jurisdiction of the division director, in accordance with Code Section 43-1-2. (b) The board shall be composed of 27 members as follows: (1) Five members known as the Division of Electrical Contractors, one of whom shall be a professional engineer engaged in electrical practice, another of whom shall have experience as an inspector with electrical inspection duties of a county or municipality or a third-party inspector regularly providing inspections to a county or municipality, and the remaining three of whom shall be licensed electrical contractors in this state; (2) Five members known as the Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers, one of whom shall have experience as an inspector with plumbing inspection duties of a county or municipality or a third-party inspector regularly providing inspections to a county or municipality, three of whom shall be master or contracting plumbers, and one of whom shall be a journeyman plumber; (3) Five members known as the Division of Conditioned Air Contractors, one of whom shall be a licensed professional engineer engaged in mechanical practice, one of whom shall have experience as an inspector with conditioned air inspection duties of a county or municipality or a third-party inspector regularly providing inspections to a county or municipality, and three of whom shall be conditioned air contractors with more than five years of installation and service experience in the trade; (4) Five members known as the Division of Low Voltage Contractors, one of whom shall be a Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-A, one of whom shall be a Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-U, one of whom shall be a Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-T, one of whom shall be a professional electrical engineer, and one of whom shall have experience as an inspector with electrical inspection duties of a county or municipality or contracted by a county or municipality to perform electrical inspections; (5) Five members known as the Division of Utility Contractors, three of whom shall be utility contractors, one of whom shall be a registered professional engineer, and one of
whom shall be an insurance company representative engaged primarily in the bonding of construction projects; and (6) Two members who shall not have any connection with the utility contracting, low voltage contracting, electrical contracting, plumbing, or conditioned air contracting businesses whatsoever but who shall have a recognized interest in consumer affairs and consumer protection concerns. (c) All members shall be appointed by the Governor, subject to confirmation by the Senate, for four-year terms. Each member shall serve until a successor has been duly appointed and qualified. Members shall be eligible for reappointment. Vacancies occurring prior to the expiration of a term, for whatever cause, shall be filled by appointment of the Governor for the unexpired portion of the term. Any member who ceases to meet the qualifications required for original appointment during the member's term shall automatically forfeit membership on the board. (d) Each member of the board shall take an oath of office before the Governor or the Governor's designee to faithfully perform the duties of such office. (e) The Governor may remove any member for failure to attend meetings, neglect of duty, incompetence, revocation or suspension of professional trade license, or other dishonorable conduct. (f) Members of the board shall be reimbursed as provided for in subsection (f) of Code Section 43-1-2.
43-14-4. (a) The board chairperson shall be rotated among the five divisions enumerated in Code Section 43-14-3. In the instance of a vacancy by the board chairperson, the members of the board shall select a current member to serve the former board chairperson's unexpired term. The individual selected to fill the vacancy shall be a member of the same division as the previous board chairperson.
(b) The board shall meet at the call of the board chairperson or upon the recommendation of a majority of its members. (c) Each division within the board shall also elect from its membership a division chairperson who shall serve for a term of two years. Any vacancy in the office of division chairperson shall be filled by one of the members for the unexpired term. (d) Any member elected division chairperson may serve more than one consecutive term of office. (e) Each division shall carry out its powers and duties provided for in this chapter with the assistance of the division director and staff of the board. (f) Each division shall meet at the call of its division chairperson. (g) Three members of each division shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business of such division.
43-14-5. The board shall have the power to: (1) Request from the various state departments and other agencies and authorities of the state and its political subdivisions and their agencies and authorities such available information as it may require in its work; and all such agencies and authorities shall furnish such requested available information to the board within a reasonable time; (2) Provide by regulation for reciprocity with other states in the registration and licensing of electrical contractors, master plumbers, journeyman plumbers, low voltage contractors, utility contractors, or conditioned air contractors and in the certification of utility contracting foremen, provided that such other states have requirements substantially similar to the requirements in force in this state for registration, licensure, and certification; provided, further, that a similar privilege is offered to residents of this state; (3) Adopt an official seal for its use and modify such seal as the board deems necessary;
(4) Establish rules and regulations, consistent with this chapter, for the protection of the health, safety, and welfare of the public and for regulating the licensing of electrical contracting, plumbing contracting, low voltage contracting, utility contracting, and conditioned air contracting professionals; and (5) Upon notice and hearing authorized and conducted in accordance with Code Section 43-14-17 and any rules and regulations promulgated by the board, either by the board directly or through a valid delegation of the board's enforcement power to a division thereof, assess civil penalties in an amount up to $10,000.00 per violation against any person found to be in violation of any requirement of this chapter.
43-14-6. (a) The Division of Electrical Contractors, with respect to applicants for a license to engage in or licensees engaging in the business of electrical contracting; the Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers, with respect to applicants for a license to engage in or licensees engaging in the business of plumbing as a Master Plumber Class I, Master Plumber Class II, or journeyman plumber; the Division of Low Voltage Contractors, with respect to applicants for a license to engage in or licensees engaging in the business of low voltage contracting; the Division of Utility Contractors, with respect to applicants for a license to engage in or licensees engaging in the business of utility contracting and with respect to applicants for a certificate to be a utility manager or utility foreman or holders of a utility manager or utility foreman certificate; and the Division of Conditioned Air Contractors, with respect to applicants for a license to engage in or licensees engaging in the business of conditioned air contracting, shall: (1) Assess and approve examination requirements for each class or type of license or certificate issued under this chapter, as follows: (A) The Division of Electrical Contractors shall approve separate examinations for Electrical Contractor Class I and Electrical Contractor Class II;
(B) The Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers shall approve separate examinations for Master Plumber Class I, Master Plumber Class II, and Journeyman Plumbers; (C) The Division of Conditioned Air Contractors shall approve separate examinations for Conditioned Air Contractor Class I and Conditioned Air Contractor Class II; and (D) The Division of Low Voltage Contractors shall approve separate examinations for Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-A, Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-T, Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-G, and Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-U; (2) Register and license or grant a certificate and issue renewal licenses and renewal certificates biennially to all persons meeting the qualifications for a license or certificate. The following licenses or certificates shall be issued by the divisions: (A) Electrical Contractor Class I; (B) Electrical Contractor Class II; (C) Master Plumber Class I; (D) Master Plumber Class II; (E) Journeyman Plumber; (F) Conditioned Air Contractor Class I; (G) Conditioned Air Contractor Class II; (H) Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-A; (I) Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-T; (J) Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-G; (K) Low Voltage Contractor Class LV-U; (L) Utility Contractor; (M) Utility Manager (certificate); and (N) Utility Foreman (certificate); (3) Investigate, with the aid of the division director, alleged violations of this chapter or other laws and rules and regulations of the board relating to the profession;
(4) After notice and hearing, have the power to reprimand any person, licensee, or certificate holder, or to suspend, revoke, or cancel the license or certificate of or refuse to grant, renew, or restore a license or certificate to any person, licensee, or certificate holder upon any one of the following grounds: (A) The commission of any false, fraudulent, or deceitful act or the use of any forged, false, or fraudulent document in connection with the license or certificate requirements of this chapter or the rules and regulations of the board; (B) Failure at any time to comply with the requirements for a license or certificate under this chapter or the rules and regulations of the board; (C) Engaging in any fraudulent, deceptive, or dishonest conduct in the practice of the profession regulated under this chapter that is likely to deceive, defraud, or harm the public, including knowingly making misleading, deceptive, untrue, or fraudulent representations in the practice of the profession or on any document connected therewith; (D) Knowingly aiding, abetting, assisting, or permitting any unlicensed or noncertified person or entity to engage in the practice of the profession regulated under this chapter, including by lending, leasing, renting, assigning, or otherwise allowing the use of one's license, or by combining or conspiring with such person or entity to evade the provisions of this chapter; (E) Knowingly performing any act which in any way assists an unlicensed or noncertified person to practice such profession; (F) Violating, directly or indirectly, or assisting in or abetting any violation of any provision of this chapter or any rule or regulation of the board; or (G) The performance of any faulty, inadequate, inefficient, or unsafe electrical contracting, plumbing, low voltage contracting, utility contracting, or conditioned air contracting likely to endanger life, health, or property. The performance of any work that does not comply with the standards set by state codes or by local codes in
jurisdictions where such codes are adopted, provided that such local codes are as stringent as the state codes, or by other codes or regulations which have been adopted by the board, shall be prima-facie evidence of the faulty, inadequate, inefficient, or unsafe character of such electrical, plumbing, low voltage contracting, utility contracting, or conditioned air contracting; provided, however, that the board, in its sole discretion, for good cause shown and under such conditions as it may prescribe, may restore a license to any person whose license has been suspended or revoked; (5) Review amendments to or revisions in the state minimum standard codes as prepared pursuant to Part 1 of Article 1 of Chapter 2 of Title 8; and the Department of Community Affairs shall be required to provide to the division director a copy of any amendment to or revision in the state minimum standard codes at least 45 days prior to the adoption thereof; and (6) Do all other things necessary and proper to exercise their powers and perform their duties in accordance with this chapter. (b) The divisions mentioned in subsection (a) of this Code section shall also hear appeals resulting from the suspension of licenses by an approved municipal or county licensing or inspection authority pursuant to Code Section 43-14-16.
43-14-7. (a) All orders and processes of the board and the divisions of the board shall be signed and attested by the division director; and any notice or legal process necessary to be served upon the board or the divisions may be served upon the division director. (c) In accordance with Code Section 43-1-2, the division director shall be authorized to review each application for the issuance, renewal, or reinstatement of a license under this chapter, and upon determining that an application satisfies all of the qualifications, attainments, and other conditions required for the issuance, renewal, or reinstatement of a particular license under this chapter and the rules and regulations promulgated pursuant to this chapter, shall be authorized, on behalf of the board, to issue, renew, or reinstate the license of the applicant.
