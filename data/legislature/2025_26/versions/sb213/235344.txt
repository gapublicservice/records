Floor Amend 1 AM 60 0005
ADOPTED
Senators Kirkpatrick of the 32nd, Strickland of the 42nd, Cowsert of the 46th, and Rhett of the 33rd offered the following amendment #1:
Amend SB 213 (LC 60 0080) by striking "as an unlawful, unfair, and deceptive trade practice" on lines 3 and 4.
By inserting before "to provide for definitions;" on line 4 the following: to limit enforcement to public enforcement by the Attorney General;
By replacing "Without otherwise limiting the definition of unfair or deceptive acts or practices under this part, it" on lines 49 and 50 with "It".
By inserting between lines 56 and 57 the following: (d) Any violation of subsection (b) of this Code section shall constitute an unfair or deceptive act or practice in the conduct of consumer transactions under Part 2 of Article 15 of this chapter, the 'Fair Business Practices Act of 1975'; provided, however, that enforcement against such violations shall only be by public enforcement by the Attorney General pursuant to this part and shall not be enforceable through a private right of action under Code Section 10-1-399.
By redesignating subsection (d) on line 57 as subsection (e).
By inserting between lines 64 and 65 the following:
Floor Amend 1 AM 60 0005
SECTION 3. Said part is further amended in subsection (a) of Code Section 10-1-399, relating to civil actions for violations and remedies, by striking "Any" at the beginning of subsection (a), and inserting "Except as otherwise provided, any" in lieu thereof.
By redesignating Section 2 as Section 4.
