Senator Hufstetler of the 52nd offered the following amendment #1:
Amend the Senate Committee on Regulated Industries and Utilities substitute to SB 94 (LC 55 0491S) by replacing line 2 with the following: public transportation, so as to revise provisions relating to the regulation of public utilities; to prohibit certain costs incurred by an electric utility as a result of providing electric services to commercial data centers from being included in any rates or charges of such electric utility unless certain conditions are met; to reestablish a consumer utility counsel to represent consumers
By inserting in between lines 14 and 15 the following: Title 46 of the Official Code of Georgia Annotated, relating to public utilities and public transportation, is amended by adding a new Code section to read as follows: "46-2-25.4. (a) Notwithstanding the provisions of Code Section 46-3-11, no costs incurred by an electric utility, including, but not limited to, costs associated with increased fuel requirements, generation costs, and transmission costs that: (1) Are related to the provision of electric services to commercial data centers; and (2) Would not have been incurred but for the electric demand of such commercial data centers, shall be included in any rates or charges approved or allowed to go into effect under Code Section 46-2-25 or 46-2-26 unless such rates or charges are designed to recover such costs solely from commercial data centers or are prorated based on electric demand.
