SECTION 3. Said article is further amended by adding a new Code section to read as follows: "43-34-103.1. (a) As used in this Code section, the term: (1) 'Applicant' means an individual applying to be licensed as a physician assistant in this state. (2) 'GCIC' means the Georgia Crime Information Center established under Article 2 of Chapter 3 of Title 35. (b) The board is authorized to conduct and receive criminal background checks by the submission of fingerprints or other identifying information to the Federal Bureau of Investigation through GCIC.

(c) In order to determine an applicant's suitability as a physician assistant, the board shall require such applicant to furnish the board with a full set of fingerprints or other identifying information to enable criminal background checks to be conducted. (d) The board shall submit the fingerprints or other identifying information furnished by an applicant to GCIC. GCIC is authorized to submit such fingerprints or information to the Federal Bureau of Investigation for a national criminal background check. (e) GCIC shall notify the board in writing of its findings, including the inability to identify a criminal record. All findings received by the board shall be used for the exclusive purpose of carrying out its responsibilities under this Code section, shall not be a public record, shall be confidential, and shall not be disclosed to any other person or agency, except to any person or agency which otherwise has a legal right to inspect such data. Any records obtained in furtherance of this Code section shall be maintained by the board pursuant to laws regarding such records and the rules and regulations of the Federal Bureau of Investigation and GCIC, as applicable. (f) By applying for a license under this article, an applicant consents to the performance of criminal background checks as provided for in this Code section. The applicant shall be responsible for any fees associated with the performance of such background checks."
